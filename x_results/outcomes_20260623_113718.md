# Outcomes

The engagement with VONQ is designed to deliver tangible business improvements by establishing an investor-ready exit reporting suite that enables transparent, data-driven equity narratives. By validating KPI frameworks, identifying data gaps, and producing actionable dashboard concepts, VONQ will be equipped to underpin its M&A sale process with credible, scalable reporting essential for investor communications and decision-making. Success will be measured by the readiness of Phase 1 artefacts, enabling rapid transition to implementation and minimizing risks associated with timeline delays or incomplete data.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Investor-Ready Reporting Design:** VONQ will have a validated exit reporting suite, including dashboard mock-ups and conceptual data models, enabling effective presentation of business performance to potential acquirers and advisors.
- **Clear Equity Narrative and KPI Framework:** Senior leadership and advisors will gain a prioritized set of KPIs, aligned to the equity narrative, covering commercial, financial, and operational metrics essential for transaction reporting and stakeholder confidence.
- **Data Gap Visibility and Remediation Priorities:** The business will benefit from a comprehensive assessment of data gaps across source systems and actionable recommendations for remediation, ensuring readiness for due diligence and investor scrutiny.
- **Enhanced Stakeholder Communication:** Structured reporting outputs and documentation will facilitate transparent, credible engagement with investors, advisors, and leadership throughout the sale process.
- **Scalable, Automation-Ready Infrastructure:** The conceptual data model and tooling recommendations will establish a foundation for future automation, allowing ongoing data refreshes and integration as business requirements evolve post-transaction.
- **Reduced Transaction Risk:** By addressing critical reporting and data readiness issues in advance, VONQ minimizes risks of delay, reputational concerns, and challenges in presenting a compelling equity narrative to buyers [Client Questionnaire].

Success will be demonstrated by the completion and sign-off of Phase 1 design artefacts—ready to initiate Phase 2 within the exit timeline and enabling VONQ to present credible, data-backed narratives for its M&A process.
