# Outcomes

The engagement with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC is expected to deliver measurable enhancements to data-driven decision making across the client’s pipeline and marketing operations. By developing automated, integrated Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting, PURE will gain granular visibility into the effectiveness of their marketing spend and the conversion journey from lead generation to assets under management. These improvements will empower leadership and stakeholders to optimize business strategies with actionable insights.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Granular Pipeline Insights:** Enables deeper analysis of lead conversion pathways and performance bottlenecks, allowing for targeted improvements across the customer journey.

- **Actionable Marketing ROI Measurement:** Provides integrated, accurate tracking of Customer Acquisition Cost and associated return on investment, informing smarter allocation of marketing resources.

- **Automated, Reliable Reporting:** Eliminates manual data aggregation and increases the frequency of pipeline and CAC reporting, ensuring timely access to critical business metrics.

- **Leadership Empowerment with Strategic KPIs:** Delivers clear, visualized dashboards and KPI definitions, equipping senior leadership with the information needed to make informed growth-oriented decisions.

- **Scalable Data Maturity:** Establishes a flexible data platform that supports future integration needs and reporting expansion, positioning PURE for ongoing business growth and operational scalability.

- **Mitigated Visibility Risk:** Addresses the lack of integrated CAC and pipeline insights, reducing risks of under-evaluated marketing effectiveness and growth strategies.
