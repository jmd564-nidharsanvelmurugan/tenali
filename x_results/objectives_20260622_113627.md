# Objectives

- Deliver a comprehensive Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data model and reporting suite to provide granular insights into pipeline performance and marketing return on investment.
- Automate data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes to ensure timely and consistent analytics across all relevant platforms.
- Enable leadership to gain a detailed, actionable understanding of the journey from lead generation to AUM, as well as precise measurement of marketing effectiveness and spend.
- Enhance Power BI dashboards with clear KPI definitions and improved visualizations to support better decision-making and user experience.
- Strengthen pipeline reporting and customer acquisition cost tracking to drive operational efficiency and data-driven growth.
- Establish a scalable reporting platform capable of integrating with Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Azure Fabric, supporting future data sources and evolving business requirements.
