# Outcomes

The engagement with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC will deliver measurable improvements to pipeline reporting and customer acquisition analytics. By developing granular Lead-to-AUM (L2A) conversion and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) metrics, the initiative empowers leadership with actionable insights that drive strategic decision-making, optimize marketing spend, and increase operational efficiency. The newly automated, integrated reporting solution will enhance data maturity, enabling Pure to scale its analytics capabilities alongside business growth.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Granular Pipeline Performance Insights:** Provide stakeholders with detailed visibility into the lead-to-AUM journey, leveraging conversion metrics to identify opportunities and bottlenecks within the client acquisition funnel.
- **Optimized Marketing ROI Measurement:** Enable marketing teams and leadership to assess the effectiveness of campaigns through integrated CAC reporting, supporting data-driven allocation of resources for maximum return.
- **Automated Reporting Processes:** Streamline data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh cycles, reducing manual workload and minimizing the risk of human error in pipeline and cost tracking.
- **Improved Data Quality and Integration:** Ensure reliable, accurate reporting by integrating QuickBooks data and conducting comprehensive quality assessments, addressing previous gaps in CAC insights for more robust analysis.
- **Executive-Ready Power BI Dashboards:** Enhance user experience through clear, visually compelling KPI definitions and dashboards, facilitating faster, more confident business decisions at all levels.
- **Scalable Analytics Foundation:** Establish a mature, extensible data platform that supports future reporting needs and integration with new data sources, positioning Pure Financial Advisors for continued growth and innovation.
