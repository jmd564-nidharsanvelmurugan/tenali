# Objectives

- Establish a unified and detailed data model for Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) to provide clear, actionable insights into sales and marketing performance.  
- Enable automated data loading, processing, and reporting to ensure accuracy, timeliness, and reduced manual effort.  
- Deliver full visibility of the customer journey from initial lead through to assets under management, supporting data‑driven decision‑making by leadership and marketing teams.  
- Enhance Power BI dashboards with standardized KPIs and intuitive visualizations that clearly communicate performance outcomes.  
- Create a scalable reporting foundation capable of integrating new data sources and supporting future business growth.
