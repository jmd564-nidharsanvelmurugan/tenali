# Objectives

- Establish a comprehensive exit reporting suite featuring clearly defined equity narratives and a prioritised KPI framework.
- Automate data ingestion and recurring refresh processes from all relevant source systems to ensure timely, transaction-ready reporting.
- Deliver intuitive dashboards that provide investor-grade visibility into key business metrics supporting the company’s equity narrative.
- Standardise KPI definitions, calculation logic, and navigation structure to enhance user experience and consistency in reporting.
- Enhance data infrastructure readiness and reporting processes to meet the scrutiny required for the exit and investor communications.
- Ensure the proposed data model and reporting solution are scalable, supporting integration with Finance, CRM, ERP, and Qlik Warehouse as a foundation for future extensibility and automation.
