# Outcomes

This engagement will deliver measurable business impact and strategic value by enabling PURE Financial Advisors LLC to achieve deeper visibility into its Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) performance. The initiative aligns with PURE’s B2C financial advisory model and the objectives of key stakeholders, including Lee Equity Partners, by strengthening data‑driven decision‑making and marketing ROI measurement across the U.S. operations.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Granular Lead‑to‑AUM Visibility:** Development of a unified L2A data model will provide detailed insights into the full customer journey from lead generation through AUM conversion, enabling leadership to identify high‑performing acquisition channels and optimize conversion strategies.  
- **Accurate Customer Acquisition Cost Measurement:** Integration of QuickBooks and Salesforce data will allow precise calculation of CAC metrics, improving transparency into marketing spend efficiency and supporting more informed budget allocation decisions.  
- **Automated and Reliable Reporting:** Implementation of automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines will eliminate manual reporting processes, ensuring consistent, timely, and accurate updates to Power BI dashboards.  
- **Improved Marketing ROI Analysis:** Enhanced reporting capabilities will enable PURE to quantify the return on marketing investments with greater confidence, linking spend directly to AUM growth and conversion outcomes.  
- **Strengthened Data Maturity and Scalability:** Establishing a scalable data foundation within Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse will position PURE for future expansion of data sources and advanced analytics, supporting long‑term growth and operational efficiency.  
- **Informed Leadership Decision‑Making:** Delivery of clear, KPI‑driven Power BI dashboards will empower senior management to make evidence‑based decisions on pipeline performance, resource allocation, and strategic marketing initiatives.
