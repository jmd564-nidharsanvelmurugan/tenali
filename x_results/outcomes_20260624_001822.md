# Outcomes

This engagement will empower PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC with accurate, automated, and granular Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting. The solution will deliver actionable insights into pipeline performance and marketing ROI, enabling leadership to make data-driven decisions and optimize resource allocation. Success will be measured by improved visibility and refined KPIs, driving greater data maturity and scalable reporting for continued business growth.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Granular Pipeline Visibility:** Provide detailed tracking of the lead journey from initial contact to asset conversion, improving understanding of pipeline performance.
- **Actionable Marketing ROI Insights:** Measure and analyze marketing spend effectiveness using Customer Acquisition Cost KPIs, enabling smarter marketing investments.
- **Automated Reporting and Data Flows:** Reduce manual effort through automation of data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes, ensuring timely and accurate insights.
- **Enhanced Leadership Decision-Making:** Equip stakeholders with clear, Power BI-driven dashboards, offering intuitive visualizations and definitions of key metrics.
- **Scalable Data & Reporting Foundation:** Establish a robust, future-ready data platform capable of integrating additional sources and supporting Pure’s long-term growth strategies.
