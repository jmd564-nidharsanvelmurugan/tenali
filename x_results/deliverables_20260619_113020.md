# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with **Pure Financial Advisors LLC**, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

---

**1. KPI Book**  
- Comprehensive documentation of Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) KPIs, including definitions, calculation logic, and data source mapping.  

- Alignment of KPI taxonomy with industry standards and Pure’s internal reporting framework.  

- Clear linkage between KPIs and business outcomes to support marketing ROI analysis.  

---

**2. Power BI Mock‑Ups (8 Views)**  
- Design of up to eight Power BI dashboard mock‑ups illustrating the future‑state L2A and CAC reporting experience.  

- Visualization of key metrics, conversion funnels, and marketing performance insights.  

- Layout and navigation blueprint to guide the subsequent build phase and user experience design.  

---

**3. Data Model Design**  
- Detailed schema for the integrated L2A and CAC data model connecting Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Fabric, and QuickBooks.  

- Logical and physical data model diagrams showing relationships, hierarchies, and data flows.  

- Documentation of data transformation logic and integration points for automated ingestion pipelines.  

---

**4. Data Gap Assessment**  
- Identification and evaluation of missing or incomplete data elements required for accurate L2A and CAC reporting.  

- Assessment of data quality, consistency, and readiness across Salesforce, Tamarac, and QuickBooks sources.  

- Recommendations for remediation actions and prioritization of data improvement initiatives.  

---

**5. Technical Approach**  
- Detailed description of the proposed technical solution architecture, including integration methods for QuickBooks and Salesforce.  

- Specification of batch processing pipelines within Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse.  

- Outline of automation, refresh cadence, and governance considerations for scalable reporting.  

---

**6. Phase 3B Implementation Plan**  
- Structured roadmap detailing activities, dependencies, and timelines for the build and deployment phase.  

- Definition of milestones, resource requirements, and success criteria for implementation.  

- Sequencing of integration, testing, and rollout steps to ensure smooth transition from design to production.  

---

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with **Pure Financial Advisors LLC**’s leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
