# Business Impact

- **Granular Marketing ROI Visibility:** The enhanced Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting will provide PURE Financial Advisors with precise insight into marketing effectiveness, enabling data‑driven allocation of spend and optimization of acquisition channels.  
- **Operational Efficiency through Automation:** Automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines will eliminate manual reporting processes, reducing reporting cycle times and freeing up analyst capacity for higher‑value analysis.  
- **Improved Decision‑Making Accuracy:** A unified data model integrating Salesforce, QuickBooks, and Tamarac will establish a single source of truth for pipeline and cost data, improving confidence in performance metrics used by leadership and investors.  
- **Enhanced Stakeholder Transparency:** Consistent, KPI‑driven Power BI dashboards will allow senior leadership and Lee Equity Partners to monitor lead conversion and marketing ROI in real time, strengthening governance and investor confidence.  
- **Scalable Data Foundation:** The Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse‑based architecture will support future data sources and reporting needs, ensuring scalability as PURE expands its client base and service offerings.  
- **Financial Performance Optimization:** By linking marketing spend to AUM growth, PURE can identify high‑yield acquisition strategies, potentially lowering CAC by 10–15% and improving overall marketing efficiency.  

By delivering these outcomes, this engagement will enable PURE Financial Advisors LLC to transform its pipeline and marketing performance management, creating a scalable, insight‑driven foundation that supports sustainable growth and long‑term value creation in the competitive U.S. financial advisory market.
