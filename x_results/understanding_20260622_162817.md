# Understanding

- Lack of a data platform impedes the ability to efficiently consolidate, manage, and analyze business-critical information across revenue, pipeline, and operational reporting.
- Manual data processes lead to inefficiencies and increased risk of errors in key reporting activities.
- Current state limits visibility into revenue bridging and pipeline metrics, complicating performance tracking and decision-making.
- Inconsistent data handling routines delay reporting cycles and create barriers to timely insights for business stakeholders.
- The organization requires foundational capabilities for centralized data management and reporting to support data-driven decision-making.
- Effective support for data advisory and value creation initiatives is required as part of the company’s strategic relationship with private equity ownership.
