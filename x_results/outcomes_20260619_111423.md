# Outcomes

This engagement will deliver measurable business impact and strategic value by enabling PURE Financial Advisors LLC to gain granular visibility into its Lead-to-AUM (L2A) pipeline and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) performance. The initiative aligns with PURE’s B2C financial advisory model and supports both internal leadership and private equity stakeholders in driving data-informed growth and marketing efficiency.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Granular Lead-to-AUM Visibility:** The new data model and reporting suite will provide detailed insights into each stage of the client acquisition journey, allowing leadership to identify conversion bottlenecks and optimize pipeline performance.  
- **Accurate Customer Acquisition Cost Measurement:** Integration of QuickBooks and Salesforce data will enable precise calculation of CAC metrics, improving understanding of marketing spend effectiveness and ROI.  
- **Automated and Reliable Reporting:** Automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines will ensure consistent, timely, and accurate reporting, reducing manual effort and improving decision-making speed.  
- **Improved Marketing ROI Analysis:** Enhanced Power BI dashboards will allow marketing teams to assess campaign performance and allocate budgets based on data-driven insights into lead quality and conversion outcomes.  
- **Strengthened Data Maturity and Scalability:** Establishing a robust, scalable data foundation within Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse will position PURE for future expansion of analytics capabilities and integration of additional data sources.  
- **Increased Stakeholder Confidence in Data-Driven Decisions:** Consistent, transparent reporting on L2A and CAC metrics will provide senior leadership and investment partners with trusted insights to guide strategic growth initiatives.
