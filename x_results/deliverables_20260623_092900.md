# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with VONQ, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

1. **Equity Narratives Definition**
   - Comprehensive articulation of investor-facing equity narratives relevant to the exit transaction.
   - Alignment of narrative themes with VONQ’s business strengths, growth story, and investment highlights.
   - Summary document in PowerPoint or Word format delivered in Week 1 for stakeholder review and validation.

2. **Metrics That Matter KPI Framework**
   - Prioritised list of commercial, financial, and operational KPIs, including working definitions and calculation logic.
   - KPI mapping to underlying data sources and business processes.
   - Framework delivered as a structured Excel or Word matrix with supporting explanatory notes, completed by Week 2.

3. **Dashboard Mock-ups**
   - Static wireframes illustrating the proposed dashboard design, layout, and navigation structure.
   - Visualization of key metrics, trended reporting views, and visual alignment to equity narratives.
   - Mock-ups provided in PDF or PowerPoint format for interactive workshop review, delivered in Week 2.

4. **Data Feasibility Assessment**
   - Assessment and documentation of current-state data availability, quality, and integration gaps across Finance, CRM, ERP, and legacy sources.
   - Identification of critical data gaps impacting reporting readiness and proposed remediation actions.
   - Findings delivered as a written assessment report, with playback session held in Week 2.

5. **Conceptual Data Model Design**
   - High-level conceptual data model capturing entities, relationships, and key data flows necessary for the exit reporting suite.
   - Data lineage from source to dashboard to ensure transparency and auditability.
   - Model shared as a diagram (e.g., Visio or Lucidchart) with accompanying documentation, presented in Week 3.

6. **Data Infrastructure and Tooling Recommendations**
   - Recommendations on architectural approaches, data integration solutions, and supporting tooling fit for VONQ’s requirements.
   - Options analysis covering pros and cons, scalability, and alignment with the Qlik Warehouse and target operating model.
   - Recommendations summarized in a comparative options paper, delivered in Week 3.

7. **Implementation Roadmap for Phase 2**
   - Clearly sequenced plan outlining Phase 2 build activities, dependencies, milestones, and risk considerations.
   - Assignment of priority use cases, required resources, and critical path items for seamless project handover.
   - Roadmap delivered as a Gantt chart or structured project plan (PDF/Excel/PowerPoint), issued in Week 4.

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with VONQ's leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
