# Business Impact

- **Enhanced Financial Visibility:** The new Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting suite will enable PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC to accurately track marketing spend and pipeline performance, supporting data-driven budgeting and increasing clarity over financial outcomes.

- **Operational Efficiency and Automation:** Automating data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes across Salesforce, QuickBooks, and other platforms will eliminate manual data compilation steps, accelerating reporting cycles and reducing the risk of human error for impacted teams.

- **Improved Marketing ROI Measurement:** Marketing teams will gain the ability to calculate granular CAC metrics and analyze the full lead journey, enabling more precise allocation of marketing resources and supporting a higher return on marketing investments.

- **Scalable and Future-Proof Reporting:** The solution leverages Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse to lay a scalable data platform foundation, ensuring that reporting can evolve with the addition of future data sources and changing business needs.

- **Strategic Decision-Making:** Leadership will benefit from improved Power BI dashboards with robust KPI logic and clear visualizations, empowering rapid identification of pipeline bottlenecks and more effective growth strategies based on actionable insights.

This engagement will provide PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC with an integrated, automated reporting ecosystem that dramatically boosts data maturity and enables more responsive and informed business decisions. Together, these improvements deliver both immediate operational gains and strategic advantages that support Pure’s growth ambitions in the U.S. financial advisory market.
