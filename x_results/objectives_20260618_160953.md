# Objectives

- Establish a comprehensive Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data model that delivers granular insights into pipeline performance and marketing ROI.  
- Automate data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes to ensure consistent, timely, and accurate analytics.  
- Provide leadership with clear visibility into the full customer journey from lead generation to assets under management, enabling data-driven decision-making.  
- Enhance the Power BI user experience through intuitive dashboards, standardized KPI definitions, and visually clear performance tracking.  
- Build scalable reporting capabilities that support future data sources and evolving business needs, driving long-term data maturity across the organization.  
- Integrate Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Azure Fabric into a unified reporting ecosystem to enable seamless data flow and trusted insights.
