# Business Impact

- **Enhanced Marketing ROI Visibility:** The new Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting suite will provide Pure Financial Advisors with granular insight into marketing effectiveness, enabling leadership to allocate spend toward the highest‑performing channels and improve ROI measurement accuracy.  
- **Operational Efficiency Through Automation:** Automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines will eliminate manual data handling across Salesforce, QuickBooks, and Tamarac, reducing reporting cycle time by up to 50% and freeing analyst capacity for higher‑value analysis.  
- **Data‑Driven Growth Decisions:** A unified data model and Power BI dashboards will allow senior leadership and marketing teams to make informed decisions on lead conversion strategies, directly supporting Pure’s B2C growth objectives and improving lead‑to‑AUM conversion rates.  
- **Financial Transparency and Cost Control:** Integration of QuickBooks data into the reporting ecosystem will enable precise tracking of acquisition costs and profitability per lead segment, supporting tighter cost management and improved forecasting accuracy.  
- **Scalable Reporting Foundation:** The Microsoft Fabric‑based data platform will establish a scalable architecture capable of supporting future data sources and reporting needs, ensuring long‑term sustainability and readiness for continued expansion under Lee Equity Partners’ investment oversight.  
- **Stakeholder Alignment and Confidence:** Standardized KPI definitions and consistent reporting across teams will create a single source of truth, strengthening confidence in performance metrics and supporting investor‑grade transparency for private equity stakeholders.  

By delivering these outcomes, this engagement will enable Pure Financial Advisors LLC to transform its pipeline and marketing performance management approach, creating a lasting, scalable data asset that drives operational efficiency, financial clarity, and sustained growth in the competitive U.S. financial advisory market.
