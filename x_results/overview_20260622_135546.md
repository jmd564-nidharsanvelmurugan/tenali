# Overview

The data, technology, and marketing teams at PURE Financial Advisors LLC are directly involved in the company’s current pipeline and customer acquisition reporting processes. These teams utilize a range of operational systems including Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and the Power BI reporting suite. The technology environment is further supported by cloud-based infrastructure on Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse, with integration efforts underway to connect and ingest data from QuickBooks.

Presently, Power BI serves as the primary analytics tool for reporting information derived from Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks data sources. While most processes leverage these integrated platforms, there is an ongoing requirement to evaluate and facilitate data connections—particularly to ingest and ensure the quality of data from QuickBooks for effective customer acquisition cost (CAC) reporting. The current state includes batch data processing via automated pipelines within the Microsoft Fabric platform.
