# Objectives

- Establish a comprehensive Lead-to-AUM and Customer Acquisition Cost data model and reporting suite to deliver granular insights into pipeline performance and marketing ROI.
- Automate data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes to ensure timely, accurate, and consistent analytics.
- Provide leadership with detailed visibility into the journey from initial lead to assets under management, enabling data-driven evaluation of marketing effectiveness.
- Enhance Power BI dashboards with clear KPI definitions and improved visualizations for an intuitive, actionable user experience.
- Improve pipeline reporting and customer acquisition cost tracking to support robust business decision-making and operational efficiency.
- Enable scalable data and reporting infrastructure capable of integrating additional data sources and accommodating future growth requirements.
- Ensure seamless integration of the reporting platform with Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Microsoft Fabric data systems.
