# Understanding

- The client lacks granularity in current pipeline reporting, particularly in understanding the progression from lead to assets under management (Lead‑to‑AUM), limiting visibility into conversion dynamics.  
- There is insufficient integration of marketing spend and revenue data, resulting in a lack of Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) insight across the client acquisition journey.  
- The current reporting environment does not ingest or blend data from QuickBooks, creating data gaps and reducing the completeness of acquisition cost analysis.  
- The client needs to build capabilities for detailed L2A and CAC data modeling—enabling granular, end-to-end tracking of marketing performance and pipeline conversion.  
- The client requires automation of data ingestion and transformation pipelines to support regular, reliable generation of L2A and CAC reporting.  
- The new system will be used by data and technology teams, marketing teams, and senior leadership—each with distinct needs for access to accurate, timely insights into pipeline performance and marketing ROI.  
- Mandatory integrations include Salesforce (for lead and pipeline data) and QuickBooks (for marketing spend and financial context), which must be connected and ingested into the reporting platform.
