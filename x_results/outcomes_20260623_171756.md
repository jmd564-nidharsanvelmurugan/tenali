# Outcomes

The engagement will empower PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC with highly granular Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting, directly supporting more informed business decisions and optimization of marketing spend. By integrating automated, scalable reporting into existing systems, PURE will gain accurate, actionable insights that drive improved pipeline performance and transparency in customer acquisition strategies.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Actionable L2A and CAC Insights:** Delivery of enhanced Lead-to-AUM and Customer Acquisition Cost reporting to enable detailed analysis of pipeline performance and marketing ROI.
- **Granular Conversion Metrics:** Provision of accurate, automated Lead-to-AUM conversion metrics facilitating clear tracking of lead progression to assets under management.
- **Improved Marketing Effectiveness:** Integration of CAC KPIs within Power BI dashboards to support leadership’s evaluation of marketing strategies and return on investment.
- **Scalable Reporting Capabilities:** Implementation of solutions designed to support future data growth and expanding reporting needs, contributing to PURE’s long-term data maturity.
- **Automated Data Processes:** Establishment of end-to-end automation in data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes, ensuring timely and reliable access to updated business metrics.

All outcomes are strictly grounded in client questionnaire data.
