# Approach

The engagement with **Pure Financial Advisors LLC** is designed to address the firm’s specific challenges in achieving granular visibility across its Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting. Operating within the financial advisory and wealth management industry under a B2C model, Pure’s initiative focuses on strengthening its data foundation and reporting capabilities to better understand the customer journey and optimize marketing ROI. The approach leverages Pure’s existing Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse infrastructure while integrating new data sources such as QuickBooks to deliver a scalable, automated reporting solution.

Our methodology follows a structured, phased process that ensures alignment with Pure’s business objectives, technical environment, and stakeholder expectations. Each phase builds upon the previous one—from discovery and data assessment through to model design and implementation planning—providing a clear roadmap for delivering actionable insights and a robust data model that supports long‑term scalability.

---

**Phase 3A: Discovery & Design, Duration: 3 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 to Week 3**

**Summary:** Establish a comprehensive understanding of Pure’s current data landscape, define KPI logic, and design the target‑state L2A and CAC data model.

**Activities:**  
- Conduct workshops with data, technology, and marketing teams to align on KPI definitions and reporting priorities.  
- Review existing systems (Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, Microsoft Fabric, Synapse, Power BI) to map data flows and dependencies.  
- Assess data quality and availability, with particular focus on QuickBooks integration readiness.  
- Develop initial Power BI mock‑ups (up to 8 views) to visualize priority metrics and reporting use cases.  
- Produce a Data Gap Assessment identifying missing or incomplete data elements required for L2A and CAC reporting.  
- Document the Technical Approach and Data Model design, ensuring scalability and alignment with Pure’s existing infrastructure.

---

**Phase 3B: Build & Implementation Planning, Duration: 8–12 weeks, Timeline: Week 4 to Week 15**

**Summary:** Translate the design outputs into a detailed implementation plan and prepare the technical foundation for automated data ingestion and reporting.

**Activities:**  
- Finalize the L2A and CAC data model and validate KPI logic against source systems.  
- Design automated batch data pipelines within Microsoft Fabric for ingestion and transformation of Salesforce and QuickBooks data.  
- Define Power BI dataset structures and refresh automation processes to support ongoing reporting accuracy.  
- Conduct data quality testing and reconciliation to ensure consistency across all integrated sources.  
- Develop the Phase 3B Implementation Plan outlining build activities, dependencies, and resource requirements.  
- Present final Power BI mock‑ups and KPI Book for stakeholder review and approval.

---

This structured, phased approach ensures that **Pure Financial Advisors LLC**’s data platform and reporting capabilities are developed with precision, agility, and a clear focus on delivering measurable business outcomes. By combining robust data modeling with automated reporting processes, the engagement positions Pure to achieve deeper insight into its marketing effectiveness and pipeline performance while supporting scalable growth across its advisory operations.
