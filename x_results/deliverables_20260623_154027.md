# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with VONQ, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

1. **Equity Narratives Definition**
   - Clear articulation of equity narratives tailored to support VONQ’s M&A transaction and investor communications.
   - Documentation of key value drivers and themes to underpin the exit story.
   - Summary format suitable for presentation to stakeholders and advisors.
   - Delivered in Week 1 following project kickoff workshops.

2. **Metrics that Matter KPI Framework**
   - Prioritised list of commercial, financial, and operational KPIs aligned to approved equity narratives.
   - KPI definitions, including calculation logic and data source mapping.
   - Tabular framework format with supporting explanatory notes.
   - Delivered in Week 2 with interim review and refinement in Week 3.

3. **Dashboard Mock-Ups**
   - Static visual wireframes illustrating the proposed dashboard layouts, navigation, and key views.
   - Inclusion of KPI visualisations and representative data to demonstrate investor-ready reporting.
   - Provided as PDF and PowerPoint slides to facilitate discussion and feedback.
   - Delivered in Week 3, iterated based on feedback in subsequent sessions.

4. **Data Feasibility Assessment**
   - Analysis of current-state data availability and identification of any data gaps or issues impacting Phase 2 build.
   - Summary of findings from legacy and current data sources, including trended data coverage for a minimum of three years.
   - Recommendations for remediation prioritised by impact on KPI delivery.
   - Delivered in Week 2, reviewed jointly as part of the project working sessions.

5. **Conceptual Data Model Design**
   - High-level data model that maps core entities, relationships, and source integrations using the Qlik Warehouse as a foundation.
   - Visual diagrams and supporting technical documentation explaining data flows and transformation logic.
   - Designed for scalability and future extensibility to additional data sources.
   - Delivered in Week 3, accompanied by a walkthrough to technical leads.

6. **Data Infrastructure and Tooling Recommendations**
   - Comparative assessment of data tooling options, with pros, cons, and trade-offs for Qlik and any alternative platforms.
   - Recommendations for data pipeline automation, analytics, and dashboarding across core and feasible systems.
   - Decision summary and rationale document, with actionable next steps for Phase 2.
   - Delivered in Week 4 as part of the final engagement pack.

7. **Implementation Roadmap for Phase 2**
   - Phased, actionable plan outlining key milestones, dependencies, risks, and required resources for the Phase 2 build.
   - Identification of priority use cases and workstreams for technical implementation.
   - Provided as a timeline (Gantt chart) and supporting narrative for leadership decision-making.
   - Delivered in Week 4, ready for handover to VONQ and Barclays advisors.

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with VONQ's leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
