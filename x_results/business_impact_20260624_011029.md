# Business Impact

- **Enhanced Financial Visibility:** The implementation of granular Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting will provide PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC with precise insight into pipeline performance and marketing ROI, enabling leadership to make informed budget allocations and maximize asset acquisition efficiency.

- **Operational Efficiency Gains:** Automating data ingestion, transformation, and reporting processes across Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Azure platforms reduces manual reconciliation and report generation efforts, allowing Data and Technology teams to reallocate up to 20% of their time to higher-value analytics and engagement initiatives.

- **Improved ROI Through Data-Driven Decisions:** Enhanced pipeline and CAC dashboards in Power BI furnish marketing teams with actionable KPIs and conversion metrics, empowering targeted campaigns and resource optimization. This approach is anticipated to lower customer acquisition costs by up to 15% while increasing conversion rates from lead to AUM.

- **Strategic Value Creation:** Consolidating data sources and integrating with QuickBooks delivers a robust and scalable data model that supports future business growth. The solution’s flexible architecture enables expansion across new data feeds and reporting dimensions without costly system overhauls.

- **Competitive Differentiation:** By providing leadership and stakeholders with real-time, granular insights into prospect journeys and marketing effectiveness, Pure Financial Advisors secures a competitive edge in the wealth management industry, positioning itself as a data-driven leader focused on client acquisition and retention.

The proposed solution will drive PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC toward greater data maturity, sharper business performance analytics, and scalable reporting capabilities. By transforming pipeline and CAC tracking into automated, actionable intelligence, Pure Financial Advisors will strengthen operational agility, elevate decision-making, and unlock sustained growth opportunities.
