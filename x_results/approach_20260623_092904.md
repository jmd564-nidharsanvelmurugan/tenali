# Approach

This engagement will support VONQ’s exit preparation through the accelerated design and specification of an investor-grade reporting suite. Our methodology adopts a structured, collaborative approach—combining stakeholder workshops, business analysis, and data feasibility assessment to deliver actionable, transaction-ready design artefacts. The process is designed to align tightly with the client’s timeline, ensure buy-in from key stakeholders, and position the solution for scalable execution in Phase 2.

Phase 1 focuses exclusively on discovery and design, leveraging VONQ’s established Qlik Warehouse as the primary platform and integrating data from Finance, CRM, and ERP systems. Workshops will clarify the equity narrative, define prioritized KPIs, assess current data assets, and deliver dashboard mock-ups, a conceptual data model, tooling recommendations, and a roadmap for technical implementation. Technical development and system build-out are specifically out of scope; Phase 1 outputs serve as foundational artefacts for Phase 2 execution.

---

**Phase 1: Discovery & Design, Duration: 4 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 (29 June 2026) to Week 4 (24 July 2026)**  
**Summary:** This phase establishes the design foundations for VONQ’s exit reporting suite, including equity narratives, KPIs, dashboard mock-ups, conceptual data model, tooling recommendations, and an actionable implementation roadmap.

**Activities:**  
- Conduct kickoff and stakeholder workshops to define equity narratives and reporting imperatives  
- Perform a data feasibility assessment covering Finance, CRM, ERP, Qlik Warehouse, and legacy sources (for three-year trended data)  
- Design a Metrics that Matter KPI framework and specify calculation logic for key metrics  
- Develop static dashboard mock-ups illustrating proposed views, layout, and navigation structure  
- Draft conceptual data model, mapping required integrations and data flows into the Qlik Warehouse  
- Evaluate tooling options; produce recommendations (including trade-off analysis) for reporting suite extensibility  
- Prepare implementation roadmap detailing Phase 2 priorities, milestones, dependencies, and risks

---

Concluding this phase, VONQ will be equipped with validated exit reporting suite design artefacts—all aligned to investor requirements and ready for technical build-out in Phase 2. This structured approach ensures clarity around objectives, secures stakeholder buy-in, and mitigates risk during the critical transaction timeline, while delivering lasting business assets that underpin both the exit process and future reporting scalability.
