# Business Impact

- **Enhanced Marketing ROI Visibility:** The new Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting suite will provide PURE Financial Advisors with granular insights into marketing effectiveness, enabling data‑driven decisions that can improve ROI by 10–15%.  
- **Operational Efficiency Through Automation:** Automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines will eliminate manual data handling across Salesforce, QuickBooks, and Tamarac, reducing reporting cycle times by up to 50%.  
- **Single Source of Truth for Pipeline Performance:** Consolidating data from multiple systems into Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse will create a unified, trusted dataset that ensures consistent KPI definitions and reliable performance tracking across teams.  
- **Improved Stakeholder Decision‑Making:** Leadership and marketing teams will gain real‑time visibility into lead conversion and acquisition costs, supporting more accurate forecasting and resource allocation aligned with growth objectives.  
- **Scalable Data Platform for Future Growth:** The solution’s architecture will support integration of additional data sources and reporting needs, positioning PURE for long‑term scalability and readiness for future expansion or diligence events.  
- **Data Maturity and Governance Advancement:** Establishing standardized data models and KPI logic will strengthen PURE’s data governance foundation, improving accuracy and confidence in financial and operational reporting.  

By delivering these outcomes, this engagement will enable PURE Financial Advisors LLC to transform its pipeline and marketing performance reporting into a strategic growth engine—driving efficiency, profitability, and long‑term value creation within the competitive U.S. financial advisory market.
