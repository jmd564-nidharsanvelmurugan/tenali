# Outcomes

This engagement will deliver measurable business impact by enabling Poekmon to make data‑driven decisions on marketing efficiency and asset growth. Through the design of a unified Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting model, the project will strengthen transparency, automation, and performance tracking across the client journey. The outcomes will support Poekmon’s leadership and investment partner, Lee Equity Partners, in driving strategic growth and operational efficiency within their B2C financial advisory business.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Improved Lead‑to‑AUM Visibility:** A unified data model will provide end‑to‑end insight into the client journey from lead generation to assets under management, enabling leadership to identify conversion bottlenecks and optimize sales performance.  
- **Accurate and Actionable CAC Measurement:** Integration of QuickBooks with Salesforce and Tamarac will deliver precise, automated tracking of customer acquisition costs, allowing marketing teams to allocate budgets more effectively.  
- **Automated and Reliable Reporting:** Streamlined data pipelines within Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse will ensure reports are refreshed automatically, reducing manual effort and improving data accuracy.  
- **Enhanced Marketing ROI Analysis:** Power BI dashboards will connect marketing activities directly to asset growth outcomes, helping teams evaluate campaign effectiveness and refine strategies for higher returns.  
- **Stronger Data Governance and Scalability:** The new architecture will establish a robust foundation for future data sources and analytics needs, supporting compliance, consistency, and long‑term scalability.  
- **Faster, Insight‑Driven Decision‑Making:** Leadership will gain timely access to key performance metrics, accelerating strategic planning and investment decisions across the organization.
