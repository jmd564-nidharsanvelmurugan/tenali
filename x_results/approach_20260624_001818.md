# Approach

This engagement will leverage a systematic, data-driven methodology to enhance Pure Financial Advisors LLC’s Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting capabilities. Our team will partner closely with Pure’s data, technology, and marketing teams to design and implement scalable, automated solutions that deliver granular insights into the pipeline and optimize marketing ROI. The approach prioritizes the integration of critical data sources, development of advanced data models, and delivery of user-centric Power BI dashboards.

The methodology consists of two tightly scoped phases. Each phase is designed to mitigate risks and dependencies, drive rapid, high-impact outcomes, and deliver clear, actionable deliverables—including a detailed KPI Book, Power BI mock-ups, comprehensive data models, and a practical implementation roadmap. Activities are fully aligned to support streamlined data ingestion, robust quality assessments, and accelerated time-to-value.

**Phase 3A: Discovery & Design, Duration: 3 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 to Week 3**  
**Summary:** This phase establishes project requirements, designs the L2A and CAC data models, and defines key reporting metrics and user experiences.  
**Activities:**  
- Conduct stakeholder interviews to capture reporting requirements and success criteria  
- Analyze source systems (Salesforce, Tamarac, QuickBooks, Azure Blob Storage) for data structure and quality  
- Develop a detailed Data Gap Assessment and document integration dependencies  
- Define the L2A and CAC data models, including metric calculation logic and business rules  
- Design Power BI reporting mock-ups (up to 8 pages) with clear KPI visualizations  
- Produce a KPI Book to standardize metric definitions and governance  

**Phase 3B: Build & Implementation Planning, Duration: 8–12 weeks, Timeline: Week 4 to Week 15**  
**Summary:** This phase focuses on implementing automated data ingestion, data transformation pipelines, and integrating QuickBooks into the reporting ecosystem.  
**Activities:**  
- Build automated batch data pipelines in Microsoft Fabric for L2A and CAC reporting  
- Integrate QuickBooks data with Salesforce, Tamarac, and Azure platforms  
- Validate data quality and completeness across all reporting inputs  
- Develop and deploy scalable Power BI dashboards for L2A and CAC insights  
- Prepare technical documentation for ongoing data processes and security standards  
- Deliver a Phase 3B Implementation Plan outlining support, future scalability, and next steps  

Each phase is structured to yield tangible, reviewable deliverables and actionable recommendations. This phased approach ensures that foundational requirements are fully captured and addressed before moving to system implementation, thereby reducing risks and optimizing resource allocation. By working iteratively in partnership with Pure Financial Advisors LLC, we will enable the firm to achieve higher levels of data maturity and empower decision-makers with timely, accurate, and actionable L2A and CAC insights.
