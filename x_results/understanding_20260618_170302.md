# Understanding

- PURE seeks to address limited visibility and granularity in its existing pipeline reporting, particularly around the full Lead-to-AUM (L2A) journey and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) metrics.  
- Current reporting lacks integrated CAC insights, preventing a clear understanding of marketing efficiency and return on spend.  
- Data from key systems such as QuickBooks is not yet connected, creating gaps in financial data needed for accurate CAC analysis.  
- Manual or fragmented data ingestion processes hinder timely and consistent reporting across marketing and financial datasets.  
- The firm requires an enhanced L2A and CAC data model capable of supporting detailed Power BI reporting and KPI tracking.  
- Automated data ingestion and transformation workflows are needed to reduce manual effort and ensure consistent data refreshes.  
- Primary users include data and technology stakeholders, senior leadership, and marketing teams who rely on accurate, consolidated insights for decision-making.  
- Mandatory integrations include Salesforce and QuickBooks to enable end-to-end visibility from lead generation through to managed assets and associated acquisition costs.
