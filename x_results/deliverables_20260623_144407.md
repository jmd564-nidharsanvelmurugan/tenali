# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

1. **KPI Book**
   - Documentation of all relevant Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) key performance indicators.
   - Clear definitions, calculation logic, and data sourcing for each KPI.
   - Delivered as a PDF and PowerPoint slide deck for distribution and stakeholder review.
   - Delivered at the completion of the discovery phase (Phase 3A).

2. **Power BI Mock-ups**
   - Interactive wireframes illustrating the intended design and layout of up to 8 Power BI report views focused on L2A and CAC analytics.
   - Mock-ups include draft data visuals, filter structures, and sample KPI representations.
   - Provided in clickable prototype and static screenshot formats.
   - Delivered during the discovery phase, with iterative drafts and a final version incorporated by the end of Phase 3A.

3. **Data Model Design**
   - Comprehensive entity-relationship diagram and data flow documentation for the enhanced L2A and CAC data model.
   - Specifications for table structures, data sources, transformations, and integration logic.
   - Format includes a detailed data model document and visual diagrams (PDF and Visio files).
   - Delivered by the end of Phase 3A, setting the foundation for subsequent build activities.

4. **Data Gap Assessment**
   - Evaluation of current data availability, completeness, and quality specific to L2A and CAC reporting requirements.
   - Identification of data gaps, recommended remediation actions, and prioritization.
   - Delivered as a structured assessment report in PDF/Excel formats.
   - Provided at the conclusion of the discovery phase (Phase 3A).

5. **Technical Approach Document**
   - Detailed documentation outlining the proposed architecture and technical solution for integrating and ingesting QuickBooks data into the reporting ecosystem.
   - Description of batch processing pipelines, data transformation strategies, and integration touchpoints (Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, Microsoft Fabric).
   - Includes considerations for scalability and error handling.
   - Delivered as a technical document (PDF) by the end of Phase 3A.

6. **Phase 3B Implementation Plan**
   - Roadmap and phased timeline for building, testing, and launching the L2A and CAC reporting solution.
   - Detailed breakdown of tasks, resourcing, dependencies (e.g., QuickBooks ingestion), milestones, and risk management actions.
   - Delivered as a project plan document (MS Project or Excel) and high-level Gantt chart (PDF/PowerPoint).
   - Provided at the end of discovery (Phase 3A), guiding execution in subsequent phases (8–12 weeks duration).

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC's leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
