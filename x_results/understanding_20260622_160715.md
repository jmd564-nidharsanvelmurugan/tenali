# Understanding

- Limited granularity in existing pipeline reporting restricts visibility into critical stages from lead generation to assets under management (AUM).
- Absence of integrated Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) insights hinders the ability to accurately assess return on marketing spend.
- The need exists to ingest and integrate financial data from QuickBooks to enable consolidated CAC analysis.
- Requirement for an enhanced Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and CAC data model to enable detailed tracking and reporting.
- Automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines are needed for L2A and CAC reporting to reduce manual effort and delay.
- Data quality assessment is required for reliable CAC reporting, specifically as new data sources are integrated.
- The primary users of the enhanced reporting and analytics will be data and technology stakeholders, senior leadership, and marketing teams.
- Mandatory integrations must be established with QuickBooks and Salesforce to support the reporting requirements.
