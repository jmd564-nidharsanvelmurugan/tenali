# Objectives

- Establish a comprehensive Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data model that delivers granular insights into pipeline performance and marketing ROI.  
- Automate data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes to ensure consistent, timely, and accurate reporting.  
- Provide leadership with clear visibility into the full customer journey from lead generation through to assets under management, enabling data‑driven decision‑making.  
- Enhance the Power BI user experience with intuitive dashboards, standardized KPI definitions, and clear visualizations of key performance metrics.  
- Strengthen Pure’s data maturity by building scalable reporting capabilities that can support future data sources and evolving business needs.  
- Integrate Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Azure Fabric into a unified reporting ecosystem to create a single source of truth for marketing and pipeline analytics.
