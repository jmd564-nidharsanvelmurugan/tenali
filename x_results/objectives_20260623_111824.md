# Objectives

- Establish a fully designed exit reporting suite with clear equity narratives and a prioritised KPI framework to support the M&A sale process and investor communications.
- Enable automated, refreshable data ingestion pipelines from core source systems and recurring data refresh for transaction reporting.
- Provide senior leadership, advisors, and investors with investor-grade visibility into key commercial, financial, and operational metrics aligned to the equity narrative.
- Deliver intuitive dashboards with well-defined KPI calculation logic and structured navigation to enhance user experience for stakeholder groups.
- Standardise exit reporting processes and ensure data infrastructure readiness to meet investor scrutiny requirements.
- Develop a scalable conceptual data model and reporting solution that can integrate with Finance System, CRM, ERP, Qlik Warehouse, and additional data sources, supporting future extensibility and automation post-transaction.
