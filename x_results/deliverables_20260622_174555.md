# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

1. KPI Book  
   - Comprehensive documentation of Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) metrics and definitions  
   - Detailed logic for each KPI, including calculation methodology and business context  
   - Mapping of source data to each KPI, referencing Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks  
   - Delivered as a digital document at the conclusion of Phase 3A

2. Power BI Mock-Ups  
   - Visual wireframes and mock-ups for up to 8 Power BI report views focused on L2A and CAC reporting  
   - Inclusion of key dashboards, data visualizations, and high-level user experience design  
   - Annotated layouts showing proposed filter options and navigation structure  
   - Delivered during Phase 3A for review and feedback

3. Data Model Design  
   - Detailed design of the L2A and CAC data model, specifying entities, relationships, and key attributes  
   - Integration mapping for Salesforce, Tamarac, QuickBooks, and Azure platform sources  
   - Entity-relationship diagrams and documentation of transformation logic  
   - Delivered at the end of Phase 3A, forming the foundation for build and automation

4. Data Gap Assessment  
   - Identification and documentation of data quality issues, missing attributes, or integration challenges related to L2A and CAC reporting  
   - Impact analysis of any gaps on reporting accuracy and completeness  
   - Recommendations for remediation or workaround strategies  
   - Delivered as a report during Phase 3A

5. Technical Approach  
   - Clear articulation of the integration strategy for Salesforce and QuickBooks  
   - Technical architecture and proposed solution components, including batch data ingestion and transformation pipelines  
   - Recommendations for leveraging Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse for scalable reporting  
   - Delivered as a technical document at the end of Phase 3A

6. Phase 3B Implementation Plan  
   - Stepwise roadmap detailing major milestones, deliverable timelines, and resourcing for Phase 3B  
   - Defined entry and exit criteria for each project phase  
   - Dependencies and risk management strategies, especially around QuickBooks integration and data quality  
   - Delivered prior to commencing Phase 3B

7. Churn Prevention Strategy  
   - Analysis of churn risks based on available customer pipeline and historical AUM data  
   - Proposed metrics for early churn detection and triggers for retention interventions  
   - Recommendations for integrating churn insights into L2A and CAC dashboards in Power BI  
   - Delivered as a strategy report alongside reporting mock-ups in Phase 3A

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC’s leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
