# Objectives

- Achieve comprehensive Lead-to-AUM and Customer Acquisition Cost data models and reporting, enabling granular analysis of pipeline performance and marketing return on investment.
- Automate data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes to improve efficiency and ensure timely access to insights.
- Provide leadership with a detailed understanding of the end-to-end journey from lead generation to assets under management, specifically highlighting marketing effectiveness and cost drivers.
- Enhance user experience through improved Power BI dashboards featuring clear KPI definitions and intuitive visualizations.
- Strengthen pipeline reporting and customer acquisition cost tracking to support data-driven business decision-making.
- Develop a scalable reporting platform that supports integration with Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Azure Fabric, accommodating future data sources and expanding reporting requirements.
