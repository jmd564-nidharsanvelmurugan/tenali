# Approach

Our approach is designed to help **Poekmon**, a U.S.-based financial advisory and wealth management firm, strengthen its data-driven decision-making by improving how it reports on Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC). As a B2C business operating in a highly competitive financial services market, Poekmon’s success depends on connecting marketing efforts to asset growth. Leveraging their existing Microsoft Fabric, Azure Synapse, Salesforce, Tamarac, and Power BI infrastructure, we will design a unified, automated, and scalable reporting foundation that integrates QuickBooks data to deliver accurate, actionable insights.

We will follow a structured, five-phase methodology that moves from understanding Poekmon’s business and data landscape through to designing the data model, defining KPIs, and planning implementation. Each phase builds on the previous one, ensuring alignment with business goals, technical feasibility, and stakeholder expectations. This phased approach provides clarity, reduces risk, and ensures that the final solution is both technically sound and business-relevant.

---

**Phase 1: Discovery & Requirements Definition, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 1**

**Summary:** Establish a shared understanding of Poekmon’s business goals, data environment, and reporting needs to set a strong foundation for the project.  

**Activities:**  
- Conduct workshops with data, technology, and marketing teams to confirm objectives and success criteria.  
- Review existing systems, reports, and workflows across Salesforce, Tamarac, and Azure environments.  
- Map current lead management and QuickBooks integration processes.  
- Define key L2A and CAC metrics and their business relevance.  
- Develop a detailed project plan outlining milestones, dependencies, and responsibilities.  

---

**Phase 2: Data Source Assessment & Gap Analysis, Duration: 2 weeks, Timeline: Weeks 2–3**

**Summary:** Evaluate data quality, completeness, and integration readiness across all relevant systems to identify gaps impacting L2A and CAC reporting.  

**Activities:**  
- Profile data from Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks to assess structure and quality.  
- Identify missing, inconsistent, or duplicate data affecting KPI accuracy.  
- Document data flows, mappings, and dependencies across systems.  
- Define data processing and transformation needs within Microsoft Fabric.  
- Prioritize remediation actions and QuickBooks integration steps to enable complete CAC reporting.  

---

**Phase 3: Data Model Design & KPI Definition, Duration: 2 weeks, Timeline: Weeks 4–5**

**Summary:** Design the unified data model and define standardized KPI logic to support accurate, automated L2A and CAC reporting.  

**Activities:**  
- Develop a detailed data model combining Salesforce, Tamarac, and QuickBooks data.  
- Define calculation logic and data lineage for L2A and CAC metrics.  
- Validate model design and KPI definitions with business and technical stakeholders.  
- Align data model architecture with Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse standards.  
- Document data transformation rules and integration logic for future implementation.  

---

**Phase 4: Power BI Mock-Ups & Reporting Design, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 6**

**Summary:** Translate the data model and KPIs into intuitive Power BI dashboard designs for stakeholder review and feedback.  

**Activities:**  
- Design up to eight Power BI dashboards visualizing L2A and CAC performance.  
- Incorporate key KPIs, conversion steps, and marketing performance indicators.  
- Review mock-ups with marketing, leadership, and data teams to ensure usability.  
- Refine dashboard layouts and visual hierarchies based on feedback.  
- Document visualization specifications to guide future dashboard development.  

---

**Phase 5: Technical Approach & Implementation Planning, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 7**

**Summary:** Finalize the technical architecture and create a detailed roadmap for implementation, automation, and governance.  

**Activities:**  
- Define the end-to-end technical architecture for data integration and reporting.  
- Plan QuickBooks and Salesforce connection methods, scheduling, and automation.  
- Outline data governance, quality assurance, and refresh processes.  
- Develop a comprehensive implementation roadmap with milestones and resource needs.  
- Consolidate all deliverables into a final presentation for leadership review.  

---

This structured, phased approach ensures that Poekmon’s L2A and CAC reporting capabilities are developed with precision, agility, and a clear focus on delivering measurable business outcomes. By aligning technical design with strategic goals, the project will equip Poekmon with a scalable, automated reporting foundation that supports data-driven growth and informed decision-making.
