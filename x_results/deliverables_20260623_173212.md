# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

1. KPI Book
   - Comprehensive documentation of defined KPIs for Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting.
   - Details each metric's business logic, data sources, and calculation methodology.
   - Format: Structured PDF and editable Excel/Word file.
   - Delivered: End of Week 1.

2. Power BI Mock-ups
   - Interactive wireframes of up to eight Power BI dashboard views, illustrating proposed visualizations and report navigation.
   - Includes draft layout of L2A and CAC insights and key performance metrics.
   - Format: PowerPoint or PDF with embedded mock-ups.
   - Delivered: Week 2.

3. Data Model Design
   - Logical and physical schema for the L2A and CAC data model, mapping source data from Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks.
   - Entity-relationship diagrams and data flow documentation.
   - Format: Visual diagrams (e.g., Visio, Lucidchart) with supporting data dictionary.
   - Delivered: End of Week 2.

4. Data Gap Assessment
   - Identification and documentation of data quality gaps, missing fields, and integration issues related to QuickBooks and Salesforce data.
   - Prioritized recommendations for remediation to enable accurate reporting.
   - Format: PDF/Microsoft Word report with summarized findings and actionable recommendations.
   - Delivered: End of Week 2.

5. Technical Approach
   - Detailed technical plan for automating data ingestion, integration, and transformation across Fabric, Synapse, and Power BI.
   - Includes requirements for QuickBooks integration and workflow automation design.
   - Format: Technical specification document (PDF/Word).
   - Delivered: Week 3.

6. Phase 3B Implementation Plan
   - Phased roadmap outlining build and deployment timelines, resource allocations, and major milestones for the next implementation stage.
   - Includes risk assessment and mitigation strategies.
   - Format: PDF/Gantt chart or tabular schedule.
   - Delivered: Final week of engagement (Week 3).

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC's leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
