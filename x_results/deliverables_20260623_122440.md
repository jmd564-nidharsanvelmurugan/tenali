# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with VONQ, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

1. **Equity Narratives Definition**
   - Clear articulation of the equity narratives that will guide investor reporting and exit communications.
   - Documentation of narrative themes and supporting rationale to inform dashboard content and KPI selection.
   - Provided in a structured report format, delivered by the end of Week 1.

2. **Metrics that Matter KPI Framework**
   - Prioritised list of commercial, financial, and operational KPIs aligned to the defined equity narratives.
   - KPI calculation logic, definitions, and source mapping for transparency.
   - Presentation deck summarizing framework and rationale, delivered during Week 2.

3. **Dashboard Mock-ups**
   - Static wireframes illustrating key dashboard layouts, navigation structures, and metrics placement.
   - Mock-ups presented in PDF/PowerPoint format for stakeholder review.
   - Shared at the end of Week 2, with iteration based on feedback in Week 3.

4. **Data Feasibility Assessment**
   - Playback of current-state findings, including existing data gaps across source systems.
   - Assessment of data availability, completeness, and quality for reporting purposes.
   - Comprehensive assessment report, delivered by the end of Week 2.

5. **Conceptual Data Model Design**
   - High-level data model leveraging the Qlik Warehouse and integrating Finance, CRM, ERP, and any legacy sources for trended data.
   - Visual diagram and accompanying documentation outlining entity relationships and data flows.
   - Delivered by the end of Week 3.

6. **Data Infrastructure and Tooling Recommendations**
   - Comparative analysis of data integration and reporting tools, including pros and cons.
   - Recommendations for enhancements or alternative solutions with trade-off analysis.
   - Summarized in a recommendation report, delivered by the end of Week 3.

7. **Implementation Roadmap for Phase 2**
   - Detailed roadmap outlining priority use cases, dependencies, risks, and key milestones for Phase 2 execution.
   - Phasing plan with timelines, roles, and deliverable schedule.
   - Provided in a project plan document, delivered in Week 4.

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with VONQ’s leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
