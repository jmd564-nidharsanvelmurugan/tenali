# Approach

VONQ has initiated this engagement to rapidly develop a scalable, investor-grade exit reporting suite in preparation for a near-term sale process. Our methodology prioritizes stakeholder alignment, operational feasibility, and future scalability by building a foundation for robust exit analytics and repeatable data refreshes. The approach is structured to provide VONQ with the necessary design artefacts, data model, and implementation roadmap to navigate transaction requirements and support due diligence with clear, validated metrics.

We will employ a structured, best-practice consulting methodology, consisting of collaborative workshops, data assessment sprints, and actionable design outputs. All activities are sequenced to deliver high-velocity discovery and design within the fixed timeline, while accommodating critical dependencies—such as stakeholder input and timely data access. The engagement is planned over a single four-week Phase 1, with clear outputs to directly enable subsequent technical implementation (Phase 2), which will be governed under a separate Statement of Work.

---

**Phase 1: Discovery & Design, Duration: 4 weeks, Timeline: Week 29 June to 24 July 2026**  
**Summary:** Establish the design foundations for an exit reporting suite, including KPI framework, dashboard mock-ups, data feasibility, conceptual model, tooling recommendations, and a Phase 2 implementation roadmap.

**Activities:**  
- Initiate project kick-off, confirm governance, and onboard stakeholders from Finance, Commercial, Data/Analytics, and Leadership teams  
- Conduct equity narrative definition workshop to align on investment themes and prioritize reporting requirements  
- Facilitate “Metrics that Matter” session to co-develop a KPI framework, including definitions, calculation methods, and alignment to equity narratives  
- Assess current-state data landscape (Finance, CRM, ERP, Qlik Warehouse, and additional sources), documenting data gaps, ingestion challenges, and refresh requirements  
- Produce static dashboard mock-ups, illustrating proposed metrics, layouts, and navigation structures for investor reporting  
- Design a conceptual data model optimized for batch ingestion into the Qlik Warehouse, covering integration touchpoints, legacy data needs, and extensibility  
- Evaluate tooling options (including Qlik enhancements and alternatives), summarizing pros, cons, and alignment with scalability and automation goals  
- Develop a detailed implementation roadmap covering Phase 2 build, use cases, dependencies, risks, key milestones, and resource considerations

---

At the conclusion of Phase 1, VONQ will have a comprehensive and actionable design suite: a validated KPI framework, dashboard concepts, data gap analysis, and conceptual data model—all underpinned by clear tooling recommendations and an implementation roadmap. This phased approach ensures the project delivers rapid value, aligns to key transaction milestones, and positions VONQ for a successful, scalable execution in the critical next stage.
