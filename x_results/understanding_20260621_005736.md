# Understanding

- PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC needs to enhance granularity and accuracy in its Lead-to-AUM (L2A) pipeline and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting.
- Current pipeline reporting lacks detailed insights and fails to provide integrated CAC metrics, limiting visibility into the journey from initial lead to assets under management.
- Integration with QuickBooks is necessary to ingest financial data and enable comprehensive CAC reporting, presenting a current challenge due to incomplete connectivity and data quality assessment needs.
- A more robust L2A and CAC data model is required, with seamless data ingestion, transformation, and quality validation across Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks.
- Automated data pipelines must be established to support timely batch processing of L2A and CAC datasets within the Fabric data platform, reducing manual intervention and operational inefficiencies.
- The solution must support multiple user roles, specifically data and technology stakeholders, senior leadership, and marketing teams, who require reliable and granular reporting for decision-making.
- Mandated integrations include Salesforce and QuickBooks; connecting these platforms is essential for unified reporting and data consistency.
- Dependency on QuickBooks ingestion and access to existing data platforms currently constrains progress and must be addressed for successful reporting outcomes.
- Timeline expectations and dependency on system access (Salesforce, QuickBooks, Fabric, Synapse) impact the feasibility and continuity of the reporting initiative.
