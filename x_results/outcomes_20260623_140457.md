# Outcomes

The engagement will deliver measurable improvements in Pure Financial Advisors LLC’s reporting capabilities, enabling more granular insights into pipeline performance and marketing ROI. By implementing comprehensive Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data models integrated into the Power BI suite, Pure will gain actionable intelligence to optimize lead conversion and evaluate marketing effectiveness. These outcomes will position the client for scalable and data-driven growth, while supporting leadership with timely and automated analytics.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Granular Pipeline Insights:** Improved visibility into the Lead-to-AUM journey will empower stakeholders to identify strengths and bottlenecks in the client acquisition process, enabling targeted interventions.
- **Enhanced Marketing ROI Measurement:** Automated CAC reporting and integration of QuickBooks data will allow for accurate tracking of marketing spend effectiveness and return, driving smarter allocation of resources.
- **Automated Data Processes:** Timely, accurate ingestion and transformation of pipeline and CAC data will reduce manual effort, streamline reporting workflows, and ensure consistent metric availability.
- **Integrated Reporting Experience:** Consolidation of Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure, and QuickBooks data within Power BI will provide users with unified dashboards featuring clear KPI definitions and actionable visualizations.
- **Leadership Decision Support:** Senior leadership will benefit from precise conversion metrics and CAC KPIs, supporting evidence-based decision-making to accelerate business growth.
- **Increased Data Maturity & Scalability:** Establishing a scalable data platform and reporting suite will enable Pure to adapt to future data sources and expanding business needs, reinforcing organizational readiness for continued expansion.

These outcomes will be measured through the accuracy, automation, and depth of L2A and CAC reporting; improvements in conversion rates and marketing ROI; and the adoption of enhanced Power BI dashboards by key business stakeholders.
