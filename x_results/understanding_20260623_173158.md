# Understanding

- Limited granularity in current Lead-to-AUM (L2A) pipeline reporting hinders detailed visibility into pipeline performance.
- Absence of integrated Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) insights restricts understanding of marketing spend effectiveness.
- Enhanced L2A and CAC data model is required to support more advanced and granular reporting capabilities.
- Automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines are necessary for consistency and reliability in L2A and CAC reporting.
- Integration with both QuickBooks and Salesforce is mandatory to ensure comprehensive data coverage.
- Data quality assessment for integrated QuickBooks data is needed to validate accuracy for CAC reporting.
- Power BI reporting suite must include improved dashboards focused on L2A and CAC analysis.
- Key user personas include data and technology stakeholders, senior leadership, and marketing teams.
