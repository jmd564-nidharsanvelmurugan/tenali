# Outcomes

This engagement with **Pure Financial Advisors LLC** will deliver measurable business impact by enabling data‑driven visibility into the firm’s Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) performance. Focused on strengthening Pure’s core reporting capabilities within its B2C financial advisory model, the initiative will equip leadership and marketing stakeholders with actionable insights to optimize pipeline efficiency and marketing ROI, supporting both operational scalability and investor transparency for Lee Equity Partners.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Granular Lead‑to‑AUM Visibility:** Development of a unified L2A data model will provide detailed tracking of the customer journey from initial lead through to managed assets, enabling leadership to identify conversion drivers and optimize acquisition strategies.  
- **Accurate Customer Acquisition Cost Measurement:** Integration of QuickBooks and Salesforce data will allow precise calculation of CAC metrics, improving understanding of marketing spend effectiveness and guiding budget allocation decisions.  
- **Automated and Reliable Reporting Processes:** Implementation of automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines will ensure consistent, timely, and error‑free reporting across Power BI dashboards, reducing manual effort and improving data reliability.  
- **Enhanced Marketing ROI Insights:** The new reporting suite will deliver clear visibility into campaign performance and return on marketing investment, empowering the marketing team to refine targeting and improve lead quality.  
- **Scalable Data Foundation for Growth:** Establishing a robust, extensible data model within Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse will support future data sources and reporting needs, positioning Pure for continued data maturity and scalable analytics capabilities.  
- **Improved Decision‑Making Confidence:** Leadership will gain access to accurate, integrated insights that strengthen strategic planning, resource allocation, and performance tracking across the firm’s financial advisory operations.
