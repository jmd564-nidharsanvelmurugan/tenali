# Approach

Our engagement with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC is designed to deliver a comprehensive, scalable, and automated reporting solution for the Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) pipeline. Leveraging both best-in-class data engineering practices and established financial analytics frameworks, our methodology focuses on maximizing transparency for stakeholders, improving data-driven decision-making, and ensuring seamless integration with existing technologies such as Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Synapse, Power BI, and QuickBooks.

We will employ an agile, phased methodology to incrementally address the client’s specific requirements, with each phase building towards an actionable, integrated reporting solution. Through systematic discovery, design, and implementation, we will collaboratively validate requirements, design robust data models, and automate data ingestion, enabling PURE’s leadership and teams to gain granular pipeline insights and maximize marketing ROI.

---

**Phase 3A: Discovery & Design, Duration: 3 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 to Week 3**  
**Summary:** This phase establishes clear requirements, defines KPIs, and creates detailed mock-ups and architecture for the L2A and CAC reporting solution.

**Activities:**  
- Stakeholder workshops to confirm business requirements for L2A and CAC reporting  
- Development of a comprehensive KPI Book, including data source mapping and detailed calculation logic  
- Detailed data gap assessment covering Salesforce, Tamarac, QuickBooks, and Azure Blob Storage integration points  
- Preparation of Power BI mock-ups (up to 8 views) to visualize targeted reporting outputs  
- Drafting the technical approach and integration strategy for QuickBooks and existing platforms  
- Delivery of the Phase 3B implementation plan outlining dependencies and timeline  

---

**Phase 3B: Build & Automation, Duration: 8–12 weeks, Timeline: Week 4 to Week 15**  
**Summary:** This phase focuses on implementing automated data pipelines, integrating data sources, and building the Power BI reporting suite.

**Activities:**  
- Configuration and deployment of automated batch ingestion pipelines in Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse  
- Connection and integration of data from Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks  
- Implementation of data transformation and quality checks for L2A and CAC metrics  
- Construction of the finalized data model to support granular reporting  
- Development and user acceptance testing of the 8-page Power BI reporting suite with KPI definitions  
- Documentation of data workflows and scheduling of reporting refresh processes  

---

By following this phased, methodical approach, PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC will benefit from clear project governance, early validation of requirements, and incremental value delivery. Each deliverable is tightly aligned with business objectives, operational needs, and technical constraints, ensuring a robust solution that drives actionable insights, data quality, and long-term scalability for the organization’s future growth.
