# Approach

This engagement with VONQ focuses on rapidly designing a best-in-class exit reporting suite to underpin the client’s upcoming M&A transaction. Our methodology is collaborative, leveraging stakeholder workshops, structured assessments, and iterative playback sessions to ensure alignment with the needs of both internal leadership and external advisors such as Barclays. By combining best practices in data-driven reporting design with a pragmatic understanding of the existing Qlik Warehouse environment, we will deliver actionable artefacts that de-risk the transaction process and accelerate readiness for Phase 2 implementation.

We employ a phased approach, beginning with intensive discovery and design activities that inform a robust implementation plan. The process is anchored on clear milestones, stakeholder engagement, and the use of proven frameworks for KPI development, data modeling, and tool assessment. The outputs from each phase provide a foundation for automation and scalability, in line with VONQ’s long-term vision for a business asset-grade reporting suite.

---

**Phase 1: Discovery & Design, Duration: 4 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 (29 June) to Week 4 (24 July 2026)**

**Summary:**  
This phase delivers the essential design artefacts—narratives, KPI frameworks, dashboard mock-ups, and a conceptual data model—to guide the build and implementation in the subsequent phase.

**Activities:**  
- Conduct project initiation: confirm stakeholder groups, align expectations, and establish working protocols.  
- Facilitate workshops with client leadership, commercial/CRM, finance, and analytics teams to define equity narratives, “metrics that matter,” and reporting priorities.  
- Assess source systems (Finance, CRM, ERP, and legacy datasets) for data completeness, integration readiness, and feasibility of automated/batch ingestion into the Qlik Warehouse.  
- Develop and playback dashboard mock-ups, outlining key KPIs, calculation logic, layout, and navigation principles.  
- Design a conceptual data model using Qlik Warehouse as the foundation, including mapping of trended data, integration points, and scalability requirements.  
- Prepare a tooling and infrastructure recommendations report, detailing options, trade-offs, and alignment with future automation needs.  
- Draft an implementation roadmap for Phase 2, including prioritised use cases, dependencies, risks, and milestones.

---

**Concluding Paragraph**

Our structured, stakeholder-focused approach ensures VONQ receives actionable, investor-ready deliverables that de-risk the exit process and accelerate the transition into Phase 2 implementation. By frontloading equity narrative definition, KPI standardisation, and data model alignment within a tightly controlled timeline, this methodology positions VONQ for confident engagement with potential acquirers and enables the rapid deployment of automated, scalable reporting assets post-transaction.
