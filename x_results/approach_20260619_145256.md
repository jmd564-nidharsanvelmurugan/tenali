# Approach

The proposed engagement for **Pure Financial Advisors LLC** is designed to address the firm’s specific need for enhanced Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting within the financial advisory and wealth management context. As a B2C business operating across the United States, Pure’s success depends on its ability to understand the full customer journey—from lead generation through asset conversion—and to measure marketing ROI with precision. This approach leverages the client’s existing Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse infrastructure, integrating Salesforce, Tamarac, and QuickBooks data to deliver a unified, automated, and scalable reporting solution.

Our methodology follows a structured, phased approach that ensures alignment with Pure’s business priorities, technical environment, and stakeholder expectations. Each phase builds upon the previous one, moving from discovery and data assessment through model design, mock‑up development, and implementation planning. This progression ensures that the final deliverables—data model, KPI book, Power BI mock‑ups, and implementation roadmap—are grounded in validated data, stakeholder input, and actionable insights.

---

**Phase 1: Discovery & Current State Assessment, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 1**

**Summary:** Establish a shared understanding of Pure’s current data landscape, reporting processes, and integration points to inform downstream design activities.

**Activities:**  
- Conduct kickoff sessions with data, technology, and marketing stakeholders to confirm objectives and success criteria.  
- Review existing reporting assets within Power BI and Microsoft Fabric.  
- Document current data flows across Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks.  
- Identify data access requirements and confirm system connectivity.  
- Develop a detailed project plan and workshop schedule for subsequent phases.  

---

**Phase 2: Data Source Assessment & Gap Analysis, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 2**

**Summary:** Evaluate data availability, quality, and integration readiness to support L2A and CAC reporting requirements.

**Activities:**  
- Assess data coverage and quality across Salesforce, Tamarac, and Azure Blob Storage.  
- Review QuickBooks data structure and define ingestion requirements for CAC reporting.  
- Identify data gaps and dependencies impacting KPI calculation.  
- Recommend data cleaning and enrichment strategies for Phase 3B implementation.  
- Validate findings with stakeholders and document in a Data Gap Assessment report.  

---

**Phase 3: Data Model Design & KPI Definition, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 3**

**Summary:** Design the target‑state L2A and CAC data model and define KPI logic to enable consistent, automated reporting.

**Activities:**  
- Develop conceptual and logical data model diagrams aligned with Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse architecture.  
- Define L2A and CAC KPIs, including calculation logic, data lineage, and dimensional attributes.  
- Establish standardized naming conventions and data relationships.  
- Validate model design and KPI definitions with business and technical stakeholders.  
- Document outputs in the KPI Book and Data Model Design deliverables.  

---

**Phase 4: Power BI Mock‑ups & Reporting Design, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 4**

**Summary:** Translate business requirements and data model outputs into visual reporting concepts for stakeholder validation.

**Activities:**  
- Design up to eight Power BI mock‑ups focused on L2A and CAC insights.  
- Incorporate user feedback to refine dashboard layout, navigation, and KPI visualization.  
- Ensure alignment with Pure’s branding and reporting standards.  
- Define data refresh logic and reporting automation requirements.  
- Finalize mock‑ups for inclusion in the Design deliverable package.  

---

**Phase 5: Technical Approach & Implementation Planning, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 5**

**Summary:** Develop the technical approach and detailed roadmap for Phase 3B implementation, ensuring readiness for build and automation.

**Activities:**  
- Define the end‑to‑end data ingestion and transformation approach for QuickBooks integration.  
- Outline batch processing pipelines within Microsoft Fabric for automated data refresh.  
- Develop the Phase 3B Implementation Plan, including sequencing, dependencies, and resource requirements.  
- Validate technical design with Pure’s data and technology teams.  
- Present final deliverables—KPI Book, Data Model, Power BI Mock‑ups, and Implementation Plan—for approval.  

---

This structured, phased approach ensures that **Pure Financial Advisors LLC**’s enhanced reporting capabilities are developed with precision, agility, and a clear focus on delivering measurable business outcomes. By grounding each phase in validated data and stakeholder collaboration, the engagement will provide a scalable foundation for ongoing analytics maturity and data‑driven decision‑making.
