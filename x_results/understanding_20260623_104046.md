# Understanding

- The client is seeking to resolve limitations in their current pipeline reporting, which lacks the necessary granularity to track the full journey from lead to assets under management (AUM) and to evaluate marketing effectiveness through accurate Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting.
- Current pain points include fragmented and insufficiently detailed reporting, particularly around CAC insights and lead progression metrics.
- There is an immediate requirement to ingest and integrate financial data from QuickBooks and to assess its data quality for improved CAC calculations.
- Required capabilities include an enhanced Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and CAC data model, as well as a Power BI reporting suite that supports more robust and detailed insights.
- Automation is needed for data ingestion and transformation pipelines to ensure timely, consistent, and repeatable L2A and CAC reporting.
- The enhanced reporting and data models must serve the needs of data and technology stakeholders, senior leadership, and marketing teams who are involved in decision-making and performance analysis.
- Integration with QuickBooks and Salesforce is mandatory to create a unified and comprehensive dataset supporting the new reporting requirements.
- Project progress and outcomes are dependent on timely access to Salesforce, QuickBooks, and the established Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse data platforms.
- Constraints related to data access, data quality, and QuickBooks integration represent key dependencies for successful delivery of the initiative.
