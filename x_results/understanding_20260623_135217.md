# Understanding

- Existing pipeline reporting lacks the necessary granularity to provide clear insights into the full customer journey from lead to AUM (Assets Under Management).
- There is currently no integrated visibility into Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), limiting understanding of marketing return on investment.
- Data integration with QuickBooks has not yet been established, creating a gap in capturing complete CAC data.
- The business requires an enhanced data model specifically for Lead-to-AUM and CAC metrics, including defined KPI logic and support for custom Power BI reporting.
- Automated data ingestion and transformation processes are needed to support batch processing and ensure timely, accurate L2A and CAC reporting.
- Data quality assessment is required to ensure that integrated datasets, particularly from QuickBooks, meet reporting needs.
- Key user groups include data and technology stakeholders, senior leadership, and marketing teams—all requiring secure, reliable access to L2A and CAC dashboards.
- Integration with Salesforce and QuickBooks is mandatory to facilitate unified reporting and enable comprehensive pipeline and CAC analysis.
- The engagement’s timelines and dependencies are closely tied to the successful integration and quality verification of QuickBooks data.
