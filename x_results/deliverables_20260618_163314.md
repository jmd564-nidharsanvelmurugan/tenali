# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with PURE Financial Advisors LLC, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

1. **KPI Book**  
- Comprehensive documentation of Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) metrics, including definitions, calculation logic, and data sources.  
- Clear mapping of KPIs to business objectives and reporting views for consistent interpretation across teams.  
- Inclusion of dimension dictionary outlining segmentation and slicing parameters for analysis.  

2. **Power BI Mock-ups**  
- Design of up to eight Power BI dashboard views focused on L2A and CAC insights.  
- Visualization prototypes illustrating layout, navigation, and KPI placement for leadership review.  
- Alignment of mock-ups with PURE’s existing Power BI standards and branding guidelines.  

3. **Data Model Design**  
- Detailed schema for the L2A and CAC data model integrating Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks data.  
- Logical and physical data model diagrams showing relationships, hierarchies, and transformation logic.  
- Documentation of data lineage and governance considerations to support scalability and maintainability.  

4. **Data Gap Assessment**  
- Identification and evaluation of missing or inconsistent data elements impacting L2A and CAC reporting accuracy.  
- Recommendations for remediation actions and data quality improvement initiatives.  
- Summary of integration readiness for QuickBooks and other source systems.  

5. **Technical Approach**  
- Detailed description of the proposed architecture and integration method for QuickBooks ingestion into Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse.  
- Outline of batch processing workflows, automation logic, and refresh cadence for reporting.  
- Technical design principles ensuring performance, reliability, and alignment with PURE’s existing infrastructure.  

6. **Phase 3B Implementation Plan**  
- Structured roadmap detailing activities, milestones, and dependencies for the build and deployment phase.  
- Resource and timeline estimates aligned with the 8–12 week implementation window.  
- Risk and dependency register highlighting data access, integration, and quality considerations.  

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with PURE Financial Advisors LLC’s leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
