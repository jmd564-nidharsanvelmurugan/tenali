# Objectives

- Deliver a comprehensive Lead-to-AUM and Customer Acquisition Cost data model providing granular insights into pipeline performance and marketing ROI.
- Automate data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes for improved efficiency and timeliness.
- Enable senior leadership to gain an in-depth understanding of the customer journey from lead to AUM and the effectiveness of marketing spend.
- Enhance Power BI dashboards with clear KPI definitions and intuitive visualizations to improve user experience and decision-making.
- Improve pipeline reporting and customer acquisition cost tracking to support data-driven business process enhancements.
- Develop scalable data and reporting capabilities to support future business growth and integration of additional data sources.
- Ensure seamless integration with Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Microsoft Fabric data platforms for unified reporting.
