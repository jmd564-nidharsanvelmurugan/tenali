# Understanding

- PURE seeks to address limited visibility and granularity in its existing pipeline reporting, particularly around the Lead-to-AUM (L2A) journey and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) metrics.  
- Current reporting lacks integrated CAC insights, preventing a clear understanding of marketing efficiency and conversion performance.  
- Data ingestion from QuickBooks is not yet established, creating a gap in financial data required for accurate CAC analysis.  
- There is a need for an enhanced L2A and CAC data model to unify data from Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks.  
- Manual or fragmented data processes limit the ability to automate and refresh reporting efficiently.  
- Automated data ingestion and transformation workflows are required to ensure consistent, timely reporting.  
- Primary users include data and technology stakeholders, senior leadership, and marketing teams who rely on accurate, consolidated insights.  
- Mandatory integrations include Salesforce and QuickBooks to enable end-to-end visibility across lead, client, and financial data.
