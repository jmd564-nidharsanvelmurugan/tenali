# Overview

PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC’s operations presently involve a cross-functional collaboration between data, technology, and marketing teams focused on managing pipeline and customer acquisition reporting. These teams are actively engaged in utilizing established digital infrastructure and operational workflows that support the company’s financial advisory and asset management activities.

The current technology stack includes Salesforce and Tamarac for client and asset management, Azure Blob Storage for data warehousing, and Microsoft Fabric alongside Azure Synapse for advanced data processing and analytics. Power BI serves as the central reporting suite, offering existing capabilities for visualizing business data and metrics. QuickBooks is identified as an additional financial data source, with plans underway for future integration into the reporting ecosystem. All primary business data, including lead, pipeline, and asset records, reside across Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and the incoming QuickBooks integration.

Presently, the business faces a need to integrate and ingest data from QuickBooks and assess data quality specifically for Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting. The organization operates using batch processing pipelines within Microsoft Fabric, emphasizing established cloud and data processing platforms as the current operational foundation. No manual processes or data governance policies have been explicitly documented; areas cited for immediate attention relate to integration and data quality assessment in the context of new reporting requirements.
