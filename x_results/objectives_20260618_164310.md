# Objectives

- Establish a comprehensive Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data model that delivers granular insights into pipeline performance and marketing ROI.  
- Automate data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes to ensure consistent, timely, and accurate reporting.  
- Provide leadership with clear visibility into the full customer journey from lead generation through AUM conversion, enabling data‑driven decision‑making.  
- Enhance Power BI dashboards with standardized KPI definitions and intuitive visualizations to improve user experience and reporting clarity.  
- Build a scalable data foundation capable of integrating additional data sources and supporting future growth in PURE’s reporting capabilities.  
- Advance PURE’s overall data maturity by creating a trusted, unified reporting environment that supports strategic and operational planning.
