# Business Context

PURE Financial Advisors LLC operates within the U.S. financial advisory and wealth management sector, providing clients with personalized financial advisory services and asset management solutions. The firm follows a B2C model, engaging directly with individual investors to help them achieve long-term financial goals. Key stakeholders include Jason Carver, Client Engagement Manager, and Lee Equity Partners, the firm’s investment partner.

PURE is initiating this project to strengthen its data-driven decision-making capabilities by enhancing Lead-to-AUM (L2A) pipeline and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting. Current reporting provides limited granularity, restricting visibility into the full customer journey from lead generation to assets under management and the effectiveness of marketing spend. The initiative aims to deliver a unified, automated data model and Power BI reporting suite that integrates data from Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure platforms, and QuickBooks. By improving data ingestion, transformation, and reporting processes, PURE seeks to establish scalable, accurate insights that support leadership in evaluating pipeline performance and marketing ROI.
