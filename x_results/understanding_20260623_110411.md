# Understanding

- Limited granularity in existing pipeline reporting restricts detailed analysis of the client’s lead-to-AUM conversion journey and customer acquisition cost performance.
- Lack of integrated customer acquisition cost (CAC) insights impedes the ability to evaluate the true return on marketing spend.
- Requirement for an enhanced L2A (Lead-to-AUM) and CAC data model to support granular tracking and reporting.
- Need to automate data ingestion and transformation pipelines to streamline the production of L2A and CAC reporting.
- Integration with QuickBooks and Salesforce is mandatory to enable comprehensive data aggregation and reporting.
- Data quality assessment is required to ensure reliability and accuracy of new CAC and pipeline reporting metrics.
- Power BI reporting suite must be extended to deliver specific L2A and CAC insights for data, technology, senior leadership, and marketing teams.
- Timely access and ingestion of data are dependent on successful connectivity to primary systems, especially QuickBooks.
