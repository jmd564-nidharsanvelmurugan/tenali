# Understanding

- There is currently a lack of investor-ready reporting to support VONQ’s M&A sale process and associated stakeholder communications.
- Data gaps exist across source systems, with the Qlik Warehouse not fully covering all KPIs required for a compelling equity narrative.
- Manual, one-off data ingestion is needed for some systems due to absence of automated connections with the Qlik Warehouse, impacting efficiency and timeliness.
- There is a requirement for definition of equity narratives, a prioritised KPI framework, dashboard mock-ups, data feasibility assessment, conceptual data model design, tooling recommendations, and a phased implementation roadmap.
- Automation is needed for data ingestion and refresh pipelines from the Finance System, CRM, ERP, and legacy sources into the Qlik Warehouse, including recurring updates during the transaction.
- Senior leadership, exit advisors (Barclays), and potential acquirers/investors are the primary user roles expected to interact with or rely on the reporting suite.
- Mandatory integrations include the Finance System, CRM, ERP, and legacy sources for a minimum of three years of trended data into the Qlik Warehouse.
- Constraints include exit timeline pressures, timely data access, stakeholder availability for workshops, and ensuring quality and completeness of existing Qlik Warehouse data.
