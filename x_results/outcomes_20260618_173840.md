# Outcomes

This engagement will deliver measurable business impact and strategic value for PURE Financial Advisors LLC by transforming its Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting capabilities. The initiative aligns with PURE’s B2C financial advisory model and the objectives of key stakeholders, including Lee Equity Partners, by enabling data‑driven insights into marketing ROI and pipeline performance across the U.S. market.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Granular Lead‑to‑AUM Visibility:** Development of a comprehensive L2A data model will provide detailed insight into the customer journey from initial lead through asset conversion, enabling leadership to identify conversion drivers and optimize acquisition strategies.  
- **Accurate Customer Acquisition Cost Measurement:** Integration of QuickBooks and Salesforce data will allow precise calculation of CAC metrics, improving understanding of marketing spend efficiency and supporting more informed budget allocation decisions.  
- **Automated and Reliable Reporting:** Implementation of automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines will ensure consistent, timely, and accurate reporting in Power BI, reducing manual effort and improving operational efficiency.  
- **Enhanced Marketing ROI Analysis:** Unified L2A and CAC reporting will enable PURE to evaluate the effectiveness of marketing campaigns and channels, driving higher returns on marketing investments and improved lead quality.  
- **Improved Decision‑Making for Leadership:** With clear KPI definitions and visualizations in Power BI, senior management will gain actionable insights into pipeline performance, supporting strategic decisions that accelerate growth and asset accumulation.  
- **Foundation for Scalable Data Maturity:** Establishing a robust, integrated data model and reporting framework will position PURE for future scalability, allowing seamless expansion to additional data sources and advanced analytics capabilities.
