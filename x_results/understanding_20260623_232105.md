# Understanding

- Existing pipeline reporting lacks sufficient granularity, limiting actionable insights for leadership.
- Current reporting does not integrate Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) metrics, creating gaps in understanding marketing effectiveness.
- Data ingestion from QuickBooks is not yet established, hindering comprehensive financial tracking for CAC reporting.
- There is a requirement for an enhanced data model to support detailed Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and CAC analytics.
- Automated pipelines are needed for batch data ingestion and transformation to improve accuracy, timeliness, and reliability of reporting.
- Data quality assessment between systems is necessary to ensure the integrity of CAC and L2A metrics.
- Power BI reporting must be extended and refined to provide targeted L2A and CAC dashboards.
- The primary users include data and technology teams, senior leadership, and marketing stakeholders.
- Mandatory system integrations include Salesforce and QuickBooks.
