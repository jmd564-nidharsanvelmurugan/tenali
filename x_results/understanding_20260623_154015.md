# Understanding

- VONQ is seeking to design and build a scalable exit reporting suite to support its upcoming M&A sale process and provide investor-ready communications.
- There is currently a lack of investor-ready reporting, resulting in potential data gaps across source systems and insufficient support for all required KPIs to underpin the equity narrative.
- The existing Qlik Warehouse may not fully capture or report on all critical metrics necessary for credible transaction reporting and investor scrutiny.
- Mandatory integration is required between the Finance System, CRM, ERP, and the Qlik Warehouse, with legacy data sources also in scope for at least three years of trended data.
- Some source systems lack automated connections to the Qlik Warehouse, necessitating manual, one-off data ingestions; recurring data refresh processes must be established for sources needing updates during the transaction.
- Required capabilities include equity narrative definition, a prioritized KPI framework, dashboard mock-ups, a data feasibility assessment, conceptual data model design, tooling recommendations, and a phased implementation roadmap.
- Automation is needed for data ingestion and refresh pipelines from all relevant source systems into the Qlik Warehouse to enable timely and accurate transaction reporting.
- Key user roles include senior leadership, exit advisors (Barclays), and potential acquirers/investors.
