# Understanding  

- The client’s current data environment lacks a unified source of truth, with multiple systems producing conflicting KPI figures and no reconciled dataset available for downstream reporting.  
- Manual reconciliation processes—spread across Excel files, CSV edits, and inherited Power BI logic—create long close cycles and dependency on key individuals.  
- Incomplete and inconsistent data mappings, particularly across business groups and transformation pipelines, hinder accurate consolidation and auditability.  
- Legacy constraints in existing processes (limited input frequency, short data horizons, and aggregated structures) restrict scalability and transparency in financial reporting.  
- Missing integrations from critical systems such as Sage 200 and Microsoft Dynamics NAV result in incomplete data capture and inconsistent reporting outputs.  
- The organization requires automated data ingestion, mapping, and reconciliation workflows to reduce manual effort and improve reliability of financial and operational reporting.  
- Key user groups include finance, FP&A, and operational reporting teams who need consistent, timely access to harmonized data for analysis and decision support.  
- Data governance and access controls must ensure secure handling of financial and customer information within a private equity portfolio context.
