# Understanding

- Limited granularity in existing pipeline reporting restricts analysis of the lead-to-AUM journey and marketing return on investment.
- Current reporting lacks integrated Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) insights, impeding a full understanding of marketing spend effectiveness.
- Enhanced L2A and CAC data modeling capabilities are required to provide detailed, actionable business metrics.
- Integration and data ingestion from QuickBooks are mandatory for comprehensive CAC reporting.
- Automated data ingestion and transformation workflows are necessary to ensure consistent, up-to-date L2A and CAC reporting.
- Data quality assessment is needed to ensure reliability and accuracy in CAC metrics.
- The solution must address the needs of data and technology stakeholders, senior leadership, and marketing teams as primary users.
- Dependence on access to Salesforce, QuickBooks, and existing Fabric and Synapse data platforms is a critical factor for implementation.
