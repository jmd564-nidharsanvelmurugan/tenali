# Outcomes

This engagement will provide VONQ with a robust, investor-ready exit reporting suite designed to strengthen the credibility of the M&A process and accelerate stakeholder alignment. Through the creation of a scalable data model, prioritized KPI framework, and intuitive dashboards, VONQ’s leadership and advisors will gain clearer, data-driven insights into core business performance—enhancing transparency, positioning, and transaction readiness. These outcomes are expected to directly improve confidence among potential acquirers and facilitate timely, informed decision-making throughout the exit process.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Investor-Ready Reporting Suite:** Delivery of a validated reporting framework that equips VONQ with clear, defendable KPIs and dashboards aligned to equity narratives for presentation to acquirers and advisors.
- **Timely Transaction Enablement:** Readiness to initiate Phase 2 execution with all required reporting design artefacts completed and approved within the defined four-week window, supporting VONQ’s exit timeline and minimizing risk of delays.
- **Improved Data Transparency:** Identification and documentation of current data gaps, remediation priorities, and end-to-end reporting lineage, enabling transparent, fact-based discussions with stakeholders and acquirers.
- **Standardized KPI Framework:** Establishment of an agreed, prioritized KPI set covering commercial, financial, and operational levers critical to investor reporting and due diligence.
- **Scalable Data Infrastructure:** Recommendations and conceptual designs for a reporting solution that is extensible to additional data sources, fully refreshable, and positioned for seamless automation post-transaction.
- **Stakeholder Confidence & Reduced Risk:** Clear communication of VONQ’s business performance and equity story, reducing reputational risk, increasing advisor confidence, and supporting credible negotiations with potential buyers.
