# Understanding

- The core business problem is the absence of an investor-ready, scalable exit reporting suite to underpin the M&A sale process and facilitate clear, data-backed equity narratives for acquirers and advisors.
- Current pain points include lack of investor-ready reporting, potential data gaps across source systems, and limitations in the Qlik Warehouse’s support for all KPIs required by the equity narrative.
- There are integration challenges stemming from the fact that some source systems do not have automated connections to the Qlik Warehouse, necessitating manual, one-off data ingestions or creation of new recurring refresh workflows during the transaction.
- The required capabilities are: clear equity narrative definition, a prioritized KPI framework, dashboard mock-ups, comprehensive data feasibility assessment, conceptual data model design, tooling recommendations, and an actionable phased implementation roadmap.
- Automated workflows are needed for data ingestion and refresh pipelines from source systems (Finance, CRM, ERP, and potentially legacy data), including recurring refresh for data that will be updated throughout the transaction period.
- Mandatory integrations must be established between the Qlik Warehouse and the Finance System, CRM, ERP, as well as with legacy sources required to provide a minimum of three years’ trended data.
- Primary user roles include senior leadership, exit advisors (Barclays), and potential acquirers/investors—each requiring tailored access to reporting aligned with the equity narrative.
- The process is constrained by exit timeline pressures, stakeholder availability for workshops and review, timely provisioning of data access, and the quality and completeness of Qlik Warehouse data.
- Security and compliance requirements have not been detailed within the provided questionnaire.
