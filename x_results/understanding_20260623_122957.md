# Understanding

- VONQ requires a scalable exit reporting suite to support its M&A sale process and provide timely, investor-grade reporting.
- There is a lack of investor-ready reporting capabilities, creating challenges in presenting the equity narrative and key KPIs to potential acquirers and advisors.
- Potential data gaps exist between the Finance, CRM, ERP, and Qlik Warehouse systems, which may impact the completeness and credibility of the exit reporting suite.
- The current Qlik Warehouse may not fully support all required KPIs necessary for transaction and stakeholder communications.
- Manual or non-automated data ingestion from source systems poses a risk to timely and accurate reporting, particularly when recurring data refresh is needed during the transaction.
- A prioritised KPI framework, dashboard mock-ups, data feasibility assessment, and conceptual data model design are required to underpin exit reporting and support investor communications.
- Automated data ingestion and refresh pipelines from core source systems (Finance, CRM, ERP, and legacy sources) into the Qlik Warehouse are needed, especially for sources requiring ongoing updates during the transaction period.
- Mandatory integrations include Finance System, CRM, ERP, and potentially legacy data sources to deliver at least three years of trended data required for investor and transaction reporting.
- Senior leadership, exit advisors (Barclays), and potential acquirers or investors are primary user roles who need access to the reporting suite for decision-making and due diligence.
