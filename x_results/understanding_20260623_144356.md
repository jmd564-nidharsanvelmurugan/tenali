# Understanding

- Limited granularity in current pipeline reporting prevents clear visibility into the journey from lead to asset-under-management (AUM) conversion.
- Lack of integrated Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) insights hinders full assessment of marketing spend effectiveness.
- Need for enhanced data models supporting detailed Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and CAC reporting for improved business analysis.
- Integration with QuickBooks is required to consolidate financial data relevant to CAC calculations.
- Automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines are needed to improve the timeliness and accuracy of L2A and CAC reporting.
- Data quality assessment is essential to ensure the reliability of input data for business reporting.
- Mandatory integration with Salesforce and QuickBooks is necessary for comprehensive data flows.
- The primary users of the enhanced reporting and pipelines will be data and technology stakeholders, senior leadership, and marketing teams.
