# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with VONQ, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

1. **Equity Narratives Definition**
   - Clearly articulated equity narratives designed to support acquirer and investor communications.
   - Drafted in collaboration with key stakeholders to ensure accuracy and resonance with VONQ’s market positioning.
   - Delivered as a presentation deck and executive summary document.

2. **Metrics that Matter KPI Framework**
   - Prioritised list of financial, commercial, and operational KPIs required for investor-grade reporting.
   - Detailed definitions, calculation logic, and source mappings for each KPI.
   - Provided as a structured Excel/MS Word artifact and summary slides for inclusion in reporting packs.

3. **Dashboard Mock-ups**
   - Static visual mock-ups illustrating proposed dashboard structures, navigation flows, and key views.
   - Aligned to the defined KPI framework and equity narratives.
   - Delivered as annotated PDF markups and accompanying presentation slides.

4. **Data Feasibility Assessment**
   - Comprehensive assessment of available data across Finance, CRM, ERP, and legacy sources.
   - Identification of key data gaps, risks, and required remediation for investor-ready reporting.
   - Delivered as a written assessment report and summary playback deck.

5. **Conceptual Data Model Design**
   - High-level data model depicting integration flows, key entities, relationships, and source-to-target mapping.
   - Incorporates the Qlik Warehouse as the foundation with extensions for identified future needs.
   - Provided as a data model diagram (e.g., Visio or Lucidchart) and supporting technical documentation.

6. **Data Infrastructure and Tooling Recommendations**
   - Assessment of options for reporting tools and data infrastructure, including pros, cons, and scalability considerations.
   - Trade-off analysis and recommendations clearly articulated for Phase 2 planning.
   - Delivered as a comparative options matrix and an executive memorandum.

7. **Implementation Roadmap for Phase 2**
   - Phased plan outlining key milestones, sequencing, dependencies, and critical success factors for technical build-out.
   - Identification of priority use cases, resource needs, and risks.
   - Delivered as a Gantt chart/timeline and supporting narrative roadmap document.

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with VONQ's leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
