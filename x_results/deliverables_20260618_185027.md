# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with PURE Financial Advisors LLC, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

1. **KPI Book**  
- Comprehensive documentation of Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) metrics, including definitions, calculation logic, and data source mapping.  
- Clear alignment of KPIs with business objectives and marketing ROI measurement.  
- Inclusion of dimension dictionary and slicing attributes for consistent reporting across Power BI views.  

2. **Power BI Mock‑ups (8 Views)**  
- Design of up to eight Power BI dashboard mock‑ups illustrating the future‑state L2A and CAC reporting experience.  
- Visualization of key metrics, conversion funnels, and marketing performance insights.  
- Layout and navigation blueprint to guide the subsequent build phase and user experience refinement.  

3. **Data Model Design**  
- Detailed schema for the integrated L2A and CAC data model connecting Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Fabric, and QuickBooks.  
- Logical and physical data model diagrams showing relationships, hierarchies, and data flow.  
- Documentation of data transformation logic and integration points to support automated ingestion pipelines.  

4. **Data Gap Assessment**  
- Identification and evaluation of missing or inconsistent data elements across Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks.  
- Recommendations for data quality improvement and remediation actions.  
- Prioritization of gaps based on impact to KPI accuracy and reporting completeness.  

5. **Technical Approach Document**  
- Detailed description of the technical architecture supporting batch data ingestion and transformation within Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse.  
- Integration approach for QuickBooks and Salesforce, including data flow diagrams and control mechanisms.  
- Outline of automation, refresh cadence, and governance considerations for scalable reporting.  

6. **Phase 3B Implementation Plan**  
- Structured roadmap detailing activities, milestones, and dependencies for the build and deployment phase.  
- Sequencing of tasks related to data integration, model development, and Power BI report implementation.  
- Resource plan and timeline aligned with the 8–12‑week delivery window and dependency on QuickBooks ingestion.  

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with PURE Financial Advisors LLC's leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
