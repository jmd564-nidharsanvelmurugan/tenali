# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with PURE Financial Advisors LLC, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

1. **KPI Book**  
- Comprehensive documentation of Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) metrics, including definitions, calculation logic, and data source mapping.  

- Alignment of KPIs with business objectives to ensure consistent measurement across marketing, data, and leadership teams.  

- Inclusion of dimension dictionary outlining segmentation criteria for pipeline and acquisition analysis.  

2. **Power BI Mock‑Ups (up to 8 views)**  
- Design of interactive dashboard mock‑ups illustrating the future‑state L2A and CAC reporting suite.  

- Visualization of key metrics, conversion funnels, and marketing ROI insights for leadership review.  

- Layout and navigation blueprint to guide the subsequent build phase within Power BI.  

3. **Data Model Design**  
- Detailed schema defining relationships between Salesforce, Tamarac, QuickBooks, and Azure data sources.  

- Logical and physical data model diagrams supporting automated ingestion and transformation pipelines.  

- Documentation of data entities, attributes, and lineage to enable scalable reporting architecture.  

4. **Data Gap Assessment**  
- Identification of missing or inconsistent data elements across source systems impacting L2A and CAC reporting.  

- Evaluation of data quality and completeness, with recommendations for remediation and enrichment.  

- Prioritization of data gaps based on business impact and feasibility of resolution.  

5. **Technical Approach Document**  
- Detailed description of the proposed integration and automation strategy for QuickBooks and Salesforce data ingestion.  

- Specification of batch processing workflows within Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse environments.  

- Outline of technical dependencies, control points, and validation steps to ensure reliable data flow.  

6. **Phase 3B Implementation Plan**  
- Structured roadmap detailing activities, timelines, and resource requirements for the build and deployment phase.  

- Sequencing of tasks for data integration, model development, and Power BI report implementation.  

- Risk and dependency matrix highlighting key milestones and success criteria for project execution.  

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with PURE Financial Advisors LLC's leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
