# Approach

PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC has engaged us to deliver an enhanced reporting solution designed to provide granular, actionable insights into the Lead-to-AUM (L2A) pipeline and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC). This solution will streamline data integration, reporting, and analytics across key platforms, laying the foundation for data-driven marketing investments and pipeline management. Our methodology leverages proven data engineering and business intelligence practices, utilizing Microsoft Fabric, Azure Synapse, Power BI, and seamless integration with Salesforce and QuickBooks to deliver reliable, scalable outcomes.

Our engagement follows a structured, phased approach to ensure clarity and excellence at every stage. Each phase includes defined deliverables, stakeholder engagement, and clear activities to provide transparency. The discovery and design phase (Phase 3A) will be followed by a comprehensive implementation phase (Phase 3B), with each phase mapped to specific timelines. The work is designed to align with PURE’s objectives, minimize risk, and ensure all requirements and dependencies are addressed effectively.

---

**Phase 3A: Discovery & Design, Duration: 3 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 to Week 3**

**Summary:** Define reporting requirements, data models, and technical approach for L2A and CAC reporting.

**Activities:**
- Conduct detailed workshops with data, technology, and marketing stakeholders to confirm reporting needs and KPI definitions.
- Document data sources and map existing data flows from Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks.
- Assess data quality and gaps, with special attention to QuickBooks ingestion.
- Design L2A and CAC data models to address granular pipeline and marketing cost insight requirements.
- Develop Power BI mock-ups (up to 8 views) and KPI Book outlining calculations and visualizations.
- Prepare the Phase 3B Implementation Plan and Technical Approach documentation.

---

**Phase 3B: Build & Integration, Duration: 8–12 weeks, Timeline: Week 4 to Week 15**

**Summary:** Implement data pipelines, data models, and Power BI reporting suite with automated ingestion and integration.

**Activities:**
- Configure and validate automated batch data pipelines in Microsoft Fabric, integrating Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks.
- Build and deploy the enhanced L2A and CAC data model, ensuring comprehensive mapping and data transformation.
- Integrate QuickBooks with the data platform, resolving data ingestion and quality dependencies.
- Develop and publish the 8-view Power BI reporting suite, automated refreshes, and user access controls.
- Execute thorough testing cycles—data validation, report accuracy, and stakeholder feedback loops.
- Deliver final KPI Book, data model documentation, and user training for operational reporting.

---

This phased engagement ensures PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC receives a robust, scalable, and actionable L2A and CAC reporting suite. By sequencing discovery, design, and implementation, our approach provides measurable value at each milestone, reduces delivery risks, and ensures the final solution supports PURE’s long-term vision for data-driven growth.
