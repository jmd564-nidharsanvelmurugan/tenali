# Understanding

- Existing pipeline reporting lacks sufficient granularity to track the full journey from lead to assets under management (AUM) and provides limited insight into customer acquisition cost (CAC).
- Current reporting does not deliver integrated or comprehensive CAC insights, impacting the ability to assess marketing effectiveness and ROI.
- The process requires enhanced data models specifically for Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and CAC tracking, supported by an expanded Power BI reporting suite.
- Data ingestion and quality assessment for QuickBooks is a mandatory integration, necessary for accurate CAC reporting.
- Automated data ingestion and transformation workflows are needed to enable batch processing across the Microsoft Fabric platform for L2A and CAC reporting.
- Data and technology stakeholders, senior leadership, and marketing teams are primary users of the enhanced reporting and analytics capabilities.
- Dependencies exist on access to Salesforce, QuickBooks, and current cloud data platforms (Fabric and Synapse) for end-to-end reporting.
- Timeline and delivery are constrained by the completion of QuickBooks integration and data quality assessment before comprehensive L2A and CAC reporting can be realized.
