# Understanding

- Pure Financial Advisors seeks to address limited visibility and granularity in its existing pipeline reporting, specifically around the Lead-to-AUM (L2A) journey and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) metrics.  
- Current reporting lacks integrated CAC insights, preventing a full understanding of marketing effectiveness and conversion performance.  
- Data ingestion from QuickBooks is not yet established, creating a gap in financial data needed for accurate CAC calculations.  
- The firm requires an enhanced L2A and CAC data model capable of supporting detailed Power BI reporting and KPI tracking.  
- Automated data ingestion and transformation workflows are needed to reduce manual effort and ensure consistent, timely reporting updates.  
- Primary users will include data and technology stakeholders, senior leadership, and marketing teams who rely on accurate pipeline and acquisition metrics.  
- Mandatory integrations include Salesforce and QuickBooks to enable unified reporting across lead management and financial data sources.
