# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

1. **KPI Book**
   - Comprehensive documentation of Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) KPIs, definitions, and calculation logic.
   - Detailing of data sources, transformations, and metric ownership.
   - Delivered in PDF and PowerPoint formats.
   - Provided by the end of Phase 3A (Week 3).

2. **Power BI Mock-Ups**
   - Interactive wireframes and visual mock-ups for up to 8 Power BI report views focused on L2A and CAC reporting.
   - Illustrations of proposed dashboards for user roles and key business processes.
   - Delivered as clickable prototypes or static images (PDF/PowerPoint).
   - Delivered in Phase 3A (Week 3).

3. **Data Model Design**
   - Logical and physical data model schemas for supporting L2A and CAC analytics, integrating Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks data.
   - Data dictionary with definitions, relationships, and mapping rules.
   - Delivered as ER diagrams, schema files, and supporting documentation.
   - Delivered by the end of Phase 3A (Week 3).

4. **Data Gap Assessment**
   - Analysis and summary of gaps in existing data sources related to pipeline and CAC metrics.
   - Recommendations to address data quality, accessibility, and completeness issues.
   - Deliverable includes spreadsheet and summary report in PDF format.
   - Delivered in Phase 3A (Week 3).

5. **Technical Approach**
   - Detailed technical specification for integrating QuickBooks with the data platform and automating batch ingestion pipelines.
   - Definition of transformation logic, data mapping, and Power BI refresh processes.
   - Provided in a technical design document (Word/PDF).
   - Delivered at the close of Phase 3A (Week 3).

6. **Phase 3B Implementation Plan**
   - Roadmap outlining build activities, milestones, and resource assignments for implementing the L2A and CAC reporting suite.
   - Includes a Gantt chart visualizing sequence, dependencies, and estimated timelines (8–12 weeks for Phase 3B).
   - Delivered in PDF and editable Gantt chart file formats.
   - Delivered at the conclusion of Phase 3A (Week 3).

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC’s leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
