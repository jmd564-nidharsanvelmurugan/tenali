# Outcomes

The engagement with VONQ is designed to deliver a measurable transformation in exit reporting, equipping the company with investor-grade insights and a scalable reporting suite to underpin the upcoming transaction. By standardising KPIs and aligning dashboards to equity narratives, VONQ will enhance stakeholder communications, accelerate the exit process, and improve data readiness to support long-term business scalability.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Investor-Ready Reporting Suite:** A validated exit reporting design—featuring equity narrative-aligned dashboards and KPIs—that meets the requirements of acquirers, advisors, and internal leadership for transaction due diligence and investor presentations.

- **Accelerated Exit Timeline:** Reduction in reporting-related delays through streamlined data ingestion and automated refresh processes, allowing VONQ to meet critical exit milestones and respond efficiently to investor queries.

- **KPI Framework Standardisation:** Consistent definitions, calculation logic, and prioritised metrics provide clarity for stakeholders, supporting credible storytelling and mitigating risks associated with data gaps or inconsistent reporting.

- **Transparency on Data Gaps & Remediation Priorities:** Clear identification and documentation of existing data limitations, enabling targeted remediation and proactive risk management throughout the exit preparation phase.

- **Improved Stakeholder Decision-Making:** Leadership, advisors, and potential buyers gain improved visibility into commercial, financial, and operational performance, supporting informed investment decisions and reinforcing VONQ's equity narrative.

- **Scalable Data Infrastructure:** Conceptual data model and tooling recommendations enable future extensibility, ensuring that reporting assets deliver ongoing value and remain automatable post-transaction [Client Questionnaire].
