# Business Impact

- **Enhanced Marketing ROI Visibility:** Establishes a unified Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting framework, enabling PURE Financial Advisors to quantify marketing effectiveness and optimize spend allocation across channels.  
- **Operational Efficiency through Automation:** Automates data ingestion and transformation from Salesforce, QuickBooks, and Tamarac, reducing manual reporting effort and accelerating insight delivery cycles by an estimated 40–60%.  
- **Data‑Driven Growth Decisions:** Provides leadership with granular, actionable insights into lead conversion and asset growth, supporting more informed decisions on client acquisition strategies and advisor performance.  
- **Improved Data Accuracy and Consistency:** Introduces standardized KPI definitions and a single source of truth within Microsoft Fabric and Power BI, minimizing reporting discrepancies and improving confidence in business metrics.  
- **Scalable Reporting Foundation:** Builds a flexible, extensible data model that supports future data sources and reporting needs, ensuring scalability as PURE expands its client base and service offerings.  
- **Investor and Stakeholder Transparency:** Equips Lee Equity Partners and senior management with diligence‑grade reporting on marketing ROI and pipeline performance, strengthening confidence in PURE’s growth trajectory.  

By delivering these outcomes, this engagement will enable PURE Financial Advisors LLC to transform its pipeline and marketing performance reporting into a strategic growth engine—driving data‑led decision‑making, operational efficiency, and long‑term value creation in the competitive U.S. financial advisory market.
