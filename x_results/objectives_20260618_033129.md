# Objectives  

- Establish a unified, scalable data platform that serves as the single source of truth for all business and reporting needs.  
- Define the foundational data architecture and models required to support consistent, automated reporting across the organization.  
- Enable transparent and reliable financial and operational insights to strengthen decision-making and investor confidence.  
- Create a clear framework for mapping and reconciling customer and revenue data across systems to ensure accuracy and consistency.  
- Support future growth by designing a data environment that is adaptable to advanced analytics and automation opportunities.
