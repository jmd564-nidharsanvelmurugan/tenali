# Objectives

- Achieve a fully designed exit reporting suite that provides clear equity narratives, a prioritised KPI framework, intuitive dashboard mock-ups, a scalable conceptual data model, tooling recommendations, and an implementation roadmap to guide future phases.  
- Enable automated data ingestion and recurring refresh pipelines from core source systems—Finance System, CRM, ERP, and legacy sources—ensuring reliable and up-to-date reporting throughout the transaction process.  
- Deliver investor-grade visibility for leadership, offering insights into key commercial, financial, and operational metrics that support equity narratives and underpin the sale process.  
- Enhance user experience through dashboards that are intuitive, aligned to equity narratives, and provide clear KPI definitions, calculation logic, and structured navigation.  
- Standardise exit reporting processes, refine KPI definition, and advance data infrastructure readiness for investor scrutiny.  
- Establish a long-term foundation with outputs intended as lasting business assets that are fully automatable post-transaction, ensuring future scalability and extensibility as business requirements evolve.  
- Ensure the data model and reporting suite are extensible to additional data sources, with ongoing refresh capability and future integration flexibility beyond the current transaction.  
- Integrate the future platform with key business systems (Finance System, CRM, ERP, and Qlik Warehouse), with options for alternative tooling assessed to optimise scalability and adaptability.
