# Objectives

- Establish a fully designed exit reporting suite that provides credible, investor-grade visibility into key commercial, financial, and operational metrics supporting the equity narrative.
- Define and implement a prioritised KPI framework aligned to the requirements of the M&A sale process and investor communications.
- Enable automated, refreshable data ingestion pipelines from core business systems (Finance System, CRM, ERP, and legacy sources) to ensure timely and accurate reporting throughout the transaction.
- Deliver intuitive dashboards with clear KPI definitions, calculation logic, and a user-friendly navigation structure tailored for senior leadership, advisors, and potential investors.
- Standardise exit reporting processes, KPI calculations, and data infrastructure to meet investor scrutiny and facilitate seamless due diligence.
- Design an extensible, scalable data model and reporting suite that can be readily expanded to future data sources and supports ongoing automation and scalability beyond the transaction.
