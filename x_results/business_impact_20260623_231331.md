# Business Impact

- **Enhanced Financial Visibility:** By implementing detailed Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting, Pure Financial Advisors LLC will gain a granular understanding of the pipeline journey and marketing ROI, enabling data-driven budget allocation decisions and increasing overall return on marketing spend.

- **Operational Efficiency Gains:** Automating data ingestion and transformation from critical sources such as Salesforce and QuickBooks will eliminate manual consolidation tasks, streamline workflows for the Data, Technology, and Marketing teams, and enable faster, more reliable reporting cycles.

- **Improved Decision-Making:** Leadership and stakeholders will benefit from timely, accurate KPIs presented through intuitive Power BI dashboards. This visibility into lead conversion rates and acquisition costs supports targeted strategy adjustments, potentially reducing customer acquisition costs and improving funnel conversion by identifying bottlenecks and opportunities.

- **Strategic Value Creation:** Establishing an integrated, scalable data model and reporting suite will position Pure for long-term analytics maturity, supporting expansion to new data sources and more advanced analytics with minimal incremental investment.

- **Competitive Advantage:** By achieving best-in-class marketing attribution and pipeline analysis, Pure distinguishes itself in the wealth management sector, providing clients, investors, and partners—including key stakeholders like Lee Equity Partners—with transparent performance insights and reinforcing its reputation as a data-driven organization.

The proposed solution will drive both immediate and sustainable business value for Pure Financial Advisors LLC by delivering accurate, actionable insights, reducing operational effort, and enabling data-informed growth strategies across the organization. This foundation ensures Pure remains agile and competitive as data and reporting needs continue to evolve.
