# Objectives

- Establish a fully designed exit reporting suite that supports clear equity narratives and prioritised KPI frameworks for investor communication.
- Enable automated and refreshable data ingestion pipelines from key source systems to ensure timely and accurate transaction reporting.
- Provide senior leadership and stakeholders with investor-grade visibility into business metrics directly aligned to the equity narrative.
- Deliver intuitive, user-friendly dashboards with well-defined KPIs, calculation logic, and structured navigation to improve stakeholder experience.
- Standardise exit reporting processes and enhance data infrastructure readiness for rigorous investor scrutiny.
- Create a scalable data model and reporting suite extensible to additional data sources and able to support ongoing refresh needs beyond the transaction.
- Ensure integration of Finance System, CRM, ERP, and Qlik Warehouse, with options for alternative tooling assessed for long-term scalability.
