# Business Impact

- **Optimized Marketing ROI:** Implementing enhanced Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting will provide timely, actionable insights into the effectiveness of marketing spend, enabling Pure Financial Advisors to allocate resources towards the highest-performing channels and directly improve return on investment.

- **Reduced Manual Effort Through Automation:** Automating data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes across Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Azure Fabric eliminates manual consolidation of pipeline data. This streamlining is expected to reduce manual reporting workload by up to 40%, freeing up data and marketing teams to focus on value-add analysis and initiatives.

- **Granular Performance Visibility:** The new Power BI reporting suite, with clearly defined KPIs and up to 8 visual dashboards, delivers a granular view of the entire customer journey—from lead generation to AUM acquisition. This heightened visibility empowers leadership to rapidly identify bottlenecks in the acquisition funnel and address issues proactively.

- **Cost Control and Efficiency Gains:** With integrated CAC reporting and improved pipeline transparency, leadership can accurately track acquisition expenses and make informed decisions about budget allocation. Early detection of cost overruns and underperforming campaigns is expected to improve cost management and potentially reduce overall CAC by 10-15%.

- **Scalable, Future-Ready Data Ecosystem:** By leveraging Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse for batch-based, automated data pipelines, the solution is engineered for scalability. It supports future data source integrations and increasing reporting needs, ensuring that Pure’s data platform evolves in tandem with business growth.

- **Strategic Decision Support:** Accurate, transparent L2A and CAC metrics enhance strategic planning for senior leadership and investment partners. Access to reliable, real-time performance data fosters data-driven decisions that strengthen Pure’s position in the competitive financial advisory market.

Together, these business impacts will transform how Pure Financial Advisors understands its marketing and sales funnel, optimizes resource allocations, and tracks value creation. The solution’s automation, analytics, and scalability ensure both immediate operational gains and long-term strategic advantage, positioning Pure for sustained, data-driven growth.
