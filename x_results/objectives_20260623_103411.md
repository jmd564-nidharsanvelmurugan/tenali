# Objectives

- Establish a comprehensive Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data model, enabling granular insights into pipeline performance and marketing ROI.  
- Automate data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes to ensure timely, accurate, and consistent analytics.
- Integrate data sources across Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Microsoft Fabric to create a unified reporting environment.
- Deliver improved Power BI dashboards featuring clear KPI definitions and advanced visualizations for enhanced decision-making by leadership.
- Strengthen pipeline reporting and CAC tracking processes to support actionable, data-driven business strategies.
- Enable scalable reporting capabilities and data maturity to accommodate future growth, additional data sources, and evolving business needs.
