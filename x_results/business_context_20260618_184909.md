# Business Context

PURE Financial Advisors LLC operates in the U.S. financial advisory and wealth management sector, providing personalized financial advisory services and asset management solutions to individual clients. The firm follows a B2C business model and is supported by key stakeholders including Jason Carver, Client Engagement Manager, and Lee Equity Partners as its investment partner. PURE’s operations rely on a modern data and technology stack encompassing Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Synapse, Microsoft Fabric, Power BI, and QuickBooks.

The current initiative is driven by PURE’s need to enhance its Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) pipeline and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting. Existing pipeline reporting lacks the granularity required to fully understand the journey from lead generation to assets under management and to measure marketing effectiveness. By developing a more integrated and automated data model, PURE aims to connect financial and marketing data sources, improve data quality, and deliver actionable insights through Power BI dashboards. This effort will enable leadership to make more informed decisions on marketing ROI and pipeline performance, supporting the firm’s broader goal of advancing data maturity and scalable reporting capabilities for future growth.
