# Business Context

PURE Financial Advisors LLC operates in the U.S. financial advisory and wealth management sector, providing personalized financial advisory services and asset management solutions to individual clients. The firm follows a B2C model and is supported by key stakeholders including Jason Carver, Client Engagement Manager, and Lee Equity Partners as its investment partner. PURE’s operations rely on a modern data and technology stack encompassing Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Synapse, Microsoft Fabric, Power BI, and QuickBooks.

The current initiative is driven by PURE’s need to strengthen its Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) pipeline and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting. Existing pipeline reporting lacks the granularity required to fully understand the journey from lead generation to asset under management and to measure marketing effectiveness. By integrating QuickBooks data and enhancing data quality, PURE aims to deliver a unified, automated reporting framework that provides leadership with actionable insights into marketing ROI and conversion performance. This effort represents a key step toward building scalable, data‑driven reporting capabilities that support the firm’s continued growth and operational maturity.
