# Outcomes

This engagement will equip VONQ with a validated exit reporting design that strengthens its M&A readiness and investor communications. By defining a robust equity narrative, establishing a prioritized KPI framework, and mapping data gaps, the project ensures that senior leadership, advisors, and potential acquirers can access clear, actionable insights. Success is measured by the client's ability to confidently present data-backed narratives and initiate Phase 2 of the exit process within the projected timeline.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Investor-Ready Reporting Suite:** Provides a fully validated design for dashboards and reporting, enabling leadership to communicate a credible equity story to acquirers and advisors.
- **Prioritised KPI Framework:** Delivers a structured set of commercial, financial, and operational KPIs aligned to investor requirements and the equity narrative, supporting informed decision-making.
- **Clear Data Gap Identification:** Offers visibility into missing or inconsistent data across source systems, with defined remediation priorities to ensure completeness ahead of transaction scrutiny.
- **Scalable Data Model Foundations:** Establishes a flexible conceptual data model that can integrate additional sources and support recurring refreshes post-transaction, securing long-term business asset value.
- **Accelerated Exit Preparation:** Facilitates a swift, stakeholder-driven design process, enabling VONQ to initiate Phase 2 build activities without delay and maintain alignment with exit timeline constraints.
- **Enhanced Stakeholder Confidence:** Reduces reputational risk and builds trust with advisors, potential buyers, and leadership by demonstrating rigorous data readiness and structured exit planning.

These outcomes directly address VONQ’s strategic objectives, ensuring successful Phase 1 completion and positioning the business for a seamless transition into Phase 2 of the exit process.
