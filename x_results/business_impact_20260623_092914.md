# Business Impact

- **Accelerated Exit Readiness:** By delivering a fully designed exit reporting suite and investor-ready KPI framework within a 4-week timeframe, VONQ can expedite the M&A process, minimizing delays and maximizing transaction value during a strategic sale window.

- **Robust Financial Transparency:** The solution empowers finance, commercial, and leadership teams with dashboards aligned to equity narratives, ensuring comprehensive, data-backed visibility into commercial, operational, and financial performance. This level of transparency mitigates risks of data inconsistencies and supports credible engagement with potential acquirers and advisors.

- **Operational Efficiency & Automation:** Automating data ingestion and refresh pipelines from source systems into the Qlik Warehouse reduces manual data handling for recurring transaction reporting—potentially saving up to 50% of the time currently spent on manual aggregation and validation, based on industry averages for similar SaaS reporting transformations [Maximize Your SaaS Value with Automated Reporting](https://www.gartner.com/en/documents/3993674).

- **Strategic Decision Support:** The prioritised KPI framework and conceptual data model deliver investor-grade insights, enabling leadership to articulate a clear, data-driven equity narrative. This increases confidence among acquirers and enhances VONQ's ability to command a premium valuation.

- **Future-Proof, Scalable Architecture:** Establishing a phased roadmap, extensible data model, and tooling recommendations ensures that the reporting suite and infrastructure can scale post-transaction, supporting additional data sources and business growth without expensive rework.

VONQ’s engagement in this initiative will strengthen its position in the market by enabling rapid, credible, and insightful reporting that meets or exceeds investor expectations. The phase 1 deliverables lay the foundation for a seamless transition into technical development, mitigate reputational and transaction timeline risks, and create business assets with lasting strategic value.
