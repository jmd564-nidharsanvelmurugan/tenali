# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with VONQ, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

1. **Equity Narratives Definition**
   - A clear articulation of VONQ’s equity stories, aligned with key value drivers for the exit process.
   - Documentation of stakeholder-validated positioning statements for use in M&A and investor communications.
   - Delivered in a presentation deck and reference document by end of Week 1.

2. **Metrics that Matter KPI Framework**
   - A prioritized framework of critical KPIs spanning commercial, financial, and operational dimensions.
   - Definitions, calculation logic, and mapping of each KPI to the relevant equity narrative.
   - Excel-based KPI register and supporting documentation, provided by end of Week 2.

3. **Dashboard Mock-ups**
   - Static wireframes illustrating the proposed dashboard layouts, visualizations, and navigation structure.
   - Alignment of dashboard views to user personas, reporting needs, and key KPIs.
   - Delivered as annotated PDF or clickable prototype by end of Week 3.

4. **Data Feasibility Assessment**
   - Comprehensive review and playback of current-state data architecture, availability, and data quality across all in-scope systems.
   - Identification and documentation of data gaps and remediation priorities for exit reporting readiness.
   - Summary report and findings presentation, delivered during Week 2 stakeholder workshops.

5. **Conceptual Data Model Design**
   - A scalable, high-level data model diagram tailored to VONQ’s reporting and integration requirements.
   - Description of data flows from source systems (Finance, CRM, ERP, legacy sources) to the Qlik Warehouse.
   - Delivered as a technical schematic and supporting documentation by end of Week 3.

6. **Data Infrastructure and Tooling Recommendations**
   - Assessment of current infrastructure and proposed options for enhancements or alternative tools required for exit reporting.
   - Trade-off analysis of tooling scenarios, covering pros and cons, scalability, and future extensibility.
   - Recommendations document and decision matrix, delivered in Week 3.

7. **Implementation Roadmap for Phase 2**
   - Detailed, actionable roadmap for the build and deployment phase, outlining priority use cases, dependencies, risks, and key milestones.
   - Phased plan including preliminary resource estimates, sequencing, and timelines to support a successful transition to Phase 2.
   - Delivered as a Gantt chart and planning document by end of Week 4.

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with VONQ's leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
