# Approach

This engagement with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC will employ a structured, phased methodology to deliver a comprehensive Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data model and reporting suite. Our approach emphasizes collaboration between data, technology, and marketing teams, utilizing a blend of business analysis, technical design, and iterative build activities. By leveraging existing platforms—Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Fabric, Synapse, and Power BI—alongside new integrations with QuickBooks, we will ensure seamless data flows and scalable, actionable reporting.

The methodology prioritizes discovery-driven design and robust automation, ensuring that all solution components align with the client’s expectations for scalability, governance, and usability. Each phase is defined with clear deliverables, success criteria, and timelines, maintaining focus on detailed KPI logic, automated data ingestion, and intuitive visualization via Power BI. Our phased approach ensures transparency, risk mitigation, and a logical progression from discovery through solution deployment.

---

**Phase 3A: Discovery & Design, Duration: 3 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 to Week 3**  
**Summary:** Develop the solution blueprint—including KPI definitions, Power BI mock-ups, and an actionable data model design—to establish a clear foundation for build activities.

**Activities:**
- Conduct stakeholder workshops to confirm L2A and CAC reporting requirements and prioritize granular insights.
- Document and define KPIs, calculation logic, and business rules for L2A and CAC metrics in a comprehensive KPI Book.
- Assess current data sources (Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks) and evaluate data quality, integration points, and automation needs.
- Create Power BI mock-ups (up to 8 pages) illustrating target dashboards, user flows, and visualization priorities.
- Define the technical architecture, data model design, and transformation pipeline logic, covering batch processing in Microsoft Fabric.
- Prepare a Data Gap Assessment and a detailed Phase 3B Implementation Plan aligning project dependencies and sequencing.

---

**Phase 3B: Build & Implementation, Duration: 8–12 weeks, Timeline: Week 4 to Week 15**  
**Summary:** Integrate data sources, automate ingestion and transformation, and deploy the L2A and CAC reporting suite in Power BI.

**Activities:**
- Configure and validate batch integration between QuickBooks, Salesforce, and the Azure Fabric/Synapse platform.
- Implement automated data pipelines for ingestion, transformation, and refresh of L2A and CAC datasets according to the Data Model and Technical Approach.
- Complete Power BI dashboard development aligned with prior mock-ups, ensuring KPI alignment and user experience objectives.
- Conduct end-to-end data quality testing and validation, with stakeholder walk-throughs for feedback and acceptance.
- Finalize the KPI Book and Technical Documentation, ensuring long-term maintainability and knowledge transfer.
- Deliver Phase 3B Implementation Plan updates, with recommendations for further scalability and future enhancements.

---

This phased approach maximizes alignment between business objectives and technical execution, providing PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC with actionable insights, automated processes, and a robust foundation for scalable data-driven decision-making. By following this structured methodology, the engagement delivers high-value outcomes while ensuring transparency, adaptability, and a clear path to long-term reporting maturity.
