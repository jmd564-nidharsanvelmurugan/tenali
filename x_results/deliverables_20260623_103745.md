# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

1. **KPI Book**
   - Comprehensive documentation of Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) metrics.
   - Detailed definitions, formulas, and logic for each KPI relevant to pipeline and marketing ROI.
   - Includes supporting rationale for KPI selection and business impact.
   - Delivered in a digital format at the conclusion of the discovery phase.

2. **Power BI Mock-ups**
   - Wireframes/prototypes of the enhanced Power BI dashboards, limited to 8 distinct views.
   - Visualizations centered on L2A and CAC reporting requirements.
   - Incorporates key data elements and intended user interactions for data and marketing teams.
   - Provided as interactive files and static images during the design phase.

3. **Data Model Design**
   - Detailed architecture and schema for the Lead-to-AUM and CAC data model.
   - Specifies entity relationships, data flows, and integration points for Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks.
   - Includes data dictionary and mapping documentation.
   - Delivered as technical diagrams and documentation during the design phase.

4. **Data Gap Assessment**
   - Assessment of data quality and completeness for reporting requirements.
   - Identification of missing or insufficient data points, especially regarding QuickBooks integration.
   - Recommendations for remediation to ensure reliable insights.
   - Provided as a written report at the end of the discovery phase.

5. **Technical Approach**
   - Description of technical methodologies for automating data ingestion and transformation pipelines.
   - Integration strategies for QuickBooks, Salesforce, and other primary data sources within Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse.
   - Detailed plan for automated batch processing and reporting refresh cycles.
   - Delivered as a whitepaper and presentation during the design phase.

6. **Phase 3B Implementation Plan**
   - Phased project roadmap outlining key milestones, timelines, and dependencies for solution build and deployment.
   - Incorporates resource requirements, risk mitigation strategies, and quality assurance checkpoints.
   - Aligns delivery schedule with integration and data quality assessment dependencies.
   - Provided as a project management document at the conclusion of the design phase, prior to build.

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC's leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
