# Outcomes

This engagement will deliver measurable business impact and strategic value by enabling PURE Financial Advisors LLC to achieve data-driven visibility into its Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) performance. The initiative aligns with PURE’s B2C financial advisory model and the priorities of key stakeholders, including Lee Equity Partners, by strengthening marketing ROI measurement and pipeline transparency across the U.S. operations.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Granular Lead-to-AUM Visibility:** Development of a unified L2A data model will provide detailed insights into the full customer journey from lead generation through to assets under management, enabling leadership to identify conversion drivers and optimize acquisition strategies.  
- **Accurate Customer Acquisition Cost Measurement:** Integration of QuickBooks and Salesforce data will allow precise calculation of CAC metrics, improving understanding of marketing spend efficiency and supporting data-backed budget allocation decisions.  
- **Automated and Reliable Reporting Processes:** Implementation of automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines will eliminate manual reporting steps, ensuring consistent, timely, and accurate updates to Power BI dashboards.  
- **Enhanced Marketing ROI Insights:** The new reporting suite will enable PURE to evaluate the effectiveness of marketing campaigns and channels, directly linking spend to AUM growth and improving strategic investment decisions.  
- **Improved Leadership Decision-Making:** With standardized KPIs and clear visualizations in Power BI, senior management will gain actionable insights into pipeline performance, supporting faster and more confident business decisions.  
- **Foundation for Scalable Data Maturity:** Establishing a robust, integrated data model within Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse will position PURE for future expansion of reporting capabilities and additional data sources as the business grows.
