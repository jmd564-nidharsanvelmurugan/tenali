# Business Impact

- **Accelerated Exit Readiness:** The delivery of a validated exit reporting suite, including equity narratives, prioritised KPI frameworks, dashboard mock-ups, and data model designs, will expedite VONQ’s ability to present investor-ready insights. This translates to a reduction in transaction preparation time and minimises the risk of exit delays, supporting completion within the 4-week Phase 1 timeline.

- **Operational Efficiency and Automation:** Automating data ingestion and recurring refresh pipelines from Finance, CRM, and ERP systems into the Qlik Warehouse will streamline reporting workflows for finance, commercial, and analytics teams. By replacing fragmented manual reporting with refreshable processes, VONQ can expect improved data accuracy and reduced manual effort during transaction prep and investor communications.

- **Enhanced Data-Driven Decision-Making:** A robust, investor-aligned KPI framework and dashboard suite will empower senior leadership and advisors to gain granular visibility into commercial, financial, and operational performance. This supports transparent equity narratives and enables informed strategic decisions throughout the sale process.

- **Mitigation of Transaction and Reputational Risk:** Addressing data gaps and developing remediation priorities will reduce the risk of presenting incomplete or non-credible equity narratives to acquirers. This strengthens stakeholder trust—including advisors (Barclays) and potential buyers—and decreases exposure to reputational risk.

- **Scalable Strategic Asset Creation:** Design outputs—including the conceptual data model and implementation roadmap—will become lasting business assets. The reporting infrastructure is purpose-built for post-transaction scalability, enabling future integration of additional data sources and continued automation, supporting VONQ’s long-term growth objectives.

By delivering a comprehensive and scalable exit reporting solution, this engagement positions VONQ for a successful M&A process, competitive differentiation, and sustained business growth. The initiative not only addresses immediate sale requirements—but also establishes a foundation for ongoing operational excellence and strategic value creation.
