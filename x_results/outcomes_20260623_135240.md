# Outcomes

The engagement with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC is designed to significantly elevate the firm’s ability to track, analyze, and optimize its lead-to-assets-under-management (L2A) pipeline and customer acquisition cost (CAC). By deploying advanced reporting and automation across integrated data sources, PURE will gain granular, actionable insights into pipeline performance and marketing effectiveness, empowering leadership to make data-driven decisions that drive both growth and efficiency.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Granular Pipeline Insights:** PURE will benefit from automated, detailed L2A reporting that reveals each stage of the lead journey, providing clarity on conversion rates and opportunities for targeted improvement.
- **Actionable Marketing ROI Analysis:** Leadership will gain robust CAC metrics, allowing for precise measurement of marketing spend effectiveness and facilitating higher return on investment decisions.
- **Enhanced Data Integration:** Seamless ingestion and consolidation of data from Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks will eliminate silos and enable comprehensive pipeline and CAC analytics.
- **Automated Reporting Efficiency:** Streamlined data ingestion, transformation, and refresh processes will reduce manual workload, minimize errors, and ensure timely availability of key business metrics.
- **Scalable Reporting Foundation:** The data model and reporting suite will be built to scale, supporting future data sources and reporting needs as PURE continues to grow and evolve.
- **Improved Decision-Making:** With accurate, automated, and insightful Power BI dashboards, senior leadership and marketing teams will have the tools needed to make faster, more informed business decisions.

These outcomes will be measured using Lead-to-AUM conversion metrics and Customer Acquisition Cost KPIs, with success defined by the delivery of accurate, insightful, and automated reporting solutions integrated into PURE’s Power BI environment, driving enhanced visibility and actionable business intelligence.
