# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

1. **KPI Book**
   - Comprehensive documentation of Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) KPIs.
   - Includes definitions, calculation logic, and data source mapping for all key metrics.
   - Delivered as a PDF and editable Word document.
   - Provided at the conclusion of the discovery phase (Phase 3A).

2. **Power BI Mock-ups**
   - Development of up to 8 detailed, interactive mock-up views representing future-state L2A and CAC dashboard designs.
   - Includes wireframes and visualization samples with a focus on user experience and actionable insights.
   - Supplied as a PowerPoint or PDF deliverable, with working prototypes as needed.
   - Delivered during the design phase and reviewed with business stakeholders.

3. **Data Model Design**
   - Detailed schema of the enhanced L2A and CAC data model, integrating with Salesforce and QuickBooks sources.
   - Data dictionary and entity-relationship diagrams illustrating core data relationships.
   - Provided as a PDF and Visio/ERD files.
   - Delivered mid-way through the design phase for technical review and sign-off.

4. **Data Gap Assessment**
   - Systematic identification of data quality issues, missing fields, and integration gaps impacting L2A and CAC reporting.
   - Includes a prioritized remediation action plan with recommendations.
   - Presented in Excel and summary report formats.
   - Delivered at the end of discovery and data analysis, prior to initiating detailed design work.

5. **Technical Approach**
   - Comprehensive technical playbook outlining data ingestion processes (including QuickBooks integration), transformation, and reporting pipeline automation.
   - Details on architecture, scalability considerations, and integration dependencies.
   - Supplied as a technical design document (Word/PDF).
   - Delivered alongside the data model design for joint technology review.

6. **Phase 3B Implementation Plan**
   - Sequenced roadmap with activities, ownership, and timelines for implementing the L2A and CAC reporting solution.
   - Clearly defined milestones for data integration, pipeline automation, dashboard development, and user acceptance testing.
   - Delivered as a Gantt chart (PDF and Excel), with supporting narrative.
   - Provided at the close of the design phase as a guide for the build phase.

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC's leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
