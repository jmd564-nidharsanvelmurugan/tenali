# Objectives

- Deliver a comprehensive Lead-to-AUM and Customer Acquisition Cost data model and reporting suite that provides granular insights into pipeline performance and marketing ROI.
- Automate data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes to ensure timely and accurate reporting.
- Enable leadership to gain a deeper understanding of the client journey from initial lead to assets under management, and optimize strategies based on detailed marketing effectiveness metrics.
- Enhance user experience by improving Power BI dashboards, including clear KPI definitions and intuitive visualizations.
- Improve pipeline reporting and customer acquisition cost tracking processes to support data-driven decision-making.
- Establish scalable reporting capabilities and data maturity to support future growth and the integration of additional data sources.
- Integrate seamlessly with Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Microsoft Fabric to ensure a unified and efficient data ecosystem.
