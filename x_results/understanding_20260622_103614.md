# Understanding

- Existing pipeline reporting lacks the necessary granularity to provide detailed insights into the full Lead-to-AUM (L2A) journey and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) metrics.
- Current reports do not offer integrated visibility into CAC, limiting the client’s ability to measure the effectiveness and ROI of marketing expenditures.
- Integration challenges exist, particularly with ingesting and connecting data from QuickBooks, which is required for accurate CAC reporting.
- There is a need to automate data ingestion and transformation pipelines for L2A and CAC reporting to reduce manual work and improve reporting efficiency.
- Required capabilities include building an enhanced L2A and CAC data model, expanding the Power BI reporting suite, ensuring successful data ingestion from QuickBooks, and establishing robust data quality assessment.
- Data and technology stakeholders, senior leadership, and marketing teams are the primary users of the enhanced reporting and analytics.
- Mandatory system integrations for this project include Salesforce and QuickBooks to ensure comprehensive data coverage for L2A and CAC metrics.
- Constraints affecting the project include data access limitations, variable data quality, and dependencies on timely QuickBooks integration.
- The timeline for the initiative is structured into Phase 3A (3 weeks) and Phase 3B (8–12 weeks), each with dependencies on data access and integration milestones.
