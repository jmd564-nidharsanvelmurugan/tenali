# Business Impact

- **Enhanced Financial Visibility:** The new Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data model provides PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC with granular insights into pipeline performance and marketing ROI, allowing leadership to pinpoint high-performing channels and optimize resource allocation.

- **Operational Efficiency via Automation:** Automating data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes across Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Azure platforms significantly reduces manual reporting effort, accelerating data availability and reliability for quarterly reviews and campaign evaluations.

- **Quantifiable Cost Savings:** Integrated CAC reporting enables the marketing team to accurately track acquisition costs and identify areas of spend reduction, supporting a decrease in customer acquisition costs and improved budgeting precision over time.

- **Strategic Growth Enablement:** By establishing scalable data and reporting infrastructure within Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse, PURE lays the foundation for continued growth, enabling easy expansion to additional data sources and future reporting needs as the business scales.

- **Competitive Advantage through Data-Driven Decision Making:** Leveraging Power BI dashboards with clear KPI definitions empowers leadership and stakeholders to act on actionable intelligence, differentiating PURE in the financial advisory market through superior marketing and pipeline transparency.

The proposed solution delivers immediate and ongoing value by transforming PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC’s approach to pipeline and marketing analytics. Enhanced visibility and automation will drive operational efficiency, financial optimization, and strategic growth, positioning PURE to make more informed decisions and maximize return on investment as it continues to expand its advisory and asset management offerings in the United States.
