# Understanding

- The client lacks an investor-ready reporting suite to support the M&A sale process and effective stakeholder communications.
- There are potential data gaps across source systems, which could undermine the completeness and credibility of the equity narrative required for exit reporting.
- The existing Qlik Warehouse platform may not fully support all required KPIs vital for presenting the business's equity story to advisors and potential acquirers.
- Mandatory integrations include the Finance System, CRM, ERP, and legacy sources to provide a minimum of three years of trended data.
- Data ingestion and refresh pipelines require automation, particularly for recurring updates needed during the transaction process.
- Required capabilities include equity narrative definition, a prioritised KPI framework, dashboard mock-ups, data feasibility assessment, conceptual data model design, tooling recommendations, and a phased implementation roadmap.
- Senior leadership, exit advisors (Barclays), and potential acquirers/investors are primary user roles that must be supported by the reporting suite.
- Constraints impacting the solution include readiness and completeness of existing data, stakeholder availability, and alignment with the exit process timeline.
