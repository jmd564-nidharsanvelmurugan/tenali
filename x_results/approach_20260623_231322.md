# Approach

PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC has engaged our team to deliver a comprehensive and scalable solution for Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting, leveraging their existing data and analytics platforms. Our methodology combines deep business engagement, iterative solution design, and best-practice implementation to unlock actionable insights into pipeline performance and marketing ROI. The approach prioritizes robust data integration, automated batch processes within Microsoft Fabric, and clear KPI visualization through Power BI.

The engagement will follow a two-phased structure, tightly aligned with PURE’s strategic objectives, current-state technology stack, and required deliverables. Each phase is designed to incrementally deliver tangible results, starting with discovery and solution design, followed by technical implementation and integrated reporting—ensuring business users receive immediate value and future scalability.

**Phase 3A: Discovery & Solution Design, Duration: 3 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 to Week 3**  
**Summary:** Establish the foundation for L2A and CAC reporting through requirements gathering, data gap assessment, and solution blueprinting.  
**Activities:**  
- Conduct stakeholder workshops with data, technology, and marketing teams to define detailed requirements, KPIs, and key report use cases  
- Assess current data architecture, including Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and plan for QuickBooks integration  
- Develop a gap analysis of source data and recommend data quality and ingestion strategies for CAC reporting  
- Design the L2A and CAC data model, including Power BI mock-ups (up to 8 views) reflecting desired dashboards and visualizations  
- Document technical integration approach, data flow, and refresh scheduling within Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse  
- Prepare Phase 3B Implementation Plan, laying out resource needs, dependencies, and timeline  

**Phase 3B: Data Integration, Automation & Reporting, Duration: 8–12 weeks, Timeline: Week 4 to Week 15**  
**Summary:** Implement the automated L2A and CAC data pipelines, integrate QuickBooks, and deliver the enhanced Power BI reporting suite.  
**Activities:**  
- Configure and test automated data ingestion from Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks into the Microsoft Fabric platform  
- Build and validate data transformation pipelines to support the L2A and CAC data models  
- Execute data quality assessment and remediation steps to ensure trust in reporting outputs  
- Develop and publish an 8-page Power BI report suite with clear visualization of conversion metrics, CAC KPIs, and actionable drilldowns  
- Align dashboard outputs to KPI Book specifications and facilitate user walkthroughs with business stakeholders  
- Transition solution documentation, training materials, and governance recommendations for sustainable operation  

This phased approach ensures PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS receives incremental value through rapid discovery, clear deliverables, and a tightly integrated technical implementation. By structuring the project for early alignment and iterative delivery, PURE’s leadership and teams will gain enhanced data maturity, more granular insights into marketing efficiency, and a future-proof foundation for scalable reporting.
