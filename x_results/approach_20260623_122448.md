# Approach

This engagement with VONQ is focused on designing a scalable and investor-grade exit reporting suite in preparation for a near-term sale process. The solution will enable VONQ’s leadership and advisors to communicate a credible equity narrative underpinned by robust, refreshable metrics and dashboards. Our methodology blends proven best practices in SaaS reporting design, rigorous stakeholder engagement, and a practical understanding of VONQ’s current Qlik Warehouse data environment.

We will proceed using a structured and collaborative approach, organized into a single Discovery & Design phase of four weeks. The phase incorporates both business and technical discovery, requirements validation, and iterative design sprints. Each key deliverable— from equity narrative definition to dashboard mock-ups and a conceptual data model—will be developed in close partnership with stakeholders to ensure alignment with exit goals and investor expectations. Outputs will position VONQ for accelerated delivery and stakeholder sign-off in the subsequent implementation phase.

---

**Phase 1: Discovery & Design, Duration: 4 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 (29 June 2026) to Week 4 (24 July 2026)**  
**Summary:** This phase will deliver all foundational design artifacts: equity narrative definitions, KPI framework, dashboard mock-ups, data feasibility findings, a conceptual data model, tooling recommendations, and a detailed implementation roadmap for Phase 2.

**Activities:**
- Initiate project with stakeholder kickoff, confirming roles, key outputs, and critical path.
- Conduct workshops to define investor-ready equity narratives and align on "Metrics That Matter" for exit reporting.
- Assess current-state data sources (Finance System, CRM, ERP, legacy datasets) and Qlik Warehouse readiness; perform a structured data gap analysis.
- Develop static dashboard mock-ups and build a prioritised KPI framework, including calculation logic and source mapping.
- Design a conceptual data model leveraging Qlik Warehouse, with recommendations for automated batch data ingestion and alternative tooling options.
- Prepare a comprehensive implementation roadmap identifying Phase 2 scope, critical dependencies, timelines, and key risks.

---

This phased approach enables VONQ to achieve clarity, alignment, and momentum in its exit reporting strategy. By the end of Phase 1, VONQ will possess a validated reporting suite design and actionable roadmap, directly supporting a credible and defensible equity narrative for investors and buyers. These design artifacts will ensure rapid, low-risk progression into technical build while maximizing reusability, scalability, and long-term business value.
