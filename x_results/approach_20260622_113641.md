# Approach

This engagement with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC is structured around a proven, collaborative methodology designed to deliver enhanced Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting. The approach combines stakeholder alignment, rigorous business analysis, technical solution design, and iterative development to ensure actionable insights for pipeline performance and marketing ROI. By integrating data across Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks into a scalable Microsoft Fabric and Power BI environment, we will empower data-driven decision making.

Our methodology is phased to address dependencies and business priorities. Phase 3A will focus on engaging key business and technology stakeholders, defining clear KPIs, and producing the foundational designs for data models and reporting. Phase 3B will execute the end-to-end solution build, including data integration, automation, and delivery of Power BI dashboards, while ensuring data quality and technical robustness. All activities are aligned with the project's expected outcomes and deliverables.

---

**Phase 3A: Discovery and Design, Duration: 3 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 to Week 3**  
**Summary:** Define business requirements, KPIs, technical approach, and design prototypes to guide the solution build.

**Activities:**  
- Conduct stakeholder workshops to refine Lead-to-AUM and CAC reporting requirements and business context.  
- Develop a comprehensive KPI Book detailing L2A and CAC metrics, definitions, and calculation logic.  
- Design the conceptual and logical data model, visualizing relationships across Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks.  
- Create Power BI reporting mock-ups (up to 8 views) to validate reporting requirements and user experience expectations.  
- Assess data gaps and integration points, particularly for QuickBooks ingestion and data quality.  
- Prepare a detailed Technical Approach and Phase 3B Implementation Plan, mapping out tasks, dependencies, and resource needs.

---

**Phase 3B: Solution Build and Implementation, Duration: 8–12 weeks, Timeline: Week 4 to Week 15**  
**Summary:** Build, test, and deliver the end-to-end L2A and CAC data and reporting solution leveraging Azure and Power BI platforms.

**Activities:**  
- Ingest and integrate data from QuickBooks and Salesforce into Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse, ensuring automated batch processing pipelines.  
- Implement the approved L2A and CAC data model within the Fabric platform, including transformation and storage layers.  
- Develop and validate Power BI dashboards based on Phase 3A mock-ups, incorporating advanced visualizations and KPI calculations.  
- Conduct data quality assessments and apply cleansing routines to support accurate and reliable reporting.  
- Automate data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes to ensure seamless user access to current insights.  
- Provide solution documentation, user training, and knowledge transfer sessions for data, technology, and marketing teams.

---

This phased approach enables PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC to achieve rapid alignment, clear requirements definition, and controlled delivery of a scalable L2A and CAC reporting ecosystem. By structuring the methodology around stakeholder priorities and technical dependencies, we ensure sustained project momentum, early risk mitigation, and tangible business value at every stage.
