# Approach

Pure Financial LLC has engaged our team to develop a scalable data platform supporting centralized management of revenue, pipeline, and operational reporting information. This engagement falls under Data Advisory and Value Creation, with a Design and Discovery project type specifically targeted at enabling actionable business insights for leadership and automating key processes. As Pure Financial LLC operates in the UK and does not possess existing data infrastructure, our methodology is structured around a phased, collaborative approach designed to maximize value creation while ensuring alignment with the client’s operational goals.

Our methodology prioritizes stakeholder engagement, iterative validation, and technical rigor. We utilize industry best practices to design a data platform fit for B2C financial services—addressing the commercial needs related to revenue bridge, pipeline, and operational reporting. Through a focused sequence of discovery, design, and advisory phases, we aim to deliver tangible artifacts, including a Data Platform Design Blueprint and executive recommendations, ensuring the client benefits from both technical and business perspectives.

---

**Phase 1: Discovery & Requirements, Duration: 2 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 to Week 2**  
**Summary:** Establish business, data, and reporting requirements tailored to Pure Financial LLC's commercial use cases.

**Activities:**
- Conduct stakeholder workshops and interviews to capture detailed requirements
- Map current business workflows related to revenue, pipeline, and reporting processes
- Assess data sources, gaps, and existing reporting mechanisms
- Document technical and business constraints, focusing on B2C needs
- Develop a requirements matrix prioritizing features and outcomes

---

**Phase 2: Solution Design & Blueprint, Duration: 3 weeks, Timeline: Week 3 to Week 5**  
**Summary:** Design the data platform architecture and develop the comprehensive blueprint aligned with strategic objectives.

**Activities:**
- Architect the data platform with focus on scalability, integration, and automation
- Draft visual diagrams and documentation of proposed data flows and core components
- Define integration points with potential third-party systems relevant to UK financial services
- Evaluate platform technologies suitable for Pure Financial LLC’s B2C model
- Produce the Data Platform Design Blueprint and review with stakeholders

---

**Phase 3: Value Creation Advisory, Duration: 2 weeks, Timeline: Week 6 to Week 7**  
**Summary:** Advise on implementation priorities and operational change, ensuring organizational readiness for platform adoption.

**Activities:**
- Provide executive recommendations for data platform roll-out and value realization
- Develop a roadmap for future enhancements based on operational reporting and pipeline insights
- Outline process automation opportunities to drive efficiency and accuracy
- Conduct risk assessment and mitigation planning for platform launch
- Facilitate knowledge transfer sessions to prepare client teams

---

This phased approach ensures Pure Financial LLC receives a comprehensive and actionable plan for establishing their data platform, tailored to the unique needs of the UK B2C financial services market. Each phase builds upon the previous, delivering high-impact deliverables and expert guidance that supports strategic decision-making, operational advancement, and sustainable business growth.
