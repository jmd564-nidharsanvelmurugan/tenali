# Understanding

- Existing pipeline reporting lacks sufficient granularity, limiting visibility into the journey from lead to assets under management (AUM) and the effectiveness of marketing investments.
- There is a current absence of integrated Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) insights, restricting the firm's ability to measure return on marketing spend accurately.
- Data required for CAC reporting needs to be integrated from QuickBooks, with challenges around timely and accurate data ingestion.
- Data quality assessment is needed to ensure reliable CAC reporting and decision-making.
- Enhanced Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and CAC data models are required to support detailed reporting and analytics.
- Automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines are necessary to streamline L2A and CAC reporting processes.
- Core user personas include data and technology stakeholders, senior leadership, and marketing teams, all of whom require tailored access to reporting and analytics tools.
- Mandatory integrations must be established with Salesforce and QuickBooks to enable comprehensive data capture across the reporting pipeline.
