# Understanding

- PURE seeks to address limited visibility and granularity in its existing pipeline reporting, particularly around the Lead-to-AUM (L2A) journey and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) metrics.  
- Current reporting lacks integrated CAC insights, preventing a clear understanding of marketing efficiency and return on spend.  
- Data from key systems such as QuickBooks is not yet connected, creating gaps in financial data needed for accurate CAC analysis.  
- The absence of automated data ingestion and transformation processes results in manual effort and delays in producing consistent L2A and CAC reports.  
- Enhanced data modeling and reporting capabilities are required to unify lead, asset, and cost data across Salesforce, Tamarac, and Azure platforms.  
- Automated workflows are needed to streamline data ingestion, transformation, and refresh cycles for L2A and CAC reporting.  
- Primary users include data and technology stakeholders, senior leadership, and marketing teams who rely on accurate, timely insights for decision-making.  
- Mandatory integrations include Salesforce and QuickBooks to ensure complete and reliable data for pipeline and cost reporting.
