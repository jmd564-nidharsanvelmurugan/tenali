# Overview  

The client currently operates without an established data infrastructure, relying on dispersed and manually maintained information sources to support its professional services and due diligence activities. Data is collected and stored across various operational systems and spreadsheets, with no unified platform or automated integration in place. Reporting outputs are generated through manual compilation, limiting consistency and accessibility across teams.  

As a B2C-focused private equity portfolio company, the organization’s data processes are primarily transactional and pipeline‑driven, supporting deal evaluation and diligence workflows. There is no existing cloud or data platform environment, and data management practices remain largely ad hoc. Teams depend on individual tools and local files to track performance and client activity, with limited connectivity between these sources. This current state provides the foundation for the upcoming design and discovery effort to define a structured data platform and reporting model.
