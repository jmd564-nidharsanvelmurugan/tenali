# Understanding

- PURE seeks to address limited visibility into the full Lead-to-AUM (L2A) journey and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) performance across its marketing and sales funnel.  
- Existing pipeline reporting lacks the necessary granularity to evaluate conversion effectiveness and marketing ROI.  
- Current reporting does not integrate CAC insights, limiting leadership’s ability to assess acquisition efficiency.  
- Data ingestion from QuickBooks is not yet established, creating a gap in financial data required for CAC analysis.  
- Automated data ingestion and transformation workflows are needed to reduce manual effort and ensure consistent reporting refreshes.  
- The enhanced reporting environment must support data and technology stakeholders, senior leadership, and marketing teams as primary users.  
- Mandatory integrations include Salesforce and QuickBooks to enable unified L2A and CAC reporting.
