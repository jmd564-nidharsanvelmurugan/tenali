# Approach

PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC seeks to elevate its Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) analytics, automating data pipelines and delivering actionable reporting within Power BI. Our engagement will address these objectives through a structured, collaborative process designed to maximize cross-team input and align technical solutions with business value. The methodology combines rapid discovery and design cycles with targeted technical integration and iterative validation to ensure business stakeholders receive granular insights on pipeline performance and marketing ROI.

The engagement uses Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse as the core data platforms, integrating Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks data sources. Delivery is divided into two distinct phases: a focused discovery and design cycle followed by an agile build and deployment phase. The approach ensures quick wins for stakeholder alignment in Phase 3A (3 weeks) and robust feature delivery in Phase 3B (8–12 weeks), each anchored by clear deliverables and milestone-driven outputs.

---

**Phase 3A: Discovery & Design, Duration: 3 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 to Week 3**

**Summary:** This phase lays the foundation by clarifying requirements, designing the end-to-end L2A and CAC data model, and preparing mock-ups for stakeholder review.

**Activities:**
- Conduct workshops with data, marketing, and technology teams to confirm business requirements and success metrics.
- Assess existing Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks datasets for completeness and suitability.
- Design the overall L2A and CAC data model, including KPI logic, mapping, and technical documentation.
- Develop Power BI reporting mock-ups (up to 8 views) showcasing intended dashboard layouts, visualizations, and KPI definitions.
- Prepare a detailed Data Gap Assessment and recommend technical approaches for QuickBooks data ingestion.
- Document the phase deliverables: KPI Book, Data Model, Power BI mock-ups, Data Gap Assessment, and Technical Approach.

---

**Phase 3B: Build & Implementation, Duration: 8–12 weeks, Timeline: Week 4 to Week 15**

**Summary:** This phase delivers the automated pipelines, integrated reporting suite, and scalable data platform capabilities for L2A and CAC analytics.

**Activities:**
- Implement automated batch data ingestion pipelines leveraging Microsoft Fabric for Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks.
- Conduct comprehensive data quality assessments to validate source data and monitor ingestion accuracy.
- Build, integrate, and refine the L2A and CAC data model within Azure Synapse and Fabric environments.
- Develop, iterate, and deploy the 8-page Power BI reporting suite for L2A and CAC insights, aligning with mock-up views from Phase 3A.
- Secure UAT signoff from stakeholders, including data, marketing, and senior leadership teams, and manage feedback for final adjustments.
- Produce the Phase 3B Implementation Plan and ensure all solution documentation and technical artifacts are delivered.

---

The phased approach enables PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC to achieve rapid alignment on requirements and design, while ensuring scalable, automated, and insight-driven reporting solutions are built and deployed on time. By connecting disparate data sources and integrating advanced reporting capabilities, this methodology establishes the technical foundation for continued data maturity, delivers value to business stakeholders, and supports growth with actionable analytics tailored to the firm's strategic objectives.
