# Understanding

- Current reporting does not clearly link marketing activities to resulting leads, conversions, or asset growth, limiting visibility into performance drivers.  
- There is no unified data model connecting Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks, creating fragmented and incomplete reporting for Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC).  
- Cost data from QuickBooks is not yet integrated, preventing accurate calculation of CAC and full understanding of marketing spend effectiveness.  
- Manual data preparation and report updates create inefficiencies, increase the risk of errors, and delay access to current insights.  
- Data quality and consistency issues across systems reduce confidence in existing reports and hinder decision-making.  
- Teams across data, technology, marketing, and leadership require consistent, automated, and detailed reporting to support performance tracking and strategic planning.  
- Integrations with Salesforce and QuickBooks are essential to achieve complete visibility across the customer acquisition and asset management lifecycle.  
- Reports must be accurate, timely, and accessible to multiple user groups, supporting governance and compliance expectations within a financial services environment.
