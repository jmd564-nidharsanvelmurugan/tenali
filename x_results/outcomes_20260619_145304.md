# Outcomes

This engagement will deliver measurable business impact and strategic value by enabling **Pure Financial Advisors LLC** to achieve deeper visibility into its Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) performance. The initiative aligns with Pure’s B2C financial advisory model and supports both internal leadership and private equity stakeholders in making data‑driven decisions that optimize marketing ROI and pipeline efficiency.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Granular Lead‑to‑AUM Visibility:** Development of a unified L2A data model will provide detailed insights into each stage of the client acquisition journey, allowing leadership to identify conversion bottlenecks and optimize lead management strategies.  
- **Accurate Customer Acquisition Cost Measurement:** Integration of QuickBooks and Salesforce data will enable precise CAC calculations, improving understanding of marketing spend effectiveness and guiding future investment decisions.  
- **Automated and Reliable Reporting:** Implementation of automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines will ensure consistent, timely, and error‑free reporting across Power BI dashboards, reducing manual effort and improving data trust.  
- **Enhanced Marketing ROI Analysis:** The new reporting suite will allow Pure to directly link marketing activities to asset growth outcomes, enabling evidence‑based decisions that maximize return on marketing investments.  
- **Improved Leadership Decision‑Making:** With clear KPI definitions and visualizations, senior management will gain actionable insights into pipeline performance and customer acquisition trends, supporting strategic planning and growth initiatives.  
- **Foundation for Scalable Data Maturity:** Establishing a robust, extensible data model within Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse will position Pure for future expansion of analytics capabilities and integration of additional data sources.
