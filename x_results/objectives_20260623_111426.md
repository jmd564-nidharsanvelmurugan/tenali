# Objectives

- Achieve a fully designed exit reporting suite with clear equity narratives, a prioritised KPI framework, dashboard mock-ups, a scalable conceptual data model, and actionable tooling recommendations to support M&A and investor communication goals.
- Automate data ingestion and refresh pipelines from the Finance System, CRM, and ERP into the central data platform, enabling recurring and reliable data updates during the transaction process.
- Provide senior leadership and stakeholders with investor-grade visibility into commercial, financial, and operational metrics necessary to underpin the equity narrative and accelerate the sale process.
- Deliver intuitive dashboards that improve user experience through clear KPI definitions, standardized calculation logic, and an easy-to-navigate structure, all aligned to defined equity narratives.
- Standardize and enhance exit reporting processes, establish a prioritized KPI framework, and improve data infrastructure readiness to ensure credibility and data integrity for prospective buyers and advisors.
- Ensure the solution is designed for scalability, with a data model and reporting suite extensible to new data sources and automatable beyond the immediate transaction, creating lasting business assets for VONQ’s future needs.
- Enable seamless integration with all core source systems—Finance System, CRM, ERP, and Qlik Warehouse—with flexibility to assess additional tooling options for future optimization.
