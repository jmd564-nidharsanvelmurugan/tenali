# Approach

We propose a structured, phased delivery methodology rooted in clear, automated data processes and best-practice guidance for Power BI reporting. This approach ensures the design and build of enhanced Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting capabilities that are scalable and integrated across the client's existing technology landscape.

Our methodology emphasizes collaboration across data, technology, and marketing stakeholders, leveraging batch‑oriented transformation pipelines within Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse. The focus is on delivering a robust data model design, KPI definitions, and seamless integration with QuickBooks and Salesforce to enable automated, insightful reporting for leadership.

**Phase 3A: Discovery & Design, Duration: 3 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 to Week 3**  
**Summary:** Establish foundational understanding and design of the L2A and CAC data model, Power BI mock‑ups, and technical approach.  
**Activities:**

- Inventory data sources (Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, QuickBooks) and assess integration scopes, including data quality requirements for CAC calculations.  
- Design the data model for L2A and CAC reporting, including schema definitions and transformation logic.  
- Develop the KPI Book detailing metric logic, definitions, and data source mappings.  
- Create Power BI mock‑ups (up to eight views) illustrating envisioned dashboards and visualizations.

**Phase 3B: Development & Build‑Out, Duration: 8–12 weeks, Timeline: Week 4 to Week 11–15**  
**Summary:** Build and implement automated pipelines, data model, and reporting suite, and prepare the implementation roadmap.  
**Activities:**  
- Develop batch data ingestion and transformation pipelines in Microsoft Fabric / Azure Synapse, including integration with QuickBooks and Salesforce.  
- Validate data quality and refine transformation logic for accurate L2A and CAC calculations.  
- Implement the designed data model in Fabric/Synapse and enable automated refresh mechanisms.  
- Build Power BI dashboards based on approved mock‑ups, integrating KPI logic and visuals for L2A and CAC insights.  
- Prepare the Phase 3B Implementation Plan, outlining rollout approach, dependencies, timeline milestones, and hand‑off to operations teams.

In progressing through these phases, deliverables—including the KPI Book, Power BI mock‑ups, Data Model design, Data Gap Assessment, Technical Approach, and Phase 3B Implementation Plan—will be produced incrementally, aligned with each phase’s activities and milestones.

By following this phased methodology, Pure Financial Advisors will achieve accurate, automated, and insightful L2A and CAC reporting within a scalable data platform.
