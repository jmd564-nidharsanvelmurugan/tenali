# Approach

The proposed approach for **Pure Financial Advisors LLC** is designed to address the firm’s specific need for enhanced **Lead-to-AUM (L2A)** and **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)** reporting within the financial advisory and wealth management context. As a B2C business operating across the United States, Pure’s success depends on its ability to understand the full client acquisition journey and optimize marketing ROI. This engagement leverages the existing Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse infrastructure to design and implement a scalable, automated data model and Power BI reporting suite that delivers actionable insights to leadership and marketing teams.

Our methodology follows a structured, phased approach that ensures alignment with Pure’s business objectives, technical environment, and stakeholder expectations. Each phase builds upon the previous one—starting with data access and discovery, progressing through model design and mock-up development, and culminating in a detailed implementation plan. This structure ensures that the final solution is both technically robust and strategically aligned with Pure’s growth and data maturity goals.

---

**Phase 1: Discovery & Data Access Enablement, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 0 to Week 1**

**Summary:** Establish foundational access, documentation, and alignment to enable a seamless start to the engagement.  

**Activities:**  
- Coordinate with Pure’s data and technology teams to secure access to Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Fabric, and Power BI environments.  
- Review existing documentation on data architecture, reporting, and infrastructure.  
- Schedule and confirm workshops with key stakeholders, including Jason Carver and Lee Equity Partners representatives.  
- Validate data availability and confirm dependencies for QuickBooks integration.  
- Finalize project governance, cadence, and communication plan.  

---

**Phase 2: Current State Assessment & KPI Definition, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 1 to Week 2**

**Summary:** Gain a comprehensive understanding of current data sources, reporting processes, and KPI definitions to inform the target-state design.  

**Activities:**  
- Conduct workshops to align on business model, value drivers, and key L2A and CAC metrics.  
- Review existing Power BI reports and data flows to identify current limitations and data quality issues.  
- Map data sources across Salesforce, Tamarac, and Azure Blob Storage to existing KPIs.  
- Document current-state data lineage and transformation logic.  
- Identify data gaps and dependencies impacting CAC and pipeline reporting.  

---

**Phase 3: Data Source Assessment & Target Model Design, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 2 to Week 3**

**Summary:** Design the target-state data model and define the logic required to support L2A and CAC reporting.  

**Activities:**  
- Assess data quality and completeness across Salesforce, Tamarac, and QuickBooks.  
- Define the data model structure, including entity relationships and transformation logic.  
- Develop KPI calculation logic for L2A conversion and CAC metrics.  
- Conduct a data gap assessment and recommend remediation actions.  
- Validate model design with Pure’s data and marketing teams to ensure business alignment.  

---

**Phase 4: Power BI Mock-ups & KPI Book Development, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 3 to Week 4**

**Summary:** Translate the agreed data model and KPIs into visual mock-ups and documentation to guide future build activities.  

**Activities:**  
- Design up to eight Power BI mock-ups illustrating L2A and CAC reporting views.  
- Develop the KPI Book detailing metric definitions, formulas, and data source mappings.  
- Review mock-ups with stakeholders to ensure clarity, usability, and alignment with business needs.  
- Incorporate feedback and finalize visual and data model documentation.  
- Prepare supporting materials for the technical approach and implementation planning.  

---

**Phase 5: Technical Approach & Phase 3B Implementation Plan, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 4 to Week 5**

**Summary:** Define the technical approach and roadmap for the build phase, ensuring readiness for implementation.  

**Activities:**  
- Document the end-to-end technical approach for QuickBooks integration and automated data pipelines.  
- Define batch processing workflows within Microsoft Fabric for data ingestion and transformation.  
- Outline data governance and quality assurance processes for ongoing reporting accuracy.  
- Develop the Phase 3B Implementation Plan, including timeline, resource requirements, and dependencies.  
- Present final deliverables and recommendations to Pure’s leadership for approval.  

---

This structured, phased approach ensures that **Pure Financial Advisors LLC**’s L2A and CAC reporting capabilities are developed with precision, agility, and a clear focus on delivering measurable business outcomes. By combining robust data modeling, stakeholder alignment, and actionable reporting design, the engagement positions Pure to achieve scalable, automated insights that drive informed decision-making and sustained growth.
