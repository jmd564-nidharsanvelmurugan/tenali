# Approach

PURE Financial Advisors LLC operates in the financial advisory and wealth management space, serving a B2C client base across the United States. This engagement is designed to address PURE’s specific challenge of improving visibility into its Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) pipeline and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) performance. By leveraging existing infrastructure—Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Fabric, Synapse, and Power BI—the approach focuses on designing and implementing a scalable, automated data model and reporting suite that delivers granular insights into marketing ROI and pipeline efficiency.

The methodology follows a structured, phased progression to ensure alignment with PURE’s business objectives and technical environment. Each phase builds upon the previous one, moving from discovery and design through to implementation planning. This ensures that data access, model design, and reporting outputs are developed collaboratively with stakeholders, validated through workshops, and delivered with precision and clarity.

---

**Phase 3A: Discovery & Design, Duration: 3 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 to Week 3**

**Summary:** Establish the foundation for the L2A and CAC reporting solution through data discovery, KPI definition, and target‑state model design.

**Activities:**  
- Conduct kickoff and stakeholder alignment sessions to confirm scope, dependencies, and access requirements.  
- Review existing data sources across Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks to assess data quality and availability.  
- Facilitate KPI workshops to define L2A and CAC metrics, calculation logic, and reporting granularity.  
- Develop initial Power BI mock‑ups (up to 8 views) to visualize priority reporting use cases.  
- Perform a Data Gap Assessment to identify missing or incomplete data required for CAC and pipeline reporting.  
- Document the target‑state data model and technical approach for QuickBooks integration.

---

**Phase 3B: Build & Implementation Planning, Duration: 8–12 weeks, Timeline: Week 4 to Week 15**

**Summary:** Translate the design outputs into a detailed implementation plan and technical blueprint for automated data ingestion, transformation, and reporting.

**Activities:**  
- Finalize the L2A and CAC data model, ensuring alignment with business logic and system architecture.  
- Design automated batch pipelines within Microsoft Fabric for data ingestion and transformation.  
- Define Power BI dataset structures and refresh automation processes.  
- Validate data mapping and reconciliation between Salesforce, QuickBooks, and Tamarac sources.  
- Prepare the Phase 3B Implementation Plan detailing build activities, sequencing, and resource requirements.  
- Deliver the KPI Book, finalized mock‑ups, and technical documentation for stakeholder review and approval.

---

This structured, phased approach ensures that PURE Financial Advisors LLC’s data platform and reporting capabilities are developed with precision, agility, and a clear focus on delivering measurable business outcomes aligned with stakeholder expectations.
