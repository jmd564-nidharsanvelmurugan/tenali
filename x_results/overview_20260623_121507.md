# Overview

VONQ’s current operations involve several functional teams—Finance, commercial/CRM, data/analytics, and senior leadership—actively engaged in exit preparation and investor reporting activities. These teams rely on multiple core systems, including a Finance System, CRM, ERP, and the Qlik Warehouse, which serves as the primary platform for data storage and reporting.

Reporting and analytics workflows are presently supported by Qlik-based tools and the Qlik Warehouse, with most business-critical information sourced from the Finance System, CRM, and ERP. Additional Excel-based datasets and legacy sources may also be referenced, particularly where trended data coverage of at least three years is required. Integration between these systems presents challenges; not all source systems are connected automatically to the Qlik Warehouse, necessitating manual, one-off ingestion for certain data. Where ongoing updates to source data are needed during the transaction, a refresh process has been established to maintain data currency. The Qlik Warehouse provides the underlying cloud platform infrastructure for current reporting and analytics capabilities.
