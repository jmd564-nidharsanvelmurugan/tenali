# Approach

PURE Financial Advisors LLC operates in a highly competitive financial advisory and wealth management market, where data-driven insights into client acquisition and asset growth are critical to sustaining performance and optimizing marketing ROI. This engagement is designed to address PURE’s specific challenge of limited visibility into the Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) pipeline and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) metrics by leveraging its existing Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse infrastructure. The approach builds on PURE’s current Salesforce, Tamarac, and Azure data ecosystem, introducing automated data ingestion from QuickBooks and a unified data model to deliver accurate, actionable reporting through Power BI.

The engagement follows a structured, phased methodology that ensures alignment with PURE’s business priorities, technical environment, and stakeholder expectations. Each phase builds upon the previous one—from discovery and data assessment through model design and implementation planning—ensuring that the final solution is both technically robust and strategically aligned with PURE’s growth objectives. This phased approach also allows for iterative validation with key stakeholders, ensuring that deliverables such as the KPI Book, Power BI mock‑ups, and data model design are refined and approved before moving into implementation.

---

**Phase 1: Discovery and Requirements Definition, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 0 to Week 1**

**Summary:** Establish foundational understanding of PURE’s current data landscape, reporting needs, and system dependencies to enable a focused and efficient design phase.

**Activities:**  
- Conduct kickoff and stakeholder alignment sessions with Data, Technology, and Marketing teams.  
- Review existing documentation for Microsoft Fabric, Azure Synapse, Salesforce, and Tamarac environments.  
- Confirm access to all relevant data sources and reporting assets.  
- Define project governance, meeting cadence, and communication protocols.  
- Validate scope boundaries, dependencies, and success criteria for the engagement.  

---

**Phase 2: Current State Assessment and Data Source Analysis, Duration: 2 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 to Week 3**

**Summary:** Assess existing data sources, data quality, and reporting processes to identify integration gaps and inform the target‑state data model design.

**Activities:**  
- Perform deep‑dive analysis of Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks data structures.  
- Evaluate data quality, completeness, and consistency across key L2A and CAC data points.  
- Identify integration challenges, particularly for QuickBooks ingestion.  
- Map current KPI logic and reporting flows to existing Power BI dashboards.  
- Document data gaps and dependencies to inform the Data Gap Assessment deliverable.  

---

**Phase 3: Data Model and KPI Design, Duration: 2 weeks, Timeline: Week 3 to Week 5**

**Summary:** Design the target‑state L2A and CAC data model and define KPI logic to support accurate, scalable reporting.

**Activities:**  
- Develop conceptual and logical data model designs aligned with PURE’s reporting objectives.  
- Define KPI formulas, data transformations, and source mappings for L2A and CAC metrics.  
- Validate data model design through iterative workshops with business and technical stakeholders.  
- Align on data governance and naming conventions to ensure consistency across reports.  
- Prepare the KPI Book documenting all metric definitions and data lineage.  

---

**Phase 4: Power BI Mock‑ups and Reporting Design, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 5 to Week 6**

**Summary:** Translate the data model and KPI definitions into visual reporting concepts that demonstrate the end‑state user experience.

**Activities:**  
- Design up to eight Power BI mock‑ups focused on L2A and CAC reporting views.  
- Incorporate stakeholder feedback to refine dashboard layout, navigation, and visual hierarchy.  
- Ensure alignment between mock‑ups and KPI Book definitions.  
- Validate that reporting views address leadership’s need for granular marketing ROI insights.  
- Finalize mock‑ups for inclusion in the discovery deliverables package.  

---

**Phase 5: Technical Approach and Implementation Planning, Duration: 2 weeks, Timeline: Week 6 to Week 8**

**Summary:** Define the technical approach for QuickBooks integration, data pipeline automation, and roadmap for Phase 3B implementation.

**Activities:**  
- Develop detailed technical approach for batch data ingestion and transformation within Microsoft Fabric.  
- Define architecture for integrating QuickBooks with existing Azure Synapse and Power BI environments.  
- Outline automation strategy for data refresh and reporting updates.  
- Prepare the Phase 3B Implementation Plan, including sequencing, resourcing, and estimated timelines.  
- Consolidate all discovery deliverables—KPI Book, Data Model Design, Power BI Mock‑ups, and Technical Approach—into a final presentation for stakeholder approval.  

---

This structured, phased approach ensures that PURE Financial Advisors LLC’s enhanced reporting capabilities are developed with precision, agility, and a clear focus on delivering measurable business outcomes. By grounding each phase in stakeholder validation and technical rigor, the engagement will provide PURE with a scalable, automated, and insight‑driven foundation for future growth.
