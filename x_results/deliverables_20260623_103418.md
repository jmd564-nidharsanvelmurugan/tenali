# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

1. **KPI Book**
   - Catalogs all relevant Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) metrics and definitions.
   - Provides business logic, formulas, and data lineage for each KPI.
   - Outlines measurement methodologies and reporting standards.
   - Delivered in a digital document (PDF or Word) during the discovery phase.

2. **Power BI Mock-ups**
   - Presents wireframes and initial dashboard designs limited to 8 views for L2A and CAC reporting.
   - Demonstrates proposed metric visualizations, layouts, and user interactions.
   - Includes stakeholder feedback incorporated from review sessions.
   - Delivered as Power BI files and annotated screenshots during the design phase.

3. **Data Model Design**
   - Documents the conceptual, logical, and physical data models for L2A and CAC reporting.
   - Specifies data sources, relationships, and integration points (including QuickBooks).
   - Covers data mapping, transformation logic, and storage schema.
   - Delivered as data model diagrams and supporting documentation during the design phase.

4. **Data Gap Assessment**
   - Identifies gaps, constraints, and data quality issues impacting L2A and CAC analytics.
   - Recommends remediation steps and priorities to address identified gaps.
   - Summarizes data profiling results across Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Azure platforms.
   - Delivered as a structured assessment report in the discovery phase.

5. **Technical Approach**
   - Defines the end-to-end architecture for data ingestion, transformation, and reporting pipelines.
   - Details the integration strategy for QuickBooks and other mandatory sources.
   - Describes automation, security, and scalability considerations within the current infrastructure.
   - Delivered as a technical design document prior to implementation.

6. **Phase 3B Implementation Plan**
   - Outlines a detailed roadmap and timeline for building and deploying the L2A and CAC reporting solution.
   - Lists key milestones, workstreams, dependencies, and resource allocations.
   - Provides risk assessment and mitigation strategies relevant to the implementation phase.
   - Delivered as a project plan document at the close of the design phase.

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC's leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
