# Objectives

- Establish a comprehensive Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data model that delivers granular visibility into pipeline performance and marketing ROI.  
- Automate data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes to ensure consistent, timely, and accurate insights.  
- Provide leadership with clear, data‑driven visibility into the full customer journey from lead generation through to managed assets.  
- Enhance the Power BI reporting experience with intuitive dashboards, standardized KPI definitions, and improved visualization of key performance metrics.  
- Strengthen Pure’s data maturity by building a scalable reporting foundation capable of supporting future data sources and evolving business needs.  
- Integrate core business systems—including Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Azure Fabric—into a unified reporting ecosystem that supports informed decision‑making.
