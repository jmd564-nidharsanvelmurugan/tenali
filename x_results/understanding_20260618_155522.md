# Understanding

- Pure Financial Advisors seeks to address limited granularity in existing pipeline reporting and the absence of integrated Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) insights.  
- Current reporting does not provide a detailed view of the Lead-to-AUM (L2A) journey, limiting visibility into marketing effectiveness and conversion performance.  
- Data ingestion from QuickBooks is not yet established, creating gaps in CAC tracking and overall data completeness.  
- Automated data ingestion and transformation workflows are required to reduce manual effort and ensure consistent, timely reporting updates.  
- The initiative requires development of an enhanced L2A and CAC data model to unify data across Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure, and QuickBooks.  
- Primary users will include data and technology stakeholders, senior leadership, and marketing teams who rely on accurate pipeline and acquisition metrics.  
- Mandatory integrations include Salesforce and QuickBooks to enable end-to-end visibility across lead generation, conversion, and cost tracking.
