# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

1. KPI Book
   - Comprehensive definitions and logic for all Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) KPIs.
   - Documentation of calculation methodologies, including all relevant data sources.
   - Reference layouts for user adoption and governance.
   - Delivered in electronic document format during Phase 3A.

2. Power BI Mock-ups
   - Visualization prototypes for up to 8 Power BI report views focused on L2A and CAC reporting.
   - Sample screen designs illustrating expected dashboards, KPIs, and data visualizations.
   - Collect stakeholder feedback prior to build-out.
   - Delivered as PDF/PowerPoint files with annotated mock-ups during Phase 3A.

3. Data Model Design
   - Logical and physical data model diagrams detailing entities, relationships, and integrations for L2A and CAC reporting.
   - Data mapping specifications for Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Azure.
   - Integration guidelines addressing ingestion and transformation.
   - Delivered as data model diagrams and supporting documentation by the close of Phase 3A.

4. Data Gap Assessment
   - Identification and documentation of any current gaps in data completeness, quality, or integration relevant to L2A and CAC.
   - Recommendations for remediation of identified issues.
   - Summary of impacts and mitigation actions.
   - Delivered as a structured assessment report during Phase 3A.

5. Technical Approach
   - Detailed architecture for batch-based data pipelines, integrations, and transformation logic within Microsoft Fabric.
   - Technical specifications for QuickBooks, Salesforce, and other mandatory integrations.
   - Outline of security, scalability, and automation practices.
   - Delivered as a technical architecture and process document upon Phase 3A completion.

6. Phase 3B Implementation Plan
   - Execution roadmap for the design, build, test, and deployment phases of the enhanced L2A and CAC reporting suite.
   - Milestones, deliverable schedules, and resource requirements.
   - Risk assessment with mitigation strategies.
   - Delivered as a project plan document at the conclusion of Phase 3A.

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC's leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
