# Outcomes

This engagement will deliver measurable business impact by enabling PURE Financial Advisors LLC to achieve deeper visibility into its client acquisition and asset conversion performance. Focused on developing a robust Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting framework, the initiative aligns with PURE’s B2C business model and the strategic objectives of its leadership and investment partner, Lee Equity Partners, to drive data maturity and marketing ROI transparency.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Granular Lead-to-AUM Visibility:** The new data model and reporting suite will provide detailed insights into each stage of the lead journey, allowing leadership to identify conversion drivers and optimize pipeline performance.  
- **Accurate Customer Acquisition Cost Measurement:** Integration of QuickBooks and Salesforce data will enable precise CAC calculations, supporting informed decisions on marketing spend efficiency and return on investment.  
- **Automated and Scalable Reporting Processes:** Automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines will reduce manual effort and ensure consistent, timely updates to Power BI dashboards as the business scales.  
- **Improved Marketing ROI Analysis:** Enhanced L2A and CAC reporting will allow marketing teams to evaluate campaign effectiveness and allocate resources toward the most profitable acquisition channels.  
- **Data-Driven Decision Making for Growth:** By establishing a unified and reliable reporting foundation, PURE will strengthen its ability to make strategic decisions that support long-term growth and operational efficiency.  
- **Reduced Risk of Insight Gaps:** Addressing data quality and integration dependencies will mitigate the current lack of visibility into pipeline and marketing performance, ensuring leadership has confidence in reported metrics.
