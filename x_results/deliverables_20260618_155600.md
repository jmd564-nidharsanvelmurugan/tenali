# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with Pure Financial Advisors LLC, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

1. **KPI Book**  
- Comprehensive documentation of Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) metrics, including definitions, formulas, and data sources.  
- Clear mapping of KPIs to business objectives and reporting views.  
- Alignment of KPI logic with industry standards and internal stakeholder expectations.  

2. **Power BI Mock-ups**  
- Design of up to eight Power BI dashboard views focused on L2A and CAC insights.  
- Visualization of key metrics, conversion funnels, and marketing ROI indicators.  
- Wireframes capturing layout, navigation, and user experience elements for leadership review.  

3. **Data Model Design**  
- Detailed schema for the L2A and CAC data model integrating Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Fabric, and QuickBooks data.  
- Logical and physical data model diagrams illustrating relationships, hierarchies, and data flows.  
- Documentation of data transformation logic and key data quality considerations.  

4. **Data Gap Assessment**  
- Identification and evaluation of missing or incomplete data elements across source systems.  
- Recommendations for remediation and data quality improvement to support accurate CAC reporting.  
- Summary of integration dependencies and readiness for QuickBooks ingestion.  

5. **Technical Approach**  
- Detailed description of the proposed architecture and integration method for QuickBooks and Salesforce data ingestion.  
- Outline of batch processing pipelines within Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse.  
- Technical design principles ensuring scalability, automation, and maintainability of the reporting solution.  

6. **Phase 3B Implementation Plan**  
- Structured roadmap detailing activities, milestones, and dependencies for the build phase.  
- Timeline and resource plan covering 8–12 weeks of implementation.  
- Risk and dependency matrix highlighting data access, integration, and quality checkpoints.  

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with Pure Financial Advisors LLC's leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
