# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with Pure Financial LLC, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

1. Data Platform Design Blueprint  
   - Comprehensive architecture overview detailing the proposed data platform structure and components  
   - Specification of recommended technologies, tools, and integration touchpoints  
   - Data security and governance framework tailored for regulatory compliance  
   - Delivery Format: PDF report and architecture diagrams; Delivery Date: End of Week 3

2. Revenue Bridge Analysis Framework  
   - Methodology and process workflow documentation for revenue bridge analysis  
   - Data mapping and transformation specifications for pipeline and revenue-related data  
   - Prototype template(s) for initial reporting and reconciliation  
   - Delivery Format: Written documentation with illustrative Excel/Power BI sample; Delivery Date: End of Week 4

3. Operational Reporting Requirements Catalogue  
   - Catalogue of prioritized operational reporting needs and stakeholder use cases  
   - Definition of key metrics, data sources, and distribution formats  
   - Sample wireframes/mockups for core operational reports  
   - Delivery Format: Requirements document with annotated mockups; Delivery Date: End of Week 5

4. Implementation Roadmap and Milestone Plan  
   - Detailed sequencing of tasks, dependencies, and prioritized milestones  
   - Timeline with resource and role allocations  
   - Key risk factors and mitigation strategies  
   - Delivery Format: Project plan in Gantt chart and slides; Delivery Date: End of Week 6

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with Pure Financial LLC's leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
