# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with VONQ, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

1. **Equity Narratives Definition**
   - Clear articulation of the equity narratives required for the exit and M&A process
   - Alignment of narratives with stakeholder expectations and investor requirements
   - Definition of narrative themes to be addressed in reporting
   - Delivered in a structured document; reviewed and validated in Week 1

2. **Metrics that Matter KPI Framework**
   - Prioritised list of KPIs aligned to commercial, financial, and operational performance
   - KPI calculation logic and definitions suitable for investor-grade reporting
   - Inclusion of metrics required for equity narrative support and reporting consistency
   - Framework and supporting documentation delivered by the end of Week 1

3. **Dashboard Mock-ups**
   - Static wireframes illustrating proposed dashboard layouts, navigation, and core metrics
   - Visualization of user flow and key reporting views for current- and future-state requirements
   - Mock-ups designed to guide Phase 2 dashboard development
   - Delivered as slideware or PDF assets during Week 2

4. **Data Feasibility Assessment**
   - Playback of current-state findings, including source system coverage and data availability
   - Identification and documentation of data gaps and remediation priorities
   - Assessment of feasibility for integrating legacy sources to meet three-year trended data requirements
   - Findings presented as a structured report at the close of Week 2

5. **Conceptual Data Model Design**
   - High-level data model encompassing Finance, CRM, ERP, and additional/legacy sources where required
   - Mapping of key entities, relationships, and data flow for the proposed reporting suite
   - Identification of batch data ingestion and refresh requirements
   - Visualization and supporting documentation delivered in Week 3

6. **Data Infrastructure and Tooling Recommendations**
   - Recommendations on tools and infrastructure for reporting, integrating, and scaling the solution
   - Comparative analysis of Qlik Warehouse and alternative tooling options, including pros and cons
   - Alignment with future automation and scalability objectives
   - Delivered as a recommendation report at the end of Week 3

7. **Implementation Roadmap for Phase 2**
   - Phased plan detailing priority use cases, dependencies, risks, and key milestones for Phase 2 build
   - Timeline and resource recommendations to guide next steps
   - Integration points and critical path activities clearly identified
   - Presented as a roadmap slide deck in Week 4

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with VONQ's leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
