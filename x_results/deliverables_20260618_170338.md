# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with PURE Financial Advisors LLC, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

1. **KPI Book**  
- Comprehensive documentation of Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) metrics, including definitions, calculation logic, and data source mapping.  
- Clear alignment of KPIs with business objectives and marketing ROI measurement.  
- Reference guide for ongoing reporting consistency and future scalability.  

2. **Power BI Mock‑ups (up to 8 views)**  
- Visual prototypes of the L2A and CAC dashboards, illustrating layout, navigation, and KPI visualization.  
- Design of user‑friendly views tailored for leadership, marketing, and data teams.  
- Blueprint for the final reporting suite to guide the subsequent build phase.  

3. **Data Model Design**  
- Detailed schema outlining relationships between Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Azure Fabric data entities.  
- Logical and physical data model documentation supporting automated ingestion and transformation.  
- Data dictionary defining key dimensions, hierarchies, and data lineage for transparency and governance.  

4. **Data Gap Assessment**  
- Evaluation of current data completeness, quality, and integration readiness across all source systems.  
- Identification of missing or inconsistent data elements impacting L2A and CAC reporting accuracy.  
- Prioritized remediation recommendations to strengthen data reliability before implementation.  

5. **Technical Approach Document**  
- Detailed description of the proposed architecture and integration method for QuickBooks and other systems.  
- Specification of batch processing pipelines within Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse.  
- Technical design principles ensuring scalability, automation, and maintainability of the reporting solution.  

6. **Phase 3B Implementation Plan**  
- Structured roadmap outlining activities, dependencies, and timelines for the build and deployment phase.  
- Resource and milestone planning aligned with PURE’s internal teams and data platform capabilities.  
- Risk and dependency tracking to ensure smooth transition from design to execution.  

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with PURE Financial Advisors LLC's leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
