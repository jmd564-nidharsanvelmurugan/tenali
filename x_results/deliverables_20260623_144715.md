# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

1. **KPI Book**
   - Compilation of defined Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) key performance indicators.
   - Detailed KPI logic, data source mapping, and calculation methods.
   - Documentation in digital format for easy reference during implementation.
   - Delivered during the discovery phase (Phase 3A).

2. **Power BI Mock-ups**
   - Visual prototypes of L2A and CAC reporting dashboards (limited to 8 views/pages).
   - Includes sample visualizations, layout, and drill-down navigation to validate reporting requirements.
   - Delivered as PDF and Power BI files for stakeholder review.
   - Provided by the end of the design phase (Phase 3A).

3. **Data Model Design**
   - Comprehensive data model blueprint for L2A and CAC reporting, including entity relationships, data flow, and integration points.
   - Specifications for integrating Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Azure data sources.
   - Delivered as diagrams and technical documentation.
   - Completed during the design phase (Phase 3A).

4. **Data Gap Assessment**
   - Detailed review of current data availability, data quality, and integration gaps related to L2A and CAC tracking.
   - Identification of required data remediation actions and prioritized recommendations.
   - Report provided in PDF format.
   - Delivered in the discovery phase (Phase 3A).

5. **Technical Approach**
   - End-to-end description of the technical architecture and workflows for automated data ingestion, transformation pipelines, and Power BI report refreshes.
   - Integrations with Salesforce and QuickBooks highlighted.
   - Includes technical specifications and integration requirements.
   - Delivered as technical documentation during the design phase (Phase 3A).

6. **Phase 3B Implementation Plan**
   - Step-by-step roadmap for building and deploying the L2A and CAC solution, including delivery timelines and major milestones.
   - Clear delineation of tasks, responsibilities, dependencies, and resource allocation.
   - Delivered as a Gantt chart (PDF and editable file), with supporting narrative documentation.
   - Provided at the conclusion of Phase 3A.

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC’s leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
