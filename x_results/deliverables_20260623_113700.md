# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with VONQ, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

1. **Equity Narratives Definition**
   - Provides a clearly documented set of equity narratives tailored to support VONQ’s M&A exit process.
   - Outlines key value drivers and investment themes distilled from stakeholder interviews and initial workshops.
   - Delivered in a structured presentation format and narrative document by the end of Week 1.

2. **Metrics that Matter KPI Framework**
   - Defines and prioritizes the core financial, commercial, and operational KPIs necessary for investor-grade reporting.
   - Includes KPI definitions, calculation logic, and mapping to equity narratives.
   - Supplied as a KPI matrix with supporting technical notes, delivered by the close of Week 2.

3. **Dashboard Mock-ups**
   - Produces static wireframes illustrating layout, structure, and navigation for the exit reporting suite.
   - Visualizes the prioritized KPIs within intuitive dashboards aligned to stakeholder requirements.
   - Delivered as PDF and/or interactive design files by the end of Week 3.

4. **Data Feasibility Assessment**
   - Assesses the availability, quality, and completeness of data across Finance System, CRM, ERP, and legacy sources.
   - Summarizes data gaps, limitations, and remediation priorities to inform Phase 2 planning.
   - Provided as a tabular findings report and narrative summary during Week 2, with playback to stakeholders.

5. **Conceptual Data Model Design**
   - Outlines a scalable data model concept leveraging Qlik Warehouse as the starting point.
   - Maps data sources, core entities, and relationships needed to support the exit reporting suite.
   - Delivered as a visual data model diagram and supporting design document by the end of Week 3.

6. **Data Infrastructure and Tooling Recommendations**
   - Evaluates current and alternative tools/platforms for reporting, data integration, and analytics.
   - Provides clear options, pros and cons, and recommendations based on VONQ’s requirements.
   - Supplied as a comparative analysis and recommendation memo by Week 4.

7. **Implementation Roadmap for Phase 2**
   - Defines a phased plan including priority use cases, dependencies, risks, and key milestones for the future exit reporting build.
   - Outlines clear actions, sequencing, and resource needs to guide Phase 2 execution.
   - Presented as a Gantt chart or project plan and detailed roadmap document at the conclusion of Week 4.

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with VONQ's leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
