# Outcomes

The proposed engagement will deliver measurable improvements to VONQ’s exit readiness by establishing a validated, investor-grade reporting suite. By designing clear equity narratives, standardising KPIs, and creating robust dashboard concepts, the initiative will directly support the sale process and underpin transparent stakeholder communications. The resulting infrastructure will enable data-driven decision-making, mitigate transaction risks, and position VONQ for sustained scalability post-exit.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Investor-Ready Reporting Suite:** A comprehensive design for an exit reporting solution, including a prioritised KPI framework and dashboard mock-ups, enabling credible, data-backed presentations to acquirers and advisors.
- **Clear Identification of Data Gaps:** Detailed analysis and documentation of source data gaps, with remediation priorities to ensure all critical metrics are reliably available for investor scrutiny.
- **Standardised KPI Framework:** Alignment of commercial, financial, and operational performance metrics to defined equity narratives, supporting transparent and consistent investor communications.
- **Automated Data Refresh Pipelines:** Design of scalable refresh processes for Finance, CRM, ERP, and legacy systems, ensuring the reporting suite remains current throughout the transaction and beyond.
- **Enhanced Stakeholder Confidence:** Improved visibility and reliability of business metrics, reducing reputational risks and strengthening VONQ’s position for negotiations and advisor discussions.
- **Scalability for Future Transactions:** Delivery of technical design and recommendations that allow for ongoing extensibility, ensuring the reporting infrastructure can adapt to new data sources and evolving business requirements.
