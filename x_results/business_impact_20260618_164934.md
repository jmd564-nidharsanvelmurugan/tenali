# Business Impact

- **Granular Marketing ROI Visibility:** Establishes a unified Lead-to-AUM and Customer Acquisition Cost reporting suite, enabling PURE Financial Advisors LLC to measure marketing effectiveness and optimize spend allocation across acquisition channels.  
- **Data-Driven Growth Decisions:** Empowers leadership and Lee Equity Partners with accurate, real-time insights into pipeline performance, supporting strategic decisions that drive AUM growth and improve conversion efficiency.  
- **Operational Efficiency and Automation:** Automates data ingestion and transformation processes across Salesforce, QuickBooks, and Tamarac, reducing manual reporting effort by an estimated 40–60% and improving reporting timeliness.  
- **Enhanced Data Accuracy and Consistency:** Introduces standardized KPI definitions and a single-source-of-truth data model, minimizing discrepancies across teams and ensuring confidence in reported metrics.  
- **Scalable Reporting Foundation:** Leverages Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse to create a scalable data platform capable of supporting future data sources and advanced analytics, ensuring long-term adaptability.  
- **Improved Stakeholder Alignment:** Provides transparent, actionable insights for data, marketing, and leadership teams, fostering alignment on performance drivers and accountability for growth outcomes.  

By delivering these outcomes, this engagement will enable PURE Financial Advisors LLC to transform its pipeline and marketing performance management, creating a sustainable, data-driven foundation for long-term growth and value creation in the competitive U.S. financial advisory market.
