# Understanding

- VONQ requires a scalable exit reporting suite—including a refreshable data cube, dashboards, and supporting documentation—to support its M&A sale process and investor communications.
- There is a lack of investor-ready reporting, resulting in difficulty presenting credible data-backed equity narratives to acquirers and advisors.
- Potential data gaps exist across source systems, with the current Qlik Warehouse not fully supporting all required KPIs for the equity narrative.
- Integration challenges exist: some source systems do not have automated connections into the Qlik Warehouse and require manual one-off ingestion, particularly where recurring refresh is needed during the transaction.
- Capability requirements include equity narrative definition, a prioritised KPI framework, dashboard mock-ups, data feasibility assessment, conceptual data model design, tooling recommendations, and a phased implementation roadmap.
- Automated workflows are needed for data ingestion and refresh pipelines from Finance System, CRM, and ERP into the Qlik Warehouse, including recurring refresh for sources requiring updates during the transaction.
- Mandatory integrations include Finance System, CRM, and ERP into the Qlik Warehouse; legacy sources must be included to provide a minimum of three years of trended data.
- The primary users of the exit reporting suite will be senior leadership, exit advisors (Barclays), and potential acquirers/investors.
