# Business Context

PURE Financial Advisors LLC operates in the U.S. financial advisory and wealth management sector, providing clients with personalized financial advisory services and asset management solutions. The firm follows a B2C model, serving individual investors and households seeking expert guidance on asset growth and long-term financial planning. Key stakeholders include Jason Carver, Client Engagement Manager, and Lee Equity Partners, the firm’s investment partner.

PURE is initiating this project to strengthen its data-driven decision-making capabilities by enhancing Lead-to-AUM (L2A) pipeline and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting. The current reporting environment offers limited granularity and lacks integrated insights into marketing effectiveness and conversion performance. By improving the L2A and CAC data model and automating data ingestion from systems such as Salesforce and QuickBooks, PURE aims to deliver more accurate, actionable intelligence on the journey from lead generation to managed assets. This initiative supports the company’s broader goal of achieving scalable, mature reporting capabilities that enable leadership to better evaluate marketing ROI and pipeline efficiency.
