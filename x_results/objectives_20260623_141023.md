# Objectives

- Deliver comprehensive Lead-to-AUM and Customer Acquisition Cost data models and reporting capabilities that provide granular insights into pipeline performance and marketing ROI.
- Automate data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes to ensure timely and reliable access to L2A and CAC metrics.
- Enable leadership to gain a detailed understanding of the journey from lead generation to asset acquisition and the effectiveness of marketing spend.
- Enhance Power BI dashboards to improve user experience with clear KPI definitions and visualizations for actionable decision-making.
- Improve accuracy and consistency in pipeline and customer acquisition cost tracking through integrated and scalable data workflows.
- Establish a scalable reporting infrastructure capable of supporting additional data sources and future business growth.
- Ensure seamless integration with Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Microsoft Fabric to support unified, end-to-end reporting.
