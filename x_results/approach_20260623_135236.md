# Approach

Our engagement with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC is designed to deliver a best-in-class Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting suite, leveraging the client’s Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse platforms, with integration to Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and newly onboarded QuickBooks data. The methodology prioritizes stakeholder alignment, data-driven design, and rapid iterations to ensure the solution meets business requirements for granularity, automation, and scalability.

We propose a two-phase approach, beginning with requirements discovery and solution design (Phase 3A), followed by the implementation of automated pipelines, data models, and Power BI reporting (Phase 3B). Each phase includes structured activities and clear deliverables aligned to project objectives and dependencies, particularly QuickBooks integration and data quality assessment.

---

**Phase 3A: Discovery & Design, Duration: 3 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 to Week 3**  
**Summary:** Define requirements, assess data, and design the L2A & CAC reporting solution architecture.

**Activities:**
- Conduct stakeholder workshops to capture requirements and current-state pain points.
- Perform a comprehensive data gap and quality assessment across Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks.
- Design enhanced L2A and CAC data models, sourcing strategies, and transformation logic.
- Develop Power BI mock-ups (up to 8 pages) to visualize proposed metrics, KPIs, and dashboard navigation.
- Document KPI Book, data model specification, and technical integration approach for QuickBooks.
- Prepare the Phase 3B Implementation Plan, detailing roadmap, timelines, and resource allocation.

---

**Phase 3B: Build & Implement, Duration: 8–12 weeks, Timeline: Week 4 to Week 15**  
**Summary:** Develop automated pipelines, implement data models, and deliver Power BI reporting for L2A and CAC.

**Activities:**
- Configure and automate batch data ingestion from Salesforce and QuickBooks using the Microsoft Fabric data platform.
- Develop data transformation pipelines within Azure Synapse to support L2A and CAC models.
- Implement the chosen data model and ensure integration with Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and other required sources.
- Build the Power BI reporting suite, utilizing the designed mock-ups and KPIs for dashboard delivery.
- Conduct iterative data validation and refine reports based on stakeholder feedback.
- Provide comprehensive documentation, user training, and knowledge transfer sessions for sustained adoption.

---

The value of this phased approach lies in ensuring that PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC receives a robust, scalable reporting foundation, with rapid cycles of design and feedback ahead of the build phase. This methodology minimizes project risk, accelerates time-to-insight for key business questions, and positions the client for continued data maturity and future extensibility across additional sources and analytics requirements.
