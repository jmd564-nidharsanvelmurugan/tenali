# Approach

PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC has engaged us to design and implement a robust Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data model and reporting suite. Our methodology leverages proven data management strategies, a consultative approach to KPI definition, and advanced automation of data pipelines, resulting in scalable and actionable Power BI reporting tailored to support data-driven decision-making across leadership, data, and marketing teams.

The engagement is structured into clearly defined phases to address current integration challenges, ensure seamless QuickBooks ingestion, and automate reporting processes for enhanced visibility into marketing effectiveness and pipeline health. Each phase is designed to deliver targeted artifacts—ranging from business metric documentation to technical designs and reporting mock-ups—ensuring iterative value and alignment with PURE’s growth objectives.

**Phase 3A: Discovery & Solution Design, Duration: 3 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 to Week 3**  
**Summary:** Conduct discovery sessions, assess data sources, and design a future-state data model and reporting suite.  
**Activities:**  
- Facilitate in-depth discovery workshops with key stakeholders to define L2A, CAC, and reporting requirements.
- Assess data quality and integration readiness across Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks.
- Document business logic for KPIs, including Lead-to-AUM conversion and CAC metrics, in a comprehensive KPI Book.
- Create Power BI reporting mock-ups, limited to eight views focused on L2A and CAC.
- Develop a detailed Data Model design and technical approach for QuickBooks integration.
- Conduct a Data Gap Assessment to identify any readiness or quality issues impacting reporting.

**Phase 3B: Implementation Planning & Roadmap, Duration: 8–12 weeks, Timeline: Week 4 to Week 15**  
**Summary:** Prepare implementation plans, automate data pipelines, integrate QuickBooks, and deliver the reporting suite.  
**Activities:**  
- Finalize technical design and validate with data, technology, and marketing teams.
- Build and automate data ingestion and transformation pipelines on Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse platforms.
- Configure and integrate data from QuickBooks into the unified L2A and CAC data model.
- Develop and deploy the Power BI reporting suite, ensuring accurate KPI visualizations and dashboard usability.
- Prepare and deliver technical and user documentation to support long-term platform scalability.
- Develop a Phase 3B Implementation Plan detailing milestones, resource requirements, and change management strategies.

This phased approach ensures value at every stage, beginning with requirements alignment and culminating in the delivery of automated, insightful, and scalable L2A and CAC reporting. By addressing integration dependencies and focusing on robust design and automation, PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC will gain the actionable insights necessary to maximize their marketing ROI and lead-to-AUM conversion transparency.
