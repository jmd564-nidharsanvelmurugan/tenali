# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

1. **KPI Book**
   - Comprehensive documentation of all Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) KPIs.
   - Includes detailed metric definitions, calculation logic, and data sources.
   - Provided in PDF and Excel formats for review and handoff.

2. **Power BI Mock-Ups**
   - Interactive visual prototypes of the enhanced Power BI reporting suite focused on L2A and CAC analytics.
   - Up to 8 pages/views with sample data to illustrate dashboard layouts, visualizations, and key features.
   - Delivered as Power BI (.pbix) files and accompanying PDF screenshots.

3. **Data Model Design**
   - Logical and physical data model diagrams supporting L2A and CAC reporting requirements.
   - Mapping of all relevant data entities, relationships, and data flows from sources such as Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks.
   - Delivered as PDF, Visio, or equivalent data modeling documentation.

4. **Data Gap Assessment**
   - Identification of gaps between current data assets and future-state reporting needs for L2A and CAC metrics.
   - Recommendations to address data quality issues, integration challenges, and completeness of input data.
   - Assessment report delivered in PDF format.

5. **Technical Approach**
   - Step-by-step outline of integration, ingestion, transformation, and automation processes to connect QuickBooks and other data sources.
   - Detailed guidance on batch pipeline configuration in Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse.
   - Documented in a technical specification report (PDF/Word).

6. **Phase 3B Implementation Plan**
   - Roadmap for the build and deployment phase, detailing sequencing, dependencies, resourcing, and estimated timelines (8–12 weeks).
   - Includes milestones for integration, automation, testing, and rollout of the reporting suite.
   - Delivered in PowerPoint and project plan formats.

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC's leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
