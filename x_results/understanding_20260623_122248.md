# Understanding

- VONQ needs to design and build a scalable exit reporting suite to support the M&A sale process and facilitate robust investor communications.
- There is a lack of investor-ready reporting, with potential data gaps across source systems that hinder comprehensive exit and KPI reporting.
- The existing Qlik Warehouse may not fully support all required KPIs critical for the equity narrative demanded by stakeholders during the transaction.
- Equity narrative definition and a prioritized KPI framework are required to align reporting with investor and advisor expectations.
- The reporting solution must include dashboard mock-ups, a data feasibility assessment, a conceptual data model, tooling recommendations, and a phased implementation roadmap.
- Automation of data ingestion and refresh pipelines from Finance System, CRM, ERP, and legacy sources into the Qlik Warehouse is required, particularly for ongoing updates during the transaction period.
- Core user groups include senior leadership, exit advisors (Barclays), and potential acquirers/investors, each with distinct data and reporting needs.
- Mandatory integration is required between Finance System, CRM, ERP, and the Qlik Warehouse, with legacy data sources needed to provide at least three years of trended data.
