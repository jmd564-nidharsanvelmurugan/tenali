# Understanding

- VONQ requires a scalable exit reporting suite—including a refreshable data cube, dashboards, and documentation—to support its M&A sale process and investor communications.
- Current pain points include an absence of investor-ready reporting, potential data gaps across source systems, and limited KPI coverage in the existing Qlik Warehouse for supporting the equity narrative.
- Key capabilities needed are equity narrative definition, a prioritised KPI framework, dashboard mock-ups, data feasibility assessment, conceptual data model design, tooling recommendations, and a phased implementation roadmap.
- Data ingestion and recurring refresh pipelines from Finance System, CRM, ERP, and legacy sources into Qlik Warehouse must be automated, especially for sources requiring updates during the transaction.
- Mandatory integrations are required for Finance System, CRM, ERP, and legacy sources (to provide a minimum of three years of trended data) into the Qlik Warehouse.
- User roles engaging with the reporting suite include senior leadership, exit advisors (Barclays), and potential acquirers/investors.
- Phase 1 dependencies include timely stakeholder workshop availability, data access readiness in Week 0, and alignment with exit timeline constraints.
- The completeness and quality of existing Qlik Warehouse data, along with access to management accounts and board packs, are critical prerequisites for the reporting solution.
