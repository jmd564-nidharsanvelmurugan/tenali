# Objectives

- Provide a comprehensive Lead-to-AUM and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data model and reporting suite that delivers granular insights into pipeline performance and marketing ROI.
- Automate data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes to ensure timely and accurate pipeline and CAC analytics.
- Enable leadership to gain a clear, detailed understanding of the client journey from lead generation through asset acquisition and the effectiveness of marketing investments.
- Enhance Power BI dashboards with improved visualizations and clearly defined key performance indicators (KPIs) for L2A and CAC.
- Improve pipeline reporting and customer acquisition cost tracking for data-driven decision making.
- Establish scalable reporting capabilities and data maturity to support future growth, evolving data sources, and expanded analytics needs.
- Ensure seamless integration with Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Microsoft Fabric to provide a unified and reliable data platform.
