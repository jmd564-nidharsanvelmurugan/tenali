# Understanding

- PURE seeks to address limited visibility and granularity in its existing pipeline reporting, particularly around the full Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) journey and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) metrics.  
- Current reporting lacks integrated CAC insights, preventing a clear view of marketing efficiency and return on spend.  
- Data from key systems such as QuickBooks is not yet connected, creating gaps in financial and acquisition cost tracking.  
- The absence of automated data ingestion and transformation processes results in manual effort and delayed reporting updates.  
- Enhanced data modeling and quality assessment are required to ensure accurate and consistent L2A and CAC reporting.  
- The system must support multiple user groups, including data and technology teams, senior leadership, and marketing stakeholders.  
- Mandatory integrations include Salesforce and QuickBooks to enable end‑to‑end visibility across lead, client, and financial data.
