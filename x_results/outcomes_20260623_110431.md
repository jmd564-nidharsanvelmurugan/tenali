# Outcomes

The proposed engagement will position PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC to achieve measurable improvements in lead pipeline visibility and marketing effectiveness. By implementing an advanced Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting suite, the organization will gain actionable insights that drive more strategic decisions, enhance operational efficiency, and support scalable growth. Success will be defined by the delivery of accurate, automated reporting integrated within Power BI, providing clear visibility into critical business KPIs.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Improved Pipeline Transparency:** Granular reporting on the journey from lead generation to asset under management will enable stakeholders to monitor, analyze, and optimize each stage of the acquisition process.
- **Actionable Marketing ROI Insights:** Enhanced CAC metrics and reporting will provide marketing and leadership teams with detailed visibility into return on investment, informing strategic allocation of marketing resources.
- **Automated Data Integration:** Seamless, automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines will reduce manual effort, minimize errors, and ensure the timely delivery of up-to-date business insights.
- **Leadership Decision Support:** Clear KPI dashboards and metric definitions in Power BI will empower senior leadership with accurate information to inform high-impact business decisions.
- **Scalable Reporting Infrastructure:** The solution will provide a robust and scalable foundation for future data sources and reporting needs, supporting PURE’s long-term growth objectives.
- **Risk Mitigation through Enhanced Visibility:** By addressing data quality and integration challenges, the organization will reduce risk associated with limited visibility into marketing effectiveness and pipeline performance.
