# Approach

This engagement with **Pure Financial Advisors LLC** is designed to address the firm’s specific challenges in achieving granular visibility across its Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting. Operating within the financial advisory and wealth management industry under a B2C model, Pure’s growth depends on understanding the full customer journey from lead generation to asset management. The approach leverages Pure’s existing Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse infrastructure while integrating new data sources—most notably QuickBooks—to create a unified, automated, and scalable reporting environment.

Our methodology follows a structured, phased framework that ensures alignment with Pure’s business objectives, technical landscape, and stakeholder expectations. Each phase builds upon the previous one, moving from discovery and design through implementation planning, with clear checkpoints for validation and stakeholder engagement. This ensures that the final solution is both technically robust and strategically aligned with Pure’s data maturity goals.

---

**Phase 3A: Discovery & Design, Duration: 3 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 to Week 3**

**Summary:** Establish the foundation for the L2A and CAC reporting solution through data discovery, KPI definition, and initial model design.

**Activities:**  
- Conduct workshops with data, technology, and marketing teams to confirm reporting requirements and success metrics.  
- Assess existing data sources (Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, QuickBooks) for completeness and quality.  
- Define KPI logic and calculation methodology for L2A and CAC metrics.  
- Develop initial Power BI mock-ups (up to 8 views) to visualize priority insights.  
- Draft the target-state data model design and validate with key stakeholders.  
- Document data gaps and integration dependencies, particularly around QuickBooks ingestion.

---

**Phase 3B: Build & Implementation Planning, Duration: 8–12 weeks, Timeline: Week 4 to Week 15**

**Summary:** Finalize the technical design and implementation roadmap, preparing Pure’s data platform for automated, scalable reporting.

**Activities:**  
- Refine and finalize the L2A and CAC data model based on feedback from Phase 3A.  
- Design automated batch data ingestion and transformation pipelines within Microsoft Fabric.  
- Develop detailed technical approach for QuickBooks integration and data quality assessment.  
- Create the KPI Book, including metric definitions, data lineage, and ownership mapping.  
- Finalize Power BI dashboard mock-ups and reporting suite specifications.  
- Prepare the Phase 3B Implementation Plan outlining build steps, dependencies, and resource requirements.

---

This structured, phased approach ensures that **Pure Financial Advisors LLC’s** data platform and reporting capabilities are developed with precision, agility, and a clear focus on delivering measurable business outcomes. By progressing from discovery through design and implementation planning, the engagement provides a scalable foundation for enhanced pipeline visibility, marketing ROI analysis, and long-term data maturity across the organization.
