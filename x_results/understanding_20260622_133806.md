# Understanding

- Limited granularity in existing pipeline reporting prevents detailed visibility across the lead-to-AUM journey and marketing spend attribution.
- Current reporting lacks integrated and accurate insights into Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), impacting the ability to measure marketing ROI.
- There is a requirement to enhance the Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and CAC data model to support more sophisticated reporting and analytics.
- Mandatory integration and automated data ingestion are needed for QuickBooks and Salesforce to unify pipeline, cost, and financial data.
- Data quality issues must be assessed and addressed, especially regarding CAC reporting dependent on combined financial and operational data sources.
- Automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines are required to enable consistent, up-to-date L2A and CAC reporting.
- The system will be used by data and technology stakeholders, senior leadership, and marketing teams for reporting and decision-making.
- Timely delivery is constrained by dependencies on QuickBooks integration and completion of data quality assessments.
