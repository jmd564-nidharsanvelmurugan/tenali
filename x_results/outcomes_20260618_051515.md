# Outcomes

This engagement will deliver measurable business impact by enabling Pure Financial Advisors LLC to achieve deeper visibility into its marketing and pipeline performance through enhanced Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting. The initiative aligns with Pure’s B2C financial advisory model and supports both internal leadership and private equity stakeholders in driving data‑driven growth decisions and optimizing marketing ROI.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Granular Pipeline Visibility and Conversion Insights:** The new L2A data model will provide detailed tracking of leads through each stage of the funnel, enabling leadership to identify conversion bottlenecks and optimize acquisition strategies.  
- **Accurate and Automated CAC Measurement:** Integration of QuickBooks and Salesforce data will allow precise calculation of customer acquisition costs, improving transparency into marketing spend efficiency and ROI.  
- **Improved Decision‑Making Through Unified Reporting:** Consolidated Power BI dashboards will deliver a single source of truth for pipeline and cost metrics, empowering senior management to make faster, evidence‑based decisions.  
- **Operational Efficiency via Automated Data Processes:** Automated ingestion and transformation pipelines will reduce manual reporting effort, ensuring consistent, timely updates and freeing teams to focus on analysis rather than data preparation.  
- **Scalable Data Foundation for Future Growth:** The enhanced data model and Fabric‑based architecture will establish a scalable framework capable of supporting additional data sources and advanced analytics as Pure’s business expands.  
- **Strengthened Marketing Accountability and ROI Tracking:** Clear KPI definitions and visualizations will enable marketing teams to directly link campaign performance to AUM growth, fostering accountability and continuous improvement.
