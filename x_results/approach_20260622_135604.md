# Approach

PURE Financial Advisors LLC is undertaking a strategic initiative to enhance the granularity, accuracy, and automation of its Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) pipeline reporting. This engagement will involve collaboration between data, technology, and marketing teams to deliver integrated insights across Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and newly ingested QuickBooks data. Our methodology is rooted in structured discovery, rigorous design, and technical execution—ensuring that all deliverables align to business needs, scalability requirements, and clear reporting outcomes.

We will utilize a phased approach, each stage building incrementally toward a scalable, automated, and actionable reporting suite within Power BI. The process is divided as follows: an initial Discovery phase focused on gap analysis and requirements gathering, followed by a Design and Build phase that culminates in end-to-end data models, automated pipelines, and an integrated reporting suite. By methodically engaging stakeholders and leveraging batch processing through Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse, Pure will realize a future-ready solution that supports both business growth and enhanced decision-making.

---

**Phase 3A: Discovery & Design, Duration: 3 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 to Week 3**  
**Summary:** This phase centers on gathering requirements, conducting data gap assessments, and defining the solution architecture for L2A and CAC reporting.

**Activities:**  
- Stakeholder workshops to confirm reporting requirements and critical KPIs  
- Data gap assessment across Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks  
- Technical approach documentation and solution architecture definition  
- Detailed KPI Book creation with definitions, data logic, and source mapping  
- Preparation of initial Power BI mock-ups (up to 8 views) focused on L2A and CAC insights  
- Roadmap development and planning for Phase 3B implementation  

---

**Phase 3B: Data Integration & Reporting Suite Build, Duration: 8–12 weeks, Timeline: Week 4 to Week 15**  
**Summary:** This phase delivers the integrated L2A and CAC data model, automated pipelines, QuickBooks ingestion, and the full Power BI reporting suite.

**Activities:**  
- Integration of QuickBooks data with existing Azure Fabric and Synapse platforms  
- Development and validation of enhanced L2A and CAC data models  
- Automated data ingestion and transformation pipeline build for batch processing  
- Comprehensive Power BI reporting suite development with 8 dashboard views  
- Data quality assessment and remediation to ensure reliable CAC calculations  
- Documentation and training for data and marketing stakeholders on reporting suite use  

---

By proceeding through these distinct phases, PURE Financial Advisors LLC will achieve a fully integrated, scalable, and automated pipeline for L2A and CAC reporting. This structured approach ensures incremental value delivery, supports transparent stakeholder engagement, and lays a foundation for continuous reporting innovation. The final solution will empower leadership with granular, actionable insights on pipeline performance and marketing ROI, supporting sustainable business growth and data maturity.
