# Objectives

- Deliver comprehensive Lead-to-AUM and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data models and a reporting suite that provide granular insights into pipeline performance and marketing return on investment (ROI).
- Automate data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes to ensure timely and accurate reporting.
- Enable leadership to gain a clear and actionable understanding of the customer journey from lead to asset under management and the effectiveness of marketing spend.
- Enhance the user experience by improving Power BI dashboards with intuitive visualizations and clearly defined key performance indicators (KPIs).
- Strengthen pipeline reporting and customer acquisition cost tracking to support informed decision-making and business growth.
- Establish a scalable data and reporting platform that can accommodate future data sources and evolving business requirements.
- Integrate seamlessly with Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, Microsoft Fabric, and Azure Synapse to unify all relevant data within the reporting framework.
