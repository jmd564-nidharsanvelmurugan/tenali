# Approach

PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC has engaged our team to design and implement an enhanced data and reporting solution focused on Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) analytics. Our methodology emphasizes a structured, phased approach leveraging industry best practices, stakeholder collaboration, and iterative delivery to maximize transparency, minimize risk, and drive impactful business outcomes aligned with PURE’s strategic objectives.

We will strategically progress through defined discovery, design, and build phases, each anchored to specific deliverables and outcomes. Our team will utilize Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse platforms for data integration, ensuring seamless data flow from Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and the newly integrated QuickBooks system. The engagement is driven by batch processing using automated pipelines and will culminate in an actionable Power BI reporting suite that delivers granular insights into marketing ROI and pipeline performance.

---

**Phase 3A: Discovery & Solution Design, Duration: 3 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 to Week 3**  
**Summary:**  
Elicit requirements, assess data sources, and design the target state for L2A and CAC reporting.  
**Activities:**  
- Conduct stakeholder interviews with Data, Technology, and Marketing teams to gather detailed reporting and analytical requirements  
- Assess the quality and availability of data from Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks  
- Develop and validate the L2A and CAC KPI Book, including logic definitions and data lineage  
- Create Power BI report mock-ups (up to 8 views) aligned to stakeholder needs  
- Design the target data model and produce the data gap assessment  
- Prepare the technical approach and the Phase 3B Implementation Plan  

---

**Phase 3B: Build & Integration, Duration: 8–12 weeks, Timeline: Week 4 to Week 15**  
**Summary:**  
Enable data ingestion, implement automation pipelines, and deliver the reporting suite with QuickBooks integration.  
**Activities:**  
- Develop, test, and operationalize automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines in Microsoft Fabric/Azure Synapse  
- Connect and ingest QuickBooks data, resolving data quality and integration challenges  
- Build the L2A and CAC data model based on approved designs  
- Implement Power BI reporting suite (up to 8 dashboards/views) with validated KPIs and visualizations  
- Conduct user acceptance testing (UAT) with business stakeholders and refine reporting outputs as needed  
- Deliver final documentation, knowledge transfer sessions, and roadmap for future scalability  

---

By structuring our engagement in clearly defined phases, PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC will benefit from early alignment, iterative validation, and reduced project risk. A collaborative and transparent methodology will ensure that all requirements are addressed, with deliverables produced in line with stakeholder expectations—ultimately providing PURE with scalable, automated, and actionable pipeline and CAC analytics that directly support their growth strategy.
