# Approach

PURE Financial Advisors LLC operates in a highly competitive financial advisory and wealth management market, where data‑driven insights into client acquisition and marketing ROI are critical to sustaining growth. This engagement is designed to address PURE’s specific challenge of limited visibility into the Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) pipeline and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) performance by leveraging its existing Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse infrastructure. The approach builds on PURE’s current Salesforce, Tamarac, and Azure data ecosystem while integrating QuickBooks to create a unified, automated, and scalable reporting foundation.

Our methodology follows a structured, phased approach that ensures alignment with PURE’s business priorities, technical environment, and timeline expectations. Each phase builds on the previous one—progressing from discovery and data assessment through model design, mock‑up development, and implementation planning—culminating in a robust, actionable reporting suite. This structure ensures that all stakeholders, from data teams to senior leadership, remain engaged and informed throughout the process.

---

**Phase 1: Discovery & Current State Assessment, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 1**

**Summary:** Establish a clear understanding of PURE’s existing data landscape, reporting processes, and integration points to inform downstream design activities.

**Activities:**  
- Conduct kickoff sessions with data, technology, and marketing stakeholders to confirm objectives and success criteria.  
- Review existing reporting assets within Power BI and Microsoft Fabric to assess current L2A and CAC visibility.  
- Document data flows across Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks.  
- Identify data access requirements and confirm system connectivity for subsequent phases.  
- Develop a detailed project plan and workshop schedule aligned with the 3‑week Phase 3A timeline.

---

**Phase 2: Data Source Analysis & Gap Assessment, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 2**

**Summary:** Evaluate data quality, completeness, and integration readiness across all relevant systems to identify gaps impacting L2A and CAC reporting.

**Activities:**  
- Profile data from Salesforce, Tamarac, and Azure Blob Storage to assess consistency and completeness.  
- Conduct preliminary QuickBooks data review to identify ingestion and mapping requirements.  
- Define data quality metrics and document key issues affecting CAC calculations.  
- Map existing data fields to target KPIs to highlight missing or misaligned attributes.  
- Prepare a Data Gap Assessment summary to guide model design and integration planning.

---

**Phase 3: Data Model Design & KPI Definition, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 3**

**Summary:** Design the target‑state L2A and CAC data model and define KPI logic to support accurate, automated reporting.

**Activities:**  
- Develop conceptual and logical data models integrating Salesforce, Tamarac, and QuickBooks data.  
- Define KPI calculation logic for L2A conversion and CAC metrics, ensuring alignment with business definitions.  
- Validate data model design with stakeholders to confirm usability and scalability.  
- Document data transformation and aggregation rules for batch processing in Microsoft Fabric.  
- Finalize the KPI Book detailing metric definitions, data lineage, and calculation methodology.

---

**Phase 4: Power BI Mock‑ups & Reporting Design, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 4**

**Summary:** Translate the data model and KPI logic into visual reporting concepts that demonstrate the future‑state Power BI experience.

**Activities:**  
- Design up to eight Power BI mock‑ups focused on L2A and CAC insights.  
- Incorporate user feedback from marketing and leadership teams to refine dashboard layouts.  
- Define navigation, filtering, and drill‑down structures to support intuitive analysis.  
- Align visualizations with PURE’s brand and reporting standards.  
- Validate mock‑ups against data model outputs to ensure consistency and feasibility.

---

**Phase 5: Technical Approach & Implementation Planning, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 5**

**Summary:** Develop the technical blueprint and roadmap for Phase 3B implementation, ensuring readiness for build and automation.

**Activities:**  
- Define the end‑to‑end data ingestion and transformation approach for QuickBooks integration.  
- Outline batch processing workflows within Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse.  
- Develop the Technical Approach document detailing architecture, dependencies, and automation design.  
- Create the Phase 3B Implementation Plan, including sequencing, resourcing, and estimated effort.  
- Present final deliverables (KPI Book, Data Model, Mock‑ups, and Implementation Plan) for stakeholder approval.

---

This structured, phased approach ensures that PURE Financial Advisors LLC’s L2A and CAC reporting capabilities are developed with precision, agility, and a clear focus on delivering measurable business outcomes. By progressing methodically from discovery through design and planning, the engagement provides a solid foundation for scalable, automated reporting that empowers leadership with actionable insights into marketing effectiveness and pipeline performance.
