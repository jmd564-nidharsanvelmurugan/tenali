# Business Impact

- **Enhanced Marketing ROI Visibility:** Establishes a unified Lead‑to‑AUM and Customer Acquisition Cost reporting suite, enabling PURE Financial Advisors to quantify marketing effectiveness and optimize spend allocation across channels.  
- **Data‑Driven Growth Decisions:** Provides leadership and PE stakeholders with granular insights into conversion performance, supporting strategic decisions that can improve lead quality and accelerate AUM growth.  
- **Operational Efficiency Gains:** Automates data ingestion and transformation processes across Salesforce, QuickBooks, and Tamarac, reducing manual reporting effort by an estimated 40–60% and improving reporting timeliness.  
- **Improved Data Accuracy and Consistency:** Introduces standardized KPI definitions and a consolidated data model within Microsoft Fabric, ensuring reliable, audit‑ready metrics for both internal management and investor reporting.  
- **Scalable Reporting Foundation:** Builds a future‑proof data platform capable of integrating additional data sources and supporting new analytics use cases, positioning PURE for sustained scalability and digital maturity.  
- **Private Equity Value Enablement:** Delivers diligence‑grade reporting assets that strengthen transparency for Lee Equity Partners, supporting portfolio oversight and readiness for future transaction events.  

By delivering these outcomes, this engagement will enable PURE Financial Advisors LLC to transform its pipeline and marketing performance management approach, creating a lasting data asset that drives operational efficiency, informed growth decisions, and long‑term value creation in the competitive U.S. financial advisory marketplace.
