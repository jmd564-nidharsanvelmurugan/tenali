# Business Context

Pure Financial Advisors LLC operates within the U.S. financial advisory and wealth management sector, providing clients with tailored financial advisory services and asset management solutions. The firm follows a B2C model, serving individual investors and households seeking professional guidance to grow and manage their assets. Key stakeholders include Jason Carver, Client Engagement Manager, and Lee Equity Partners, an investment partner supporting Pure’s growth objectives.

The business is currently focused on strengthening its data-driven decision-making capabilities by enhancing its Lead-to-AUM (L2A) pipeline and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting. Existing pipeline reporting lacks the granularity needed to fully understand the journey from lead generation to asset under management and to measure marketing effectiveness. To address this, Pure aims to design and implement a comprehensive L2A and CAC data model integrated with Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and its Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse platforms. This initiative will enable automated data ingestion, improved Power BI reporting, and more accurate insights into marketing ROI and pipeline performance, supporting leadership in making informed growth and investment decisions.
