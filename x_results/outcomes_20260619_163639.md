# Outcomes

This engagement will deliver measurable business impact and strategic value by enabling PURE Financial Advisors LLC to gain deeper visibility into its Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) pipeline and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) performance. The initiative aligns with PURE’s B2C financial advisory model and supports both internal leadership and private equity stakeholders by improving data‑driven decision making and marketing ROI transparency.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Granular Pipeline Visibility and Conversion Insights:** The new L2A data model will provide detailed tracking of leads through to assets under management, allowing leadership to identify conversion bottlenecks and optimize acquisition strategies.  
- **Accurate and Automated CAC Measurement:** Integration of QuickBooks and Salesforce data will enable precise calculation of customer acquisition costs, improving understanding of marketing spend efficiency and ROI.  
- **Improved Marketing Effectiveness and Budget Allocation:** Enhanced reporting will allow marketing teams to assess campaign performance at a granular level, supporting data‑backed decisions on resource allocation and channel optimization.  
- **Operational Efficiency Through Automation:** Automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines will reduce manual reporting effort, ensuring consistent, timely, and reliable insights across business units.  
- **Scalable Data Foundation for Future Growth:** The solution will establish a robust, extensible data architecture within Microsoft Fabric and Synapse, supporting future data sources and advanced analytics as PURE’s business expands.  
- **Increased Stakeholder Confidence in Data‑Driven Decisions:** Leadership and investment partners will gain trusted, transparent metrics on pipeline performance and marketing ROI, strengthening strategic planning and investment oversight.
