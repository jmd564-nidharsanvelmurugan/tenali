# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with Pure Financial Advisors LLC, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

1. **KPI Book**  
- Comprehensive documentation of Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) metrics, including definitions, formulas, and data sources.  
- Clear mapping of KPIs to business objectives and reporting views.  
- Establishment of standardized calculation logic to ensure consistency across dashboards and future reporting.  

2. **Power BI Mock‑ups (up to 8 views)**  
- Design of visual prototypes illustrating the future‑state L2A and CAC dashboards.  
- Layouts and navigation structure reflecting user roles and reporting priorities.  
- Visual representation of key metrics, filters, and drill‑down capabilities to guide the build phase.  

3. **Data Model Design**  
- Detailed schema for the L2A and CAC data model, including entity relationships, data flows, and transformation logic.  
- Integration mapping for Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks data sources.  
- Documentation of data lineage and dependencies within Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse environments.  

4. **Data Gap Assessment**  
- Identification of missing or incomplete data elements required for accurate L2A and CAC reporting.  
- Evaluation of data quality and readiness across Salesforce, Tamarac, and QuickBooks.  
- Recommendations for remediation actions and prioritization of data improvements.  

5. **Technical Approach Document**  
- Detailed description of the proposed architecture and integration strategy for QuickBooks ingestion.  
- Outline of batch processing pipelines within Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse.  
- Specification of automation, refresh cadence, and data transformation logic supporting the reporting suite.  

6. **Phase 3B Implementation Plan**  
- Structured roadmap detailing activities, milestones, and dependencies for the build and deployment phase.  
- Resource and timeline planning aligned with the 8–12‑week implementation window.  
- Risk and dependency tracking, including QuickBooks integration and data quality checkpoints.  

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with Pure Financial Advisors LLC’s leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
