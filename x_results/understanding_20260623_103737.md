# Understanding

- The client seeks to address limited granularity in existing pipeline reporting and a lack of integrated Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) insights.
- There is a business requirement to develop enhanced reporting capabilities focused on Lead-to-AUM (L2A) pipeline analysis and detailed CAC metrics.
- Integration challenges are present, specifically the need to connect and ingest data from QuickBooks and evaluate its data quality for reliable CAC reporting.
- The current approach requires an enhanced L2A and CAC data model and upgraded Power BI reporting suite.
- Automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines for pipeline and CAC reporting are required to reduce manual effort and improve data accuracy.
- Data and technology stakeholders, senior leadership, and marketing teams are the primary users who will interact with the new system.
- Mandatory integrations include Salesforce and QuickBooks to ensure comprehensive reporting across all relevant data sources.
- Success of the initiative is dependent on timely access to Salesforce, QuickBooks, and existing data platforms, as well as addressing constraints related to data quality and integration dependencies.
