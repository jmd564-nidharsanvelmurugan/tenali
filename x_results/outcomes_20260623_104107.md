# Outcomes

The proposed initiative for PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC will drive measurable business value by transforming how the organization measures, analyzes, and optimizes its lead pipeline and marketing investments. With more granular and automated Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting, PURE will gain clearer visibility into every stage of the customer journey, empowering smarter decisions and supporting scalable future growth.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Granular Pipeline Insights:** Deliver a comprehensive view of the pipeline from lead generation to assets under management, enabling identification of bottlenecks and conversion opportunities at each stage.

- **Actionable Marketing ROI Metrics:** Provide detailed CAC reporting and advanced Power BI dashboards, allowing marketing and leadership teams to evaluate and optimize the return on every dollar spent.

- **Automated, Accurate Reporting:** Eliminate manual data processes through automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines, reducing errors and delivering up-to-date insights for all stakeholders.

- **Integrated Data Ecosystem:** Achieve seamless data integration across Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Azure platforms, laying the foundation for robust, scalable reporting capabilities.

- **Data-Driven Decision-Making:** Empower senior leadership with precise L2A conversion metrics and CAC KPIs, supporting more informed business strategies and resource allocation.

- **Scalable Analytics Framework:** Establish a reporting solution that supports PURE’s ongoing data maturity and can evolve to accommodate future growth and additional data sources.
