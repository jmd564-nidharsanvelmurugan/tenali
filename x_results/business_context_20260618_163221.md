# Business Context

PURE Financial Advisors LLC operates in the U.S. financial advisory and wealth management sector, providing clients with personalized financial advisory services and asset management solutions. The firm follows a B2C model, serving individual investors and households seeking professional guidance to grow and manage their assets. Key stakeholders include Jason Carver, Client Engagement Manager, and Lee Equity Partners, the firm’s investment partner.

PURE is initiating this project to strengthen its data-driven decision-making capabilities by enhancing its Lead-to-AUM (L2A) pipeline and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting. The current reporting environment offers limited granularity and lacks integrated insights into marketing effectiveness and conversion performance. By developing a comprehensive L2A and CAC data model, PURE aims to deliver more precise visibility into the customer journey—from initial lead generation through to asset under management—and to better understand the return on marketing spend. This initiative will enable leadership to make more informed strategic and operational decisions, improve marketing ROI tracking, and support scalable growth through a more mature data and reporting foundation.
