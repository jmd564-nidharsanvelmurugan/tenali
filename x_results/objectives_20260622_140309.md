# Objectives

- Establish a comprehensive Lead-to-AUM and Customer Acquisition Cost data model and reporting suite to provide granular insights into pipeline performance and marketing ROI.
- Enable automated data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes across key business systems.
- Deliver clear, actionable insights to senior leadership into the journey from lead generation through asset acquisition and the effectiveness of marketing spend.
- Enhance Power BI dashboards to provide improved user experience with well-defined KPIs and visualizations.
- Strengthen pipeline reporting and customer acquisition cost tracking processes to support data-driven decision making.
- Build a scalable data platform capable of integrating with Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Azure Fabric, supporting future reporting and data source expansion.
- Advance data maturity and reporting capabilities to drive PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC’s growth and adaptability.
