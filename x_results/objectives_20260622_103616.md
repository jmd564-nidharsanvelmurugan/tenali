# Objectives

- Deliver a comprehensive Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data model and reporting suite that provides granular insights into pipeline performance and marketing ROI.
- Automate the data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes to improve efficiency and reduce manual effort.
- Enable leadership to gain a detailed understanding of the journey from lead to assets under management and the effectiveness of marketing spend.
- Enhance user experience through improved Power BI dashboards featuring clear KPI definitions and visualizations.
- Strengthen pipeline reporting and customer acquisition cost tracking to support informed decision-making and business growth.
- Establish scalable data and reporting capabilities that can accommodate future data sources, business requirements, and growth.
- Ensure seamless integration of the future platform with Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Microsoft Fabric.
