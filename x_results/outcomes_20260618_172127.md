# Outcomes

This engagement with **PURE Financial Advisors LLC** will deliver measurable business impact by transforming how the firm tracks and interprets its marketing and sales funnel performance. Focused on developing a robust Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting framework, the initiative aligns with Pure’s B2C growth model and the strategic objectives of both internal leadership and its investment partner, Lee Equity Partners. The outcome will enable data‑driven decisions that optimize marketing ROI and accelerate AUM growth.  

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Granular Lead‑to‑AUM Visibility:** The new data model and reporting suite will provide detailed insights into each stage of the lead journey, allowing leadership to pinpoint conversion drivers and bottlenecks across the pipeline.  
- **Accurate Customer Acquisition Cost Measurement:** Integration of QuickBooks and Salesforce data will enable precise calculation of CAC, supporting informed decisions on marketing spend efficiency and return on investment.  
- **Automated and Reliable Reporting Processes:** Batch‑based data ingestion and transformation pipelines will eliminate manual reporting tasks, ensuring consistent, timely, and accurate updates to Power BI dashboards.  
- **Improved Marketing ROI Analysis:** Enhanced L2A and CAC metrics will allow Pure to evaluate campaign performance and allocate resources toward the most effective acquisition channels.  
- **Strengthened Data‑Driven Decision Making:** Leadership will gain a unified, trusted view of pipeline performance, empowering strategic planning and investment decisions grounded in verified data.  
- **Foundation for Scalable Data Maturity:** The solution will establish a scalable reporting framework within Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse, supporting future data sources and long‑term analytics growth.
