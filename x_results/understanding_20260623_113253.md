# Understanding

- Preparing for a near-term exit/sale requires a scalable exit reporting suite to support the M&A process and investor communications.
- There is a lack of investor-ready reporting, with gaps across source systems that may impede clear equity narrative development.
- The existing Qlik Warehouse platform may not fully support all KPIs needed for the equity narrative relevant to the sale.
- Data ingestion from source systems (Finance, CRM, ERP, legacy sources for historical data) is not fully automated, necessitating manual one-off ingestion and recurring refresh processes.
- A prioritised KPI framework, equity narrative definition, dashboard mock-ups, feasibility assessment of data sources, conceptual data model design, and tooling recommendations are all required to underpin decision-making for the exit process.
- Automated data pipelines are needed to facilitate recurring data refreshes for transaction reporting, minimizing manual intervention.
- Senior leadership, exit advisors (e.g., Barclays), and potential acquirers/investors are the primary system users who require investor-grade visibility and clear reporting.
- Mandatory integrations include Finance System, CRM, ERP, and legacy sources (for at least three years of trended data) into the Qlik Warehouse.
- Timeline constraints exist, with stakeholder availability, data access readiness, and the quality/completeness of Qlik Warehouse data impacting project execution.
