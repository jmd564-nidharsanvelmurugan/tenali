# Business Impact

- **Enhanced Marketing ROI Visibility:** The new Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting suite will provide PURE Financial Advisors with granular insights into marketing effectiveness, enabling leadership to allocate spend toward the highest-performing channels and improve ROI tracking.  
- **Data-Driven Growth Decisions:** By integrating Salesforce, QuickBooks, and Tamarac data into a unified model, PURE will gain a single source of truth for pipeline performance, supporting more accurate forecasting and strategic growth planning.  
- **Operational Efficiency Gains:** Automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines will reduce manual reporting effort by an estimated 40–60%, freeing data and marketing teams to focus on analysis and optimization rather than data preparation.  
- **Improved Financial Transparency:** CAC and L2A metrics will allow PURE to quantify the cost of client acquisition and conversion efficiency, supporting more informed decisions on pricing, marketing investment, and client segmentation.  
- **Scalable Reporting Foundation:** The Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse-based architecture will create a scalable data environment capable of supporting future data sources and advanced analytics, ensuring long-term sustainability of PURE’s reporting capabilities.  
- **Investor-Grade Data Asset:** The initiative strengthens PURE’s data maturity and provides Lee Equity Partners with diligence-grade visibility into marketing and pipeline performance, enhancing confidence in PURE’s growth trajectory and valuation potential.  

By delivering these outcomes, this engagement will enable PURE Financial Advisors LLC to transform its pipeline and marketing performance management approach, establishing a robust, scalable data foundation that drives sustained growth, operational efficiency, and long-term value creation in the competitive U.S. financial advisory market.
