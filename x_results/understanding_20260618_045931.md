# Understanding

- Pure Financial Advisors seeks to address limited visibility and granularity in its existing pipeline reporting, particularly around the Lead-to-AUM (L2A) journey and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) metrics.  
- Current reporting lacks integrated CAC insights, preventing a clear understanding of marketing efficiency and return on spend.  
- Data from key systems such as QuickBooks is not yet ingested, creating gaps in financial alignment and hindering complete CAC analysis.  
- Automated data ingestion and transformation workflows are needed to reduce manual effort and ensure consistent, timely reporting.  
- The initiative requires an enhanced L2A and CAC data model capable of supporting detailed Power BI reporting and KPI tracking.  
- Primary users include data and technology stakeholders, senior leadership, and marketing teams who rely on accurate, consolidated insights for decision-making.  
- Mandatory integrations include Salesforce and QuickBooks to unify lead, client, and financial data for comprehensive reporting.
