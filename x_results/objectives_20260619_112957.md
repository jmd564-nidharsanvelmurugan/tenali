# Objectives

- Establish a comprehensive Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data model that delivers granular insights into pipeline performance and marketing ROI.  
- Automate data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes to ensure consistent, timely, and accurate reporting.  
- Provide leadership with clear visibility into the full customer journey from lead generation to assets under management, enabling data-driven decision-making.  
- Enhance Power BI dashboards with standardized KPI definitions and intuitive visualizations to improve user experience and reporting clarity.  
- Build a scalable reporting framework capable of supporting future data sources and evolving business needs, driving long-term data maturity across the organization.
