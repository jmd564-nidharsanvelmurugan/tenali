# Approach

This engagement with Pure Financial LLC is focused on designing and discovering a modern data platform to support the company’s strategic objectives around revenue bridge analysis, pipeline tracking, and operational reporting. Our methodology leverages a value-driven, consultative approach, working closely with key stakeholders to ensure all business requirements are captured while delivering actionable design artifacts. We place a strong emphasis on collaborative discovery, iterative validation, and alignment with industry best practices for data advisory and value creation.

Given the absence of existing data infrastructure, our team will lead Pure Financial LLC through a phased process that begins with in-depth requirement gathering and analysis, followed by data discovery and architectural blueprinting. Each phase is structured to deliver clear, tangible outputs—culminating in a data platform design blueprint and implementation roadmap. Throughout, we will apply proven methodologies tailored for B2C financial services, always ensuring transparency, knowledge transfer, and stakeholder engagement.

---
**Phase 1: Discovery & Requirements, Duration: 2 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 to Week 2**  
**Summary:** Capture business requirements and define the vision for the new data platform.  
**Activities:**  
- Conduct stakeholder interviews and workshops to gather business, operational, and technical requirements  
- Assess current processes around revenue, pipeline, and operational reporting to identify pain points and opportunities  
- Define initial data sources, scope, and target user groups  
- Map key success metrics and expected business outcomes  
- Validate commercial and technical use cases specific to B2C financial services  

**Phase 2: Data Analysis & Exploration, Duration: 2 weeks, Timeline: Week 3 to Week 4**  
**Summary:** Explore available company data and assess readiness for platform design.  
**Activities:**  
- Inventory and profile existing data sources relevant to revenue bridge, pipeline, and operations  
- Evaluate data quality, availability, and integration considerations  
- Identify data gaps and recommend interim data collection or cleansing strategies  
- Develop high-level data entity relationship diagrams  
- Conduct collaborative review sessions to refine data requirements  

**Phase 3: Solution Blueprint & Roadmap, Duration: 2 weeks, Timeline: Week 5 to Week 6**  
**Summary:** Design the recommended data platform and develop the roadmap for implementation.  
**Activities:**  
- Draft a comprehensive data platform architecture tailored to Pure Financial LLC’s commercial objectives  
- Specify recommended technologies, integration components, security, and governance frameworks  
- Define phased implementation roadmap aligned with business priorities  
- Facilitate architecture walkthroughs and gather stakeholder feedback  
- Produce and deliver the final Data Platform Design Blueprint and supporting documentation  

---

By structuring the design and discovery project into these focused phases, we ensure Pure Financial LLC receives a data platform blueprint that is business-aligned, actionable, and primed for value creation. This phased approach reduces risk, supports decision-making with clear milestones, and positions the organization for rapid adoption and long-term success.
