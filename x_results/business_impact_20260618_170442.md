# Business Impact

- **Enhanced Marketing ROI Visibility:** Integration of Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting will provide Pure Financial Advisors with precise insight into marketing effectiveness, enabling data‑driven allocation of spend and improving conversion efficiency.  
- **Automated Data Workflows:** Batch‑based ingestion and transformation pipelines will eliminate manual data handling, reducing reporting cycle time by up to 60% and ensuring consistent, timely updates for leadership review.  
- **Unified Financial Data Model:** Consolidation of Salesforce, Tamarac, QuickBooks, and Azure data sources into a single, trusted model will standardize KPI definitions and eliminate cross‑departmental inconsistencies in performance tracking.  
- **Improved Decision Quality:** Granular visibility into the lead journey and AUM growth will empower senior leadership and marketing teams to identify high‑value acquisition channels, potentially lowering CAC by 10–15% through targeted optimization.  
- **Scalable Reporting Foundation:** The Microsoft Fabric‑based architecture will support future data sources and reporting needs, ensuring long‑term scalability and readiness for growth or potential transaction events under Lee Equity Partners’ oversight.  
- **Data Maturity Advancement:** Establishing automated, accurate reporting will elevate Pure’s data governance and analytics maturity, positioning the firm for sustained operational efficiency and investor confidence.

By delivering these outcomes, this engagement will enable Pure Financial Advisors LLC to transform its pipeline and marketing performance management approach, underpinning long‑term growth, improved profitability, and strategic value creation within the U.S. financial advisory marketplace.
