# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with PURE Financial Advisors LLC, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

1. **KPI Book**  
- Comprehensive documentation of Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) metrics, including definitions, calculation logic, and data source mapping.  
- Clear alignment of KPIs with business objectives and reporting requirements to ensure consistency across dashboards.  
- Inclusion of dimension dictionary outlining how metrics can be sliced by relevant business attributes (e.g., channel, advisor, region).  

2. **Power BI Mock‑ups**  
- Design of up to eight Power BI views illustrating the target‑state L2A and CAC reporting suite.  
- Visualization of key insights, user navigation, and layout structure to guide dashboard development.  
- Mock‑ups will serve as the blueprint for the subsequent build phase, ensuring stakeholder alignment on design and functionality.  

3. **Data Model Design**  
- Detailed schema for the integrated L2A and CAC data model, defining entities, relationships, and data flows across Salesforce, Tamarac, QuickBooks, and Azure Fabric.  
- Documentation of data transformation logic and integration points to support automated ingestion and reporting.  
- Technical specifications to enable scalable and maintainable data architecture aligned with PURE’s existing infrastructure.  

4. **Data Gap Assessment**  
- Identification and evaluation of missing or incomplete data elements required for accurate L2A and CAC reporting.  
- Assessment of data quality, completeness, and consistency across source systems.  
- Recommendations for remediation actions and prioritization to ensure reliable reporting outputs.  

5. **Technical Approach**  
- Detailed description of the proposed integration and automation strategy, including QuickBooks ingestion and transformation workflows.  
- Definition of batch processing pipelines within Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse environments.  
- Outline of technical dependencies, control points, and validation steps to ensure robust data operations.  

6. **Phase 3B Implementation Plan**  
- Structured roadmap detailing activities, timelines, and resource requirements for the build and deployment phase.  
- Sequencing of tasks across data ingestion, model development, and Power BI report creation.  
- Identification of key milestones, dependencies, and risk mitigation actions to support successful execution.  

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with PURE Financial Advisors LLC’s leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
