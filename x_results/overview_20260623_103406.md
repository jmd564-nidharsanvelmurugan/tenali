# Overview

PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC currently relies on the collaborative efforts of its data, technology, and marketing teams to support pipeline and customer acquisition reporting. The organization's operational landscape includes a suite of integrated systems and platforms such as Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, Microsoft Fabric, Azure Synapse, and Power BI. Integration of QuickBooks is underway, with each system contributing to the management and analysis of lead and asset data.

Today, the main reporting and analytics environment at PURE is built around Power BI, leveraging data ingested from Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and soon QuickBooks. The primary cloud and data platform infrastructure consists of Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse, facilitating storage, processing, and pipeline management. Data integration challenges are present, notably in connecting and ingesting from QuickBooks and assessing data quality for customer acquisition cost (CAC) reporting.
