# Understanding

- Limited granularity exists in current pipeline reporting, restricting the ability to analyze the detailed journey from lead generation to assets under management (AUM).
- The current reporting environment lacks integrated, actionable insights into Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), inhibiting transparency around marketing return on investment.
- Enhanced Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and CAC data models are required to enable more comprehensive and segmented reporting.
- Data ingestion and integration from QuickBooks are mandatory to support accurate CAC analysis and unified financial visibility.
- Automated data ingestion and transformation workflows are required for consistent, up-to-date L2A and CAC reporting without reliance on manual data handling.
- Data quality assessments are needed to ensure the reliability of CAC calculations and pipeline analytics.
- Key user groups who will interact with or rely on the solution include data and technology stakeholders, senior leadership, and marketing teams.
- Dependencies exist around timely access to Salesforce, QuickBooks, and the existing Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse data platforms for successful integration and reporting.
