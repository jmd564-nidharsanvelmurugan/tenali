# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with PURE Financial Advisors LLC, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

1. **KPI Book**  
- Comprehensive documentation of Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) metrics, including definitions, calculation logic, and data sources.  
- Clear mapping of KPIs to business objectives and reporting use cases for marketing and leadership teams.  
- Establishment of standardized KPI naming conventions and dimensional hierarchies for consistent reporting.  

2. **Power BI Mock‑ups (up to 8 views)**  
- Design of interactive dashboard mock‑ups illustrating L2A and CAC insights, conversion funnels, and marketing ROI visualizations.  
- Layout and navigation framework defining user experience, data filters, and drill‑down capabilities.  
- Visual prototypes serving as the blueprint for the subsequent build phase within Power BI.  

3. **Data Model Design**  
- Logical and physical data model outlining relationships between Salesforce, Tamarac, QuickBooks, and Azure Fabric data sources.  
- Definition of data entities, attributes, and transformation logic supporting L2A and CAC reporting.  
- Documentation of integration points and data lineage to ensure scalability and maintainability.  

4. **Data Gap Assessment**  
- Identification and evaluation of missing or inconsistent data elements across source systems impacting CAC and pipeline reporting.  
- Recommendations for remediation actions and data quality improvement initiatives.  
- Prioritization of gaps based on business impact and feasibility of resolution.  

5. **Technical Approach Document**  
- Detailed description of the proposed architecture and integration method for QuickBooks ingestion into the existing Azure Fabric environment.  
- Specification of batch processing workflows, data transformation pipelines, and refresh schedules.  
- Outline of governance, validation, and monitoring mechanisms to ensure reliable data operations.  

6. **Phase 3B Implementation Plan**  
- Structured roadmap detailing activities, milestones, and dependencies for the build and deployment phase.  
- Resource and timeline planning aligned with the 8–12‑week implementation window.  
- Risk and dependency matrix highlighting QuickBooks integration and data quality considerations.  

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with PURE Financial Advisors LLC's leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
