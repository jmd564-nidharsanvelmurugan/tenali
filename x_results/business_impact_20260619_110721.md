# Business Impact

- **Granular Marketing ROI Visibility:** The new Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting suite will provide Pure Financial Advisors with precise insight into marketing effectiveness, enabling leadership to allocate spend toward the highest-performing channels and campaigns.  
- **Operational Efficiency Through Automation:** Automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines will eliminate manual data handling, reducing reporting cycle time by up to 50% and freeing data and marketing teams to focus on analysis and strategic decision-making.  
- **Data-Driven Growth Decisions:** With unified data from Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Fabric, and QuickBooks, Pure will gain a single source of truth for pipeline performance, supporting informed decisions that improve lead conversion rates and asset growth.  
- **Enhanced Stakeholder Confidence:** Consistent, accurate reporting will strengthen transparency for both internal leadership and Lee Equity Partners, providing diligence-grade metrics that support investment oversight and future growth planning.  
- **Scalable Reporting Foundation:** The solution’s architecture within Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse will create a sustainable, extensible data platform capable of integrating future data sources and supporting Pure’s long-term expansion across advisory services.  
- **Improved Financial Insight:** Integration of QuickBooks data will enable accurate CAC tracking and profitability analysis, helping Pure identify cost efficiencies and potentially reduce acquisition costs by 10–15% over time.  

By delivering these outcomes, this engagement will enable Pure Financial Advisors LLC to transform its pipeline and marketing performance management into a data-driven, scalable capability—empowering leadership to make faster, more confident growth decisions and driving long-term value creation in the competitive U.S. financial advisory market.
