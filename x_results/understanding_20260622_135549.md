# Understanding

- Limited granularity in the existing Lead-to-AUM pipeline reporting is hindering actionable analysis of pipeline performance and marketing ROI.
- Current reporting does not provide integrated or detailed Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) insights, creating a gap in measuring return on marketing spend.
- The requirement exists for an enhanced Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and CAC data model to support deeper and more effective reporting.
- Power BI reporting suite needs to deliver dedicated L2A and CAC dashboards with clear KPI visualizations and definitions.
- Automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines are needed, particularly for integrating data from QuickBooks, to ensure timely and accurate reporting.
- Mandatory integrations include connecting and ingesting data from both QuickBooks and Salesforce platforms.
- Data and technology teams, senior leadership, and marketing teams are the primary user groups requiring access to the reporting tools and data model.
- There are dependencies and constraints related to accessing Salesforce, QuickBooks, and the existing Fabric and Synapse data platforms, as well as ensuring data quality for accurate CAC calculations.
