# Understanding

- PURE seeks to address limited visibility into the full Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) journey and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) performance, which currently restricts insight into marketing effectiveness and pipeline conversion.  
- Existing pipeline reporting lacks sufficient granularity, preventing leadership from understanding detailed lead progression and return on marketing spend.  
- Data from key systems such as QuickBooks is not yet integrated, creating gaps in CAC reporting and limiting the ability to link financial data with marketing and pipeline metrics.  
- Current processes rely on partially manual data handling and disconnected data sources, reducing efficiency and consistency in reporting.  
- The initiative requires an enhanced L2A and CAC data model, improved Power BI reporting, and automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines.  
- Primary users include data and technology teams responsible for data management, senior leadership seeking performance insights, and marketing teams tracking acquisition efficiency.  
- Mandatory integrations include Salesforce and QuickBooks to ensure complete and accurate data flow across the reporting ecosystem.
