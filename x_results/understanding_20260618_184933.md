# Understanding

- PURE seeks to address limited granularity in existing pipeline reporting and the absence of integrated Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) insights.  
- Current reporting does not provide sufficient visibility into the full Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) journey, limiting understanding of marketing effectiveness.  
- Data ingestion from QuickBooks is not yet established, creating a gap in financial data required for accurate CAC reporting.  
- Automated data ingestion and transformation workflows are needed to reduce manual effort and ensure consistent, timely reporting.  
- The initiative requires an enhanced L2A and CAC data model capable of supporting detailed Power BI reporting.  
- Primary users include data and technology stakeholders, senior leadership, and marketing teams who rely on accurate performance metrics.  
- Mandatory integrations include Salesforce and QuickBooks to enable unified pipeline and cost reporting.
