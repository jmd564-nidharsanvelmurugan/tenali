# Approach  

Our engagement with **Pokemnss**, a US‑based B2C portfolio company, is designed to address the unique challenges of building a scalable data platform from the ground up. As a PE‑backed business with no existing infrastructure, Pokemnss requires a robust foundation to enable consistent, transparent, and automated reporting across its operations. Our approach combines deep due diligence expertise with modern data design principles to ensure the resulting platform supports both immediate reporting needs and long‑term growth objectives.  

We employ a structured, phased methodology that moves from discovery through design and implementation planning. Each phase builds on the insights of the previous one, ensuring alignment with Pokemnss’s business priorities, data realities, and stakeholder expectations. This disciplined progression allows us to translate strategic goals into actionable technical designs and implementation roadmaps that are both practical and scalable.  

**Phase 0: Pre‑Kickoff, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 0**  

**Summary:** Establish the foundation for a seamless project start by ensuring access, documentation, and stakeholder alignment.  

**Activities:**  
- Coordinate with Pokemnss and its PE sponsor to secure access to all relevant systems and data sources.  
- Gather existing documentation on reporting, infrastructure, and system configurations.  
- Schedule workshops and recurring meetings to maintain cadence and transparency.  
- Conduct an initial deep‑dive into any available data repositories or reconciliation sources.  
- Confirm project governance structure and communication protocols.  

**Phase 1: Current Data Landscape & KPI Alignment, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 1**  

**Summary:** Define the key metrics and understand the current data environment to inform the target‑state architecture.  

**Activities:**  
- Facilitate a “Metrics that Matter” workshop to align on core KPIs and growth metrics.  
- Document metric definitions, calculation logic, and data source mappings.  
- Review existing systems and reporting processes to identify constraints and opportunities.  
- Begin outlining the target‑state data architecture and model design.  
- Recommend best‑fit Azure tooling and services for future implementation.  

**Phase 2: Data Source Assessment & Initial Model Design, Duration: 2 weeks, Timeline: Weeks 2–3**  

**Summary:** Assess data availability and begin developing the initial data model to support priority reporting.  

**Activities:**  
- Identify and catalogue all relevant data sources for customer and revenue reporting.  
- Conduct a data gap assessment to determine missing or incomplete data elements.  
- Prioritize data coverage issues and propose remediation or enrichment strategies.  
- Define KPI calculation logic and reconciliation processes.  
- Draft initial dashboard mock‑ups to visualize reporting use cases.  

**Phase 3: Model Refinement & Dashboard Finalization, Duration: 2 weeks, Timeline: Weeks 3–4**  

**Summary:** Refine the data model and finalize dashboard designs through iterative collaboration with management.  

**Activities:**  
- Review and validate initial data model design with Pokemnss stakeholders.  
- Iterate dashboard mock‑ups based on feedback and evolving business requirements.  
- Confirm logic definitions and ensure consistency across metrics and reports.  
- Integrate insights from data gap assessments into model refinement.  
- Prepare documentation to inform implementation planning.  

**Phase 4: Solution Design & Implementation Planning, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 5**  

**Summary:** Finalize the end‑state data model and develop a detailed roadmap for platform build and reporting rollout.  

**Activities:**  
- Confirm the final data model design based on system learnings and stakeholder input.  
- Develop implementation recommendations for the data platform build and augmentation.  
- Provide data cleaning and enrichment recommendations for Phase 2 execution.  
- Define the roadmap for dashboard and reporting build‑out.  
- Present the consolidated design and implementation plan for approval.  

This structured, phased approach ensures that **Pokemnss’s** data platform and reporting capabilities are developed with precision, agility, and a clear focus on delivering measurable business outcomes aligned with investor and management expectations.
