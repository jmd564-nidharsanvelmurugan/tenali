# Understanding

- Pure seeks to address limited visibility and granularity in its existing pipeline reporting, particularly around the Lead-to-AUM (L2A) journey and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) metrics.  
- Current reporting lacks integrated CAC insights, preventing a clear view of marketing efficiency and return on spend.  
- Data from key systems such as QuickBooks is not yet connected, creating gaps in financial and acquisition cost reporting.  
- The absence of automated data ingestion and transformation processes results in manual effort and delayed reporting cycles.  
- Enhanced data modeling is required to support detailed L2A and CAC analysis across Salesforce, Tamarac, and Azure data sources.  
- Automated workflows are needed to streamline data ingestion, transformation, and refresh processes for consistent reporting.  
- Primary users include data and technology teams responsible for data management, senior leadership seeking performance insights, and marketing teams tracking acquisition efficiency.  
- Mandatory integrations include Salesforce and QuickBooks to ensure complete and accurate pipeline and cost data.
