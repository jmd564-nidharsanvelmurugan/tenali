# Understanding

- The client seeks to improve the granularity and depth of Lead-to-AUM (L2A) pipeline and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting.
- Existing pipeline reporting lacks the necessary level of detail and does not provide integrated insights into customer acquisition costs.
- There is a current gap in integrating QuickBooks data into the CAC reporting process and a need to assess data quality for accurate reporting.
- The project requires an enhanced L2A and CAC data model, along with a Power BI reporting suite capable of handling expanded data sets.
- Automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines are necessary to support reliable, up-to-date L2A and CAC reporting.
- Mandatory integration with both QuickBooks and Salesforce is needed to ensure comprehensive and consolidated reporting.
- Primary users of the enhanced reporting and data models include data and technology stakeholders, senior leadership, and marketing teams.
- Success is contingent on reliable access to Salesforce, QuickBooks, and existing Fabric and Synapse data platforms, as well as overcoming constraints related to data access, data quality, and timely QuickBooks integration.
