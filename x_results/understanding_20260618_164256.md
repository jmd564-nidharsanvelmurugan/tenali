# Understanding

- PURE seeks to address limited granularity in existing pipeline reporting and the absence of integrated Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) insights.  
- Current reporting does not provide a clear view of the full Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) journey, limiting visibility into marketing effectiveness and conversion performance.  
- Data ingestion from QuickBooks is not yet established, creating a gap in capturing and analyzing cost data required for CAC reporting.  
- Automated data ingestion and transformation workflows are needed to reduce manual effort and ensure consistent, timely updates to L2A and CAC metrics.  
- The data and technology teams, senior leadership, and marketing users require access to accurate, role‑relevant reporting to support decision‑making.  
- Integration with Salesforce and QuickBooks is mandatory to enable unified reporting across lead, client, and financial data sources.  
- Data quality assessment is required to ensure reliability of inputs used in L2A and CAC calculations.
