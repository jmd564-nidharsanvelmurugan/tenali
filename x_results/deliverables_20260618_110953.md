# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with PURE Financial Advisors LLC, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

1. **KPI Book**  
- Comprehensive documentation of Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) metrics, including definitions, calculation logic, and data source mapping.  
- Clear alignment of KPIs with business objectives and marketing ROI measurement.  
- Inclusion of dimension dictionary for slicing and analyzing performance across relevant business segments.  

2. **Power BI Mock‑ups (up to 8 views)**  
- Design of visual prototypes illustrating the future‑state L2A and CAC dashboards.  
- Layouts capturing key insights, user navigation, and KPI visualization standards.  
- Mock‑ups serving as the blueprint for the subsequent build phase within Power BI.  

3. **Data Model Design**  
- Detailed schema outlining relationships between Salesforce, Tamarac, QuickBooks, and Azure data sources.  
- Logical and physical data model documentation supporting automated ingestion and transformation pipelines.  
- Definition of data structures required for scalable L2A and CAC reporting.  

4. **Data Gap Assessment**  
- Identification and evaluation of missing or inconsistent data elements across source systems.  
- Recommendations for remediation and data quality improvement to enable accurate CAC reporting.  
- Prioritization of data gaps based on impact to reporting accuracy and feasibility of resolution.  

5. **Technical Approach Document**  
- Detailed description of integration methodology for QuickBooks and other source systems within Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse.  
- Specification of batch processing workflows, data transformation logic, and refresh automation.  
- Outline of technical dependencies, control points, and governance considerations for implementation.  

6. **Phase 3B Implementation Plan**  
- Structured roadmap detailing activities, milestones, and timelines for the build and deployment phase.  
- Resource plan and sequencing aligned with dependencies on QuickBooks ingestion and data quality assessment.  
- Clear delineation of in‑scope and out‑of‑scope items to maintain project focus and delivery efficiency.  

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with PURE Financial Advisors LLC's leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
