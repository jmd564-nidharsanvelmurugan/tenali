# Approach

This engagement with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC is designed to deliver an enhanced Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data model and a comprehensive Power BI reporting suite. These efforts will empower stakeholders with actionable insights into their business pipeline, marketing ROI, and client retention strategies, while promoting data-driven decision-making. All workstreams are aligned to support automation, data integration, and reporting scalability on the Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse platforms, ensuring the solution will meet both current and future-state requirements.

Our methodology is structured into two key phases: Design (Phase 3A) and Build (Phase 3B). The Design phase focuses on clarifying requirements, architecting the solution, and aligning stakeholders on KPI definitions and reporting logic. The Build phase implements the approved architecture, integrating necessary data sources (including QuickBooks), automating data pipelines, building Power BI dashboards, and enabling churn prevention analytics. Each phase concludes with clear, actionable deliverables and provides sign-off points to ensure alignment with business goals.

---

**Phase 3A: Design & Blueprinting, Duration: 3 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 to Week 3**  
**Summary:** This phase defines requirements, details the data model and KPIs, and produces mock-ups and the technical implementation roadmap.

**Activities:**  
- Host discovery workshops with data, technology, and marketing teams to capture detailed reporting and integration requirements  
- Develop a comprehensive KPI Book defining L2A, CAC, and churn prevention metrics, with detailed calculation logic and mapped data sources  
- Produce Power BI mock-ups (up to 8 views) to validate dashboard requirements and visualizations with stakeholders  
- Design the L2A and CAC data model, including entity relationships, source mappings, and update/refresh strategies  
- Assess data quality and identify gaps, particularly for QuickBooks integration and reporting granularity  
- Prepare the Phase 3B Implementation Plan, outlining timelines, dependencies, and testing criteria

---

**Phase 3B: Build & Implementation, Duration: 8–12 weeks, Timeline: Week 4 to Week 15**  
**Summary:** This phase delivers the integrated data model, automated data pipelines, Power BI suite, and churn prevention analytics in a scalable production environment.

**Activities:**  
- Establish secure, batch-based connectors and automated data ingestion pipelines for Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks into Microsoft Fabric  
- Implement the approved L2A and CAC data model, including data transformation and harmonization processes in Azure Synapse  
- Develop and deploy the Power BI reporting suite with 8 interactive dashboards, ensuring alignment with the KPI Book and mock-ups  
- Build and validate churn prevention analytics as part of the Power BI suite, enabling early identification of at-risk clients and proactive engagement  
- Conduct data quality monitoring, validation, and user acceptance testing to ensure reliable insights and accuracy of metrics  
- Deliver solution documentation, technical handover, and conduct end-user training for data, marketing, and leadership teams

---

This phased approach ensures PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC realizes enhanced visibility into its client journey and marketing effectiveness, with a robust and scalable data platform supporting future analytics needs. By focusing on clear deliverables and incorporating churn prevention analytics, the proposed methodology not only strengthens reporting and decision support today, but also establishes a foundation for sustained, data-driven growth.
