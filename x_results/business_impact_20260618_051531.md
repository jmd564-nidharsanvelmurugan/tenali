# Business Impact

- **Granular Marketing ROI Visibility:** Enhanced Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting will provide Pure Financial Advisors with precise insight into marketing effectiveness, enabling data‑driven allocation of spend and improved return on marketing investment.  
- **Operational Efficiency Through Automation:** Automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines will eliminate manual reporting processes, reducing reporting cycle times and freeing up analyst capacity for higher‑value analysis.  
- **Unified Data Model for Decision‑Making:** A consolidated L2A and CAC data model across Salesforce, QuickBooks, and Tamarac will establish a single source of truth, improving confidence in performance metrics and supporting consistent executive reporting.  
- **Improved Conversion and Growth Tracking:** Granular visibility into the lead journey from initial contact to AUM conversion will allow leadership to identify bottlenecks and optimize conversion strategies, directly supporting revenue growth.  
- **Scalable Reporting Foundation:** The Microsoft Fabric‑based data platform and Power BI suite will create a scalable foundation capable of integrating future data sources, ensuring long‑term adaptability as Pure expands its client base and marketing channels.  
- **Enhanced Stakeholder Transparency:** Clear, standardized KPI definitions and dashboards will strengthen communication with internal leadership and investment partners, supporting informed strategic decisions and portfolio oversight.  

By delivering these outcomes, this engagement will enable Pure Financial Advisors LLC to transform its marketing and pipeline reporting into a strategic growth engine—driving operational efficiency, improving marketing ROI, and establishing a scalable data foundation that supports long‑term value creation in the competitive U.S. financial advisory market.
