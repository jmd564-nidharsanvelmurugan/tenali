# Outcomes

This engagement will deliver a measurable transformation in VONQ's M&A readiness by establishing a robust, scalable exit reporting suite. Through a validated design aligned to investor requirements, leadership and key stakeholders will gain the tools needed to confidently present data-backed equity narratives, streamline reporting workflows, and accelerate the transaction process. The outcomes are designed to minimize delays, enable data-driven insights, and ensure that the reporting infrastructure becomes a lasting asset for the business.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Investor-Ready KPI Framework:** A comprehensive set of prioritized KPIs, aligned to equity narratives, providing clear visibility into commercial, financial, and operational metrics required for investor reporting and due diligence.

- **Validated Exit Reporting Design:** Completion and sign-off of design artefacts—including dashboard mock-ups and data model—ensuring readiness to guide Phase 2 build and support timely stakeholder communications throughout the exit process.

- **Data Gap Identification and Remediation Priorities:** Explicit mapping of data gaps across core and legacy systems, enabling targeted remediation efforts and elevating data quality for investor scrutiny.

- **Automated Data Ingestion and Refresh Processes:** Design of refreshable batch data pipelines from key source systems into the Qlik Warehouse, reducing manual intervention and ensuring consistent, up-to-date transaction reporting.

- **Improved Stakeholder Confidence:** Enhanced ability to present credible, data-backed equity narratives to acquirers and advisors, minimizing reputational risk and maximizing transaction value.

- **Scalable Reporting Infrastructure:** Establishment of a conceptual data model and reporting suite that are extensible to additional data sources and automatable post-transaction, enabling future scalability and business growth.
