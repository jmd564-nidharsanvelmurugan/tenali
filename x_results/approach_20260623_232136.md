# Approach

PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC has engaged our team to elevate sales pipeline and marketing analytics through the development of an enhanced Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data model and reporting suite. Our methodology is rooted in close stakeholder collaboration, rigorous data assessment, and rapid, iterative design—ensuring the solution delivers actionable insights for data, technology, and marketing teams while aligning with the firm’s growth strategy. Emphasis will be placed on seamless integration with key systems such as Salesforce, Tamarac, QuickBooks, Azure Blob Storage, and the Microsoft Fabric data platform.

The engagement will follow a two-phased approach, balancing agility and precision. Phase 3A will focus on design discovery, integration planning, and foundational data modeling. Phase 3B will transition to building the automated solution, iterating Power BI dashboards, and enabling self-service analytics. This structure ensures that deliverables—including KPI Book, mock-ups, data model, and technical plans—are precisely tailored to PURE’s objectives, timeline, and technology ecosystem.

**Phase 3A: Design & Discovery, Duration: 3 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 to Week 3**  
**Summary:** Establish solution foundations through business alignment, requirements gathering, and technical and data discovery.

**Activities:**  
- Conduct stakeholder workshops to confirm reporting requirements, L2A and CAC definitions, and business priorities  
- Perform assessment of available data in Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks to ensure completeness and quality  
- Develop draft KPI Book with clear metric logic, sources, and reporting requirements  
- Prepare low-fidelity Power BI mock-ups (up to 8 views) to visualize user experience and KPI presentation  
- Document technical integration approach for QuickBooks and Salesforce, including data flow mapping and dependencies  
- Deliver Data Gap Assessment identifying potential challenges and remediation strategies

**Phase 3B: Build & Implementation, Duration: 8–12 weeks, Timeline: Week 4 to Week 15**  
**Summary:** Build the L2A and CAC data model, automate integration pipelines, and deliver enhanced reporting in Power BI.

**Activities:**  
- Design, develop, and validate the L2A and CAC data model integrated with data from Salesforce, Tamarac, QuickBooks, and Azure  
- Build automated batch data ingestion and transformation pipelines within the Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse environment  
- Integrate QuickBooks data and address identified data quality issues for robust CAC reporting  
- Develop and refine an 8-page Power BI reporting suite with clear KPI definitions, advanced visualizations, and user-centric workflows  
- Facilitate user acceptance testing (UAT) and incorporate stakeholder feedback  
- Finalize and deliver all documentation: technical approach, KPI Book, data model architecture, and Phase 3B Implementation Plan

The phased approach provides a structured yet adaptive framework to deliver a future-ready analytics platform that enables PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC to make data-driven decisions with enhanced transparency, efficiency, and scalability. By focusing on business needs and leveraging best-in-class data technologies, this methodology will ensure a seamless journey from requirements discovery through to implementation of a sustainable reporting solution.
