# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with PURE Financial Advisors LLC, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

1. **KPI Book**  
- Comprehensive documentation of Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) KPIs, including definitions, calculation logic, and data source mapping.  

- Alignment of KPI taxonomy with industry standards and PURE’s internal reporting framework to ensure consistency across dashboards.  

- Inclusion of dimension dictionary outlining key attributes for slicing and analyzing performance metrics.  

2. **Power BI Mock‑Ups (up to 8 views)**  
- Design of visual prototypes for the L2A and CAC reporting suite, capturing layout, structure, and functionality requirements.  

- Representation of priority metrics and insights to guide user experience and dashboard development.  

- Validation sessions with PURE’s data and marketing teams to confirm usability and alignment with reporting needs.  

3. **Data Model Design**  
- Detailed schema for the L2A and CAC data model, defining entities, relationships, and data flows across Salesforce, Tamarac, QuickBooks, and Azure Fabric.  

- Documentation of transformation logic and data lineage to support automated ingestion and reporting.  

- Scalability considerations to accommodate future data sources and reporting enhancements.  

4. **Data Gap Assessment**  
- Evaluation of current data availability, completeness, and quality across all relevant systems.  

- Identification of missing or inconsistent data elements impacting L2A and CAC reporting accuracy.  

- Recommendations for remediation and data quality improvement actions.  

5. **Technical Approach Document**  
- Detailed description of the technical architecture and integration strategy, including QuickBooks ingestion and transformation pipelines.  

- Specification of batch processing workflows within Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse environments.  

- Outline of automation, refresh cadence, and governance considerations for sustained reporting performance.  

6. **Phase 3B Implementation Plan**  
- Structured roadmap detailing activities, milestones, and dependencies for the build and deployment phase.  

- Resource and timeline planning aligned with the 8–12‑week implementation window.  

- Risk and dependency tracking to ensure smooth transition from design to execution.  

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with PURE Financial Advisors LLC's leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
