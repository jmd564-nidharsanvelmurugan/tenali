# Objectives

- Establish a comprehensive Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data model that delivers granular visibility into the full client acquisition journey.  
- Provide leadership with actionable insights into marketing performance and return on investment through enhanced Power BI reporting.  
- Automate data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes to ensure consistent, timely, and accurate insights.  
- Improve user experience with intuitive dashboards, standardized KPI definitions, and clear visualizations that support data-driven decision-making.  
- Build a scalable reporting framework capable of integrating additional data sources and supporting PURE’s long-term data maturity and growth objectives.
