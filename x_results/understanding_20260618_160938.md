# Understanding

- Pure Financial Advisors seeks to address limited visibility and granularity in its existing pipeline reporting, particularly around the Lead-to-AUM (L2A) journey and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) metrics.  
- Current reporting lacks integrated CAC insights, preventing a clear understanding of marketing efficiency and return on spend.  
- Data ingestion and integration challenges exist, especially with connecting QuickBooks to the existing data ecosystem for accurate CAC reporting.  
- The firm requires an enhanced L2A and CAC data model to unify data from Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks.  
- Automated data ingestion and transformation workflows are needed to reduce manual effort and ensure consistent, timely reporting.  
- Primary users include data and technology stakeholders, senior leadership, and marketing teams who rely on accurate, consolidated insights.  
- Mandatory integrations include Salesforce and QuickBooks to enable end-to-end visibility across lead, financial, and marketing data.
