# Objectives

- Achieve a comprehensive Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data model and reporting suite that delivers granular insights into pipeline performance and marketing ROI.
- Automate data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes to ensure consistent, up-to-date analytics.
- Enable leadership to gain a detailed understanding of the journey from lead to AUM and the effectiveness of marketing expenditures.
- Enhance Power BI dashboards with clearly defined KPIs and improved visualizations for intuitive user experience across business stakeholders.
- Improve pipeline reporting and customer acquisition cost tracking to support data-driven decision making.
- Establish scalable reporting capabilities and a mature data platform to accommodate future growth and evolving data sources.
- Ensure seamless integration of the reporting and data platform with Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Microsoft Fabric for unified analysis and insights.
