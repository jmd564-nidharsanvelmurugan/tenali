# Objectives

- Deliver a comprehensive Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data model and reporting suite that provides granular insights into pipeline performance and marketing ROI.
- Automate data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes to ensure timely and accurate information.
- Empower leadership with detailed visibility into the customer journey from lead to AUM and clear metrics on marketing effectiveness.
- Enhance user experience through improved Power BI dashboards featuring clear KPI definitions and interactive visualizations.
- Advance the maturity and scalability of PURE’s reporting capabilities to support future data sources and evolving analytical needs.
- Ensure seamless integration with Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Microsoft Fabric to enable unified and reliable data flows.
