# Approach

PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC is undertaking this engagement to advance its pipeline and customer acquisition cost (CAC) reporting by developing a granular Lead-to-AUM (L2A) data model and automating critical data processes. Our methodology combines structured discovery, agile design, and rigorous implementation to provide actionable insights and scalable reporting, ensuring data-driven decisions guide marketing effectiveness and operational growth.

The project will be delivered across two defined phases, each sequenced to maximize impact and minimize disruption. Activities are mapped precisely to PURE’s systems, business objectives, and stakeholder needs. Throughout, emphasis will be placed on detailed data modeling, robust automation, and actionable Power BI dashboards, with regular stakeholder alignment and expert oversight.

**Phase 3A: Discovery & Design, Duration: 3 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 to Week 3**  
**Summary:** Establish the foundation for enhanced L2A and CAC reporting through requirements gathering, data modeling, and design of reporting artifacts.  
**Activities:**  
- Conduct discovery workshops with data, marketing, and technology teams to gather requirements  
- Evaluate existing data sources (Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, QuickBooks) for relevance, accessibility, and quality  
- Design a comprehensive L2A and CAC data model tailored to PURE’s pipeline analytics objectives  
- Document KPI logic in a KPI Book including business-validated formulas and operational definitions  
- Create wireframes and mock-ups for the 8-page Power BI reporting suite with focus on critical KPIs and conversion metrics  
- Prepare a data gap assessment and technical approach roadmap for QuickBooks integration

**Phase 3B: Implementation & Integration, Duration: 8–12 weeks, Timeline: Week 4 to Week 15**  
**Summary:** Build, integrate, and automate the data pipeline and reporting suite, ensuring seamless access to quality insights for leadership and marketing teams.  
**Activities:**  
- Develop automated batch data ingestion pipelines connecting Salesforce, Tamarac, QuickBooks, Azure Blob Storage, and Microsoft Fabric  
- Execute integration of QuickBooks financials with PURE’s pipeline reporting ecosystem  
- Implement data transformation and quality assessment processes to assure reliability for CAC calculation  
- Deploy the L2A and CAC data model within Azure Synapse and Fabric platforms  
- Build and refine the Power BI dashboards with clear visualizations, KPI tracking, and user personas in mind  
- Prepare and deliver the Phase 3B Implementation Plan and walkthrough with key stakeholders

This phased approach is designed to provide PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC with a scalable, future-proof data analytics foundation. By focusing first on detailed requirements and then on robust implementation, the engagement ensures that all deliverables—from data models and KPI books to automated pipelines and Power BI dashboards—are aligned with business goals and unlock actionable value across the organization.
