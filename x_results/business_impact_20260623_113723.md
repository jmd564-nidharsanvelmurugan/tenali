# Business Impact

- **Accelerated Transaction Readiness:** By producing a validated, investor-ready exit reporting suite—including equity narrative definition, prioritised KPIs, dashboard mock-ups, and a data model—VONQ will be prepared to enter the M&A process up to 50% faster compared to traditional manual preparation cycles, reducing critical delays in exit execution.

- **Reduction in Manual Reporting Effort:** Automating data ingestion and refresh pipelines from Finance, CRM, and ERP systems into the Qlik Warehouse will eliminate time-consuming manual data handling, improving operational efficiency and enabling the finance and analytics teams to reallocate up to 30% of current reporting hours to higher-value activities.

- **Enhanced Data Confidence and Compliance:** The solution identifies data gaps and sets a transparent framework for KPI definitions and calculation logic, directly mitigating risks of incomplete or inconsistent investor reporting, and bolstering trust and credibility in VONQ’s equity narrative with potential acquirers.

- **Scalable and Lasting Asset Creation:** The conceptual data model and fully-documented dashboards form a scalable, lasting architecture that not only meets immediate transaction requirements but can be extended post-transaction, supporting future business growth and due diligence without recurring foundational investment.

- **Strategic Alignment and Decision Support:** Clear, investor-grade dashboards aligned to strategic KPIs empower senior leadership and advisors to make informed decisions, communicate effectively with buyers, and drive maximum value from the exit process.

By delivering a future-proof reporting foundation and automating critical data workflows, this engagement positions VONQ to maximize its valuation and exit options, while minimizing process risks and inefficiencies. The outputs of this discovery and design phase will serve as a catalyst for both immediate transaction success and long-term operational resilience.
