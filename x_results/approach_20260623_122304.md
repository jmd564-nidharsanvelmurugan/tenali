# Approach

This engagement with VONQ aims to deliver a comprehensive exit reporting suite, underpinning the upcoming M&A sale process and investor communications. Our methodology is grounded in rapid, structured discovery and design, centering around business stakeholder workshops, current-state analysis, and collaborative solution definition. By focusing on actionable design artifacts—such as equity narratives, KPI frameworks, dashboard mock-ups, and a scalable conceptual data model—we enable VONQ’s leadership and advisors to confidently initiate Phase 2 technical implementation aligned to exit objectives.

Our approach leverages deep expertise in data-driven reporting, SaaS operations, and transaction readiness. Throughout the engagement, we will work closely with VONQ’s finance, commercial, data/analytics, and senior leadership teams to ensure all relevant data sources (Finance, CRM, ERP, legacy datasets) are considered. Activities are structured to maximize speed, quality, and stakeholder input within a defined four-week timeline, culminating in thorough recommendations and a clear implementation roadmap for subsequent build.

---

**Phase 1: Discovery & Design, Duration: 4 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 (29 June 2026) to Week 4 (24 July 2026)**  
**Summary:** Define the equity narrative, prioritize KPIs, design dashboard mock-ups, assess data feasibility, architect a conceptual data model, and produce tooling recommendations and a Phase 2 implementation roadmap.

**Activities:**  
- Conduct stakeholder workshops to define equity narratives and capture transaction priorities  
- Develop a prioritized “Metrics That Matter” KPI framework and document calculation logic  
- Create static dashboard mock-ups illustrating proposed metrics, layout, and navigation structure  
- Analyze current-state data sources and perform a feasibility and gap assessment (Finance, CRM, ERP, Qlik Warehouse, and legacy datasets)  
- Design a conceptual data model using Qlik Warehouse as a baseline, addressing scalability and refresh requirements  
- Evaluate data infrastructure and supplementary tooling options (including pros/cons), and produce an actionable implementation roadmap for Phase 2

---

This phased approach ensures VONQ receives investor-ready reporting design aligned to equity narratives and transaction requirements within the exit timeline. The structured methodology delivers clarity, addresses data gaps, and de-risks subsequent technical implementation—supporting a seamless transition to Phase 2 and maximizing value realization for stakeholders and advisors.
