# Understanding

- The client faces a critical need to design and build a scalable exit reporting suite—including a refreshable data cube, dashboards, and supporting documentation—to support the M&A sale process and facilitate effective investor communications.
- A primary pain point is the lack of investor-ready reporting, resulting in insufficient visibility for acquirers and advisors during the transaction.
- There are potential data gaps across source systems, with the existing Qlik Warehouse not fully supporting all required KPIs necessary for the equity narrative.
- Mandatory integration is required between the Finance System, CRM, ERP, and Qlik Warehouse; legacy data sources must also be included to provide at least three years of trended data.
- Some source systems do not have automated connections to the Qlik Warehouse, requiring manual one-off ingestion and the establishment of recurring refresh processes for those data sources needing regular update during the transaction.
- The solution must enable equity narrative definition, a prioritised KPI framework, dashboard mock-ups, data feasibility assessment, conceptual data model design, tooling recommendations, and a phased implementation roadmap.
- Senior leadership, exit advisors (Barclays), and potential acquirers/investors are the primary user roles requiring access to and use of the reporting suite.
- Timely availability and readiness of data, as well as alignment of dashboards and metrics to investor expectations, are essential due to the tight exit timeline and stakeholder dependencies.
