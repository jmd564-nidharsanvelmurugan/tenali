# Approach

VONQ’s engagement is structured to deliver a robust, investor-grade exit reporting suite that supports the planned sale process and enhances stakeholder communications. Our methodology is tailored for mid-market B2B SaaS environments and prioritizes rapid, high-quality delivery of design artifacts essential for a seamless Phase 2 implementation. Leveraging a blend of targeted workshops, stakeholder engagement, and best practices in data and reporting design, we ensure that each activity aligns with the exit timeline and addresses the unique requirements of commercial, financial, and operational reporting.

Our phased approach begins with an intensive Discovery & Design phase, focusing on defining equity narratives, KPI frameworks, dashboard mock-ups, and a conceptual data model. Throughout the engagement, we collaborate closely with VONQ stakeholders, Barclays (advisors), and JMANGROUP to validate findings, mitigate risks, and ensure all deliverables are actionable. Technology choices and processes will be continuously evaluated for extensibility and future automation, using the existing Qlik Warehouse as the central data platform, with Finance, CRM, ERP, and other mutually agreed sources in scope.

---

**Phase 1: Discovery & Design, Duration: 4 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 (29 June) to Week 4 (24 July 2026)**  
**Summary:** Define the exit reporting suite’s equity narratives, prioritized KPI framework, dashboard mock-ups, data model, tool recommendations, and implementation roadmap, laying a solid foundation for subsequent technical build-out.

**Activities:**  
- Conduct stakeholder workshops to define equity narratives and preliminary “Metrics that Matter” KPI framework  
- Inventory and assess data sources (Finance, CRM, ERP, Qlik Warehouse, legacy/Excel files), documenting data quality and gaps  
- Develop static dashboard mock-ups, including navigation structure, KPI calculation logic, and approval pathways  
- Design a high-level conceptual data model leveraging Qlik Warehouse, detailing major integration points and source-to-target mappings  
- Evaluate and recommend data tooling and infrastructure options, with detailed pros, cons, and future scalability considerations  
- Develop a phased implementation roadmap that outlines use cases for Phase 2, dependencies, risks, and key milestones  

---

**Concluding Paragraph**

This structured, collaborative approach ensures VONQ receives decision-ready design assets that de-risk the sale process and equip leadership with clear, aligned narratives for investors. By systematically addressing business drivers, data feasibility, and technical integration early, we lay the groundwork for an accelerated and successful implementation in Phase 2—enabling VONQ to meet tight exit timelines while building enduring business value.
