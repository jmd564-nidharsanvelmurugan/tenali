# Understanding

- Limited granularity in current pipeline reporting and lack of integrated Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) insights hinder marketing and operational decision-making.
- Existing reporting does not provide detailed visibility into the entire journey from lead generation to assets under management (AUM).
- The current process lacks an automated data ingestion pipeline from QuickBooks, creating gaps in CAC calculation and reporting accuracy.
- Data quality assessment is required to ensure reliable metrics, particularly for CAC reporting.
- Enhanced data models for Lead-to-AUM and CAC are needed to accommodate advanced analytics and more actionable insights.
- Integration with both Salesforce and QuickBooks is mandatory to capture and unify all relevant pipeline and financial data.
- Automation of data ingestion and transformation pipelines is required to support timely, consistent, and scalable reporting.
- The new reporting solution must serve data and technology stakeholders, senior leadership, and marketing teams for improved decision support.
