# Outcomes

The engagement with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC will deliver transformative improvements in the visibility, accuracy, and actionability of pipeline and marketing analytics. By establishing advanced Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting, the project will empower stakeholders with precise, timely insights to drive data-informed decisions and optimize the client’s marketing investments and operational strategies.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Actionable Pipeline Insights:** Granular L2A reporting will enable leadership to closely monitor and analyze the progression of leads through the funnel, supporting targeted interventions to accelerate conversion to assets under management.
- **Optimized Marketing ROI:** Advanced CAC metrics and integrated reporting will provide clear, quantifiable insights into the effectiveness of marketing spend, enabling data-driven allocation of resources for maximum impact.
- **Automated, Reliable Analytics:** Full automation of data ingestion, transformation, and reporting processes will ensure stakeholders have access to continuously up-to-date and trustworthy analytics without manual intervention.
- **Improved Decision-Making:** Comprehensive dashboards in Power BI, with clearly defined KPIs, will allow business and marketing teams to make faster, well-informed decisions based on real-time data views.
- **Scalable Reporting Capabilities:** The foundation established by this initiative will support future data integration and enhanced reporting needs, ensuring PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC can easily scale analytics as the business grows.
- **Reduced Risk from Data Blind Spots:** Enhanced and accurate reporting will address prior gaps in visibility over pipeline and marketing performance, reducing the risk of missed opportunities or ineffective strategy execution.
