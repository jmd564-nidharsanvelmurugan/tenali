# Objectives

- Enable data-driven decision making to reduce customer churn in the direct-to-consumer model.
- Provide actionable insights for leadership to understand and address churn drivers.
- Improve operational workflows for managing customer retention using advanced data science.
- Facilitate seamless integration of the solution with existing data infrastructure.
- Support scalability to accommodate growth and evolving business needs.
- Enhance user experience for internal teams utilizing churn analytics and data advisory tools.
