# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

1. **KPI Book**
   - Comprehensive documentation of all metrics and Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting.
   - Detailed definitions, calculation logic, and business context for each KPI.
   - Delivered in electronic document format (PDF/Word), serving as the reference standard for all metrics.
   - Delivered at the end of the initial discovery phase (Phase 3A).

2. **Power BI Mock-ups**
   - Visual prototypes of the enhanced L2A and CAC dashboards, limited to eight distinct report views.
   - Includes proposed layouts, data visualizations, and user workflows for key reporting scenarios.
   - Provided in interactive Power BI format and supplementary image snapshots (PDF/PNG).
   - Delivered iteratively by the midpoint and completion of Phase 3A.

3. **Data Model Design**
   - Detailed conceptual and logical data models for enhanced L2A and CAC reporting.
   - Entity-relationship diagrams, data flow designs, and data mapping documents.
   - Delivered in diagrammatic (Visio or similar tool) and documentation formats.
   - Draft delivered during Phase 3A, with final version at phase close.

4. **Data Gap Assessment**
   - Identification and analysis of any gaps in current data sources, including completeness, quality, and integrity specifically for QuickBooks, Salesforce, and other relevant systems.
   - Recommendations for addressing data issues and mitigating integration risks.
   - Delivered as a written report (PDF/Word).
   - Provided by the end of Phase 3A.

5. **Technical Approach**
   - Comprehensive guidance on solution architecture, technology integration, and automation strategies for data ingestion and transformation from QuickBooks and Salesforce into Microsoft Fabric.
   - Detailed technical requirements, integration flowcharts, and implementation considerations.
   - Delivered as a technical design document (PDF/Word).
   - Completed by the conclusion of Phase 3A.

6. **Phase 3B Implementation Plan**
   - Sequenced roadmap outlining activities, milestones, roles, and dependencies for the build and rollout of the new L2A and CAC reporting solution.
   - Includes timelines, resource allocation, risk mitigation steps, and change management considerations.
   - Delivered as a project plan document (PDF/Word or MS Project format).
   - Presented at the close of Phase 3A for client review and alignment prior to Phase 3B kickoff.

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC’s leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
