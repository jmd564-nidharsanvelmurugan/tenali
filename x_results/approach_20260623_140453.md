# Approach

PURE Financial Advisors LLC has identified the need to enhance its Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting to drive greater transparency, data-driven decision making, and improved marketing ROI. Our approach leverages a structured, phased methodology rooted in best practices for data model design, data integration, and advanced analytics dashboarding. We ensure all enhancements are seamlessly delivered on the client’s existing Azure, Microsoft Fabric, Azure Synapse, and Power BI platforms, with integration to Salesforce and QuickBooks, enabling a holistic and scalable solution.

This engagement will be conducted through two tightly scoped phases aligned with PURE’s strategic priorities and dependencies: initial Discovery & Design (Phase 3A) and Implementation & Automation (Phase 3B). Each phase is informed by clearly defined timelines and deliverables, ensuring appropriate stakeholder involvement, early validation, and business continuity throughout the project lifecycle. Our methodology emphasizes collaborative workshops, incremental validation, transparent documentation, and technical rigor to deliver actionable insights into every step of the client journey and associated marketing cost.

---

**Phase 3A: Discovery & Design, Duration: 3 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 to Week 3**  
**Summary:** Define requirements, assess data readiness, and create the foundational reporting and data model design for enhanced L2A and CAC analytics.

**Activities:**  
- Facilitate stakeholder workshops to finalize detailed reporting requirements for L2A and CAC.
- Assess data sources (Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, QuickBooks) and conduct a comprehensive Data Gap Assessment.
- Design the conceptual and logical data model supporting L2A and CAC reporting.
- Document KPI definitions, transformation logic, and reporting requirements in a KPI Book.
- Produce high-fidelity Power BI dashboard mock-ups (up to 8 views) to validate visualization needs.
- Detail the technical integration approach, especially for QuickBooks ingestion and data quality assessment.

---

**Phase 3B: Implementation & Automation, Duration: 8–12 weeks, Timeline: Week 4 to Week 15**  
**Summary:** Build, test, and automate the end-to-end data ingestion, transformation, and Power BI reporting suite for L2A and CAC analyses.

**Activities:**  
- Develop and operationalize automated batch ingest pipelines from Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Azure Blob Storage using Microsoft Fabric.
- Implement the designed L2A and CAC data model within Azure Synapse and ensure data quality monitoring.
- Build, iterate, and validate the 8-page Power BI reporting suite as per approved wireframes.
- Enable automated data transformation and report refresh workflows for near real-time analytics.
- Conduct user acceptance testing sessions with data, marketing, and leadership stakeholders.
- Prepare comprehensive technical and operational documentation, and deliver a detailed Phase 3B Implementation Plan and roadmap.

---

By segmenting the engagement into these defined phases, PURE Financial Advisors LLC will benefit from clear project milestones, early visibility of key reporting assets, and reduced implementation risk. This phased approach ensures that business users and leadership gain critical, actionable insights into pipeline performance and marketing effectiveness, while the technical foundation remains extensible and scalable for future analytics needs.
