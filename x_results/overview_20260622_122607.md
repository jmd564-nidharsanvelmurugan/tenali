# Overview

The Data, Technology, and Marketing teams at PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC are directly engaged in managing the firm’s pipeline and customer acquisition reporting. These processes leverage a range of systems and platforms including Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, Microsoft Fabric, Azure Synapse, and Power BI, with ongoing efforts to integrate QuickBooks into the data environment. 

The existing reporting and analytics needs are primarily supported by the Power BI reporting suite. Data for current reporting is sourced from Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks. The firm’s cloud and data infrastructure is built on Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse platforms. At this time, a requirement to connect and ingest data from QuickBooks has been identified, along with the need to assess incoming data quality to support comprehensive customer acquisition cost reporting. No manual processes or specific performance issues are noted in the client’s documentation.
