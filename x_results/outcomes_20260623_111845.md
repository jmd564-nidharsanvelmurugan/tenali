# Outcomes

This engagement will empower VONQ to confidently navigate its upcoming exit process by establishing a robust, investor-ready reporting suite. By aligning data, KPIs, and dashboards to the equity narrative, VONQ will strengthen credibility with acquirers and advisors, mitigate transaction risks, and accelerate stakeholder communications. The measurable impact will be seen through standardized, automated reporting that supports informed decision-making and seamless transition to the next transaction phase.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Investor-Ready Reporting Design:** A validated and scalable exit reporting framework, underpinned by clear equity narratives and prioritised KPIs, will enable transparent communication with potential acquirers and advisors.

- **Actionable Data Gap Analysis:** Identification and prioritisation of data gaps will guide remediation efforts, ensuring all critical financial, commercial, and operational data is available and reliable for investor scrutiny.

- **Improved Stakeholder Confidence:** Transparent, intuitive dashboards and well-defined KPI logic will provide senior leadership and advisors with consistent, trusted visibility into business performance, reducing the risk of delays and reputational concerns.

- **Accelerated Transaction Readiness:** Automated, refreshable data pipelines and a detailed implementation roadmap will expedite the exit process, allowing VONQ to meet tight transaction timelines and requirements.

- **Scalable Post-Transaction Capability:** The future-state reporting suite and data model will serve as lasting business assets, designed for ongoing scalability and automation beyond the immediate M&A process.

- **Mitigation of Exit-Related Risks:** By delivering standardized, investor-grade reporting and process automation, VONQ will minimize risks related to data credibility, inefficient manual processes, and incomplete KPI coverage during the sale process.
