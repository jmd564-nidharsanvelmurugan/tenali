# Approach

This engagement with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC is focused on designing and building an enhanced Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting solution, leveraging the client’s existing Microsoft Fabric, Azure Synapse, and Power BI infrastructure. Our methodology combines stakeholder collaboration, modern data integration, and agile report design to deliver actionable insights supporting both operational and strategic decision-making. 

We adopt a phased approach to ensure rapid value delivery and alignment with business priorities. By breaking the engagement into sequential phases—each with defined deliverables and clear timelines—we mitigate risk and ensure a seamless path from data model design through to automated reporting and integration with Salesforce and QuickBooks. This approach guarantees transparency, measurable progress, and stakeholder involvement at every stage.

---

**Phase 3A: Design & Planning, Duration: 3 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 to Week 3**  
**Summary:** Detailed requirements gathering and design of the L2A and CAC data model, reporting suite, and technical approach for integrations.

**Activities:**  
- Conduct discovery workshops with data, technology, and marketing teams.  
- Document KPI logic and calculation requirements in the KPI Book.  
- Design the enhanced L2A and CAC data model and assess data sources (Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks).  
- Prepare Power BI mock-ups (up to 8 views) illustrating future reporting and dashboard layouts.  
- Complete data gap assessment and document the technical approach for data integration and automation.  
- Develop the Phase 3B Implementation Plan and roadmap.

---

**Phase 3B: Build & Integration, Duration: 8–12 weeks, Timeline: Week 4 to Week 15**  
**Summary:** Development and deployment of the data pipelines, automation, and Power BI reporting suite, incorporating new integrations and automated processes.

**Activities:**  
- Configure and integrate automated data ingestion pipelines (batch) for QuickBooks and Salesforce in the Microsoft Fabric platform.  
- Transform and validate data for the unified L2A and CAC data model, ensuring data quality and completeness.  
- Develop and test Power BI reporting suite with targeted L2A and CAC dashboards and visualizations.  
- Define and document data governance and refresh processes for ongoing operations.  
- Conduct user acceptance testing (UAT) with key business stakeholders and iterate on deliverable improvements as needed.  
- Provide enablement and knowledge transfer to Pure Financial Advisors’ teams.

---

This phased approach ensures that all critical aspects—from data source integration to end-user reporting—are comprehensively addressed while maintaining flexibility to adapt to emerging insights or challenges. Through close collaboration and transparent delivery checkpoints, PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC will attain scalable, accurate, and actionable pipeline and ROI reporting capabilities that support data-driven growth and operational excellence.
