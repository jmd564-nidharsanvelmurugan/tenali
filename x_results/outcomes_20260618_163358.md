# Outcomes

This engagement will deliver measurable business impact by enabling PURE Financial Advisors LLC to gain granular visibility into its Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) performance. The initiative aligns with PURE’s B2C financial advisory model and the strategic objectives of both internal leadership and its investment partner, Lee Equity Partners, by improving data-driven decision making and marketing ROI transparency.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Granular Lead-to-AUM Visibility:** Development of a comprehensive L2A data model will allow leadership to track the full client acquisition journey from initial lead through to managed assets, improving understanding of conversion dynamics and pipeline efficiency.  
- **Accurate Customer Acquisition Cost Insights:** Integration of QuickBooks and Salesforce data will enable precise CAC calculations, providing actionable insights into marketing spend effectiveness and return on investment.  
- **Automated and Reliable Reporting:** Implementation of automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines will ensure consistent, timely, and accurate Power BI reporting, reducing manual effort and improving data reliability.  
- **Improved Marketing ROI Measurement:** Enhanced reporting will empower marketing teams to evaluate campaign performance and optimize resource allocation based on clear, data-backed ROI metrics.  
- **Strengthened Data Maturity and Scalability:** Establishing a scalable data foundation within Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse will support future data sources and reporting needs, advancing PURE’s long-term vision for data maturity and growth.  
- **Informed Strategic Decision Making:** Leadership will gain a unified view of pipeline performance and acquisition costs, enabling more confident decisions around marketing strategy, resource planning, and growth investments.
