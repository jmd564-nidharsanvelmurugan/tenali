# Objectives

- Establish a fully designed exit reporting suite that delivers clear, investor-grade equity narratives and supports the M&A sale process.  
- Define and implement a prioritised KPI framework that standardises metrics across commercial, financial, and operational domains for enhanced investor reporting.  
- Automate refreshable data ingestion and recurring data refresh pipelines from key source systems to ensure timely, accurate transaction reporting.  
- Enable senior leadership and stakeholders to gain comprehensive, intuitive visibility into business performance via dashboards aligned to equity narratives and clearly defined KPIs.  
- Improve exit reporting processes, KPI standardisation, and data infrastructure readiness to withstand investor and acquirer scrutiny.  
- Ensure the data model and reporting suite are scalable and extensible, supporting additional data sources and ongoing refresh requirements beyond the transaction phase.  
- Integrate the reporting suite with existing Finance, CRM, ERP, and Qlik Warehouse systems, while maintaining flexibility for future tooling enhancements.
