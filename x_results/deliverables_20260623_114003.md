# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with VONQ, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

1. **Equity Narratives Definition**
   - Clear articulation of the core equity narratives to underpin the M&A sale process.
   - Mapping of key business themes to target investor personas.
   - Documentation delivered in workshop output summaries and a consolidated equity narrative deck.
   - Delivered by end of Week 1 following stakeholder workshops.

2. **Metrics that Matter KPI Framework**
   - Prioritised framework of investor-relevant KPIs across commercial, financial, and operational dimensions.
   - Detailed KPI definitions, including calculation logic and supporting data sources.
   - Excel and presentation formats provided for ease of review and pivoting.
   - Delivered by end of Week 2, with interim updates following initial requirements gathering.

3. **Dashboard Mock-ups**
   - Static wireframes illustrating proposed dashboard layouts, navigation structure, and visualizations.
   - Inclusion of prioritized KPIs, data views, and sample drill-downs as per Phase 1 scope.
   - Deliverables provided as annotated PDF and PowerPoint files.
   - Delivered by end of Week 3, enabling stakeholder feedback cycles prior to finalization.

4. **Data Feasibility Assessment**
   - Assessment of data availability and quality across all required sources (Finance, CRM, ERP, legacy).
   - Identification and documentation of data gaps, risks, and recommendations for remediation.
   - Playback session and written summary report with supporting tables.
   - Delivered by mid-Week 2, with findings shared for collaborative issue resolution.

5. **Conceptual Data Model Design**
   - High-level data model blueprint reflecting required entities, relationships, and data flows.
   - Focused on Qlik Warehouse, with extensibility options for additional or alternative data sources.
   - Delivered in both diagrammatic (e.g., Visio, PowerPoint) and narrative formats.
   - Delivered by end of Week 3, for inclusion in summary design pack.

6. **Data Infrastructure and Tooling Recommendations**
   - Comparative analysis of recommended tools, platforms, and data pipeline options—including pros, cons, and trade-off rationale.
   - Specific focus on integration feasibility, scalability, and operational efficiency for Phase 2.
   - Delivered as a written report with summary slides.
   - Delivered by end of Week 3.

7. **Implementation Roadmap for Phase 2**
   - Phased plan outlining key activities, dependencies, prioritized use cases, and risk mitigations to guide the build phase.
   - Defined milestones, critical path, and decision points for leadership review.
   - Roadmap provided as a Gantt chart with supporting narrative.
   - Delivered by end of Week 4 as the capstone planning artifact.

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with VONQ's leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
