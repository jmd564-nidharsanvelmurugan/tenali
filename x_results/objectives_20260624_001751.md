# Objectives

- Deliver a comprehensive Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data model and reporting suite providing granular insights into pipeline performance and marketing ROI.
- Automate data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes to streamline information flow and improve reporting accuracy.
- Enable leadership to gain a detailed understanding of the customer journey from lead generation to asset conversion, with clear measurement of marketing spend effectiveness.
- Enhance user experience through improved Power BI dashboards featuring precise KPI definitions and intuitive visualizations.
- Strengthen pipeline reporting and customer acquisition cost tracking to support data-driven business decisions.
- Ensure the data platform and reporting capabilities are scalable to accommodate future data sources and evolving reporting needs, with seamless integration to Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Azure Fabric.
