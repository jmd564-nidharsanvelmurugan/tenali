# Outcomes

The engagement with VONQ will deliver a transformative impact by equipping the business with investor-ready reporting and data-driven insights to underpin the upcoming M&A exit process. By designing a scalable exit reporting suite and prioritized KPI framework, VONQ will be positioned to present robust equity narratives, streamline stakeholder communications, and accelerate exit readiness. The success of this initiative will be measured by the creation of actionable Phase 1 design artefacts and the client's ability to confidently progress to Phase 2 within the critical transaction timeline.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Validated Exit Reporting Suite:** A comprehensive and investor-grade reporting design, including data model, dashboards, and KPI definitions, ready to guide the technical build and support the sale process.
- **Investor-Ready KPI Framework:** Clear, prioritized commercial, financial, and operational KPIs aligned to equity narratives, enabling leadership to substantiate VONQ’s value proposition for acquirers and advisors.
- **Data Gap Identification and Remediation Priorities:** Transparent playback of current-state findings, highlighting data gaps and actionable remediation steps to ensure compliance with investor reporting standards.
- **Improved Leadership Insight:** Investor-grade visibility into critical business metrics, empowering senior leadership and advisors with reliable information for decision-making and stakeholder communications.
- **Accelerated Exit Readiness:** Automation recommendations for data ingestion and refresh pipelines, mitigating risks of reporting delays and enhancing VONQ’s credibility among advisors and potential buyers.
- **Scalable Data Infrastructure:** A conceptual data model and tooling recommendations that enable extensibility to additional sources and future automation, establishing lasting assets post-transaction.

These outcomes will equip VONQ with the tools, reporting clarity, and confidence necessary to present compelling, data-backed narratives to investors, minimize transaction risks, and optimize business value during the exit process.
