# Objectives

- Establish a fully designed exit reporting suite that supports the equity narrative, provides a prioritised KPI framework, and includes dashboard concepts aligned to investor needs.
- Automate data ingestion and refresh pipelines from relevant source systems, enabling recurring updates to support transaction reporting requirements.
- Deliver investor-grade visibility for senior leadership into key business metrics critical for underpinning the M&A sale process.
- Enhance user experience for leadership, advisors, and potential acquirers through intuitive dashboards with clear KPI definitions and logical navigation.
- Standardise exit reporting processes, KPI calculation logic, and data infrastructure to ensure readiness for investor scrutiny.
- Lay the foundation for lasting, scalable business assets by ensuring the solution and technology choices are extensible, refreshable, and automatable post-transaction.
- Integrate the future-state platform with Finance System, CRM, ERP, and Qlik Warehouse, while evaluating options for additional tooling and data sources as needed.
