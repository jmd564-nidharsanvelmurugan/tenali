# Outcomes

The engagement with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC will deliver measurable improvements in reporting capabilities and strategic decision-making for pipeline management and marketing effectiveness. By implementing enhanced Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) models, the business will gain actionable insights and clarity into conversion metrics and marketing ROI, empowering leadership and functional teams to optimize operations for sustainable growth.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Granular L2A and CAC Insights:** Provides accurate and detailed visibility into the complete lead journey and associated acquisition costs, enabling targeted actions to improve pipeline conversion rates.
- **Data-Driven Decision Making:** Empowers senior leadership and stakeholders to base strategic decisions on robust, automated reporting with clear KPIs, fostering greater confidence in business planning.
- **Improved Marketing ROI Visibility:** Enables comprehensive assessment of return on marketing spend, supporting optimization of resource allocation and campaign effectiveness.
- **Automated Reporting Processes:** Establishes efficient data ingestion and transformation pipelines, reducing manual effort and ensuring consistent metric refreshes, improving operational efficiency.
- **Scalable Reporting Platform:** Builds a solution designed to support future growth with seamless integration to new data sources and evolving reporting needs.
- **Enhanced Power BI Dashboards:** Delivers user-friendly dashboards with clear KPI visualizations, improving interpretation and dialogue across data, technology, and marketing teams.
