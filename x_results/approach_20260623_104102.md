# Approach

PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC seeks to elevate its pipeline and marketing reporting capabilities by designing and implementing enhanced Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data models and dashboards. Our engagement is centered on providing actionable insights to improve marketing ROI and pipeline performance, leveraging the client’s technology ecosystem that includes Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, Microsoft Fabric, Azure Synapse, and Power BI, with integration of QuickBooks as a new data source.

Our methodology follows a two-phase approach: an initial Design phase to establish requirements, prototype deliverables, and plan for implementation, followed by a Build phase focused on integration, automation, and deployment. Throughout both phases, we emphasize collaboration with client stakeholders, adherence to project dependencies and timelines, and the delivery of high-quality, scalable reporting solutions tailored to PURE’s business objectives.

---

**Phase 3A: Design & Planning, Duration: 3 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 to Week 3**  
**Summary:** Establish clear reporting requirements, design data models, and prepare technical plans to serve as the foundation for build and implementation.

**Activities:**  
- Conduct discovery sessions with data, marketing, and technology teams to gather requirements for L2A and CAC reporting  
- Develop comprehensive KPI Book documenting metrics, calculation logic, and business context  
- Design data model architecture, integrating Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks  
- Create Power BI mock-ups (up to 8 views) illustrating expected dashboards and visualizations  
- Perform data gap analysis and assess QuickBooks data quality for integration readiness  
- Prepare Technical Approach and Phase 3B Implementation Plan outlining steps for automation and deployment  

---

**Phase 3B: Build & Integration, Duration: 8–12 weeks, Timeline: Week 4 to Week 15**  
**Summary:** Deliver the production-ready solution by integrating data sources, automating pipelines, and deploying advanced reporting within Power BI.

**Activities:**  
- Implement automated batch data ingestion from QuickBooks into Microsoft Fabric  
- Integrate Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks in Fabric/Synapse environment  
- Develop and optimize L2A and CAC data models for scalability and future data sources  
- Build Power BI reporting suite with 8 views, aligned to defined KPIs and user personas  
- Establish automated transformation pipelines and reporting refresh processes  
- Conduct user acceptance testing, refine dashboards and documentation, and support transition to operational use  

---

This phased approach ensures a logical progression from requirements gathering through solution design and implementation, addressing PURE’s key dependencies and constraints. Each phase builds critical capabilities to empower leadership with granular, automated insights, driving data maturity and scalable reporting aligned with PURE’s growth strategy. The methodology ensures all project deliverables are produced with clarity, technical rigor, and business value.
