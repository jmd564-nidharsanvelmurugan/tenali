# Overview

Pure Financial LLC currently operates without an existing data platform or centralized data infrastructure. Core business activities are managed without reliance on automated data solutions, and data processes remain decentralized.

As of today, the company’s revenue tracking, pipeline management, and operational reporting functions do not utilize any integrated systems or analytics tools. There are no established data sources, databases, or cloud platforms supporting these operations. All data handling and reporting is performed independently of a formal data platform, with no automated integration across business units or processes.
