# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with VONQ, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

1. **Equity Narratives Definition**
   - Clear articulation of the core equity narratives to support VONQ’s M&A sale process.
   - Documentation of key business themes and value drivers tailored for investor and advisor communications.
   - Format: Structured Word/PDF report, delivered at the end of Week 1.

2. **Metrics that Matter KPI Framework**
   - A prioritized framework of commercial, financial, and operational KPIs aligned with equity narratives.
   - Detailed KPI definitions including calculation logic, data sources, and rationale.
   - Format: Excel workbook and supporting documentation, delivered by the end of Week 2.

3. **Dashboard Mock-Ups**
   - Static visual representations and wireframes illustrating proposed dashboard layouts and user navigation.
   - Visual mapping of KPIs, filters, and key dashboard views tailored to stakeholder needs.
   - Format: PDF mock-ups or screenshots, delivered by the end of Week 3.

4. **Data Feasibility Assessment**
   - Analysis and playback of current-state data coverage, data gaps, and areas requiring remediation.
   - Assessment includes findings from workshops and data sample reviews.
   - Format: PowerPoint or Word report, delivered by the end of Week 2.

5. **Conceptual Data Model Design**
   - High-level design model mapping core data entities, relationships, and data flows for the exit reporting suite.
   - Model anchored to the Qlik Warehouse with consideration for additional data sources.
   - Format: Diagram and technical documentation, delivered by the end of Week 3.

6. **Data Infrastructure and Tooling Recommendations**
   - Evaluation of current and potential new toolsets or enhancements to support scalability and automation.
   - Trade-off analysis for Qlik Warehouse and alternative solutions, with clear pros and cons.
   - Format: Comparative analysis in a structured report, delivered by the end of Week 4.

7. **Implementation Roadmap for Phase 2**
   - Phased action plan outlining key milestones, dependencies, risks, and recommended timelines for the Phase 2 build.
   - Sequencing of priority use cases and data integrations, with clear owners and deliverable dates.
   - Format: Gantt chart and supporting documentation, delivered by the end of Week 4.

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with VONQ's leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
