# Outcomes

This engagement will deliver measurable improvements in PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC’s ability to analyze and act on detailed pipeline and marketing effectiveness data. By implementing automated Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting, the organization will gain granular visibility into every stage of the customer journey, empowering leadership and marketing teams to optimize resource allocation and drive business growth.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Granular Pipeline Intelligence:** Actionable insights into the journey from lead generation to assets under management, improving visibility into sales and marketing performance.
- **Accurate CAC Measurement:** Automated and integrated reporting of Customer Acquisition Cost, enabling precise evaluation of marketing spend efficiency and ROI.
- **Improved Decision-Making:** Empowered leadership with user-friendly Power BI dashboards containing clearly defined KPIs, supporting informed and timely strategic decisions.
- **Operational Efficiency:** Automation of data ingestion, transformation, and reporting processes reduces manual effort and ensures timely, accurate analytics delivery.
- **Scalable Data Maturity:** Enhanced data infrastructure and reporting capabilities to support future growth and new data sources, ensuring long-term value and flexibility.
- **Risk Mitigation:** Minimized risk of overlooked pipeline issues and suboptimal marketing strategies by providing comprehensive, real-time insights into key business drivers.
