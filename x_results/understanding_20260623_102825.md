# Understanding

- Limited granularity in current pipeline reporting restricts the ability to analyze the journey from lead to AUM and accurately track marketing ROI.
- Lack of integrated insights into Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) inhibits effective evaluation of marketing spend and return.
- Data quality concerns and dependency on QuickBooks integration pose challenges to producing reliable CAC reporting.
- Required capabilities include a robust Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and CAC data model, enhanced Power BI reporting suite, and data ingestion from QuickBooks.
- Automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines are necessary to efficiently produce L2A and CAC reports.
- System usage will span data and technology stakeholders, senior leadership, and marketing teams, each needing tailored access to granular insights.
- Mandatory integrations include connecting Salesforce and QuickBooks to centralize and harmonize pipeline, acquisition, and financial data.
- Access, data quality, and timely integration with QuickBooks represent key operational constraints during the project.
