# Business Impact

- **Enhanced Financial Transparency:** The implementation of advanced Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting will enable PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC to gain more granular insights into the true ROI of marketing initiatives, supporting data-driven decisions that maximize revenue generation and minimize unnecessary spend.

- **Automated Data Integration and Efficiency Gains:** Automating the ingestion and transformation of data streams from platforms such as QuickBooks, Salesforce, and Tamarac will streamline reporting processes and reduce manual effort, freeing up valuable resources across data, technology, and marketing teams.

- **Improved Pipeline Conversion Analysis:** With detailed tracking of the customer journey from lead to AUM, leadership will have real-time visibility into conversion rates at each stage, allowing for targeted interventions that can increase client acquisition and overall assets under management (AUM).

- **Scalability for Future Growth:** The design and deployment of scalable data models and reporting suites integrated with Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse will allow PURE to seamlessly incorporate additional data sources and adapt to future analytics and reporting requirements, ensuring continued alignment with business expansion goals.

- **Strategic Competitive Differentiation:** By delivering an integrated, automated reporting suite in Power BI with clear KPI definitions, PURE will be equipped to present superior business intelligence to stakeholders and investment partners such as Lee Equity Partners, strengthening strategic positioning in the competitive financial advisory market.

This initiative will provide PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC with accurate, timely, and actionable insights into marketing effectiveness and pipeline health—empowering smarter investments in growth, more efficient operations, and a robust foundation for sustained strategic advantage in the wealth management sector.
