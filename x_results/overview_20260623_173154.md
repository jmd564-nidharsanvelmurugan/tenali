# Overview

PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC’s operations related to pipeline and customer acquisition reporting involve the data, technology, and marketing teams. These teams leverage a combination of systems and platforms, including Salesforce for client relationship management, Tamarac for portfolio management, Azure Blob Storage for data storage, and the Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse platforms, which support data integration and analytics. Power BI serves as the primary reporting suite, with existing dashboards providing visibility into key business metrics.

The company’s primary data sources include Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks, the latter of which is scheduled for integration. Manual processes are not specifically detailed. Integration efforts are currently focused on connecting and ingesting financial data from QuickBooks and conducting data quality assessments to support enhanced customer acquisition cost reporting. The present infrastructure delivers a foundation for data-driven insights, with all analytic activities operating on established Microsoft data platforms.
