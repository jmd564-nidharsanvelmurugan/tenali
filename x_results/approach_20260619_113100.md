# Approach

The engagement with **Pure Financial Advisors LLC** is designed to address the firm’s specific need for enhanced Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting within the financial advisory and wealth management space. As a B2C business operating across the United States, Pure requires a data‑driven foundation that connects marketing performance to asset growth. This approach leverages the company’s existing Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse infrastructure while integrating new data sources such as QuickBooks to deliver a unified, automated, and scalable reporting environment.

Our methodology follows a structured, phased approach that ensures alignment with Pure’s business priorities, technical landscape, and stakeholder expectations. Each phase builds upon the previous one—progressing from discovery and data assessment through model design and implementation planning—culminating in a robust foundation for future reporting scalability. This structure ensures that insights are actionable, data quality is validated, and the final deliverables are ready for seamless transition into the build phase.

---

**Phase 1: Discovery & Requirements Definition, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 1**

**Summary:** Establish a shared understanding of business goals, data landscape, and reporting requirements to set the foundation for design and integration activities.

**Activities:**  
- Conduct kickoff sessions with data, technology, and marketing stakeholders to confirm objectives and success criteria.  
- Review existing documentation for Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Fabric, and Power BI environments.  
- Map current reporting workflows and identify dependencies for QuickBooks integration.  
- Define key L2A and CAC metrics and confirm alignment with leadership expectations.  
- Develop a detailed project plan and workshop schedule for subsequent phases.  

---

**Phase 2: Data Source Assessment & Gap Analysis, Duration: 2 weeks, Timeline: Weeks 2–3**

**Summary:** Evaluate current data sources, assess data quality, and identify integration and transformation requirements for the L2A and CAC model.

**Activities:**  
- Perform data profiling across Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks.  
- Identify data gaps, inconsistencies, and quality issues impacting CAC and pipeline reporting.  
- Document data lineage and mapping between systems to support model design.  
- Define ingestion and transformation requirements for batch processing in Microsoft Fabric.  
- Prioritize remediation actions and dependencies for QuickBooks data integration.  

---

**Phase 3: Data Model Design & KPI Definition, Duration: 2 weeks, Timeline: Weeks 4–5**

**Summary:** Design the target‑state L2A and CAC data model and define KPI logic to support accurate, automated reporting.

**Activities:**  
- Develop conceptual and logical data models integrating Salesforce, Tamarac, and QuickBooks data.  
- Define KPI calculation logic for L2A conversion and CAC metrics, including data source mapping.  
- Validate model design and KPI definitions with business and technical stakeholders.  
- Align data model structure with Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse architecture standards.  
- Document data transformation rules and dependencies for implementation.  

---

**Phase 4: Power BI Mock‑ups & Reporting Design, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 6**

**Summary:** Translate business requirements and data model outputs into visual reporting prototypes for stakeholder validation.

**Activities:**  
- Design up to eight Power BI mock‑ups focused on L2A and CAC insights.  
- Incorporate agreed KPI definitions and data hierarchies into dashboard layouts.  
- Conduct review sessions with marketing and leadership teams to refine visualizations.  
- Capture feedback and finalize mock‑ups for inclusion in the design deliverables.  
- Document visualization logic and data dependencies for the build phase.  

---

**Phase 5: Technical Approach & Implementation Planning, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 7**

**Summary:** Finalize the technical design, integration approach, and roadmap for Phase 3B implementation.

**Activities:**  
- Define the end‑to‑end technical architecture for data ingestion, transformation, and reporting automation.  
- Develop the QuickBooks integration approach, including data flow and scheduling logic.  
- Outline data governance and quality management recommendations.  
- Prepare the Phase 3B Implementation Plan detailing sequencing, resourcing, and dependencies.  
- Consolidate all deliverables—KPI Book, Data Model, Mock‑ups, and Technical Approach—into the final presentation.  

---

This structured, phased approach ensures that **Pure Financial Advisors LLC** develops its L2A and CAC reporting capabilities with precision, agility, and a clear focus on measurable business outcomes. By aligning technical design with strategic objectives, the engagement positions Pure to achieve scalable, automated, and insight‑driven reporting that supports continued growth and operational excellence.
