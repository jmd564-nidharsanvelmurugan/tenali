# Business Impact

- **Enhanced Marketing ROI Visibility:** Establishes granular Lead‑to‑AUM and Customer Acquisition Cost reporting, enabling Pure Financial Advisors to precisely measure marketing effectiveness and optimize spend allocation across channels.  
- **Operational Efficiency Gains:** Automates data ingestion and transformation processes across Salesforce, QuickBooks, and Tamarac, reducing manual reporting effort by an estimated 40–60% and improving reporting timeliness.  
- **Data‑Driven Decision Making:** Provides leadership with a unified, trusted view of pipeline performance, empowering faster, evidence‑based decisions on lead conversion strategies and resource prioritization.  
- **Scalable Reporting Architecture:** Builds a future‑ready data model within Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse, ensuring scalability for new data sources and supporting Pure’s long‑term growth and data maturity objectives.  
- **Improved Financial Transparency:** Integrates financial data from QuickBooks to deliver accurate CAC metrics, strengthening Pure’s ability to track profitability and marketing return at both campaign and portfolio levels.  
- **Stakeholder Alignment and Accountability:** Creates standardized KPI definitions and Power BI dashboards that align data, technology, and marketing teams around common performance metrics, fostering cross‑functional accountability.  

By delivering these outcomes, this engagement will enable Pure Financial Advisors LLC to transform its pipeline and marketing performance management approach, establishing a scalable, data‑driven foundation that supports sustained growth, improved profitability, and long‑term value creation in the competitive U.S. financial advisory market.
