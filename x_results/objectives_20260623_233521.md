# Objectives

- Deliver a comprehensive Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data model and reporting suite that provides granular insights into pipeline performance and marketing return on investment.
- Automate data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes to improve efficiency and data quality.
- Enable leadership to gain a detailed understanding of the customer journey from lead origin to AUM conversion and the effectiveness of marketing spend.
- Enhance user experience with intuitive Power BI dashboards, clear KPI definitions, and actionable visualizations.
- Improve pipeline reporting and customer acquisition cost tracking to support data-driven decision-making.
- Establish a scalable data platform and reporting framework capable of supporting future data sources, evolving analytics requirements, and ongoing business growth.
- Ensure seamless integration of the reporting solution with Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Microsoft Fabric.
