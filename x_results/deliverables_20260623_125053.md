# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

1. **KPI Book**
   - Comprehensive documentation of Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) metrics and logic.
   - Definitions, formulas, and calculation methodologies for each KPI.
   - Source mapping of metrics across Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks.
   - Delivered in PDF and editable Word formats by the conclusion of Phase 3A.

2. **Power BI Mock-Ups**
   - Up to 8 Power BI report view designs focused on L2A and CAC reporting requirements.
   - Representation of proposed visuals, data fields, and user interactions.
   - Alignment with PURE’s stakeholder needs and use cases.
   - Delivered as PowerPoint or PDF files during the mid-point of Phase 3A.

3. **Data Model Design**
   - Logical and physical data model diagrams for the L2A and CAC reporting datasets.
   - Entity, attribute, and relationship definitions covering Salesforce, Tamarac, QuickBooks, and Azure platforms.
   - Data flow mappings illustrating integration and transformation logic.
   - Delivered as Visio, draw.io, or PDF files by the end of Phase 3A.

4. **Data Gap Assessment**
   - Identification and summary of data quality, completeness, and integration gaps impacting L2A and CAC reporting.
   - Recommendations for remediation and validation steps.
   - Prioritized action items to resolve critical data issues before build phases.
   - Delivered in Excel and summary slide format at the close of Phase 3A.

5. **Technical Approach Document**
   - Detailed solution architecture for integrating QuickBooks and Salesforce with the Fabric data platform.
   - Data ingestion, transformation, and automation pipeline specifications for L2A and CAC metrics.
   - Security, access controls, and compliance considerations relevant to the engagement.
   - Delivered as a PDF and Word document by the completion of Phase 3A.

6. **Phase 3B Implementation Plan**
   - Phased build roadmap with detailed timelines, milestones, and resource requirements for implementation.
   - Dependencies on data ingestion, integration readiness, and stakeholder reviews.
   - RACI (Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed) chart for key tasks and phases.
   - Delivered in Excel and slide format at the completion of Phase 3A.

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC's leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
