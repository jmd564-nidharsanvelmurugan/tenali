# Approach

VONQ is preparing for a near-term exit and requires a comprehensive, scalable exit reporting suite to support the M&A process and stakeholder communications. Our engagement methodology leverages a structured, stakeholder-driven discovery and design approach to ensure that all business-critical requirements are surfaced, validated, and translated into actionable design artifacts for rapid execution. Throughout the engagement, we will work collaboratively with key client stakeholders and advisors to align deliverables to both internal priorities and investor expectations, while ensuring data feasibility, automation readiness, and future scalability.

The engagement will be executed in clearly defined phases, each with specific objectives, activities, and output artifacts. The methodology emphasizes iterative workshops, stakeholder playback, and transparent trade-off analysis to de-risk the future technical build and provide immediate clarity for decision-makers. All work will strictly adhere to the four-week delivery timeline, ensuring alignment with VONQ’s exit objectives and timeline constraints.

---

**Phase 1: Discovery & Design, Duration: 4 weeks, Timeline: 29 June 2026 to 24 July 2026**  
**Summary:** This phase will define the equity narratives, prioritise KPIs, validate data feasibility, design dashboards and data models, and set out a clear implementation roadmap for the exit reporting suite.

**Activities:**  
- Conduct a kick-off workshop with key stakeholders to confirm objectives, review data access, and agree detailed timelines.  
- Define the equity narratives, covering commercial, financial, and operational dimensions critical to M&A positioning.  
- Establish the "Metrics that Matter" KPI framework and hold stakeholder sessions to prioritise and validate key metrics.  
- Assess data feasibility, including gap analysis across Finance System, CRM, ERP, and legacy sources, and playback current-state data quality and integration findings.  
- Create static dashboard mock-ups and wireframes, illustrating proposed metrics, layouts, and navigation structures.  
- Develop a conceptual data model using Qlik Warehouse as the foundation, and produce tooling recommendations with trade-off analysis and a phased implementation roadmap.

---

In summary, our phased approach will deliver a validated exit reporting design—including equity narratives, KPI frameworks, dashboard concepts, and a conceptual data model—with a clear roadmap to guide the technical implementation. By anchoring our methodology in stakeholder collaboration, data-driven analysis, and actionable design decisions, we ensure VONQ is equipped with investor-ready reporting assets that can underpin the M&A process and support long-term business scalability. This structured approach minimises risk and positions VONQ to move confidently into the build phase on an accelerated timetable.
