# Objectives

- Establish a comprehensive Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data model that delivers granular insights into pipeline performance and marketing ROI.  
- Automate data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes to ensure consistent, timely, and accurate reporting.  
- Provide leadership with clear visibility into the full customer journey from lead generation through to assets under management, enabling data‑driven decision‑making.  
- Enhance Power BI dashboards with intuitive visualizations and standardized KPI definitions to improve user experience and reporting clarity.  
- Build scalable reporting capabilities that support future data sources and evolving business needs, driving long‑term data maturity across the organization.
