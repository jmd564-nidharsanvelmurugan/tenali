# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with Poekmon, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

1. **KPI Book**  
- Clear definitions and standardized calculation logic for Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) metrics.  

- Mapping of each KPI to its underlying data sources and alignment with Poekmon’s business goals.  

- Documentation ensuring consistency with industry benchmarks and internal reporting standards.  

2. **Power BI Mock‑Ups (8 Views)**  
- Design of up to eight Power BI dashboard views illustrating L2A and CAC performance.  

- Visual representations of key metrics, conversion stages, and marketing outcomes.  

- Layout and interaction blueprints to guide the development of production dashboards.  

3. **Data Model Design**  
- Comprehensive data model architecture integrating Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Fabric, and QuickBooks data.  

- Entity‑relationship diagrams showing data flows, joins, and dependencies.  

- Documentation of transformation logic, business rules, and integration points across systems.  

4. **Data Gap Assessment**  
- Identification of missing, incomplete, or inconsistent data affecting L2A and CAC reporting accuracy.  

- Evaluation of data quality, lineage, and consistency across all connected systems.  

- Prioritized recommendations for remediation and data improvement initiatives.  

5. **Technical Approach**  
- Detailed description of the end‑to‑end technical setup, including integration of Salesforce and QuickBooks.  

- Definition of data processing pipelines leveraging Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse.  

- Plans for automation, data refresh scheduling, and governance controls.  

6. **Phase 3B Implementation Plan**  
- Comprehensive roadmap outlining tasks, timelines, and resource requirements for solution build and deployment.  

- Defined milestones, success criteria, and dependencies for implementation.  

- Step‑by‑step plan for integration, testing, and rollout to production.  

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with Poekmon’s leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
