# Understanding

- VONQ requires a rapidly developed, scalable exit reporting suite to support its M&A sale process and ensure effective investor communications.
- The current reporting environment lacks investor-ready outputs and may not provide complete coverage of the required KPIs essential for constructing credible equity narratives.
- Data gaps exist across source systems, and the Qlik Warehouse may not sufficiently support all metrics required by prospective acquirers and advisors for due diligence.
- Some integration challenges are present, with certain source systems not having automated connections into the Qlik Warehouse, necessitating manual data ingestion and recurring refresh processes during the transaction period.
- Capabilities needed include the definition of equity narratives, a prioritised KPI framework, static dashboard mock-ups, data feasibility assessments, a conceptual data model, tooling recommendations, and a phased implementation roadmap.
- Automated data ingestion and refresh pipelines are required for Finance System, CRM, ERP, and relevant legacy sources to provide ongoing and reliable data updates as part of the exit process.
- Key user roles for the reporting suite include senior leadership, exit advisors (e.g., Barclays), and potential acquirers or investors.
- Mandatory integrations encompass the Finance System, CRM, ERP, and legacy data sources (to supply at least three years of trended data) into the Qlik Warehouse.
