# Objectives

- Establish a comprehensive Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data model that delivers granular insights into pipeline performance and marketing ROI.  
- Automate data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes to ensure consistent, timely, and accurate analytics.  
- Provide leadership with clear visibility into the full customer journey from lead generation through asset conversion, enabling data‑driven decision‑making.  
- Enhance the Power BI user experience with intuitive dashboards, standardized KPI definitions, and clear visualizations of key performance metrics.  
- Strengthen pipeline and CAC tracking processes to support scalable, data‑mature reporting capabilities aligned with Pure’s long‑term growth vision.  
- Ensure seamless integration across Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Azure Fabric to create a unified, trusted reporting environment.
