# Approach

PURE Financial Advisors LLC operates in the financial advisory and wealth management sector, where data-driven insights are critical to understanding client acquisition efficiency and marketing ROI. This engagement is designed to address PURE’s specific challenge of limited granularity in Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting by leveraging its existing Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse infrastructure. The approach focuses on integrating key systems—Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks—into a unified data model that supports automated, scalable, and insightful Power BI reporting for leadership and marketing teams.

Our methodology follows a structured, phased approach that ensures alignment with PURE’s business objectives, technical environment, and timeline expectations. Each phase builds upon the previous one, from discovery and data assessment through to model design and implementation planning. This ensures that the final solution is both technically robust and strategically aligned with PURE’s goal of achieving enhanced data maturity and reporting precision.

---

**Phase 1: Discovery & Current-State Assessment, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 1**

**Summary:** Establish a foundational understanding of PURE’s current data landscape, systems, and reporting processes to inform subsequent design activities.

**Activities:**  
- Conduct kickoff sessions with key stakeholders (Data, Technology, and Marketing teams).  
- Review existing documentation for Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Fabric, Synapse, and Power BI.  
- Assess current pipeline and CAC reporting capabilities and identify data access requirements.  
- Map existing data flows and dependencies across systems.  
- Define success criteria and confirm scope boundaries for the engagement.  

---

**Phase 2: Data Source Review & Gap Assessment, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 2**

**Summary:** Evaluate data availability, quality, and integration readiness across all relevant systems, with a focus on QuickBooks ingestion and reconciliation.

**Activities:**  
- Review data structures and key fields in Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks.  
- Identify data quality issues and gaps impacting L2A and CAC reporting.  
- Document data lineage and dependencies for each source system.  
- Develop recommendations for data cleaning and enrichment.  
- Validate data access and ingestion feasibility within Microsoft Fabric.  

---

**Phase 3: KPI Definition & Data Model Design, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 3**

**Summary:** Define the target-state data model and KPI logic to support L2A and CAC reporting requirements.

**Activities:**  
- Collaborate with stakeholders to finalize KPI definitions and calculation logic.  
- Design the conceptual and logical data model for L2A and CAC reporting.  
- Map data sources to model entities and establish transformation logic.  
- Validate model design against business requirements and scalability needs.  
- Document data model specifications for technical implementation.  

---

**Phase 4: Power BI Mock-ups & Reporting Design, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 4**

**Summary:** Translate business and data model requirements into visual reporting prototypes to align on insights and user experience.

**Activities:**  
- Develop up to eight Power BI mock-ups focused on L2A and CAC insights.  
- Align dashboard structure with leadership and marketing reporting needs.  
- Incorporate KPI definitions and data visualizations for clarity and usability.  
- Conduct review sessions with stakeholders for feedback and iteration.  
- Finalize mock-ups to inform the implementation plan and data model validation.  

---

**Phase 5: Implementation Planning & Technical Approach, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 5**

**Summary:** Finalize the technical approach, implementation roadmap, and recommendations for Phase 3B execution.

**Activities:**  
- Develop the detailed technical approach for QuickBooks integration and automated data pipelines.  
- Define batch processing workflows within Microsoft Fabric for data ingestion and transformation.  
- Outline the Phase 3B Implementation Plan, including sequencing, dependencies, and resource needs.  
- Document data governance and quality management recommendations.  
- Deliver the KPI Book, Data Model Design, and Technical Approach as final discovery outputs.  

---

This structured, phased approach ensures that PURE Financial Advisors LLC’s L2A and CAC reporting capabilities are developed with precision, agility, and a clear focus on delivering measurable business outcomes. By aligning technical design with business priorities and leveraging existing Azure and Power BI infrastructure, the engagement sets a strong foundation for scalable, automated, and insight-driven reporting.
