# Objectives

- Deliver a comprehensive Lead-to-AUM and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data model and reporting suite that provides detailed, actionable insights into pipeline performance and marketing return on investment.
- Automate data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes to ensure efficient, accurate, and timely availability of L2A and CAC metrics.
- Enable leadership to gain granular visibility into the entire customer journey from initial lead to assets under management and measure the effectiveness of marketing spend with clear KPI definitions.
- Improve user experience through enhanced Power BI dashboards featuring intuitive visualizations and standardized key metrics.
- Strengthen pipeline reporting and customer acquisition cost tracking to support data-driven decision-making and operational efficiency.
- Establish scalable reporting capabilities and a flexible data platform architecture able to accommodate future data sources, expanded reporting requirements, and continued organizational growth.
- Ensure seamless integration with core systems, including Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, Microsoft Fabric, and Azure Synapse, for unified, reliable data insights.
