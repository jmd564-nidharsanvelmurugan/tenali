# Objectives

- Deliver a comprehensive Lead-to-AUM and Customer Acquisition Cost data model and reporting suite to provide granular insights into pipeline performance and marketing ROI.
- Automate end-to-end processes for data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refreshes to ensure timely and accurate analytics.
- Empower leadership with detailed visibility into the journey from lead to AUM and clear measurement of marketing effectiveness.
- Enhance Power BI dashboards with improved KPI definitions and intuitive visualizations to support data-driven decision-making.
- Strengthen pipeline reporting and customer acquisition cost tracking by integrating data from Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Azure Fabric platforms.
- Establish a scalable and mature data platform capable of supporting future data sources and expanding reporting needs for organizational growth.
