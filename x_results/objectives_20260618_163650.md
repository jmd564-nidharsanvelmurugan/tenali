# Objectives

- Establish a comprehensive Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data model delivering granular insights into pipeline performance and marketing ROI.  
- Automate data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes to ensure consistent and timely analytics.  
- Provide leadership with clear visibility into the full customer journey from lead generation through asset conversion.  
- Enhance Power BI dashboards with standardized KPI definitions and intuitive visualizations for improved decision-making.  
- Build scalable reporting capabilities that support future data sources and align with Pure’s long-term data maturity vision.  
- Integrate Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Azure Fabric into a unified reporting ecosystem for seamless data flow and accuracy.
