# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with VONQ, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

1.  
**Equity Narratives Definition**  
- Formal documentation outlining equity narratives tailored for the exit process and investor communications.  
- Clear articulation of the business value proposition and strategic positioning for stakeholders.  
- Provided in presentation and report format during Week 1.  

2.  
**Metrics that Matter KPI Framework**  
- Structured framework detailing prioritized commercial, financial, and operational KPIs essential for investor-grade reporting.  
- Includes KPI definitions, calculation logic, and alignment to equity narratives.  
- Delivered as a comprehensive Excel spreadsheet and supporting documentation, reviewed at the Week 1 workshop.  

3.  
**Dashboard Mock-Ups**  
- Static dashboard mock-ups illustrating proposed layouts, metrics, and navigation structures.  
- Wireframes reflecting investor reporting requirements and equity narrative alignment.  
- Provided as PDF and design files by Week 2, aligned to workshop feedback.  

4.  
**Data Feasibility Assessment**  
- Playback report summarizing current-state findings and identifying data gaps across financial, CRM, ERP, and legacy sources.  
- Feasibility analysis for integration, data quality, and trended data readiness for a minimum three-year period.  
- Delivered in report format, completed in Week 2.  

5.  
**Conceptual Data Model Design**  
- High-level conceptual data model leveraging Qlik Warehouse as the foundation for the exit reporting suite.  
- Includes entity relationship diagrams and refresh pipeline design, specifying integration points.  
- Provided as technical diagrams and accompanying documentation by Week 3.  

6.  
**Data Infrastructure and Tooling Recommendations**  
- Detailed recommendations covering data infrastructure options and reporting tool selection.  
- Analysis of pros, cons, scalability, and automation capabilities to support the future-state solution.  
- Delivered in a structured report and presentation format in Week 3.  

7.  
**Implementation Roadmap for Phase 2**  
- Phased project roadmap covering priority use cases, dependencies, risks, and key milestones for the build phase.  
- Includes timeline, resource allocation, and exit process alignment.  
- Provided as a Gantt chart and action plan document by Week 4.  

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with VONQ's leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
