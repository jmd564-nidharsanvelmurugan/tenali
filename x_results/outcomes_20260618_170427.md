# Outcomes

This engagement with **PURE Financial Advisors LLC** will deliver measurable business impact by enabling data‑driven visibility into the firm’s Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) performance. By aligning with Pure’s B2C financial advisory model and the strategic objectives of both internal leadership and Lee Equity Partners, the initiative will strengthen marketing ROI measurement and pipeline optimization through automated, integrated reporting.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Granular Lead‑to‑AUM Visibility:** Development of a unified L2A data model will provide detailed insights into each stage of the client acquisition journey, enabling leadership to identify conversion bottlenecks and optimize marketing strategies.  
- **Accurate Customer Acquisition Cost Measurement:** Integration of QuickBooks and Salesforce data will allow precise calculation of CAC metrics, improving understanding of marketing spend efficiency and guiding budget allocation decisions.  
- **Automated and Scalable Reporting:** Implementation of automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines will ensure consistent, timely updates to Power BI dashboards, reducing manual effort and supporting future data growth.  
- **Improved Marketing ROI Insights:** Enhanced Power BI reporting will deliver clear KPI definitions and visualizations, allowing leadership to assess the effectiveness of campaigns and make informed investment decisions.  
- **Strengthened Data‑Driven Decision Making:** The new reporting suite will elevate Pure’s data maturity, providing a reliable foundation for strategic planning and performance tracking across the firm’s advisory and asset management operations.  
- **Reduced Operational Risk from Data Gaps:** Addressing data quality and integration dependencies will mitigate reporting inaccuracies, ensuring confidence in insights used for pipeline and marketing performance evaluation.
