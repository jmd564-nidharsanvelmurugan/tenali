# Understanding

- Pure Financial Advisors seeks to address limited visibility and granularity in its existing pipeline reporting, particularly around the Lead-to-AUM (L2A) journey and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) metrics.  
- Current reporting lacks integrated CAC insights, preventing a clear understanding of marketing efficiency and conversion performance.  
- Data ingestion from QuickBooks is not yet established, creating a gap in capturing and analyzing cost data required for CAC reporting.  
- There is a need for an enhanced L2A and CAC data model that consolidates data from Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks.  
- Automated data ingestion and transformation workflows are required to reduce manual effort and ensure consistent, timely reporting.  
- The system must support data and technology stakeholders, senior leadership, and marketing teams as primary users of the reporting outputs.  
- Mandatory integrations include Salesforce and QuickBooks to enable end-to-end visibility across lead, client, and financial data.
