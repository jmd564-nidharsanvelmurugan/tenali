# Objectives

- Establish a comprehensive Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data model and reporting suite to deliver granular insights into pipeline performance and marketing return on investment.
- Automate data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes to ensure timely and reliable L2A and CAC analytics.
- Provide leadership with a detailed, actionable understanding of the journey from lead generation to assets under management, along with clear visibility into marketing spend effectiveness.
- Enhance Power BI dashboards with intuitive KPI definitions and visualizations to improve usability for data, technology, and marketing stakeholders.
- Improve pipeline reporting and customer acquisition cost tracking to support more informed decision-making and operational efficiency.
- Enable scalable data platform and reporting capabilities that can support future data sources and evolving business needs.
- Ensure seamless integration with Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Microsoft Fabric to create a unified data ecosystem for reporting and analysis.
