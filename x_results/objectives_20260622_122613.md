# Objectives

- Deliver a comprehensive Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data model and reporting suite that provides detailed, actionable insights into pipeline performance and return on marketing investment.
- Automate data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes to ensure timely and accurate pipeline and CAC analytics.
- Enable leadership to gain a granular understanding of the customer journey from initial lead through to assets under management, alongside clear measures of marketing effectiveness.
- Enhance user experience with improved Power BI dashboards, featuring clear KPI definitions and intuitive visualizations.
- Improve the accuracy and efficiency of pipeline reporting and customer acquisition cost tracking across the organization.
- Establish a scalable data platform and reporting solution capable of supporting future data sources, additional analytics needs, and company growth.
- Achieve seamless integration with Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Azure Fabric to maximize data connectivity and reporting capabilities.
