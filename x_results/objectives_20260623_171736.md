# Objectives

- Deliver a comprehensive Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data model and reporting suite to provide granular insights into pipeline performance and marketing ROI.
- Automate data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes to enable efficient and timely access to updated metrics.
- Enable senior leadership to gain a detailed understanding of the journey from lead generation to assets under management, as well as clear visibility into marketing effectiveness and associated costs.
- Enhance user experience through improved Power BI dashboards featuring clear KPI definitions and intuitive visualizations for L2A and CAC metrics.
- Improve pipeline reporting and customer acquisition cost tracking by integrating data from Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Microsoft Fabric platforms.
- Establish scalable reporting capabilities and data maturity to support future growth and expansion of PURE Financial Advisors LLC’s analytical footprint.
