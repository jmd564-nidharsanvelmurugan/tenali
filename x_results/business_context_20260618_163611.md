# Business Context

PURE Financial Advisors LLC operates within the U.S. financial advisory and wealth management sector, providing personalized financial advisory services and asset management solutions to individual clients. The firm follows a B2C model, focusing on helping clients grow and manage their assets through tailored investment strategies and financial planning. Key stakeholders include Jason Carver, Client Engagement Manager, and Lee Equity Partners, serving as an investment partner supporting Pure’s growth objectives.

The business is currently seeking to enhance its data and reporting capabilities by developing a more detailed Lead-to-AUM (L2A) pipeline and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting framework. This initiative is driven by the need for greater visibility into the client journey—from initial lead generation through to assets under management—and to better understand the return on marketing spend. Existing reporting through Power BI provides foundational insights but lacks the granularity and integration required for comprehensive performance analysis. By improving data ingestion, particularly from QuickBooks, and strengthening the underlying data model, Pure aims to deliver automated, accurate, and actionable reporting that supports leadership decision-making and marketing optimization.
