# Business Impact

- **Enhanced Marketing ROI Visibility:** The new Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting suite will provide PURE Financial Advisors with granular insights into marketing effectiveness, enabling leadership to allocate spend toward the highest‑performing channels and improve ROI measurement accuracy.  
- **Data‑Driven Growth Decisions:** By integrating Salesforce, QuickBooks, and Tamarac data into a unified model, PURE will gain a single source of truth for pipeline performance, supporting more confident decisions on lead nurturing, conversion strategies, and asset growth initiatives.  
- **Operational Efficiency Gains:** Automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines will reduce manual reporting effort by an estimated 40–60%, freeing data and marketing teams to focus on analysis and optimization rather than data preparation.  
- **Improved Financial Transparency:** CAC and L2A metrics will be standardized across the organization, allowing consistent tracking of acquisition costs and conversion rates—critical for Lee Equity Partners’ portfolio oversight and PURE’s internal performance management.  
- **Scalable Reporting Foundation:** The Microsoft Fabric‑based data model will establish a scalable infrastructure capable of supporting future data sources and advanced analytics, ensuring PURE’s reporting capabilities evolve with business growth.  
- **Investor‑Grade Data Maturity:** The initiative strengthens PURE’s data governance and reporting credibility, positioning the firm with diligence‑ready metrics that enhance confidence among investors and stakeholders.

By delivering these outcomes, this engagement will enable PURE Financial Advisors LLC to transform its pipeline and marketing performance management approach, creating a lasting, scalable data asset that underpins long‑term growth, operational efficiency, and value creation in the U.S. financial advisory marketplace.
