# Overview

VONQ’s current operational environment involves multiple teams—including finance, commercial/CRM, data and analytics, and senior leadership—whose activities contribute to ongoing exit preparation and investor reporting. These teams are supported by a suite of established enterprise systems: the Finance System, CRM, and ERP collectively serve as the foundation for generating, managing, and storing key business data. The Qlik Warehouse functions as the central reporting and data platform, with Qlik-based reporting constituting the primary toolset for analytics and dashboarding. 

Primary data sources feeding into this environment include the Finance System, CRM, ERP, and the Qlik Warehouse, with supplemental Excel datasets and external systems considered on a case-by-case basis. While the Qlik Warehouse provides the backbone for data aggregation and reporting, some source systems currently lack automated integration and require manual, one-off data ingestion. This creates a scenario where recurring data refresh processes must be established for systems that require regular updates during significant business events, such as the exit transaction. Legacy sources may also be leveraged to supply at least three years of trended data as needed. The existing cloud/data platform infrastructure is anchored by the Qlik Warehouse, which hosts core datasets and reporting assets.
