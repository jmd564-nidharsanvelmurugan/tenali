# Objectives

- Deliver comprehensive Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data models and reporting that provide granular insights into pipeline performance and marketing return on investment.
- Automate the data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes to streamline workflow and reduce manual intervention.
- Enable leadership to gain a clear, detailed understanding of the client journey from lead generation through to assets under management, and the effectiveness of marketing expenditures.
- Enhance user experience with improved Power BI dashboards featuring clearly defined KPIs and intuitive visualizations.
- Improve pipeline reporting and customer acquisition cost tracking to support informed decision-making and operational effectiveness.
- Advance the organization’s data maturity and establish scalable reporting capabilities to accommodate future data sources and evolving business needs.
- Ensure seamless integration of the future-state platform with Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Microsoft Fabric to support comprehensive data connectivity and reporting.
