# Overview

fc currently operates with an established data platform as part of its direct-to-consumer operations in Europe. The platform is utilized by relevant teams responsible for managing customer data and analyzing churn patterns. As part of its existing infrastructure, fc leverages its internal data science capabilities to support ongoing design and discovery initiatives related to churn analytics.

At present, the client’s environment includes cloud-based infrastructure that connects various data sources and databases crucial for monitoring customer behavior. Teams working with these systems have access to reporting and analytics tools integrated within the platform, enabling them to process and interpret retention-related metrics. All systems are aligned with fc’s advisory and data science focus, reflecting the client’s readiness to optimize its current data landscape.
