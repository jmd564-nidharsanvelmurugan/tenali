# Business Impact

- **Enhanced Marketing ROI Transparency:** Establishes a unified Lead-to-AUM and CAC reporting framework, enabling PURE Financial Advisors to quantify marketing effectiveness and optimize spend allocation across acquisition channels.  
- **Data-Driven Growth Decisions:** Empowers leadership with granular insights into lead conversion and asset growth, supporting evidence-based decisions that improve client acquisition efficiency and AUM expansion.  
- **Operational Efficiency Gains:** Automates data ingestion and transformation processes across Salesforce, QuickBooks, and Azure Fabric, reducing manual reporting effort and cycle times by an estimated 40–50%.  
- **Improved Data Accuracy and Consistency:** Introduces standardized KPI definitions and a single source of truth for pipeline and CAC metrics, minimizing discrepancies and ensuring confidence in executive reporting.  
- **Scalable Reporting Foundation:** Builds a flexible, extensible data model within Microsoft Fabric and Power BI, allowing PURE to seamlessly integrate future data sources and scale analytics as the business grows.  
- **Private Equity Visibility and Value Creation:** Provides Lee Equity Partners with diligence-grade reporting on marketing efficiency and growth levers, strengthening portfolio oversight and supporting long-term value realization.  

By delivering these outcomes, this engagement will enable PURE Financial Advisors LLC to transform its marketing and pipeline reporting into a strategic growth engine—driving data maturity, operational efficiency, and sustained value creation in the competitive U.S. financial advisory market.
