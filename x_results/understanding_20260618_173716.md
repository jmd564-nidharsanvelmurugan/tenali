# Understanding

- PURE seeks to address limited visibility into the full Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) journey and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) performance within its current reporting environment.  
- Existing pipeline reporting lacks sufficient granularity, preventing detailed analysis of lead conversion and marketing effectiveness.  
- The absence of integrated CAC insights across systems limits the ability to measure true return on marketing spend.  
- Data ingestion from QuickBooks is not yet established, creating a gap in financial data needed for accurate CAC reporting.  
- Automated data ingestion and transformation workflows are required to reduce manual effort and ensure consistent reporting refreshes.  
- The enhanced reporting will be used by data and technology stakeholders, senior leadership, and marketing teams who rely on accurate, timely insights.  
- Mandatory integrations include Salesforce and QuickBooks to unify lead, client, and financial data for comprehensive L2A and CAC analysis.
