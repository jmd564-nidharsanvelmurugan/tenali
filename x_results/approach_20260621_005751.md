# Approach

PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC requires a robust solution to enhance its Lead-to-AUM (L2A) pipeline and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting, delivering granular and actionable insights into pipeline performance and marketing ROI. This engagement will follow a phased methodology designed to move from discovery and data assessment through to design, integration, and the buildout of a scalable reporting suite. The approach leverages established best practices for designing data models, automating data pipelines, and delivering business intelligence via Power BI within the Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse environments.

Our methodology entails close collaboration with client stakeholders, the Data, Technology, and Marketing teams. Each phase is structured to build sequentially—first gathering requirements and assessing data quality, then producing technical designs and mock-ups, and finally implementing integrations and automation. All deliverables are precisely aligned with business objectives, including integrated CAC insights, improved Power BI dashboards, and scalable pipeline reporting.

---

**Phase 3A: Discovery & Design, Duration: 3 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 to Week 3**  
**Summary:** Identify requirements, assess data gaps, and produce foundational designs/documentation for L2A and CAC reporting.

**Activities:**  
- Conduct stakeholder workshops to define reporting objectives and required KPIs  
- Assess current data sources (Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, QuickBooks) for completeness and quality  
- Document data gaps and integration requirements, especially for QuickBooks  
- Develop initial data model schema for L2A and CAC in alignment with business goals  
- Create Power BI mock-ups (up to 8 views) focused on pipeline and CAC insights  
- Prepare KPI Book detailing metric definitions and calculation logic  

---

**Phase 3B: Build & Integration, Duration: 8–12 weeks, Timeline: Week 4 to Week 15**  
**Summary:** Implement data model, automate data pipelines, and deliver the integrated reporting suite.

**Activities:**  
- Develop and validate data ingestion pipelines for batch processing in Microsoft Fabric, including QuickBooks integration  
- Automate data transformation workflows for L2A and CAC metrics  
- Design and build scalable reporting architecture in Azure Synapse  
- Finalize Power BI dashboards using business-approved mock-ups and KPI logic  
- Perform data quality assessments and resolve identified gaps  
- Prepare and deliver the technical approach documentation and Phase 3B Implementation Plan  

---

By following this methodical, phased approach, PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC will achieve a future-state solution that automates data collection, delivers integrated and granular insights, and enables leadership to make informed decisions on pipeline and marketing effectiveness. The engagement is designed for scalability and ongoing growth, ensuring that reporting capabilities adapt to new data sources and business requirements.
