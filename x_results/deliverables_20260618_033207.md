# Deliverables  

Throughout the engagement with Pokemnss, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:  

1. **“Metrics that Matter” Definition & Alignment**  
- Facilitation of an initial workshop to consolidate and align on the key performance metrics that best represent business performance and growth.  

- Documentation of agreed metric definitions, calculation methodologies, and data sources to ensure consistency across reporting.  

- Creation of a reference guide summarizing the “metrics that matter” to inform subsequent data model design and reporting outputs.  

2. **Conceptual Data Model Design**  
- Development of a comprehensive conceptual data model capturing all in-scope data sources, entities, and relationships.  

- Visualization of end-to-end data flows from source systems through to output tables, ensuring scalability and flexibility for future analytics use cases.  

- Documentation of data lineage and dependencies to support transparent governance and future platform build activities.  

3. **KPI Feasibility & Data Gap Assessment**  
- Evaluation of data availability, granularity, and quality across systems to determine the feasibility of reporting on defined KPIs.  

- Identification of data gaps, inconsistencies, and process limitations impacting metric accuracy and completeness.  

- Development of a remediation plan outlining required data capture enhancements, process changes, or system updates.  

4. **Target-State Data Platform Blueprint**  
- Definition of the target-state architecture for the data platform, including integration layers, data storage, and reporting components.  

- Mapping of data flows and control points to ensure traceability and auditability across the data lifecycle.  

- Recommendations for scalable design principles to support future automation and advanced analytics capabilities.  

5. **Dashboard Mock-Ups & Reporting Design**  
- Design of visual mock-ups for priority dashboards and reports aligned to the agreed “metrics that matter.”  

- Definition of layout, structure, and functionality requirements to guide future implementation.  

- Documentation of user interaction flows and visualization standards to ensure consistency and usability across reporting outputs.  

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with Pokemnss's leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
