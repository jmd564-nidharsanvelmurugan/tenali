# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with Pure Financial LLC, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

1. Data Platform Design Blueprint  
   - Comprehensive documentation outlining the recommended data platform architecture suitable for B2C financial services.  
   - Detailed specifications of core platform components, data flows, and integration points.  
   - Visual diagrams illustrating system interactions and data pipeline architecture.  
   - Delivered as a presentation and technical report at the conclusion of the discovery phase.

2. Revenue Bridge and Pipeline Reporting Framework  
   - Framework for structuring revenue bridge and pipeline reporting within the new data platform.  
   - Definition of key metrics, dimensions, and calculation methodologies.  
   - Sample dashboard wireframes and reporting templates.  
   - Delivered as a report and annotated design artifacts during the solution design phase.

3. Operational Reporting Requirements Document  
   - Requirements gathering documentation capturing specific operational reporting needs unique to Pure Financial LLC.  
   - Stakeholder-validated list of KPIs and reporting use cases for operational teams.  
   - Mapping of data sources and reporting frequencies for each operational report.  
   - Delivered as a structured requirements document during the discovery phase.

4. Implementation Roadmap  
   - Step-by-step plan for implementing the recommended data platform, reporting frameworks, and automation processes.  
   - Sequenced milestones and estimated timelines for each project phase.  
   - Identification of dependencies, resource needs, and potential risks.  
   - Delivered as a project plan and Gantt chart at the close of the design phase.

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with Pure Financial LLC's leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
