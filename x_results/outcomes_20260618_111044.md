# Outcomes

This engagement will deliver measurable business impact and strategic value by enabling PURE Financial Advisors LLC to gain granular visibility into its Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) pipeline and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) performance. The initiative aligns with PURE’s B2C financial advisory model and the objectives of key stakeholders, including Lee Equity Partners, by strengthening data‑driven decision‑making and marketing ROI measurement across the U.S. operations.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Granular Lead‑to‑AUM Visibility:** The new L2A data model will provide detailed insights into each stage of the client acquisition journey, allowing leadership to identify conversion bottlenecks and optimize marketing and sales strategies.  
- **Accurate Customer Acquisition Cost Measurement:** Integrated CAC reporting will enable PURE to precisely track marketing spend effectiveness, improving budget allocation and ROI analysis across campaigns.  
- **Automated and Reliable Reporting Processes:** Automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines will eliminate manual reporting inefficiencies, ensuring consistent, timely, and accurate Power BI dashboards for leadership review.  
- **Improved Marketing ROI and Decision‑Making:** Enhanced analytics will empower marketing and executive teams to make evidence‑based decisions on lead generation investments, directly linking spend to AUM growth outcomes.  
- **Strengthened Data Maturity and Scalability:** Establishing a robust, scalable data foundation within Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse will support future data sources and reporting needs, driving long‑term growth and operational efficiency.  
- **Reduced Risk of Limited Visibility:** By resolving current gaps in pipeline and CAC reporting, PURE will mitigate the risk of under‑informed marketing decisions and ensure sustained transparency into performance metrics.
