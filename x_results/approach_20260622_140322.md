# Approach

PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC is embarking on a data-centric initiative to enhance its pipeline reporting and deliver actionable insights into Lead-to-AUM (L2A) conversion and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC). Our methodology leverages industry best practices in data modeling, ETL pipeline automation, and dashboard design, ensuring scalable and reliable analytics. The engagement will be organized into two distinct phases, centering on design, integration, and build activities, and adapted to PURE’s enterprise data platform and reporting requirements.

Each phase is structured to address specific business needs, align with the client's operational timeline, and deliver tangible artifacts. By integrating Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks within the Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse platforms, we will enable batch data ingestion and advanced Power BI reporting, maximizing pipeline visibility and marketing ROI for all stakeholders.

---

**Phase 3A: Design & Discovery, Duration: 3 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 to Week 3**  
**Summary:** This phase focuses on establishing requirements, designing the L2A/CAC data model, and identifying gaps for successful reporting and integration with QuickBooks and Salesforce.

**Activities:**  
- Conduct workshops with data, technology, and marketing teams to define reporting requirements and KPIs  
- Perform a comprehensive Data Gap Assessment across Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks  
- Design the L2A and CAC data model, including KPI definitions and calculation logic  
- Develop Power BI mock-ups (limited to 8 views) to visualize reporting outputs and user experience  
- Document the Technical Approach for QuickBooks integration and automated data pipelines  
- Prepare the Phase 3B Implementation Plan with project milestones and dependencies  

---

**Phase 3B: Integration & Build, Duration: 8–12 weeks, Timeline: Week 4 to Week 15**  
**Summary:** This phase delivers the automated data pipelines, QuickBooks integration, and the full Power BI reporting suite, enabling batch data processing and scalable analytics.

**Activities:**  
- Build and deploy batch ETL pipelines on Microsoft Fabric to automate data ingestion and transformation  
- Integrate QuickBooks data, ensuring robust mapping to L2A and CAC metrics  
- Validate data quality across all sources and perform remediation for reliable reporting  
- Configure and test the Power BI reporting suite for L2A and CAC dashboards (8 pages/views)  
- Collaborate with stakeholder teams for user acceptance testing (UAT) and refinements  
- Document solution architecture, operational guides, and maintenance procedures  

---

By following this structured, phased approach, PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC will attain granular visibility into its customer journey and marketing investments, with scalable and automated analytics. The phased engagement ensures alignment with business objectives, guarantees delivery of actionable insights, and advances PURE’s data maturity and reporting capabilities.
