# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with PURE Financial Advisors LLC, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

1. **KPI Book**  
- Comprehensive documentation of Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) metrics, including definitions, calculation logic, and data sources.  
- Clear mapping of KPIs to business objectives and reporting use cases.  
- Establishment of standardized measurement frameworks for consistent reporting across teams.  

2. **Power BI Mock‑Ups (8 Views)**  
- Design of up to eight Power BI dashboard views illustrating L2A and CAC insights.  
- Visualization of key metrics, conversion funnels, and marketing ROI trends.  
- Wireframes capturing layout, navigation, and user experience requirements for leadership and marketing teams.  

3. **Data Model Design**  
- Detailed schema for the integrated L2A and CAC data model connecting Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks.  
- Logical and physical data model diagrams defining relationships, hierarchies, and data flows.  
- Documentation of data transformation logic and integration points within Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse.  

4. **Data Gap Assessment**  
- Identification of missing or incomplete data elements required for accurate L2A and CAC reporting.  
- Evaluation of data quality, consistency, and completeness across source systems.  
- Recommendations for remediation and data enrichment to support reliable analytics.  

5. **Technical Approach Document**  
- Detailed description of the technical architecture and integration strategy for QuickBooks ingestion and automated data pipelines.  
- Specification of batch processing workflows within Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse.  
- Outline of data governance, refresh cadence, and performance considerations for scalable reporting.  

6. **Phase 3B Implementation Plan**  
- Roadmap outlining activities, milestones, and dependencies for the build and deployment phase.  
- Sequenced timeline covering development, testing, and rollout of the L2A and CAC reporting suite.  
- Resource plan and risk mitigation framework ensuring smooth transition from design to implementation.  

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with PURE Financial Advisors LLC's leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
