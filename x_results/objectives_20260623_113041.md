# Objectives

- Establish a fully designed exit reporting suite that delivers clear equity narratives and a prioritized KPI framework to guide investor communications and support the sale process.
- Automate data ingestion and recurring refresh pipelines from core source systems to ensure timely and reliable transaction reporting.
- Enable leadership to access investor-grade visibility into key business metrics that substantiate the equity narrative and underpin exit readiness.
- Provide intuitive dashboards featuring clear KPI definitions, calculation logic, and user-friendly navigation, enhancing stakeholder confidence and usability.
- Standardize exit reporting processes and prepare data infrastructure to meet investor scrutiny and transaction requirements.
- Ensure the solution functions as a lasting business asset, with technology choices supporting ongoing automation and scalability post-transaction.
- Design the data model and reporting suite for extensibility, allowing integration with additional data sources and consistent refresh capability beyond the exit event.
- Integrate the platform with Finance System, CRM, ERP, and Qlik Warehouse, with flexibility for future tooling options subject to feasibility assessment.
