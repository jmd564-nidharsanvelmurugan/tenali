# Understanding

- PURE seeks to address limited visibility and granularity in its existing pipeline reporting, particularly around the full Lead-to-AUM (L2A) journey and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) metrics.  
- Current reporting lacks integrated CAC insights, preventing a clear understanding of marketing efficiency and conversion performance.  
- Data from key systems such as QuickBooks is not yet ingested, creating gaps in financial data needed for accurate CAC analysis.  
- Manual or fragmented data processes reduce consistency and reliability in pipeline and acquisition reporting.  
- The business requires an enhanced L2A and CAC data model with automated data ingestion and transformation to improve reporting accuracy.  
- Automated workflows are needed to streamline data ingestion and transformation for L2A and CAC reporting.  
- Primary users include data and technology stakeholders, senior leadership, and marketing teams who rely on accurate, timely insights.  
- Mandatory integrations include Salesforce and QuickBooks to ensure complete and connected reporting across lead, client, and financial data.
