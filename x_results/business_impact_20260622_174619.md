# Business Impact

- **Optimized Marketing ROI:** By developing enhanced Lead-to-AUM (L2A) pipeline and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting, PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC will be able to connect marketing spend directly to asset growth, enabling the business to reallocate resources to the most effective channels and improve marketing ROI.

- **Increased Operational Efficiency:** Automating data ingestion and transformation processes across Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks will significantly reduce manual data handling and minimize reporting errors, freeing data and marketing teams to focus on value-added activities and reducing time spent on report preparation by up to 50%.

- **Granular Pipeline and Cost Insights:** Improved Power BI dashboards provide senior leadership with a detailed view of the lead journey through to asset acquisition and associated costs, equipping stakeholders with accurate and timely KPIs such as Lead-to-AUM conversion rates and real-time CAC. This enables better forecasting and strategic decision-making.

- **Churn Prevention and Client Retention:** The integration of churn prevention analytics into reporting allows marketing and relationship teams to proactively identify and address at-risk clients, protecting existing assets under management and minimizing revenue loss due to attrition.

- **Strategic Scalability:** The solution’s architecture on Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse offers a scalable framework, supporting future integrations and growth without requiring repeated technology re-investment. This positions PURE for long-term expansion and evolving reporting needs.

- **Enhanced Stakeholder Confidence:** Delivering precise, actionable data and improved KPI visibility enables data-driven discussions with investment partners and executives, fostering trust and transparency in marketing, pipeline, and retention strategies.

These impacts collectively enable PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC to translate data into measurable business outcomes—optimizing costs, retaining valuable clients, and driving growth through smarter, insight-driven decisions. The project lays the foundation for a data-mature organization with the agility to respond to market opportunities and shareholder expectations.
