# Objectives

- Deliver a comprehensive Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data model and reporting suite to provide granular insights into pipeline performance and marketing ROI.
- Automate data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes to enhance reporting efficiency and consistency.
- Enable leadership to gain a detailed understanding of the journey from lead to AUM and the return on marketing spend.
- Improve Power BI dashboards, ensuring clarity in KPI definitions and visualizations for end users.
- Enhance pipeline reporting and customer acquisition cost tracking to support better-informed business decisions.
- Establish a scalable data platform and reporting solution capable of supporting future data sources and evolving reporting needs.
- Ensure seamless integration of the platform with Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Microsoft Fabric.
