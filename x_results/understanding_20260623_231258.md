# Understanding

- Need for enhanced Lead-to-AUM (L2A) pipeline and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting to provide more granular insights into the client journey and marketing ROI.
- Existing pipeline reporting lacks sufficient detail and does not deliver integrated CAC analytics.
- Requirement for an improved L2A and CAC data model to drive accurate, insightful metrics.
- Mandatory integration with QuickBooks and Salesforce to enable comprehensive data ingestion and unified reporting.
- Automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines necessary to ensure timely and efficient L2A and CAC reporting.
- Data quality assessment is required to support the accuracy and reliability of CAC insights.
- Data, technology, senior leadership, and marketing team members will directly utilize the new analytics and reporting capabilities.
