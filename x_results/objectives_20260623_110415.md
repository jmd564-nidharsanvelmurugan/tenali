# Objectives

- Establish a comprehensive Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data model that delivers granular insights into pipeline performance and marketing return on investment.
- Automate data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes to ensure accuracy, timeliness, and operational efficiency.
- Enable leadership to access detailed, actionable insights on the journey from lead generation to asset under management, supporting informed decision-making.
- Enhance user experience with improved Power BI dashboards featuring clear KPI definitions and intuitive visualizations.
- Strengthen pipeline reporting and customer acquisition cost tracking processes to drive better measurement and optimization of marketing efforts.
- Build a scalable data platform and reporting solution capable of supporting future data sources, expanded reporting needs, and long-term business growth.
- Integrate seamlessly with Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and the Microsoft Fabric environment to ensure unified and reliable data access.
