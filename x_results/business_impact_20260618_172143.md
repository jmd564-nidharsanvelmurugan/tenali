# Business Impact

- **Granular Marketing ROI Visibility:** Establishes a unified Lead‑to‑AUM and Customer Acquisition Cost reporting framework, enabling Pure Financial Advisors to precisely measure marketing effectiveness and optimize spend allocation across channels.  
- **Data‑Driven Growth Decisions:** Empowers leadership and Lee Equity Partners with actionable insights into conversion performance, supporting strategic decisions that drive AUM growth and client acquisition efficiency.  
- **Operational Efficiency Through Automation:** Automates data ingestion and transformation across Salesforce, QuickBooks, and Tamarac, reducing manual reporting effort and cycle times by an estimated 40–50%.  
- **Enhanced Data Accuracy and Consistency:** Introduces standardized KPI definitions and a single source of truth within Microsoft Fabric, improving confidence in performance metrics and eliminating cross‑departmental discrepancies.  
- **Scalable Reporting Architecture:** Builds a future‑ready data model and Power BI suite that can seamlessly scale to new data sources and reporting needs as Pure expands its client base and service offerings.  
- **Improved Financial Transparency:** Integrates financial and marketing data to provide a clear view of acquisition costs relative to AUM growth, strengthening accountability and supporting investor reporting requirements.  

By delivering these outcomes, this engagement will enable Pure Financial Advisors LLC to transform its pipeline and marketing performance management, creating a scalable, insight‑driven foundation that supports sustained growth, operational excellence, and long‑term value creation in the competitive U.S. wealth management market.
