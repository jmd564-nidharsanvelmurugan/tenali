# Business Impact

- **Enhanced Marketing ROI Visibility:** The new Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting suite will provide Pure Financial Advisors with granular insights into marketing effectiveness, enabling leadership to allocate spend toward the highest-performing channels and campaigns.  
- **Operational Efficiency Through Automation:** Automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines will eliminate manual reporting steps, reducing time spent on data preparation by up to 50% and improving reporting accuracy across Salesforce, QuickBooks, and Tamarac sources.  
- **Data-Driven Growth Decisions:** With unified L2A and CAC metrics in Power BI, senior leadership and marketing teams can make faster, evidence-based decisions on lead conversion strategies and asset growth, directly supporting Pure’s B2C expansion goals.  
- **Improved Data Quality and Consistency:** Integration of QuickBooks and standardized KPI definitions will create a single source of truth for financial and marketing data, reducing inconsistencies and enabling reliable performance tracking across departments.  
- **Scalable Reporting Foundation:** The enhanced data model and Microsoft Fabric-based architecture will support future data sources and reporting needs, ensuring scalability as Pure’s client base and AUM continue to grow.  
- **Private Equity Stakeholder Confidence:** By delivering diligence-grade reporting on pipeline performance and marketing ROI, the initiative strengthens transparency and supports Lee Equity Partners’ oversight of portfolio value creation.  

By delivering these outcomes, this engagement will enable Pure Financial Advisors LLC to transform its pipeline and marketing performance management approach, establishing a scalable, data-driven foundation that underpins long-term growth and value creation in the U.S. financial advisory marketplace.
