# Outcomes

The engagement with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC will drive measurable business impact by equipping key stakeholders with deeper insights into both pipeline performance and marketing return on investment (ROI). By implementing a comprehensive Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting suite, the organization will achieve improved decision-making, greater data maturity, and scalable reporting capabilities that directly support business growth in the financial advisory sector.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Granular Pipeline Performance Insights:** Enable leadership and operational teams to analyze every stage of the client journey from lead generation through asset acquisition, supporting more effective conversion strategies.
- **Actionable Marketing ROI Reporting:** Deliver integrated CAC data allowing the marketing team to precisely track return on spend and optimize future acquisition investments based on robust, automated analytics.
- **Automated Data Processes:** Reduce manual effort and error risk through automated data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh, ensuring ongoing accuracy and timeliness of pipeline metrics.
- **Integrated KPI Dashboard:** Provide a Power BI dashboard with clearly defined, trusted conversion and CAC KPIs, supporting transparent, real-time performance monitoring and decision support.
- **Scalable Reporting Platform:** Establish a future-ready architecture that seamlessly integrates with Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Microsoft Fabric, allowing for expansion and adaptability as data needs evolve.
- **Enhanced Leadership Decision-Making:** Empower senior management with accurate and insightful L2A and CAC analytics, minimizing blind spots in marketing effectiveness and pipeline management, and reducing risk associated with limited visibility.

Success will be measured by the accuracy, automation, and actionable nature of the reporting solution, with key metrics including Lead-to-AUM conversion rates and Customer Acquisition Cost KPIs. Achieving these outcomes will strengthen PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC’s ability to make informed, data-driven decisions, driving sustained growth and operational excellence.
