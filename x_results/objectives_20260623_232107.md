# Objectives

- Establish a comprehensive Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data model and reporting suite for granular analysis of the sales pipeline and marketing ROI.
- Automate data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes to ensure timely and accurate insights.
- Deliver enhanced Power BI dashboards that provide clear KPI definitions, improved visualization, and a streamlined user experience.
- Enable leadership to gain deeper insights into the journey from lead acquisition to assets under management and the effectiveness of marketing investments.
- Improve business processes related to pipeline reporting and customer acquisition cost tracking for more informed decision-making.
- Build a data platform that is scalable and adaptable to support additional data sources and expanding reporting needs as the organization grows.
- Ensure seamless integration with Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Microsoft Fabric to unify operational and financial data.
