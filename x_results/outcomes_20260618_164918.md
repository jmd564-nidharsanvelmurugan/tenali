# Outcomes

This engagement will deliver measurable business impact by enabling PURE Financial Advisors LLC to gain deeper visibility into its Lead-to-AUM (L2A) pipeline and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) performance. By leveraging existing Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse infrastructure, the initiative aligns with PURE’s B2C business model and the strategic objectives of both senior leadership and Lee Equity Partners to drive data maturity and optimize marketing ROI.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Granular Lead-to-AUM Visibility:** The new data model and reporting suite will provide detailed insights into each stage of the lead journey, allowing leadership to identify conversion bottlenecks and improve overall pipeline efficiency.  
- **Accurate Customer Acquisition Cost Measurement:** Integration of QuickBooks and Salesforce data will enable precise calculation of CAC metrics, supporting informed decisions on marketing spend and resource allocation.  
- **Automated and Reliable Reporting Processes:** Batch-based data ingestion and transformation pipelines will ensure consistent, timely, and error-free updates to Power BI dashboards, reducing manual intervention and improving operational reliability.  
- **Improved Marketing ROI Analysis:** Enhanced L2A and CAC reporting will allow the marketing team to evaluate campaign effectiveness and optimize strategies based on data-driven insights into cost per acquisition and asset growth outcomes.  
- **Strengthened Data-Driven Decision Making:** The unified reporting environment will empower senior leadership with accurate, actionable intelligence to guide investment, growth, and client acquisition strategies across the organization.  
- **Foundation for Scalable Data Maturity:** Establishing a robust and extensible data model will position PURE for future integration of additional data sources and advanced analytics capabilities, supporting long-term scalability and continuous improvement.
