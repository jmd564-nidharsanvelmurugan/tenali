# Approach

This engagement with VONQ is focused on delivering a robust, design-led foundation for an exit reporting suite that fully supports the organisation’s near-term sale process. Our methodology emphasises rapid alignment with key stakeholders, data-driven validation of reporting requirements, and the systematic design of artefacts—providing a clear blueprint for the subsequent technical build. By combining industry best practices in data strategy with pragmatic stakeholder engagement, the approach ensures that VONQ’s reporting suite is scalable, actionable, and specifically tailored for investor-grade communications.

The process is structured into a single Discovery & Design phase, lasting four weeks. This phase includes workshops, data assessment, conceptual modelling, and iterative playback sessions to confirm findings and design artefacts. All activities are sequenced to ensure smooth information flow, mitigate project risks, and enable well-informed decisions in preparation for Phase 2 implementation. Deliverables from this phase will be production-ready design artefacts, ensuring stakeholder buy-in and readiness for a seamless handover into execution.

---

**Phase 1: Discovery & Design, Duration: 4 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 (29 June 2026) to Week 4 (24 July 2026)**  
**Summary:** Define exit reporting requirements, design the reporting suite, and provide prioritised recommendations and a detailed implementation roadmap.

**Activities:**
- Conduct project kick-off, data access validation, and alignment meeting with VONQ and stakeholder representatives.  
- Facilitate stakeholder workshops to define the equity narrative, key value drivers, and prioritised KPI framework for investor reporting.  
- Document current- and future-state data flows; perform a data feasibility assessment across the Finance, CRM, ERP systems, and the Qlik Warehouse.  
- Develop and socialise static dashboard mock-ups, showing proposed metrics, layout, and navigation structure, and refine based on feedback.  
- Define a conceptual data model, including KPI calculation logic and recommendations for scalable tooling and infrastructure enhancements.  
- Produce a comprehensive implementation roadmap for Phase 2, detailing priority use cases, delivery dependencies, identified data gaps, and actionable remediation steps.  

---

In summary, this phased approach enables VONQ to thoroughly define its reporting vision, validate technical feasibility, and document the critical design artefacts required for a sale-ready, investor-grade reporting suite—all within a rapid four-week window. The deliverables ensure transparency, alignment, and a clear path to Phase 2 execution, providing lasting value to VONQ’s exit process.
