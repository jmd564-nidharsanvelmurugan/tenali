# Understanding

- The client must design and build a scalable exit reporting suite—including a refreshable data cube, dashboards, and documentation—to support the M&A sale process and facilitate investor communications.  
- Current pain points include lack of investor-ready reporting, potential data gaps across source systems, and limitations in the existing Qlik Warehouse in supporting all required KPIs for the equity narrative.  
- Some source systems do not have automated connections into the Qlik Warehouse, necessitating manual data ingestion and establishing recurring refresh processes for sources that need updating during the transaction.  
- Required capabilities include equity narrative definition, a prioritized KPI framework, dashboard mock-ups, data feasibility assessment, conceptual data model design, tooling recommendations, and a phased implementation roadmap.  
- Automated workflows are needed to enable data ingestion and refresh pipelines from source systems into the Qlik Warehouse, especially for sources requiring continual updates during the transaction.  
- User roles include senior leadership, exit advisors (Barclays), and potential acquirers/investors, all of whom require investor-grade visibility into key business metrics.  
- Mandatory integrations encompass Finance System, CRM, ERP, and legacy sources providing a minimum of three years of trended data into the Qlik Warehouse.  
- Constraints impacting the initiative include exit timeline requirements, stakeholder availability, timely data access provisioning, and concerns regarding the quality and completeness of the existing Qlik Warehouse data.
