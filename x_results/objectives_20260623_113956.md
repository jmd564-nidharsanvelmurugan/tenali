# Objectives

- Deliver a fully designed exit reporting suite with clear equity narratives and a prioritised KPI framework to guide the M&A sale process.
- Establish automated, refreshable data ingestion pipelines from all primary and legacy source systems to ensure recurring and reliable transaction reporting.
- Provide leadership with investor-grade visibility into key business metrics that underpin robust, data-driven equity narratives.
- Create intuitive dashboards that offer clear KPI definitions, calculation logic, and streamlined navigation aligned with investor expectations.
- Standardise exit reporting and KPI definitions to enhance the organisation’s readiness for investor scrutiny and facilitate efficient reporting processes.
- Design a scalable and extensible data model and reporting suite that can accommodate additional data sources and ongoing refresh post-transaction.
- Ensure seamless integration of the future platform with Finance System, CRM, ERP, and Qlik Warehouse as a foundation for future technology choices and scalability.
