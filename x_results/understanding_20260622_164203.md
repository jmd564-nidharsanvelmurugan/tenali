# Understanding

- Manual processes for revenue bridge analysis, pipeline tracking, and operational reporting create inefficiencies and increase risk of errors.
- Lack of a unified data platform results in fragmented data management, limiting visibility into key commercial metrics.
- No existing infrastructure causes delays in decision-making and reduces scalability for future growth initiatives.
- Current workflows lack automation, requiring redundant effort from business users and analysts.
- Operational teams are unable to access real-time data, negatively impacting responsiveness and performance monitoring.
- Requirements include centralized data access, automated reporting capabilities, and robust pipeline tracking functionality.
- The system must support diverse user roles including business analysts, financial managers, and operational leaders.
- As a private equity portfolio company, enhanced data controls and compliance with financial reporting standards are required.
