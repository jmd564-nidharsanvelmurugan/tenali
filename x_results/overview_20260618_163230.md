# Overview

PURE Financial Advisors’ data, technology, and marketing teams currently manage the firm’s lead pipeline and customer acquisition reporting processes. These functions rely on a connected ecosystem of platforms including Salesforce for CRM, Tamarac for portfolio management, Azure Blob Storage for data hosting, Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse for data processing, and Power BI for analytics and visualization. QuickBooks is also part of the financial data landscape and is planned for integration into the broader reporting environment.

The existing reporting suite is built in Power BI, drawing from multiple data sources—Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks—to support operational and performance insights. The firm’s infrastructure is anchored in Microsoft’s cloud environment, leveraging Fabric and Synapse to store and process data. While manual processes are not specified, integration challenges currently center on connecting and ingesting data from QuickBooks and ensuring data quality for customer acquisition cost reporting.
