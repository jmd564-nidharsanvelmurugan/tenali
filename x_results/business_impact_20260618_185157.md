# Business Impact

- **Enhanced Marketing ROI Visibility:** The new Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting suite will provide PURE Financial Advisors with granular insights into marketing effectiveness, enabling leadership to allocate spend toward the highest-performing channels and improve ROI measurement accuracy.  
- **Operational Efficiency Through Automation:** Automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines will eliminate manual data preparation, reducing reporting cycle times by up to 50% and freeing data and marketing teams to focus on analysis and strategic decision-making.  
- **Data-Driven Growth Decisions:** By integrating Salesforce, QuickBooks, and Tamarac data into a unified model, PURE will gain a single source of truth for pipeline performance, supporting more confident decisions on lead conversion strategies and AUM growth initiatives.  
- **Improved Financial Transparency:** CAC and L2A metrics will be standardized across the organization, ensuring consistent definitions and reliable tracking of acquisition costs and conversion rates—critical for Lee Equity Partners’ oversight and investment performance evaluation.  
- **Scalable Reporting Foundation:** The Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse-based architecture will create a scalable data environment capable of supporting future data sources and advanced analytics, positioning PURE for long-term data maturity and expansion.  
- **Leadership Insight Enablement:** Enhanced Power BI dashboards will deliver clear, actionable KPIs to senior leadership, improving visibility into pipeline health and enabling proactive management of marketing and acquisition performance.  

By delivering these outcomes, this engagement will enable PURE Financial Advisors LLC to transform its pipeline and marketing performance management approach, establishing a robust, scalable data foundation that drives sustained growth, operational efficiency, and long-term value creation in the competitive U.S. financial advisory market.
