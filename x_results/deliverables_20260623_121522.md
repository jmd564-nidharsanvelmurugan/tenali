# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with VONQ, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

1. **Equity Narratives Definition**
   - Clearly articulated equity narratives tailored to the exit and investor reporting context.
   - Documentation of business value drivers underpinning each narrative.
   - Delivered as a written report and supporting presentation deck at the end of Week 1.

2. **Metrics that Matter KPI Framework**
   - Prioritized framework of commercial, financial, and operational KPIs essential for the equity narrative.
   - KPI calculation logic, clear definitions, and source mapping for each metric.
   - Provided as a structured spreadsheet and supporting documentation by the end of Week 2.

3. **Dashboard Mock-ups**
   - Static wireframes illustrating proposed dashboard layouts, views, and key navigation structures.
   - Visual representation of prioritized KPIs and narrative alignment.
   - Delivered as PDF and accessible design files (e.g., Figma or PowerPoint) by the end of Week 3.

4. **Data Feasibility Assessment**
   - Assessment report detailing current-state data availability, data gaps, and limitations across source systems.
   - Playback session of findings, including recommendations for addressing critical data issues.
   - Delivered as a written report and executive summary presentation at the end of Week 2.

5. **Conceptual Data Model Design**
   - High-level, scalable data model design leveraging the existing Qlik Warehouse as the foundation.
   - Visual schematics, entity relationship diagrams, and data flow documentation.
   - Provided as diagrams and design document by the end of Week 3.

6. **Data Infrastructure and Tooling Recommendations**
   - Evaluation of viable data tooling and infrastructure options to support refreshable KPI reporting.
   - Options analysis, including pros, cons, and trade-offs for Qlik and potential alternative tools.
   - Delivered as a comparative recommendations report by the end of Week 3.

7. **Implementation Roadmap for Phase 2**
   - Phased execution plan outlining priority use cases, dependencies, risks, and key milestones.
   - Actionable timeline supporting the transition to technical development and dashboard build.
   - Provided as a Gantt chart and supporting roadmap document at project close (end of Week 4).

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with VONQ’s leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
