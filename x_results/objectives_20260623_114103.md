# Objectives

- Establish a fully designed exit reporting suite supporting clear equity narratives, aligned KPI frameworks, and conceptual data models.
- Automate refreshable data ingestion pipelines from Finance, CRM, and ERP systems, ensuring recurring updates for transaction reporting.
- Deliver intuitive dashboards with standardized KPI definitions, calculation logic, and navigation structure to enhance user experience for leadership, advisors, and potential investors.
- Provide investor-grade visibility into key commercial, financial, and operational metrics that underpin the sale process and equity narrative.
- Enable scalable data model and reporting architecture extensible to additional data sources and adaptable for ongoing refresh beyond the transaction.
- Position solution outputs as lasting business assets with technology choices facilitating future automation and post-transaction scalability.
