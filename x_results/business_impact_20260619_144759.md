# Business Impact

- **Data‑Driven Growth Decisions:** Unified L2A and CAC reporting will give Poekmon’s leadership and Lee Equity Partners clear visibility into how marketing spend converts to assets under management, enabling smarter allocation of growth investments.  
- **Operational Efficiency and Automation:** Automated data pipelines across Salesforce, Tamarac, and QuickBooks will reduce manual reporting effort by up to 50%, improving accuracy and freeing teams to focus on analysis rather than data preparation.  
- **Accurate Customer Acquisition Cost Tracking:** Integration of financial data from QuickBooks with lead data from Salesforce will deliver precise CAC insights, helping marketing teams optimize campaigns and potentially lower acquisition costs by 10–15%.  
- **Enhanced Marketing ROI Visibility:** Power BI dashboards will link marketing activities directly to asset growth, allowing real‑time evaluation of campaign performance and faster adjustment of marketing strategies.  
- **Scalable and Future‑Ready Data Model:** The new data architecture built on Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse will provide a single source of truth that can easily accommodate new data sources and business lines as Poekmon expands.  
- **Improved Decision Velocity:** Centralized, trusted metrics will enable leadership to make faster, evidence‑based decisions, accelerating strategic planning and investment cycles.  

By delivering these outcomes, this engagement will enable Poekmon to transform its marketing and sales performance management, creating a lasting data foundation that supports sustained growth, operational excellence, and long‑term value creation in the competitive U.S. financial advisory market.
