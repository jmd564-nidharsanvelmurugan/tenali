# Approach

The engagement with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC is designed to deliver a robust Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting solution, leveraging advanced data integration and analytics capabilities. By implementing an automated and scalable data pipeline across Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks—culminating in enhanced Power BI dashboards—this solution will empower stakeholders with actionable insights into pipeline performance and the return on marketing investments. Our methodology centers on a collaborative, phased approach that emphasizes requirements gathering, technical design, and seamless implementation, ensuring all project objectives are met within the agreed timeline.

The engagement will proceed in two main phases, each with clearly defined deliverables and milestones. In Phase 3A (Design), our team will focus on understanding key business processes, documenting KPI logic, mapping data sources, and creating functional mock-ups and a technical approach. Phase 3B (Build & Implementation) will operationalize the design through integration, automation, and deployment efforts, while addressing data quality and inter-system dependencies, particularly the QuickBooks integration. Activities and effort for each phase are detailed below.

---

**Phase 3A: Design & Planning, Duration: 3 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 to Week 3**  
**Summary:** Conduct discovery sessions, define metrics and data models, assess gaps, produce mock-ups, and prepare for implementation.

**Activities:**  
- Facilitate discovery workshops with business and technical stakeholders to document L2A and CAC requirements and workflows  
- Develop a comprehensive KPI Book detailing metric definitions, calculation logic, and sourcing requirements  
- Analyze and map data from Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks to the enhanced reporting requirements  
- Create Power BI mock-ups (limited to 8 views) reflecting required dashboards and visualizations  
- Conduct a data gap assessment and document the technical approach for batch processing pipelines and QuickBooks integration  
- Develop the Phase 3B Implementation Plan, including dependencies, resource needs, and detailed timelines  

---

**Phase 3B: Build & Implementation, Duration: 8–12 weeks, Timeline: Week 4 to Week 15**  
**Summary:** Integrate new data sources, automate ingestion and transformation, implement the L2A and CAC reporting suite, and ensure data quality.

**Activities:**  
- Design and implement the L2A and CAC data model using Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse, integrating with Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Azure Blob Storage  
- Develop and automate batch data ingestion and transformation pipelines, focusing on scalable, maintainable architecture  
- Execute data quality assessments and resolve identified data issues critical for CAC and pipeline reporting accuracy  
- Configure and enhance the Power BI reporting suite to deliver the required insights, dashboards, and KPI logic  
- Ensure user acceptance testing (UAT) with key stakeholders and iterate based on feedback  
- Complete project documentation and transition knowledge to Pure’s internal teams for sustainable operations and future scalability  

---

This phased approach ensures that PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC receives clear, actionable, and high-quality deliverables aligned with business objectives at each stage. By prioritizing data model design, stakeholder validation, and automated integration, we will provide the foundation for scalable and transparent reporting that drives strategic decision-making and maximizes marketing ROI.
