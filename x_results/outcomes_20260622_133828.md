# Outcomes

The proposed engagement will significantly enhance PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC’s ability to measure, understand, and improve its lead-to-asset (L2A) conversion pipeline and customer acquisition cost (CAC) metrics. By automating data integration and enabling robust, actionable reporting, Pure will gain deeper visibility into marketing ROI and pipeline effectiveness, empowering leadership to make informed, data-driven decisions that support growth and operational maturity.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Granular Pipeline Insights:** Achieve detailed visibility into each stage of the lead-to-AUM journey, allowing teams to identify bottlenecks and optimize engagement strategies based on precise conversion data.

- **Improved Marketing ROI Measurement:** Enable clear, accurate reporting on marketing spend effectiveness by directly tying investments to both lead generation and ultimate asset acquisition through integrated CAC analysis.

- **Automated, Reliable Reporting:** Reduce manual efforts and potential errors by automating data ingestion, transformation, and Power BI reporting refreshes, ensuring stakeholders have timely access to accurate KPIs.

- **Actionable Leadership Insights:** Empower senior leadership and marketing stakeholders with visualizations and dashboards that clarify key performance indicators—facilitating rapid, confident business decisions.

- **Scalable Analytical Foundation:** Establish a future-ready data model and reporting framework capable of adapting to new data sources and evolving business needs, supporting long-term growth and increased data maturity.

- **Risk Mitigation Through Enhanced Visibility:** Minimize the risks associated with limited pipeline and marketing transparency by delivering integrated insights that highlight gaps, trends, and opportunities across the business.
