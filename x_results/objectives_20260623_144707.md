# Objectives

- Achieve a comprehensive Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data model and reporting suite that delivers granular insights into pipeline performance and marketing ROI.
- Automate data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes to enable timely and accurate insights with reduced manual intervention.
- Provide senior leadership with detailed, actionable understanding of each stage in the client journey from lead generation to asset under management, and clear measurement of marketing effectiveness.
- Enhance user experience for data, technology, and marketing teams through improved Power BI dashboards offering clear KPI definitions and intuitive, actionable visualizations.
- Strengthen and modernize pipeline reporting and customer acquisition cost tracking to support more effective business decision making.
- Enable scalable and future-ready data and reporting platforms, capable of integrating new data sources and supporting PURE's ongoing business growth.
- Ensure seamless integration of key systems, specifically Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Microsoft Fabric, as foundational components of the unified data and reporting environment.
