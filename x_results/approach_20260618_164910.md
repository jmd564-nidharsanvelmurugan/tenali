# Approach

PURE Financial Advisors LLC operates in a highly competitive financial advisory and wealth management market, where data-driven insights into client acquisition and asset growth are critical to sustaining performance and optimizing marketing ROI. This engagement is designed to address PURE’s specific challenge of limited visibility into the Lead-to-AUM (L2A) pipeline and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) metrics by leveraging its existing Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse infrastructure. The approach focuses on integrating Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks data to deliver a unified, automated, and scalable reporting solution that empowers leadership with actionable insights.

Our methodology follows a structured, phased approach that ensures alignment with PURE’s business objectives, technical environment, and stakeholder expectations. Each phase builds upon the previous one—progressing from discovery and data assessment through to model design, mock-up development, and implementation planning. This ensures that the final solution is both technically robust and strategically aligned with PURE’s growth and data maturity goals.

---

**Phase 1: Discovery and Requirements Definition, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 1**

**Summary:** Establish a shared understanding of current systems, data sources, and reporting needs to define the foundation for the L2A and CAC solution.

**Activities:**  
- Conduct kickoff sessions with data, technology, and marketing teams to confirm scope and objectives.  
- Review existing documentation for Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Fabric, Synapse, and Power BI environments.  
- Identify key stakeholders and confirm data access requirements for all relevant systems.  
- Map current reporting workflows and dependencies related to pipeline and CAC reporting.  
- Define success criteria and confirm deliverables for the design phase.  

---

**Phase 2: Data Source Assessment and Gap Analysis, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 2**

**Summary:** Evaluate data quality, completeness, and integration readiness across all source systems to inform model design and ingestion strategy.

**Activities:**  
- Assess data structures and relationships within Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks.  
- Identify data quality issues and gaps impacting L2A and CAC reporting.  
- Document data lineage and transformation logic currently in use.  
- Recommend data cleaning and enrichment strategies for Phase 3B implementation.  
- Validate data availability for KPI calculation and reporting requirements.  

---

**Phase 3: Data Model and KPI Design, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 3**

**Summary:** Design the target-state L2A and CAC data model and define KPI logic to support accurate and consistent reporting.

**Activities:**  
- Develop conceptual and logical data models aligned with PURE’s reporting needs.  
- Define KPI formulas, data mappings, and dimensional hierarchies for L2A and CAC metrics.  
- Align KPI definitions with industry standards and internal reporting practices.  
- Validate model design with stakeholders to ensure business and technical alignment.  
- Document data model specifications for use in the implementation phase.  

---

**Phase 4: Power BI Mock-ups and Reporting Design, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 4**

**Summary:** Translate the data model and KPI definitions into visual reporting concepts through Power BI mock-ups.

**Activities:**  
- Design up to eight Power BI mock-ups illustrating L2A and CAC dashboards.  
- Incorporate user feedback from marketing and leadership teams to refine visualizations.  
- Ensure dashboards provide clear KPI definitions, drill-down capabilities, and actionable insights.  
- Validate alignment between mock-ups and data model outputs.  
- Finalize mock-ups for inclusion in the discovery deliverables.  

---

**Phase 5: Technical Approach and Implementation Planning, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 5**

**Summary:** Finalize the technical design and develop a detailed implementation roadmap for Phase 3B execution.

**Activities:**  
- Define the technical approach for QuickBooks integration and automated data ingestion pipelines.  
- Outline data transformation and refresh processes within Microsoft Fabric.  
- Develop the Phase 3B Implementation Plan, including sequencing, dependencies, and resource needs.  
- Consolidate findings into the KPI Book, Data Model Design, and Technical Approach deliverables.  
- Present final recommendations and roadmap to PURE’s leadership and key stakeholders.  

---

This structured, phased approach ensures that PURE Financial Advisors LLC’s L2A and CAC reporting capabilities are developed with precision, agility, and a clear focus on delivering measurable business outcomes. By progressing methodically from discovery through design and planning, the engagement provides a solid foundation for scalable, automated, and insight-driven reporting aligned with PURE’s strategic growth objectives.
