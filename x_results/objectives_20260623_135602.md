# Objectives

- Deliver a comprehensive Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data model and reporting suite that provides granular insights into pipeline performance and marketing ROI.
- Automate data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes to ensure timely and accurate reporting.
- Enable leadership to gain a clear and detailed understanding of the customer journey from lead to AUM, and precise measurement of marketing return on investment.
- Improve the user experience with enhanced Power BI dashboards featuring well-defined key performance indicators and intuitive visualizations.
- Strengthen pipeline reporting and customer acquisition cost tracking processes to support informed decision-making.
- Ensure the data platform and reporting solutions are scalable to accommodate future data sources and evolving reporting needs.
- Integrate seamlessly with Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Microsoft Fabric to consolidate data and support unified reporting.
