# Objectives

- Achieve a fully designed exit reporting suite that provides clear equity narratives, prioritized KPI frameworks, dashboard mock-ups, a scalable conceptual data model, and actionable tooling recommendations to support investor communications and the sale process.
- Automate refreshable data ingestion pipelines from Finance, CRM, and ERP systems into the Qlik Warehouse, enabling recurring data refreshes for transaction reporting and reducing manual effort.
- Deliver investor-grade visibility for senior leadership and stakeholders into key business metrics essential to the equity narrative and M&A decision-making.
- Enhance user experience through intuitive dashboards aligned with equity narratives, featuring clear KPI definitions, calculation logic, and a standardized navigation structure.
- Standardize exit reporting, KPI definitions, and data infrastructure readiness to meet investor scrutiny and ensure consistent, credible transaction reporting.
- Establish a solution that becomes a lasting business asset, fully automatable and scalable post-transaction, with future-proof technology choices enabling ongoing extensibility.
- Ensure the future reporting suite is extensible to additional data sources and capable of refreshable updates beyond the exit transaction.
- Integrate the future platform with Finance System, CRM, ERP, and Qlik Warehouse, with additional data sources considered subject to feasibility and strategic alignment.
