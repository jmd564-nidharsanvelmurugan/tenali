# Outcomes

This engagement will deliver measurable business impact and strategic value by enabling Pure Financial Advisors LLC to achieve deeper visibility into its Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) performance. The initiative aligns with Pure’s B2C financial advisory model and the objectives of key stakeholders, including Lee Equity Partners, by strengthening data-driven decision-making and optimizing marketing ROI across the customer acquisition pipeline.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Granular Pipeline Visibility and Conversion Insights:** The new L2A data model will provide end-to-end transparency into the customer journey from lead generation to assets under management, enabling leadership to identify conversion drivers and optimize acquisition strategies.  
- **Accurate and Automated CAC Reporting:** Integration of QuickBooks and Salesforce data will deliver precise, automated CAC metrics, reducing manual effort and improving confidence in marketing spend analysis.  
- **Improved Marketing ROI Measurement:** Enhanced Power BI dashboards will allow marketing teams to assess campaign effectiveness and allocate budgets based on data-backed performance insights.  
- **Data-Driven Leadership Decision Support:** Senior management will gain timely, reliable insights into pipeline performance and cost efficiency, supporting strategic growth and investment decisions.  
- **Scalable Reporting Foundation for Future Growth:** The solution will establish a robust, extensible data architecture within Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse, ensuring scalability for future data sources and advanced analytics capabilities.  
- **Reduced Operational Risk Through Data Quality and Automation:** Automated ingestion and transformation pipelines will minimize data errors and dependency on manual processes, improving overall reporting reliability and governance.
