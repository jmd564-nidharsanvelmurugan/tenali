# Objectives

- Achieve a fully designed exit reporting suite providing clear equity narratives, a prioritized KPI framework, dashboard mock-ups, a scalable conceptual data model, and actionable tooling recommendations to guide future implementation.
- Automate data ingestion and ongoing refresh processes from Finance System, CRM, ERP, and legacy sources into the Qlik Warehouse to support transaction reporting and recurring data updates.
- Deliver investor-grade visibility for senior leadership and advisors into key financial, commercial, and operational metrics aligned with equity narratives to underpin decision-making in the sale process.
- Standardize and enhance exit reporting processes, KPI definitions, and data infrastructure, improving readiness for investor scrutiny and support of the M&A transaction.
- Create intuitive dashboards with clear calculation logic and navigation, making investor reporting accessible, transparent, and aligned with critical business performance indicators.
- Ensure the future reporting suite and data model are fully extensible and refreshable, supporting additional data sources and automation requirements beyond the transaction to serve as lasting business assets.
