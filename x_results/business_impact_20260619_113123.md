# Business Impact

- **Granular Marketing ROI Visibility:** Establishes a unified Lead‑to‑AUM and CAC reporting framework that quantifies marketing effectiveness, enabling leadership to identify high‑performing channels and optimize spend allocation.  
- **Data‑Driven Growth Decisions:** Empowers Pure Financial Advisors to make informed strategic decisions on client acquisition and retention by linking lead sources directly to AUM outcomes.  
- **Operational Efficiency Gains:** Automates data ingestion and transformation across Salesforce, QuickBooks, and Tamarac, reducing manual reporting effort and improving data refresh timeliness by an estimated 40–60%.  
- **Enhanced Financial Transparency:** Integrates financial and marketing data to deliver accurate CAC metrics, supporting more precise forecasting and profitability analysis across campaigns and client segments.  
- **Scalable Reporting Foundation:** Builds a flexible data model within Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse, ensuring scalability for future data sources and evolving business needs.  
- **Investor‑Grade Data Confidence:** Provides Lee Equity Partners and senior leadership with a trusted, audit‑ready data asset that strengthens performance tracking and supports future diligence or growth initiatives.  

By delivering these outcomes, this engagement will enable **Pure Financial Advisors LLC** to transform its marketing and pipeline reporting into a strategic growth engine—driving smarter investment decisions, improving operational efficiency, and underpinning long‑term value creation in the competitive U.S. financial advisory market.
