# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with PURE Financial Advisors LLC, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

1. **KPI Book**  
- Definition and documentation of Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) KPIs.  
- Detailed calculation logic, data source mapping, and dimensional breakdowns.  
- Alignment of KPI definitions with industry standards and PURE’s internal reporting framework.  
- Reference guide for ongoing reporting consistency and governance.  

2. **Power BI Mock-ups (8 Views)**  
- Design of up to eight Power BI dashboard views focused on L2A and CAC insights.  
- Visualization of key metrics, conversion funnels, and marketing ROI trends.  
- Layout and navigation prototypes to guide user experience and dashboard development.  
- Feedback sessions with stakeholders to validate design and usability.  

3. **Data Model Design**  
- Comprehensive schema for L2A and CAC data integration across Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure, and QuickBooks.  
- Logical and physical data model diagrams detailing relationships, hierarchies, and data flows.  
- Documentation of data transformation logic and key data entities.  
- Foundation for scalable and automated reporting architecture.  

4. **Data Gap Assessment**  
- Identification of missing or incomplete data elements required for L2A and CAC reporting.  
- Evaluation of data quality, completeness, and consistency across source systems.  
- Recommendations for remediation and data enrichment strategies.  
- Prioritization of data improvement actions based on business impact.  

5. **Technical Approach Document**  
- Detailed plan for QuickBooks integration and automated data ingestion pipelines.  
- Description of batch processing workflows within Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse.  
- Technical architecture overview including data flow diagrams and control points.  
- Guidelines for scalability, performance, and maintainability of the solution.  

6. **Phase 3B Implementation Plan**  
- Roadmap outlining activities, milestones, and dependencies for the build phase.  
- Resource and timeline planning for an 8–12 week implementation window.  
- Sequencing of integration, testing, and deployment tasks.  
- Risk mitigation and change management considerations.  

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with PURE Financial Advisors LLC's leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
