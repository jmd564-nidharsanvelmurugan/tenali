# Understanding

- Limited granularity in existing pipeline reporting is restricting visibility into lead progression and conversion.
- There is an absence of integrated and detailed Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) insights, inhibiting accurate measurement of marketing effectiveness.
- Data ingestion from QuickBooks is not yet established and presents an integration requirement for accurate CAC reporting.
- Assessment and improvement of data quality across key systems are necessary to underpin reliable Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and CAC analytics.
- An enhanced L2A and CAC data model is required to support new reporting and analytics needs.
- Automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines are needed to ensure timely and accurate reporting on L2A and CAC metrics.
- The solution must support distinct user roles, including data and technology stakeholders, senior leadership, and marketing teams.
- Mandatory integrations with both Salesforce and QuickBooks are essential for the desired reporting capabilities.
- Engagement timelines and deliverables are dependent on timely access to data and successful integration of QuickBooks.
