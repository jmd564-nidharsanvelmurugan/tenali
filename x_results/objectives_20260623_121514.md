# Objectives

- Establish a fully designed exit reporting suite featuring clear equity narratives and a prioritized KPI framework, supporting the sale process and investor communications.
- Enable automated, refreshable data ingestion pipelines from Finance, CRM, ERP, and legacy sources to ensure ongoing data availability for transaction reporting.
- Provide senior leadership and stakeholders with investor-grade visibility into key commercial, financial, and operational metrics required for M&A due diligence.
- Deliver intuitive dashboards aligned to equity narratives, with clearly defined KPIs, calculation logic, and user-friendly navigation structure.
- Standardize exit reporting processes and ensure the data infrastructure is ready for investor scrutiny, improving business readiness for the sale.
- Lay the foundation for long-term scalability by designing data models and reporting tools that are extensible to additional data sources and fully automatable post-transaction.
- Integrate the reporting suite with core systems (Finance System, CRM, ERP, Qlik Warehouse), while assessing tooling options for future expansion and adaptability.
