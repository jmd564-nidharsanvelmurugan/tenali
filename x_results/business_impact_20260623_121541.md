# Business Impact

- **Accelerated Exit Readiness:** By delivering a validated exit reporting suite—including a refreshable data cube, dashboard mock-ups, and a clear KPI framework—VONQ will significantly reduce the time and effort required to prepare for acquisition, minimizing the risk of exit delays and supporting adherence to a compressed transaction timeline.

- **Financial Reporting Excellence:** The new reporting suite will provide investor-grade visibility into key commercial and financial performance metrics, addressing current data gaps and enabling data-backed equity narratives. This transparency supports higher valuation potential and increases buyer confidence during diligence.

- **Operational Efficiency Gains:** Automation of data ingestion and recurring refresh processes from core systems (Finance, CRM, ERP) into Qlik Warehouse will streamline data management and reduce manual reporting effort by an estimated 40–60% during the transaction window, freeing up critical staff resources for value-adding activities.

- **Strategic Value Creation:** Establishing a prioritized KPI framework and a scalable data model underpins long-term business objectives, ensuring that reporting assets produced during the exit process become enduring capabilities that VONQ can leverage for future decision support and operational performance monitoring.

- **Competitive Advantage in M&A Process:** The ability to rapidly deliver clear, investor-ready dashboards—aligned to agreed equity narratives—positions VONQ as a well-prepared, data-driven business, mitigating reputational and process risks while enhancing appeal to acquirers and investors.

VONQ’s engagement in this initiative will yield a lasting competitive edge by embedding strong data governance, advanced reporting capabilities, and a future-proof data model. Collectively, these improvements will drive transaction efficiency, support valuation maximization, and prepare the organization for sustained growth post-exit.
