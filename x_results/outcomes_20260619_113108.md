# Outcomes

This engagement with **Pure Financial Advisors LLC** will deliver measurable business impact by transforming how the firm tracks and optimizes its marketing and pipeline performance. Focused on developing a robust Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting framework, the initiative aligns with Pure’s B2C growth model and the strategic objectives of both senior leadership and its investment partner, Lee Equity Partners. The outcome will enable data‑driven decisions that directly link marketing spend to asset growth and client acquisition efficiency.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Granular Lead‑to‑AUM Visibility:** Establishment of a unified L2A data model will provide detailed insights into each stage of the client acquisition journey, enabling leadership to pinpoint conversion drivers and optimize pipeline performance.  
- **Accurate Customer Acquisition Cost Measurement:** Integration of QuickBooks and Salesforce data will deliver precise CAC metrics, allowing marketing teams to assess ROI and allocate budgets based on verified cost‑to‑conversion ratios.  
- **Automated and Reliable Reporting:** Implementation of automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines will ensure consistent, timely, and error‑free reporting across Power BI dashboards, reducing manual intervention and improving data trust.  
- **Improved Marketing ROI Analysis:** Enhanced Power BI visualizations will connect marketing activities to AUM outcomes, empowering leadership to evaluate campaign effectiveness and refine strategies for higher return on spend.  
- **Strengthened Data Maturity and Scalability:** The new data model and reporting suite will establish a scalable foundation for future analytics expansion, supporting Pure’s long‑term vision of data‑driven growth and operational efficiency.  
- **Reduced Decision Latency:** By consolidating key performance metrics into a single reporting environment, senior stakeholders will gain faster access to actionable insights, accelerating strategic and investment decisions.
