# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with **Pure Financial Advisors LLC**, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

---

**1. KPI Book**  
- Comprehensive documentation of Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) metrics, including definitions, formulas, and data sources.  

- Clear mapping of KPIs to business objectives, ensuring consistent interpretation across marketing, data, and leadership teams.  

- Inclusion of dimension dictionary outlining how metrics can be sliced by lead source, campaign, and asset segment.  

---

**2. Power BI Mock‑Ups (up to 8 views)**  
- Design of interactive dashboard mock‑ups illustrating the target‑state L2A and CAC reporting experience.  

- Visualization of key KPIs, conversion funnels, and marketing ROI insights to guide dashboard development.  

- Layout and navigation blueprint to support user experience design and stakeholder validation.  

---

**3. Data Model Design**  
- Detailed schema for the integrated L2A and CAC data model, connecting Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks.  

- Logical and physical data model diagrams showing relationships, hierarchies, and data flow across systems.  

- Documentation of data transformation logic and key data quality rules to support automated ingestion.  

---

**4. Data Gap Assessment**  
- Identification and quantification of missing or inconsistent data elements impacting L2A and CAC reporting accuracy.  

- Evaluation of data quality across Salesforce, Tamarac, and QuickBooks sources.  

- Recommendations for remediation actions and prioritization based on business impact and feasibility.  

---

**5. Technical Approach Document**  
- Detailed description of the proposed integration and automation strategy within Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse.  

- Specification of batch processing pipelines, data refresh cadence, and transformation logic.  

- Technical design for QuickBooks ingestion, including data mapping and validation steps.  

---

**6. Phase 3B Implementation Plan**  
- Structured roadmap outlining activities, milestones, and dependencies for the build and deployment phase.  

- Timeline and resource plan covering 8–12 weeks of implementation, aligned with QuickBooks integration readiness.  

- Risk and dependency register to ensure proactive management of data access and quality constraints.  

---

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with **Pure Financial Advisors LLC**'s leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
