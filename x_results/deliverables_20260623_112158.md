# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with VONQ, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

1. **Equity Narratives Definition**
   - Documentation of investor-grade equity narratives tailored to VONQ’s transaction objectives.
   - Clear articulation of the business storylines that underpin the M&A process.
   - Delivered as a written report and presentation slides in Week 1.

2. **Metrics that Matter KPI Framework**
   - Prioritised framework of commercial, financial, and operational KPIs aligned to equity narratives.
   - Detailed definitions and calculation logic for each KPI, reflecting data source feasibility.
   - Provided as an editable spreadsheet and summary report in Week 2.

3. **Dashboard Mock-ups**
   - Static wireframes illustrating the proposed dashboards, including layout, navigation, and KPI placement.
   - Visual examples of views required for senior leadership, advisors, and potential investors.
   - Delivered as PDF and PowerPoint files in Week 3.

4. **Data Feasibility Assessment**
   - Findings and playback from current-state data review, including identification of data gaps and remediation priorities.
   - Assessment of source data readiness and recommendations for resolving critical gaps.
   - Delivered as a formal assessment document and playback presentation in Week 2.

5. **Conceptual Data Model Design**
   - High-level conceptual data model leveraging the Qlik Warehouse as a base, extensible to additional sources.
   - Entity-relationship diagrams, data lineage mapping, and key integration touchpoints.
   - Delivered as diagrams (Visio/PDF) and technical documentation in Week 3.

6. **Data Infrastructure and Tooling Recommendations**
   - Options analysis of candidate data infrastructure and tooling, with clear pros and cons for each scenario.
   - Guidance on batch data refresh processes and automated ingestion for in-scope systems.
   - Delivered as a recommendation report in Week 4.

7. **Implementation Roadmap for Phase 2**
   - Phased plan detailing use cases, dependencies, risks, and key milestones required for solution implementation.
   - Timeline and resource implications for Phase 2, with clear decision points for VONQ leadership.
   - Provided as a Gantt chart and documented roadmap in Week 4.

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with VONQ's leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
