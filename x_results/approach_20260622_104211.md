# Approach

PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC has engaged our team to deliver an enhanced Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting suite that enables granular insights into pipeline performance and marketing ROI. The engagement is designed to address current challenges around data availability, integration (most notably QuickBooks), and the need for automated, scalable data processes to empower data-driven decisions across the organization. Our methodology leverages proven data management practices, stakeholder collaboration, and iterative design, ensuring alignment with key business objectives and full integration into the Power BI reporting environment.

The proposed approach adopts a phased model to systematically address all requirements and dependencies while maintaining flexibility for iterative feedback. We begin with a discovery and design phase focused on requirements alignment, data assessment, and reporting wireframes, followed by a build and implementation phase centered on automating data ingestion, model development, and dashboard delivery. Each phase is structured to yield concrete deliverables, actionable insights, and technical assets that empower the PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC team and drive long-term value.

---

**Phase 3A: Discovery & Design, Duration: 3 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 to Week 3**  
**Summary:** This phase establishes the foundation for all reporting enhancements through requirements gathering, data analysis, KPI definition, reporting mockups, and technical planning.

**Activities:**
- Conduct stakeholder workshops to validate business goals, reporting requirements, and key performance indicators (KPIs).
- Perform data gap assessment across Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and prepare for QuickBooks integration.
- Define calculation logic and lineage for all L2A and CAC KPIs, resulting in a comprehensive KPI Book.
- Develop initial Power BI mock-ups (up to 8 report pages) incorporating feedback from stakeholders.
- Design the L2A and CAC data model and document the technical approach for integrating QuickBooks data.
- Prepare the Phase 3B Implementation Plan covering sequencing, resource needs, and risk mitigation strategies.

---

**Phase 3B: Build & Implementation, Duration: 8–12 weeks, Timeline: Week 4 to Week 15**  
**Summary:** This phase operationalizes the solution by building the data model, implementing automated data pipelines, integrating QuickBooks, and deploying enhanced Power BI dashboards.

**Activities:**
- Develop and deploy automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines in Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse to source data from Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks.
- Implement data quality assessment and reconciliation routines to ensure reliability of reporting inputs.
- Build the Lead-to-AUM and CAC data models based on the technical design, ensuring scalability for future needs.
- Configure and publish the finalized 8-page Power BI report suite, optimizing dashboards for clarity and actionable insights.
- Conduct user training sessions and facilitate adoption through documentation and knowledge transfer.
- Complete project closure activities, including delivery of all documentation and Phase 3B Implementation Plan milestones.

---

By structuring this engagement in clearly defined phases, we ensure that PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC receives deeply tailored, actionable deliverables at each milestone, with ample opportunity for stakeholder input and iterative refinement. The phased approach mitigates risk, ensures alignment with business objectives, and accelerates time to value—establishing a foundation for scalable, insightful pipeline and marketing performance analytics that will grow with PURE’s business needs.
