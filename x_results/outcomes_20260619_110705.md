# Outcomes

This engagement will deliver measurable business impact and strategic value by enabling **Pure Financial Advisors LLC** to achieve data-driven visibility into its Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) performance. The initiative aligns with Pure’s B2C financial advisory model and supports both internal leadership and private equity stakeholders in optimizing marketing ROI and pipeline efficiency through automated, accurate reporting.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Granular Lead-to-AUM Visibility:** Development of a unified L2A data model will provide detailed insights into the full customer journey from lead generation to assets under management, enabling leadership to identify conversion drivers and optimize acquisition strategies.  
- **Accurate Customer Acquisition Cost Measurement:** Integration of QuickBooks and Salesforce data will allow precise calculation of CAC metrics, improving understanding of marketing spend effectiveness and supporting budget allocation decisions.  
- **Automated and Scalable Reporting:** Implementation of automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines will reduce manual effort, ensure timely updates, and create a scalable foundation for future reporting enhancements.  
- **Improved Marketing ROI Analysis:** Enhanced Power BI dashboards will deliver actionable insights into campaign performance and return on marketing investment, empowering data-backed decision-making across marketing and leadership teams.  
- **Strengthened Data Maturity and Governance:** Establishing consistent KPI definitions and standardized data logic will advance Pure’s data maturity, ensuring reliable analytics and supporting long-term scalability across its Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse platforms.  
- **Reduced Operational Risk from Data Silos:** By integrating key systems and improving data quality, the solution will mitigate risks associated with fragmented reporting, ensuring leadership has a single source of truth for pipeline and acquisition performance.
