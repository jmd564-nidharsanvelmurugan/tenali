# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with **Pure Financial Advisors LLC**, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

---

**1. KPI Book**  
- Definition and documentation of Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) metrics, including calculation logic and data sources.  

- Establishment of standardized KPI naming conventions and dimensional attributes for consistent reporting.  

- Alignment of KPI definitions with business stakeholders to ensure clarity and usability across teams.  

---

**2. Power BI Mock‑Ups (8 Views)**  
- Design of up to eight Power BI dashboard mock‑ups illustrating L2A and CAC insights, pipeline performance, and marketing ROI.  

- Visualization of key metrics, filters, and drill‑down capabilities to guide the final report build.  

- Incorporation of user experience best practices to support intuitive navigation and decision‑making.  

---

**3. Data Model Design**  
- Development of a detailed L2A and CAC data model architecture integrating Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks.  

- Definition of data relationships, hierarchies, and transformation logic to support automated reporting.  

- Documentation of schema, data lineage, and integration points within Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse.  

---

**4. Data Gap Assessment**  
- Identification of missing or incomplete data elements required for accurate L2A and CAC reporting.  

- Evaluation of data quality, completeness, and consistency across source systems.  

- Recommendations for remediation actions and prioritization of data improvement initiatives.  

---

**5. Technical Approach Document**  
- Detailed description of the technical solution for QuickBooks integration and automated data ingestion pipelines.  

- Specification of batch processing workflows within Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse.  

- Outline of security, scalability, and maintenance considerations for the proposed architecture.  

---

**6. Phase 3B Implementation Plan**  
- Comprehensive roadmap detailing activities, milestones, and dependencies for the build and deployment phase.  

- Timeline and resource plan covering 8–12 weeks of implementation, aligned with QuickBooks integration readiness.  

- Identification of key risks, mitigation strategies, and success checkpoints for project execution.  

---

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with **Pure Financial Advisors LLC**’s leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
