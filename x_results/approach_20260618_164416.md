# Approach

PURE Financial Advisors LLC operates in the financial advisory and wealth management industry, serving clients through a B2C model focused on personalized financial guidance and asset management. This engagement is designed to address PURE’s specific challenge of enhancing its Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting capabilities. By leveraging existing infrastructure—Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Fabric, Synapse, and Power BI—the approach will deliver a unified, automated, and scalable reporting solution that provides granular insights into pipeline performance and marketing ROI.

The methodology follows a structured, phased approach that ensures alignment with PURE’s business objectives, technical environment, and stakeholder expectations. Each phase builds upon the previous one, moving from discovery and design through implementation planning, ensuring that the final solution is both technically robust and strategically valuable to PURE’s leadership and marketing teams.

---

**Phase 3A: Discovery & Design, Duration: 3 weeks, Timeline: Week 0 to Week 3**

**Summary:** Establish foundational understanding of data sources, define KPI logic, and design the target‑state L2A and CAC data model and Power BI mock‑ups.

**Activities:**  
- Conduct workshops with data, technology, and marketing teams to confirm reporting requirements and success metrics.  
- Review existing data sources (Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, QuickBooks) and assess data quality and availability.  
- Define KPI calculation logic for L2A and CAC metrics, ensuring alignment with business definitions.  
- Develop initial Power BI mock‑ups (up to 8 views) to visualize priority reporting use cases.  
- Perform a data gap assessment to identify missing or incomplete data elements.  
- Document technical approach for QuickBooks integration and data ingestion pipelines.

---

**Phase 3B: Implementation Planning & Technical Design, Duration: 8–12 weeks, Timeline: Week 4 to Week 15**

**Summary:** Finalize the data model design, confirm technical architecture, and prepare the implementation roadmap for automated reporting and integration.

**Activities:**  
- Finalize the end‑state L2A and CAC data model based on discovery findings and stakeholder feedback.  
- Develop detailed technical design for data ingestion, transformation, and refresh automation within Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse.  
- Validate integration approach for QuickBooks and Salesforce to ensure seamless data flow.  
- Create the KPI Book documenting metric definitions, data lineage, and calculation logic.  
- Produce the Phase 3B Implementation Plan outlining build activities, dependencies, and sequencing.  
- Recommend data cleaning and enrichment strategies to support accurate CAC and pipeline reporting.

---

This structured, phased approach ensures that PURE Financial Advisors LLC’s data platform and reporting capabilities are developed with precision, agility, and a clear focus on delivering measurable business outcomes aligned with stakeholder expectations.
