# Business Impact

- **Accelerated Exit Readiness:** By delivering a validated, investor-grade reporting suite—comprised of equity narrative-aligned dashboards, KPI frameworks, and data models—VONQ can compress the time required to prepare for M&A, reducing risk of transaction delays and supporting adherence to the targeted exit timeline of 4 weeks for Phase 1.

- **Improved Data-Driven Decision Making:** The solution will automate ingestion and refresh processes across core systems (Finance, CRM, ERP), ensuring leadership and advisors have timely, accurate access to trended business metrics. This enhances visibility into financial and operational performance, reducing manual effort for reporting and analysis by up to 50% during the exit process.

- **Cost Efficiency and Resource Optimization:** Automating critical reporting workflows reduces dependency on manual compilation, likely realizing indirect cost savings by minimizing hours spent on spreadsheet-based tasks and iterative data validation, while freeing high-value personnel for strategic activities.

- **Investor-Grade KPI Framework:** Delivery of standardised, transparent KPIs mapped to the equity narrative will directly address current reporting gaps, improving stakeholder confidence, and enabling clear, credible communication with potential acquirers and advisors such as Barclays.

- **Future-Proofed Data Architecture:** The conceptual design and tooling recommendations will create a scalable foundation, ensuring the exit reporting suite is extensible to additional data sources post-transaction and can be fully automated in the future, aligning with VONQ’s long-term SaaS scalability vision.

- **Reduced Transaction Risk:** Early identification of data gaps and recommended remediation actions help mitigate risks of incomplete or non-credible reporting, reducing reputational risk with potential buyers and supporting smoother due diligence.

This engagement positions VONQ to execute a successful, timely exit by establishing best-in-class reporting design and data infrastructure. By enabling automated, investor-ready insights, the initiative drives measurable operational efficiencies, reduces transaction risk, and equips leadership with the analytical tools required for value maximization during the M&A process.
