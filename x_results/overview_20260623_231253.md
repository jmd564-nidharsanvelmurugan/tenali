# Overview

The current state at PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC involves close collaboration among the data, technology, and marketing teams, who are primarily responsible for managing pipeline and customer acquisition reporting. These functions are supported by a sophisticated suite of systems and platforms, including Salesforce and Tamarac for client and portfolio management, Azure Blob Storage for scalable data storage, and a combination of Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse to form the backbone of the organization's cloud data infrastructure. Power BI is in use as the principal reporting and analytics tool, enabling stakeholders to visualize and explore business data.

Key data sources currently integrated into the client’s operational environment include Salesforce, Tamarac, and Azure Blob Storage, with efforts underway to bring QuickBooks data into the existing ecosystem. While the Power BI reporting suite provides core analytics capabilities, there is an ongoing need to connect and ingest data from QuickBooks to support customer acquisition cost reporting and assess data quality. The integration of these platforms is a focus due to current challenges related to seamless data connectivity and quality assurance across systems.
