# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with **Pure Financial Advisors LLC**, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

---

**1. KPI Book**  
- Comprehensive documentation of Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) metrics, including definitions, formulas, and data sources.  

- Standardized KPI logic and calculation methodology to ensure consistency across Power BI reporting.  

- Alignment of KPIs with business objectives and stakeholder expectations for marketing ROI and pipeline performance.  

---

**2. Power BI Mock-ups (8 Views)**  
- Design of up to eight Power BI dashboard views focused on L2A and CAC insights.  

- Visualization of key metrics, conversion funnels, and marketing performance indicators.  

- Wireframes capturing layout, navigation, and user experience requirements for leadership and marketing teams.  

---

**3. Data Model Design**  
- Detailed schema for the L2A and CAC data model integrating Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks.  

- Logical and physical data model diagrams illustrating relationships, hierarchies, and data flows.  

- Documentation of data transformation logic and mapping rules to support automated ingestion pipelines.  

---

**4. Data Gap Assessment**  
- Identification of missing or incomplete data elements across source systems impacting L2A and CAC reporting.  

- Evaluation of data quality, completeness, and consistency for QuickBooks and Salesforce integrations.  

- Recommendations for remediation and data governance improvements to support accurate reporting.  

---

**5. Technical Approach Document**  
- Detailed description of the technical architecture supporting batch data ingestion and transformation within Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse.  

- Integration approach for QuickBooks and Salesforce, including data flow diagrams and process automation logic.  

- Definition of performance, scalability, and refresh strategies for the Power BI reporting suite.  

---

**6. Phase 3B Implementation Plan**  
- Roadmap outlining activities, milestones, and dependencies for the build and deployment phase.  

- Sequenced timeline covering development, testing, and rollout of the L2A and CAC reporting solution.  

- Resource plan and governance framework ensuring smooth transition from design to implementation.  

---

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with **Pure Financial Advisors LLC**’s leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
