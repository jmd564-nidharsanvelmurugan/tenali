# Outcomes

The proposed engagement will transform PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC’s ability to analyze and optimize its customer acquisition and asset management pipeline. By delivering enhanced Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting, the business will gain actionable insights to drive improved marketing ROI, operational efficiency, and data-driven decision making. Success will be measured through more granular visibility into pipeline performance, improved KPI tracking, and streamlined reporting workflows.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Actionable Pipeline Insights:** Leadership will obtain a granular view of the lead journey and pipeline performance, enabling targeted interventions to optimize conversion rates from lead to assets under management (AUM).  
- **Improved Marketing ROI Measurement:** Enhanced CAC reporting will provide clear visibility into the effectiveness of marketing investments, allowing for precise measurement of customer acquisition costs and strategic allocation of marketing resources.  
- **Accurate, Automated Reporting:** Automation of data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refreshes will ensure that L2A and CAC insights are timely, consistent, and reliable—reducing manual workload and error risk.  
- **Data-Driven Decision Making:** With integrated and scalable reporting within Power BI, senior leadership and key stakeholders will be empowered to make informed, data-backed decisions, supporting overall business growth objectives.  
- **Scalable Analytics Foundation:** The engagement will establish a robust data platform capable of supporting future reporting and analytics needs, ensuring flexibility and scalability as new data sources and business requirements emerge.  
- **Enhanced Stakeholder Confidence:** Delivering accurate and transparent KPI tracking will increase confidence among business stakeholders and investment partners, strengthening alignment on performance goals and outcomes.
