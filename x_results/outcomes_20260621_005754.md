# Outcomes

The engagement with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC is designed to significantly elevate the organization’s data maturity and reporting capabilities. By integrating enhanced Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data models, the project will provide measurable business impact through actionable insights into pipeline performance and marketing ROI. Success will be marked by increased visibility and automation in critical analytics, empowering leadership with timely, data-driven decisions for sustained growth.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Granular Lead-to-AUM Insights:** Enables detailed tracking of the customer journey from initial lead generation to asset acquisition, facilitating optimized conversion strategies and pinpointing bottlenecks in the pipeline.
- **Actionable Marketing ROI Visibility:** Delivers advanced CAC reporting, allowing marketing teams to precisely assess the effectiveness of campaigns and allocate resources more efficiently to maximize acquisition returns.
- **Automated Data Processes:** Streamlines data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh cycles, reducing manual workload and ensuring real-time accuracy for both pipeline and CAC analytics.
- **Integrated Power BI Dashboards:** Provides senior leadership and key stakeholders with clear, visually compelling KPIs and trend analyses, supporting informed, strategic decisions across business functions.
- **Scalable Reporting Architecture:** Establishes a robust, future-ready platform capable of integrating new data sources and reporting requirements, underpinning the company’s long-term growth objectives.
- **Improved Data Quality and Reliability:** Enhances data integrity through comprehensive quality assessment and integration processes, minimizing risks associated with inconsistent or incomplete data and supporting compliance needs.

These outcomes directly address Pure’s pain points around limited reporting granularity and fragmented marketing insights, resulting in a measurable improvement in operational efficiency, marketing effectiveness, and pipeline management. Success will be evidenced by increased conversion rates, reduced customer acquisition costs, and timely, accurate reporting accessible to all relevant stakeholders.
