# Approach

PURE Financial Advisors LLC operates in a highly competitive financial advisory and wealth management market, where data-driven insights into client acquisition and marketing ROI are critical to sustaining growth. This engagement is designed to address PURE’s specific challenge of enhancing its Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting by leveraging its existing Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse infrastructure. The approach focuses on integrating key data sources—Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks—into a unified, automated data model that delivers granular visibility into the client journey and marketing effectiveness.

Our methodology follows a structured, phased approach that ensures alignment with PURE’s business objectives, technical environment, and stakeholder expectations. Each phase builds upon the previous one, from understanding data sources and defining KPIs to designing the data model, developing Power BI mock-ups, and finalizing the implementation roadmap. This disciplined progression ensures that the final solution is both technically robust and strategically aligned with PURE’s long-term data maturity goals.

---

**Phase 1: Discovery & Current-State Assessment, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 1**

**Summary:** Establish a foundational understanding of PURE’s current data landscape, systems, and reporting processes to inform design decisions.  

**Activities:**  
- Conduct kickoff sessions with data, technology, and marketing stakeholders to confirm scope and success criteria.  
- Review existing data infrastructure across Microsoft Fabric, Azure Synapse, and Power BI.  
- Assess current Salesforce, Tamarac, and Azure Blob Storage data flows supporting pipeline reporting.  
- Document existing KPI definitions and identify gaps in L2A and CAC reporting.  
- Validate data access requirements and dependencies for QuickBooks integration.  

---

**Phase 2: Data Source Assessment & Gap Analysis, Duration: 2 weeks, Timeline: Weeks 2–3**

**Summary:** Evaluate data availability, quality, and integration readiness to support the L2A and CAC data model.  

**Activities:**  
- Profile data from Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks to assess completeness and consistency.  
- Identify data quality issues and define remediation recommendations.  
- Map data fields to required KPIs and metrics for L2A and CAC reporting.  
- Conduct a data gap assessment to highlight missing or misaligned data elements.  
- Prioritize data enrichment opportunities and define “no-regret” improvements for the build phase.  

---

**Phase 3: Data Model Design & KPI Definition, Duration: 2 weeks, Timeline: Weeks 4–5**

**Summary:** Design the target-state data model and finalize KPI logic to enable accurate and scalable reporting.  

**Activities:**  
- Develop conceptual and logical data models for L2A and CAC reporting.  
- Define KPI calculation logic, data transformations, and source mappings.  
- Align KPI definitions with PURE’s business and marketing teams for validation.  
- Document data lineage and relationships across systems.  
- Prepare the KPI Book detailing metric definitions, formulas, and data dependencies.  

---

**Phase 4: Power BI Mock-ups & Reporting Design, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 6**

**Summary:** Translate the data model and KPI framework into visual reporting concepts for stakeholder review.  

**Activities:**  
- Design up to eight Power BI mock-ups illustrating L2A and CAC dashboards.  
- Incorporate user feedback to refine layout, navigation, and visualization logic.  
- Ensure alignment of visuals with KPI definitions and data model structure.  
- Validate that dashboards support leadership’s need for marketing ROI insights.  
- Finalize mock-ups for inclusion in the design deliverables package.  

---

**Phase 5: Technical Approach & Implementation Planning, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 7**

**Summary:** Define the technical architecture, integration approach, and roadmap for Phase 3B implementation.  

**Activities:**  
- Develop the technical approach for QuickBooks ingestion and automated batch processing in Microsoft Fabric.  
- Outline data transformation and refresh automation workflows.  
- Define dependencies, sequencing, and resource requirements for the build phase.  
- Prepare the Phase 3B Implementation Plan detailing timelines and milestones.  
- Present final deliverables—KPI Book, Data Model Design, Power BI Mock-ups, and Technical Approach—for stakeholder approval.  

---

This structured, phased approach ensures that PURE Financial Advisors LLC’s enhanced reporting capabilities are developed with precision, agility, and a clear focus on delivering measurable business outcomes. By progressing from discovery through design and planning, the engagement provides a solid foundation for scalable, automated, and insight-driven L2A and CAC reporting aligned with PURE’s growth objectives.
