# Understanding

- The business problem centers on insufficient granularity in current pipeline reporting and a lack of integrated Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) insights.
- There is a need to develop enhanced Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and CAC reporting to provide more detailed information about the customer journey and marketing return on investment.
- Current pain points include limited detail available in pipeline reports and missing visibility into CAC data due to system integration gaps.
- Mandatory integration is required between Salesforce and QuickBooks to support L2A and CAC reporting.
- An enhanced data model is required to support L2A and CAC insights, alongside a robust Power BI reporting suite.
- Automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines are needed to streamline L2A and CAC reporting processes.
- Key user groups include data and technology stakeholders, senior leadership, and marketing teams who rely on accurate and timely reporting.
- There are dependencies on data access, data quality, and successful ingestion of QuickBooks data for comprehensive CAC measurement.
