# Objectives

- Deliver a comprehensive Lead-to-AUM and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data model and reporting suite that provides granular insights into pipeline performance and marketing ROI.
- Automate data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes to enable timely and efficient analytics.
- Enable senior leadership to gain a detailed understanding of the entire lead journey, conversion metrics, and precise evaluation of marketing spend effectiveness.
- Improve Power BI dashboards with clear KPI definitions and intuitive visualizations for enhanced stakeholder decision-making.
- Enhance pipeline reporting and customer acquisition cost tracking to increase business process transparency and accuracy.
- Establish a scalable data platform and reporting framework capable of supporting future data sources and expanded reporting needs.
- Integrate seamlessly with Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Azure Fabric to ensure unified and reliable data flow across all relevant systems.
