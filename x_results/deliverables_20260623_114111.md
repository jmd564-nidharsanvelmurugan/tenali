# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with VONQ, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

1. **Equity Narratives Definition**
   - Documentation of investor-aligned equity stories tailored to support the exit process and underpin stakeholder communications.
   - Enumeration of key business themes, differentiators, and value drivers relevant for potential acquirers.
   - Delivered as a written report and validated through a stakeholder workshop.

2. **Metrics that Matter KPI Framework**
   - Catalogue of prioritized KPIs spanning commercial, financial, and operational performance, with working definitions.
   - KPI calculation logic, data source mapping, and any data transformation requirements.
   - Delivered as a structured reference document and KPI matrix, refined in collaboration with business owners.

3. **Dashboard Mock-ups**
   - Static visual concepts illustrating the proposed metrics, dashboard layout, and navigation paths.
   - Annotation of key features, user roles, and intended reporting views.
   - Delivered as PDF/PowerPoint mock-ups and presented during review sessions for stakeholder feedback.

4. **Data Feasibility Assessment**
   - Current-state findings summarizing data quality, accessibility, and identified gaps across Finance, CRM, ERP, and Qlik Warehouse sources.
   - Identification and prioritization of remediation needs and interim data collection requirements.
   - Delivered as a diagnostic slide deck and summary memo.

5. **Conceptual Data Model Design**
   - High-level schema mapping key data entities, relationships, and source system linkages, with scalability considerations highlighted.
   - Recommendations for extensibility and future automation.
   - Delivered as a visual model (e.g., ER diagram) with supporting documentation.

6. **Data Infrastructure and Tooling Recommendations**
   - Comparative analysis of current vs. alternative reporting toolsets, outlining options, pros, and cons specific to VONQ’s needs.
   - Proposed technical enhancements addressing batch data ingestion, recurring refreshes, and system integration challenges.
   - Delivered as a recommendation brief with decision-making criteria and phased roadmap alignment.

7. **Implementation Roadmap for Phase 2**
   - Detailed, phased plan covering priority use cases, critical dependencies, project risks, and key milestones.
   - Timeline visualization with roles, responsibilities, and transition-ready artefact handover points.
   - Delivered as a comprehensive Gantt chart and narrative plan.

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with VONQ's leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
