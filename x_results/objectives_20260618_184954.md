# Objectives

- Establish a comprehensive Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data model that delivers granular insights into pipeline performance and marketing ROI.  
- Automate data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes to ensure consistent, timely, and accurate reporting.  
- Provide leadership with clear visibility into the full customer journey from lead generation through to assets under management.  
- Enhance Power BI dashboards with standardized KPI definitions and intuitive visualizations to improve user experience and decision‑making.  
- Build scalable reporting capabilities that support future data sources and align with PURE’s long‑term vision for data maturity and growth.  
- Integrate Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Azure Fabric into a unified reporting ecosystem for seamless data flow and trusted insights.
