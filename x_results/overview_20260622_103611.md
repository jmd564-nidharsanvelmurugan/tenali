# Overview

PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC operates in the financial advisory and wealth management industry, serving clients across the United States. The business runs on a B2C model, with core offerings focused on financial advisory services and asset management. Key business stakeholders include Jason Carver, the Client Engagement Manager, and Lee Equity Partners, who act as an investment partner.

Currently, the data, technology, and marketing teams at PURE are primarily involved in pipeline and customer acquisition reporting processes. The organization's technology infrastructure includes Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, Microsoft Fabric, Azure Synapse, Power BI, and integration with QuickBooks is in progress. The existing reporting environment leverages a Power BI suite, with core data sources stemming from Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks. Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse serve as the foundational data platforms supporting these activities.

At present, the organization faces the requirement to connect QuickBooks for comprehensive data integration and to assess data quality for accurate customer acquisition cost reporting. No manual processes or data governance policies are detailed at this stage. The existing setup utilizes batch processing pipelines for data ingestion and reporting via the Fabric platform, with ongoing developments aimed at enhancing integration and overall data quality.
