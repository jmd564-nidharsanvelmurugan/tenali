# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with VONQ, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

1. **Equity Narratives Definition**
   - Documentation of investor-grade equity narratives capturing VONQ’s value proposition and positioning for the exit process.
   - Structured summary reflecting the latest commercial, financial, and operational themes relevant to acquirers and advisors.
   - Delivered as a presentation and narrative document during Week 1, for review and refinement in alignment workshops.

2. **“Metrics that Matter” KPI Framework**
   - Prioritised KPI framework defining “metrics that matter” for the equity narrative, covering commercial, financial, and operational performance.
   - Detailed descriptions, calculation logic, and rationale for each KPI relevant to M&A due diligence.
   - Delivered as a structured Excel matrix and supporting documentation by the end of Week 1.

3. **Dashboard Mock-ups**
   - Static wireframes illustrating the proposed dashboards, including layout, visualisation types, and navigation structure.
   - Dashboard views mapped to stakeholder and investor needs, aligned to the defined KPIs.
   - Delivered as annotated PDF/PowerPoint deck by Week 2, to enable collaborative review.

4. **Data Feasibility Assessment**
   - Playback of current-state data availability and identification of key data gaps impacting reporting readiness.
   - Feasibility analysis covering all in-scope Finance, CRM, and ERP systems, and legacy datasets as agreed.
   - Delivered as a written assessment document with recommendations by mid-Week 2.

5. **Conceptual Data Model Design**
   - High-level data model diagram mapping data flows from source systems into the Qlik Warehouse.
   - Supporting documentation outlining table structures, key entities, and relationships relevant to the reporting suite.
   - Delivered as a data model diagram and supporting notes in Week 3.

6. **Data Infrastructure and Tooling Recommendations**
   - Analysis of technology options for scalable reporting suite delivery, including alternatives to Qlik where relevant.
   - Recommendation matrix with pros, cons, and rationale for tooling and integration choices.
   - Delivered as a decision support document and presentation in Week 3.

7. **Implementation Roadmap for Phase 2**
   - Phased roadmap covering key initiatives, dependencies, risks, and major milestones for solution implementation.
   - Initiative prioritisation, resource considerations, and clear handover plan for Phase 2 build.
   - Delivered as a roadmap slide deck and supporting document in Week 4.

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with VONQ's leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
