# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

1. **KPI Book**
   - Comprehensive documentation of Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) metrics.
   - Detailed definitions, calculation logic, and data sources for all key performance indicators.
   - Format: Structured PDF and editable Excel deliverable.
   - Delivered at the conclusion of the Discovery phase.

2. **Power BI Mock-ups**
   - Eight (8) Power BI dashboard mock-ups highlighting L2A and CAC reporting views.
   - Annotated wireframes illustrating data fields, visualizations, and navigation elements.
   - Format: PDF and Power BI interactive files.
   - Delivered during the Discovery phase for stakeholder review.

3. **Data Model Design**
   - Entity-relationship diagrams (ERD) and logical model documentation for enhanced L2A and CAC pipeline analytics.
   - Mapping of data sources including Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks integration.
   - Format: PDF diagrams and supporting documentation.
   - Delivered at the end of Discovery phase and finalized in Design phase.

4. **Data Gap Assessment**
   - Analysis of current-state data quality and identification of missing or incomplete datasets required for L2A and CAC reporting.
   - Recommendations for resolving gaps and improving data accuracy.
   - Format: Written report (PDF) with summary tables.
   - Delivered midway through Discovery phase.

5. **Technical Approach**
   - Detailed documentation of system architecture and ETL (Extract, Transform, Load) pipeline requirements to automate data ingestion from QuickBooks and Salesforce.
   - Integration plan outlining necessary APIs and infrastructure within Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse.
   - Format: Technical whitepaper and architectural diagrams (PDF).
   - Delivered at the end of Discovery phase.

6. **Phase 3B Implementation Plan**
   - Roadmap for development, testing, and deployment activities covering the Design and Build phases.
   - Timeline, milestones, resource requirements, and dependencies for QuickBooks integration, automated reporting refreshes, and data quality improvements.
   - Format: Project management plan (Excel and PDF).
   - Delivered at the conclusion of Discovery phase to guide subsequent execution.

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC's leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
