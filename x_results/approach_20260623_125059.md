# Approach

PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC has engaged us to design and implement an enhanced Lead-to-AUM (L2A) pipeline and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting solution. This initiative will provide greater visibility and actionable insights into the effectiveness of Pure's marketing spend and customer conversion journey, supporting data-driven business growth and operational maturity. Our methodology aligns closely with Pure’s objectives: leveraging existing Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse infrastructure, integrating QuickBooks and Salesforce data, and producing sophisticated, scalable Power BI dashboards.

We employ a collaborative, phased approach focused on discovery, detailed design, and rapid build-out. Each phase will be anchored in stakeholder engagement, technical rigor, and targeted deliverables—ensuring both business and functional requirements are met. The engagement will utilize batch processing pipelines for data ingestion and transformation, automated report refreshes, and iterative review cycles to validate deliverables. Timelines and activities are strictly aligned to requirements and dependencies stated in the client questionnaire.

---

**Phase 3A: Discovery & Design, Duration: 3 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 to Week 3**  
**Summary:** Define KPIs, design the L2A and CAC data model, assess data gaps, and produce Power BI mock-ups.

**Activities:**  
- Conduct stakeholder interviews to confirm business priorities and pipeline requirements  
- Draft KPI Book detailing L2A and CAC metrics, calculation logic, and data mapping  
- Review Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks data sources for quality and integration feasibility  
- Assess data gaps and produce Data Gap Assessment report  
- Design the L2A and CAC data model leveraging Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse  
- Develop Power BI wireframes/mock-ups (up to 8 views) for reporting suite  
- Prepare Technical Approach and Phase 3B Implementation Plan

---

**Phase 3B: Build & Integration, Duration: 8–12 weeks, Timeline: Week 4 to Week 15**  
**Summary:** Implement data pipelines, integrate QuickBooks, automate batch processing, and deliver Power BI reporting suite.

**Activities:**  
- Build and configure automated data ingestion pipelines for Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks  
- Integrate QuickBooks data and validate for CAC reporting requirements  
- Implement data transformation logic for L2A and CAC metrics within Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse  
- Automate reporting refresh processes for scalable operations  
- Assemble the Power BI reporting suite (8-page), incorporating all KPI logic and visualizations  
- Conduct iterative testing and stakeholder reviews to ensure accuracy and usability  
- Deliver final documentation, including KPI Book, Technical Design, and Implementation Plan

---

Through this structured, phased methodology, Pure Financial Advisors LLC will achieve a comprehensive and scalable pipeline and CAC reporting solution. Our approach ensures transparency, accuracy, and actionable insights—driving business growth and maximizing marketing ROI, while supporting Pure’s long-term vision for data maturity and robust analytics.
