# Approach

PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC aims to enhance its Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) pipeline reporting, providing deeper visibility into client acquisition and marketing return on investment. Central to this engagement is the development of a scalable, integrated data model and reporting suite leveraging existing platforms—Microsoft Fabric, Azure Synapse, Power BI—and seamless integration of QuickBooks and Salesforce data. Our methodology is grounded in structured discovery, data-centric design, and agile build practices, ensuring alignment with defined business objectives and stakeholder needs.

The approach is organized into two core phases. Phase 3A focuses on design and requirements definition, laying the foundation for robust reporting and KPI logic. Phase 3B encompasses implementation, including building automated data pipelines, integrating QuickBooks, enhancing data quality, and configuring advanced Power BI dashboards. This phased plan facilitates risk management, stakeholder engagement, and incremental value delivery—culminating in actionable insight for senior leadership and marketing teams.

---

**Phase 3A: Discovery & Design, Duration: 3 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 to Week 3**  
**Summary:** This phase establishes and documents the foundational requirements, data logic, and reporting structures necessary for effective L2A and CAC analytics.

**Activities:**  
- Conduct stakeholder workshops with Data, Technology, and Marketing teams to refine reporting needs  
- Analyze source systems (Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, QuickBooks) for data model compatibility and gaps  
- Design the integrated L2A and CAC data models, specifying KPI logic and business calculation rules  
- Develop Power BI mock-ups (up to 8 views), incorporating key workflows and visualizations  
- Assess data quality for pipeline and CAC metrics to identify cleansing and transformation requirements  
- Prepare a Phase 3B Implementation Plan detailing technical steps, dependencies, and roadmap  

---

**Phase 3B: Solution Build & Integration, Duration: 8–12 weeks, Timeline: Week 4 to Week 15**  
**Summary:** This phase delivers the operational solution, including automated pipelines, integrations, data assurance measures, and actionable Power BI dashboards.

**Activities:**  
- Build and automate batch data ingestion pipelines on Microsoft Fabric for L2A and CAC reporting, integrating Salesforce and QuickBooks  
- Implement data transformation and quality assurance routines within Azure Synapse  
- Deploy the new L2A and CAC data model to the reporting environment, ensuring scalability and governance  
- Configure and publish Power BI dashboards, refining KPI definitions and visual interactions for end-users  
- Test end-to-end data flow, aligning metrics with business requirements and addressing integration challenges  
- Conduct user training and provide documentation to support adoption and change management  

---

By following this structured, phased approach, PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC will achieve a scalable, insightful reporting solution that improves data-driven decision making and marketing ROI analysis. Each phase is designed to build on the previous, minimizing risk and maximizing stakeholder ownership, ensuring the solution meets both current and future business needs.
