# Objectives

- Achieve a fully designed exit reporting suite featuring clear equity narratives, a prioritised KPI framework, dashboard mock-ups, and a scalable conceptual data model.  
- Enable automated data ingestion pipelines and recurring data refresh processes from source systems, ensuring up-to-date reporting throughout the transaction process.
- Provide senior leadership and advisors with investor-grade visibility into key business metrics, supporting the equity narrative and facilitating effective sale process communications.
- Enhance user experience with intuitive dashboards that align to equity narratives, incorporating clear KPI definitions, calculation logic, and navigation structure.  
- Standardise exit reporting, KPI definitions, and data infrastructure readiness to meet investor scrutiny and streamline business processes.
- Establish a solution designed as a lasting business asset, capable of full automation post-transaction and scalable with future technology choices.
- Ensure the data model and reporting suite are extensible to additional data sources and maintain ongoing refreshability beyond the immediate transaction.
- Integrate the future platform with Finance System, CRM, ERP, and Qlik Warehouse, with consideration for alternative tooling to support future growth.
