# Business Impact

- **Granular Marketing ROI Visibility:** The enhanced Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting suite will provide Pure Financial Advisors with precise insights into marketing effectiveness, enabling leadership to allocate spend toward the highest-performing channels and potentially reduce CAC by 10–15%.  
- **Operational Efficiency Through Automation:** Automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines will eliminate manual data handling across Salesforce, QuickBooks, and Tamarac, reducing reporting cycle times by up to 50% and freeing analyst capacity for higher-value analysis.  
- **Data-Driven Growth Decisions:** A unified data model and Power BI dashboards will allow senior leadership to track conversion metrics from lead to AUM in real time, supporting more informed decisions on client acquisition strategies and asset growth initiatives.  
- **Improved Data Quality and Consistency:** Integration of QuickBooks and data quality assessment within Microsoft Fabric will establish a single source of truth for financial and marketing data, ensuring accuracy and consistency across departments and stakeholder reporting.  
- **Scalable Reporting Foundation:** The future-state data platform will be designed for scalability, enabling Pure to seamlessly integrate new data sources and expand reporting capabilities as the business grows, supporting long-term data maturity and operational resilience.  

By delivering these outcomes, this engagement will enable Pure Financial Advisors LLC to transform its pipeline and marketing performance reporting into a strategic asset—driving data-led decision-making, optimizing acquisition efficiency, and underpinning sustainable growth in the competitive U.S. financial advisory market.
