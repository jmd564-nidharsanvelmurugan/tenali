# Understanding

- VONQ needs to design and build a scalable exit reporting suite that supports the M&A sale process and meets investor communication requirements.
- There is a lack of investor-ready reporting, with potential data gaps across various source systems.
- The current Qlik Warehouse may not fully support all KPIs required to articulate the equity narrative for the exit process.
- Mandatory integration of Finance System, CRM, and ERP data into the Qlik Warehouse is required, including the use of legacy sources to enable at least three years of trended data.
- Data ingestion and refresh pipelines from source systems into the Qlik Warehouse need to be automated, especially for sources requiring frequent updates during the transaction.
- Key requirements include equity narrative definition, a prioritized KPI framework, dashboard mock-ups, data feasibility assessment, conceptual data model design, and tooling recommendations.
- The intended user base includes senior leadership, exit advisors (Barclays), and potential acquirers or investors who require investor-grade, transparent insights.
- There is a dependency on timely data access, the availability of stakeholders for workshops, and the quality and completeness of existing Qlik Warehouse data.
