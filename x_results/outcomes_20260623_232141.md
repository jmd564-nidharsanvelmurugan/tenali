# Outcomes

The proposed engagement will empower PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC to achieve greater visibility and control over its sales pipeline and marketing effectiveness. By delivering automated, accurate Lead-to-AUM and Customer Acquisition Cost reporting, the organization will gain actionable insights that drive more informed decision-making and maximize return on marketing spend. This initiative is expected to significantly advance data maturity and support scalable business growth.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Granular Pipeline Analysis:** Enhanced L2A and CAC reporting enables detailed measurement of each stage in the client acquisition process, improving understanding of pipeline performance.
- **Actionable Marketing Insights:** Robust reporting on customer acquisition costs provides leadership with clear data on the ROI of marketing initiatives, facilitating more efficient budget allocation.
- **Automated, Accurate Reporting:** Streamlined data ingestion and transformation processes deliver reliable, up-to-date metrics and reduce manual effort for data and technology teams.
- **Improved Leadership Visibility:** Comprehensive Power BI dashboards arm senior leadership with timely, accurate KPIs—including Lead-to-AUM conversion rates and detailed CAC metrics—for strategic decision support.
- **Scalability for Future Growth:** The scalable data model and reporting suite lay the foundation for integrating future data sources and expanding analytics capabilities as the business evolves.
- **Data-Driven Decision Making:** By eliminating blind spots in pipeline and marketing reporting, the organization can act on trusted insights to enhance operational efficiency and fuel sustainable growth.
