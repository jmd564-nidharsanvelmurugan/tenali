# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with PURE Financial Advisors LLC, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

1. **KPI Book**  
- Comprehensive documentation of Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) KPIs, including definitions, calculation logic, and data source mapping.  

- Alignment of KPI taxonomy with industry standards and PURE’s internal reporting framework.  

- Clear linkage between KPIs and business outcomes to support marketing ROI analysis.  

2. **Power BI Mock-ups (up to 8 views)**  
- Design of interactive dashboard mock-ups illustrating L2A and CAC insights across the customer journey.  

- Visualization of key metrics, conversion funnels, and marketing performance indicators.  

- Layout and navigation blueprint to guide the subsequent build phase within Power BI.  

3. **Data Model Design**  
- Detailed schema for the integrated L2A and CAC data model connecting Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks.  

- Definition of data relationships, hierarchies, and transformation logic to support automated reporting.  

- Documentation of data lineage and governance considerations for scalable future-state architecture.  

4. **Data Gap Assessment**  
- Identification and evaluation of missing or inconsistent data elements impacting L2A and CAC reporting accuracy.  

- Recommendations for remediation and data quality improvement across source systems.  

- Prioritization of data gaps based on business impact and feasibility of resolution.  

5. **Technical Approach Document**  
- Detailed description of the technical integration strategy, including QuickBooks ingestion and transformation pipelines within Microsoft Fabric.  

- Specification of batch processing workflows, data refresh cadence, and validation checkpoints.  

- Outline of dependencies, assumptions, and integration testing plan to ensure robust data flow.  

6. **Phase 3B Implementation Plan**  
- Structured roadmap detailing activities, milestones, and timelines for the build and deployment of the L2A and CAC reporting suite.  

- Resource allocation and sequencing aligned with PURE’s internal teams and system dependencies.  

- Risk mitigation and change management considerations to support smooth implementation.  

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with PURE Financial Advisors LLC's leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
