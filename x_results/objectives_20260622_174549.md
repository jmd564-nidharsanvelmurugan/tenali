# Objectives

- Provide a comprehensive Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data model and reporting suite offering granular insights into pipeline performance and marketing return on investment.
- Automate data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes to ensure timely and accurate management information.
- Enable leadership to access precise and actionable insights regarding the journey from lead to asset under management and the effectiveness of marketing spend.
- Improve user experience through enhanced Power BI dashboards featuring clear KPI definitions and intuitive visualizations.
- Strengthen pipeline reporting and customer acquisition cost tracking to drive more informed business decisions and operational efficiency.
- Establish scalable reporting capabilities and data maturity to support future growth, integration of additional data sources, and evolving analytical needs.
- Ensure seamless and robust integration of the reporting platform with Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Microsoft Fabric data infrastructure.
