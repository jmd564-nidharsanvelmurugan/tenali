# Understanding

- Pure Financial Advisors seeks to address limited granularity in existing pipeline reporting and the absence of integrated Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) insights.  
- The current reporting process does not provide detailed visibility into the full Lead-to-AUM (L2A) journey, limiting understanding of marketing effectiveness.  
- Data ingestion from QuickBooks is not yet established, creating integration gaps that hinder accurate CAC measurement.  
- The firm requires an enhanced L2A and CAC data model capable of supporting detailed Power BI reporting and data quality assessment.  
- Manual or fragmented data handling processes need replacement with automated ingestion and transformation workflows to improve consistency and timeliness.  
- Key user groups include data and technology stakeholders, senior leadership, and marketing teams who rely on accurate, unified reporting for decision-making.  
- Mandatory integrations include Salesforce and QuickBooks to ensure complete visibility across lead generation, conversion, and financial performance.
