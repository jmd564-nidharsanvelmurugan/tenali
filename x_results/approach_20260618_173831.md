# Approach

PURE Financial Advisors LLC operates in the financial advisory and wealth management sector, serving individual clients through a B2C model. This engagement is designed to address PURE’s specific challenge of enhancing its Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting capabilities. By leveraging existing infrastructure—Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Fabric, Synapse, and Power BI—the approach focuses on integrating QuickBooks data, improving data quality, and developing a scalable data model that delivers granular insights into marketing ROI and pipeline performance.

The methodology follows a structured, phased approach that ensures alignment with PURE’s business priorities and technical environment. Each phase builds upon the previous one, moving from discovery and design through implementation planning. This progression enables the teams to validate assumptions early, mitigate integration risks, and deliver a robust foundation for automated, insight‑driven reporting.

---

**Phase 3A: Discovery & Design, Duration: 3 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 to Week 3**

**Summary:** Establish the foundational understanding of data sources, define KPI logic, and design the target‑state L2A and CAC data model.

**Activities:**  
- Conduct stakeholder workshops with data, technology, and marketing teams to confirm reporting requirements.  
- Assess existing data sources (Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, QuickBooks) for completeness and quality.  
- Define KPI logic and calculation methodology for L2A and CAC metrics.  
- Develop initial Power BI mock‑ups (up to 8 views) to visualize priority insights.  
- Perform a data gap assessment to identify missing or inconsistent data elements.  
- Document the technical approach for QuickBooks integration and data ingestion pipelines.

---

**Phase 3B: Implementation Planning & Technical Design, Duration: 8–12 weeks, Timeline: Week 4 to Week 15**

**Summary:** Finalize the data model design, confirm technical architecture, and prepare the implementation roadmap for automated reporting and integration.

**Activities:**  
- Validate and finalize the end‑state L2A and CAC data model based on feedback from Phase 3A.  
- Design automated batch data ingestion and transformation pipelines within Microsoft Fabric.  
- Define data cleaning and enrichment recommendations to address identified gaps.  
- Develop detailed technical specifications for QuickBooks integration and data flow orchestration.  
- Create the Phase 3B Implementation Plan outlining build activities, dependencies, and resource requirements.  
- Deliver the KPI Book, finalized Power BI mock‑ups, and data model documentation for stakeholder approval.

---

This structured, phased approach ensures that PURE Financial Advisors LLC’s data platform and reporting capabilities are developed with precision, agility, and a clear focus on delivering measurable business outcomes aligned with stakeholder expectations.
