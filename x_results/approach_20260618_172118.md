# Approach

The engagement with **PURE Financial Advisors LLC** is designed to address the firm’s specific need for enhanced **Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A)** and **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)** reporting within the financial advisory and wealth management space. As a B2C business operating across the United States, Pure requires a unified, data‑driven view of its marketing and sales funnel to better understand conversion efficiency and marketing ROI. This approach leverages the company’s existing Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse infrastructure, integrating Salesforce, Tamarac, and QuickBooks data to deliver a scalable, automated reporting solution in Power BI.

Our methodology follows a structured, phased approach that ensures alignment with Pure’s business priorities, technical landscape, and timeline expectations. Each phase builds upon the previous one—starting with data access and discovery, progressing through model design and mock‑ups, and culminating in a detailed implementation plan. This structure ensures that all stakeholders—from data and technology teams to senior leadership—are engaged at the right points, enabling a seamless transition from design to build.

---

**Phase 1: Discovery & Data Access Enablement, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 0 to Week 1**

**Summary:** Establish foundational access, documentation, and alignment to ensure the project begins with full visibility into existing systems and data sources.

**Activities:**  
- Coordinate with internal stakeholders to secure access to Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Fabric, and Power BI environments.  
- Gather and review existing data architecture, infrastructure, and reporting documentation.  
- Schedule and confirm workshops with data, technology, and marketing teams.  
- Validate data availability and reconciliation sources to ensure alignment with prior reporting outputs.  
- Confirm project governance, communication cadence, and deliverable expectations.

---

**Phase 2: Current State Assessment & KPI Definition, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 1 to Week 2**

**Summary:** Understand the current data landscape, define key metrics, and align on business logic for L2A and CAC reporting.

**Activities:**  
- Conduct workshops to consolidate KPI definitions and business terms across teams.  
- Review existing Power BI reports and data models to identify current limitations.  
- Map data sources and fields required for L2A and CAC calculations.  
- Identify data quality issues and dependencies, particularly around QuickBooks integration.  
- Document agreed‑upon KPI logic and data source mapping for inclusion in the KPI Book.

---

**Phase 3: Data Source Assessment & Target Model Design, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 2 to Week 3**

**Summary:** Evaluate data readiness and design the target‑state data model to support enhanced L2A and CAC reporting.

**Activities:**  
- Assess data from Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks for completeness and consistency.  
- Perform a data gap assessment to identify missing or low‑quality data elements.  
- Develop the conceptual and logical data model for L2A and CAC reporting.  
- Define data transformation and ingestion requirements for batch processing in Microsoft Fabric.  
- Validate model design with stakeholders to ensure alignment with reporting needs.

---

**Phase 4: Power BI Mock‑ups & Data Model Finalization, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 3 to Week 4**

**Summary:** Translate business requirements into visual reporting concepts and finalize the data model for implementation.

**Activities:**  
- Design up to eight Power BI mock‑ups illustrating L2A and CAC dashboards.  
- Iterate mock‑ups with business and technical stakeholders for feedback and refinement.  
- Finalize KPI logic and data relationships within the model.  
- Document data lineage and transformation logic for future build activities.  
- Prepare the draft KPI Book and Data Model Design deliverables for review.

---

**Phase 5: Technical Approach & Implementation Planning, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 4 to Week 5**

**Summary:** Define the technical approach and roadmap for Phase 3B implementation, ensuring readiness for build and automation.

**Activities:**  
- Develop the detailed technical approach for QuickBooks integration and automated data pipelines.  
- Outline the architecture for batch data processing within Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse.  
- Define testing, validation, and refresh processes for automated reporting.  
- Prepare the Phase 3B Implementation Plan, including dependencies, sequencing, and resource requirements.  
- Conduct a final review session with stakeholders to confirm alignment and next steps.

---

This structured, phased approach ensures that **PURE Financial Advisors LLC**’s L2A and CAC reporting capabilities are developed with precision, agility, and a clear focus on delivering measurable business outcomes. By grounding each phase in data quality, stakeholder alignment, and technical rigor, the engagement positions Pure to achieve scalable, automated, and insight‑driven reporting that supports its growth objectives.
