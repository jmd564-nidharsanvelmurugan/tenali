# Approach

PURE Financial Advisors LLC operates in a highly competitive financial advisory and wealth management market, where data-driven insights into client acquisition and marketing ROI are critical to sustaining growth. Our approach is designed to address PURE’s specific challenge of enhancing Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting by leveraging its existing Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse infrastructure. By integrating data from Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks, we will deliver a unified, automated, and scalable reporting solution that empowers leadership with actionable insights into pipeline performance.

Our methodology follows a structured, phased approach that ensures alignment with PURE’s business objectives, technical environment, and stakeholder expectations. Each phase builds upon the previous one—progressing from discovery and data assessment through to model design, mock-up development, and implementation planning. This ensures that the final solution is both technically robust and strategically aligned with PURE’s long-term data maturity goals.

---

**Phase 1: Discovery & Current-State Assessment, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 1**

**Summary:** Establish a foundational understanding of PURE’s current data landscape, systems, and reporting processes to inform subsequent design activities.

**Activities:**  
- Conduct kickoff sessions with data, technology, and marketing stakeholders to confirm objectives and success criteria.  
- Review existing documentation for Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Fabric, Synapse, and Power BI environments.  
- Assess current L2A and CAC reporting capabilities and identify data quality or integration gaps.  
- Map data flows across systems to understand dependencies and ingestion requirements.  
- Define access protocols and confirm data availability for QuickBooks integration.  

---

**Phase 2: Data Source Analysis & Gap Assessment, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 2**

**Summary:** Evaluate data sources and identify gaps or inconsistencies that may impact the design of the L2A and CAC data model.

**Activities:**  
- Profile data from Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks to assess completeness and accuracy.  
- Identify missing or inconsistent data elements required for CAC and pipeline reporting.  
- Document data lineage and transformation logic for key metrics.  
- Develop recommendations for data cleaning, enrichment, and standardization.  
- Validate findings with PURE’s data and technology teams to ensure alignment.  

---

**Phase 3: KPI Definition & Reporting Design, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 3**

**Summary:** Define the KPI framework and reporting logic that will underpin the L2A and CAC reporting suite.

**Activities:**  
- Facilitate workshops with marketing and leadership teams to finalize KPI definitions and calculation logic.  
- Align on key metrics for Lead-to-AUM conversion and Customer Acquisition Cost tracking.  
- Map KPIs to data sources and confirm transformation logic.  
- Draft the KPI Book documenting definitions, formulas, and data mappings.  
- Validate KPI logic through sample data extracts and stakeholder review.  

---

**Phase 4: Data Model Design & Power BI Mock-ups, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 4**

**Summary:** Design the target-state data model and develop Power BI mock-ups to visualize the future reporting experience.

**Activities:**  
- Design the L2A and CAC data model architecture within Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse.  
- Define relationships, hierarchies, and data transformations required for reporting.  
- Develop up to eight Power BI mock-ups illustrating key dashboards and visualizations.  
- Iterate mock-ups based on stakeholder feedback to ensure usability and clarity.  
- Align data model design with PURE’s scalability and integration requirements.  

---

**Phase 5: Technical Approach & Implementation Planning, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 5**

**Summary:** Finalize the technical approach and develop a detailed implementation plan for Phase 3B execution.

**Activities:**  
- Define the end-to-end data ingestion and transformation pipeline for batch processing.  
- Document the technical approach for QuickBooks integration and automation workflows.  
- Develop the Phase 3B Implementation Plan outlining build activities, dependencies, and timelines.  
- Provide recommendations for data quality monitoring and governance.  
- Present final deliverables including the KPI Book, data model design, and Power BI mock-ups for approval.  

---

This structured, phased approach ensures that PURE Financial Advisors LLC’s enhanced L2A and CAC reporting capabilities are developed with precision, agility, and a clear focus on delivering measurable business outcomes. By grounding each phase in data-driven insights and stakeholder collaboration, the engagement will establish a scalable foundation for ongoing analytics maturity and marketing performance optimization.
