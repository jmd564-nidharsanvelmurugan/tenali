# Outcomes

This engagement will position VONQ for a successful exit by delivering an investor-grade reporting suite that supports clear, data-driven equity narratives. By establishing robust KPI frameworks, actionable dashboards, and scalable data models, VONQ will be able to provide credible insights to stakeholders, mitigate transactional risks, and streamline exit timeline delivery.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Investor-Ready Reporting Suite:** Delivery of validated dashboard mock-ups and a comprehensive KPI framework aligned with equity narratives, ensuring VONQ can confidently present strengths and value to potential acquirers and advisors.
- **Clear Data Gap Visibility:** Identification and prioritization of current data gaps, empowering leadership to remediate critical issues before transaction due diligence, thus reducing risk of delays and reputational exposure.
- **Standardized KPI Framework:** Introduction of clear, investor-relevant metric definitions and calculation logic, enabling consistent and transparent reporting across commercial, financial, and operational domains.
- **Scalable Data Model Foundation:** Creation of a conceptual data model and extensible reporting architecture, ensuring readiness for integration of future data sources and seamless post-transaction scalability.
- **Improved Stakeholder Decision-Making:** Enhanced ability for senior leadership, advisors, and potential buyers to access accurate, investor-grade insights that underpin decision-making and streamline communication throughout the exit process.
- **Phase 2 Implementation Readiness:** Completion and sign-off of all design artefacts—KPI framework, dashboard mock-ups, data model, and roadmap—ensuring VONQ is fully prepared to proceed rapidly into build and deployment within exit timelines.

Success will be measured by sign-off on Phase 1 deliverables, timely identification and prioritization of data gaps, and stakeholder confidence in the reporting suite’s ability to support the M&A sale process.
