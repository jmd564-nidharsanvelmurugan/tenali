# Approach

PURE Financial Advisors LLC operates in a highly competitive financial advisory and wealth management market, where data-driven insights into client acquisition and marketing ROI are critical to sustaining growth. This engagement is designed to address PURE’s specific challenge of limited visibility into the Lead-to-AUM (L2A) pipeline and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) performance. By leveraging the firm’s existing Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse infrastructure, the approach focuses on designing and implementing a scalable, automated data model and Power BI reporting suite that delivers granular, actionable insights across the client journey.

Our methodology follows a structured, phased approach that ensures alignment with PURE’s business priorities, technical environment, and stakeholder expectations. Each phase builds upon the previous one—progressing from discovery and data assessment through to model design, mock-up development, and implementation planning. This ensures that the final solution is both technically robust and strategically aligned with PURE’s long-term data maturity goals.

---

**Phase 1: Discovery and Current-State Assessment, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 1**

**Summary:** Establish a foundational understanding of PURE’s current data landscape, systems, and reporting processes to inform subsequent design activities.

**Activities:**  
- Conduct kickoff sessions with data, technology, and marketing stakeholders to confirm objectives and success criteria.  
- Review existing data architecture documentation for Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Fabric, and Synapse.  
- Assess current Power BI reporting suite to identify gaps in L2A and CAC visibility.  
- Validate data access and connectivity requirements, including QuickBooks integration dependencies.  
- Define meeting cadence, communication plan, and deliverable expectations for the engagement.  

---

**Phase 2: Data Source Assessment and Gap Analysis, Duration: 2 weeks, Timeline: Weeks 2–3**

**Summary:** Evaluate data availability, quality, and integration readiness across all relevant systems to inform the target-state data model design.

**Activities:**  
- Profile data from Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks to assess completeness and consistency.  
- Identify data quality issues impacting L2A and CAC reporting accuracy.  
- Map data flows and dependencies between systems to support automated ingestion pipelines.  
- Document data gaps and recommend remediation or enrichment strategies.  
- Prioritize data sources and metrics critical to initial reporting use cases.  

---

**Phase 3: Data Model and KPI Design, Duration: 2 weeks, Timeline: Weeks 4–5**

**Summary:** Develop the target-state L2A and CAC data model and define KPI logic to support accurate, consistent reporting.

**Activities:**  
- Design the conceptual and logical data model for L2A and CAC reporting within Microsoft Fabric.  
- Define KPI calculation logic, data transformations, and metric hierarchies.  
- Align data model design with business definitions validated by key stakeholders.  
- Document data lineage and mapping from source systems to reporting layers.  
- Prepare the KPI Book detailing metric definitions, data sources, and calculation methodologies.  

---

**Phase 4: Power BI Mock-Up Development, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 6**

**Summary:** Translate business requirements and data model outputs into visual reporting prototypes for stakeholder validation.

**Activities:**  
- Develop up to eight Power BI mock-ups focused on L2A and CAC insights.  
- Incorporate user experience best practices to ensure clarity and usability.  
- Validate dashboard layouts and KPI visualizations with marketing and leadership teams.  
- Refine mock-ups based on feedback to ensure alignment with decision-making needs.  
- Finalize visual design standards for future reporting scalability.  

---

**Phase 5: Technical Approach and Implementation Planning, Duration: 1 week, Timeline: Week 7**

**Summary:** Define the technical architecture, integration approach, and roadmap for Phase 3B implementation.

**Activities:**  
- Develop the technical approach for QuickBooks integration and automated data ingestion pipelines.  
- Outline data transformation and refresh processes within Microsoft Fabric.  
- Define batch processing schedules and monitoring mechanisms.  
- Prepare the Phase 3B Implementation Plan detailing build activities, dependencies, and resource requirements.  
- Present final deliverables including the KPI Book, data model design, mock-ups, and technical documentation.  

---

This structured, phased approach ensures that PURE Financial Advisors LLC’s L2A and CAC reporting capabilities are developed with precision, agility, and a clear focus on delivering measurable business outcomes. By progressing methodically from discovery through design and planning, the engagement provides a solid foundation for scalable, automated reporting that empowers leadership with actionable insights into marketing performance and client acquisition efficiency.
