# Outcomes

The engagement with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC is designed to elevate the organization's data maturity and reporting precision by delivering robust Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) analytics. By automating data processes and integrating critical systems, leadership and marketing teams will gain actionable insights into the effectiveness of their pipeline and marketing investments. This will directly impact decision quality, operational efficiency, and support scalable growth.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Granular Pipeline Insights:** Enable detailed tracking from lead generation through AUM conversion, providing leadership with clear visibility into each stage of the customer journey and conversion performance.
- **Actionable Marketing ROI Analytics:** Provide integrated CAC reporting that equips stakeholders with precise metrics to assess and optimize marketing spend effectiveness.
- **Automated Data Operations:** Establish automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines for L2A and CAC reporting, significantly reducing manual intervention and accelerating reporting cycles.
- **Improved Decision Support:** Deliver accurate Power BI dashboards with well-defined KPIs, supporting data-driven decisions for senior leadership and marketing teams.
- **Scalable Reporting Framework:** Implement a flexible, scalable data model and reporting suite capable of adapting to future data sources and business growth requirements.
- **Risk Mitigation:** Address limited visibility risks by enabling comprehensive, timely, and high-quality reporting on pipeline and marketing performance, ensuring the business remains responsive and informed.
