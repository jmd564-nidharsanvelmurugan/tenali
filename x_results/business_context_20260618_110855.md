# Business Context

PURE Financial Advisors LLC operates in the U.S. financial advisory and wealth management sector, providing personalized financial advisory services and asset management solutions to individual clients. The firm follows a B2C model, focusing on helping clients grow and manage their assets through tailored investment strategies and financial planning. Key stakeholders include Jason Carver, Client Engagement Manager, and Lee Equity Partners, the firm’s investment partner.

PURE is undertaking this initiative to strengthen its data-driven decision-making capabilities by enhancing its Lead-to-AUM (L2A) pipeline and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting. The current reporting environment offers limited granularity and lacks integrated insights into marketing effectiveness and conversion performance. By improving the L2A and CAC data models and integrating data from Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure platforms, and QuickBooks, PURE aims to deliver more accurate, automated, and actionable reporting. This effort will enable leadership to better understand the journey from lead generation to asset growth and optimize marketing ROI, supporting the firm’s long-term vision of scalable, mature data and reporting capabilities.
