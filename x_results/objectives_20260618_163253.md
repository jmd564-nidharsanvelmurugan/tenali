# Objectives

- Establish a comprehensive Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data model that delivers granular visibility into the full client acquisition journey.  
- Provide leadership with actionable insights into pipeline performance and marketing ROI through enhanced Power BI reporting.  
- Automate data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes to ensure consistent, timely, and accurate insights.  
- Improve user experience with intuitive dashboards, standardized KPI definitions, and clear visualizations for decision-making.  
- Build a scalable reporting foundation capable of integrating additional data sources and supporting future growth initiatives.  
- Strengthen PURE’s data maturity by enabling a unified, trusted reporting environment across Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Azure Fabric.
