# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with VONQ, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

1. **Equity Narratives Definition**
   - Detailed documentation of the proposed equity stories that underpin the transaction and support the M&A sale process.
   - Alignment of narratives to stakeholder priorities, ensuring relevance for investor communications.
   - Delivered as a written report and presentation during Week 1 workshop.

2. **Metrics that Matter KPI Framework**
   - A prioritised list of critical commercial, financial, and operational KPIs required for investor reporting.
   - Clear definitions and calculation logic for each KPI, tailored to support equity narratives.
   - Delivered as an Excel/KPI register and supporting documentation by the end of Week 2.

3. **Dashboard Mock-ups**
   - Static wireframes illustrating the proposed structure, layout, and navigation of the exit reporting dashboards.
   - Visual representation of key metrics, views, and dashboard use cases to guide the Phase 2 build.
   - Delivered as PDF/PowerPoint mock-ups and discussed in a collaborative workshop during Week 3.

4. **Data Feasibility Assessment**
   - Review and playback of the current-state data landscape, highlighting data availability, integrity, and any identified gaps.
   - Recommendations for data remediation and mapping of trended data availability (minimum three years) from Finance, CRM, ERP, and legacy sources.
   - Delivered as a findings report and summary presentation by the end of Week 2.

5. **Conceptual Data Model Design**
   - High-level, extensible conceptual data model tailored to VONQ’s Qlik Warehouse and reporting requirements.
   - Documentation of model structure, source system mapping, and relevant design assumptions.
   - Delivered as a design document and model diagram in Week 3.

6. **Data Infrastructure and Tooling Recommendations**
   - Assessment and recommendation of reporting and analytics tooling options, with pros, cons, and suitability for future scalability.
   - Identification of automation opportunities and required enhancements to the Qlik Warehouse and associated pipelines.
   - Delivered as a written technical report by Week 4.

7. **Implementation Roadmap for Phase 2**
   - Phased plan covering priority use cases, technical dependencies, key milestones, and risk considerations.
   - Readiness checklist highlighting actions needed to transition from design to build in Phase 2.
   - Delivered in roadmap document format, reviewed with stakeholders in the final project week.

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with VONQ’s leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
