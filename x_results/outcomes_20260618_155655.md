# Outcomes

This engagement will deliver measurable business impact and strategic value by enabling Pure Financial Advisors LLC to achieve deeper visibility into its Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) performance. Through enhanced data modeling and automated reporting, the initiative aligns with Pure’s B2C growth objectives and supports both internal leadership and private equity stakeholders in making data-driven decisions on marketing ROI and pipeline efficiency.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Granular Lead-to-AUM Visibility:** The new L2A data model will provide detailed insights into each stage of the client acquisition journey, allowing leadership to identify conversion bottlenecks and optimize marketing strategies.  
- **Accurate Customer Acquisition Cost Measurement:** Integrated CAC reporting will deliver precise cost-per-client metrics by combining Salesforce, QuickBooks, and marketing data, enabling better evaluation of campaign effectiveness and spend efficiency.  
- **Automated and Reliable Reporting Processes:** Batch-based data ingestion and transformation pipelines will ensure consistent, timely updates to Power BI dashboards, reducing manual effort and improving data accuracy.  
- **Improved Marketing ROI Analysis:** Enhanced Power BI views will allow stakeholders to directly link marketing investments to AUM growth, supporting evidence-based decisions on budget allocation and channel performance.  
- **Scalable Data Foundation for Future Growth:** The solution will establish a robust, extensible data architecture within Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse, positioning Pure for future integration of additional data sources and advanced analytics capabilities.  
- **Increased Data Confidence and Decision Quality:** By addressing data quality and integration challenges, leadership will gain trusted insights that strengthen strategic planning and operational performance across acquisition and retention initiatives.
