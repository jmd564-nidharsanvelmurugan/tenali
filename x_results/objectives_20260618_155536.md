# Objectives

- Establish a comprehensive Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data model that delivers granular visibility into the full client acquisition journey.  
- Provide leadership with actionable insights into pipeline performance and marketing ROI through enhanced Power BI reporting.  
- Automate data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes to ensure accuracy, timeliness, and consistency of insights.  
- Improve user experience with intuitive dashboards, standardized KPI definitions, and clear visualizations supporting data-driven decision-making.  
- Build a scalable reporting foundation capable of integrating additional data sources and supporting future growth in data maturity.
