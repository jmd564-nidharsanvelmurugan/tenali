# Approach

This engagement with VONQ is designed to deliver a comprehensive and investor-ready exit reporting suite that supports the M&A sale process and associated stakeholder communications. The methodology leverages a structured, phased approach rooted in collaborative workshops, data feasibility assessments, and design artefacts—ensuring each deliverable is highly actionable and aligned to business objectives. The proposal prioritizes rapid execution, scalability, and data-driven narratives, tailored for the needs of senior leadership, exit advisors, and future acquirers.

The work will be structured into clearly defined phases. Phase 1 focuses exclusively on Discovery and Design, laying the foundation for an automated, extensible reporting solution. Activities in this phase will address equity narrative development, KPI framework definition, dashboard mock-ups, tooling recommendations, and a conceptual data model, culminating in an implementation roadmap to guide subsequent technical development. Only the timeline and technologies specified in the client questionnaire are used.

---

**Phase 1: Discovery & Design, Duration: 4 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 (29 June 2026) to Week 4 (24 July 2026)**  
**Summary:** Establishes the strategic and technical foundation for the exit reporting suite, aligning all stakeholders and validating feasibility ahead of Phase 2.

**Activities:**  
- Conduct stakeholder workshops to define equity narratives, reporting priorities, and user requirements  
- Develop and validate a Metrics that Matter KPI framework tailored for investor reporting  
- Create static dashboard mock-ups illustrating key metrics, layout, and navigation structure  
- Perform a comprehensive data feasibility assessment, including playback of current-state findings and identification of data gaps  
- Design a conceptual data model leveraging Qlik Warehouse as the primary platform, integrating Finance, CRM, ERP, and legacy sources as feasible  
- Produce data infrastructure and tooling recommendations, including an options trade-off analysis  
- Prepare a phased implementation roadmap setting out priority use cases, dependencies, risks, and key milestones for Phase 2

---

By adopting this phased approach, VONQ will benefit from clear alignment across business stakeholders, actionable design artefacts, and a validated pathway to investor-ready reporting. The outputs of Phase 1 will enable a seamless transition to technical implementation in Phase 2, ensuring efficient execution within the exit timeline and supporting the long-term scalability of business assets.
