# Approach

PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC seeks to advance its data-driven decision making by developing a granular Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting suite. This engagement will focus on a phased methodology, designed to create a scalable data model, automate integration pipelines, and deliver actionable insight via Power BI, aligning with the client’s growth and operational goals. The consulting approach leverages a blend of business discovery, technical design, and implementation planning to ensure seamless integration with Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Azure-based platforms.

Our methodology is grounded in best practices for enterprise data management and reporting. Each phase is structured to deliver incremental value, starting with discovery and data gap assessment, advancing to data model and reporting suite design, and culminating in technical integration and implementation planning. Activities are executed in close collaboration with PURE’s data, technology, and marketing teams, ensuring alignment with stakeholder expectations and rapid delivery within client-defined timelines.

---
**Phase 3A: Discovery & Design, Duration: 3 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 to Week 3**

**Summary:** Establish foundational understanding, assess data gaps, and design the target L2A and CAC data model and reporting suite.

**Activities:**
- Conduct stakeholder interviews to validate business objectives and success metrics
- Assess existing data sources (Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, QuickBooks) for coverage and quality
- Design the L2A and CAC data model to support detailed pipeline and marketing reporting
- Prepare Power BI report mock-ups (up to 8 views) for client review and feedback
- Develop KPI Book documenting metric logic, calculations, and business context
- Complete data gap assessment and define technical approach for QuickBooks integration

---
**Phase 3B: Build & Integration Planning, Duration: 8–12 weeks, Timeline: Week 4 to Week 15**

**Summary:** Implement automated data ingestion pipelines, integrate QuickBooks, and deliver the full reporting suite, alongside technical and implementation planning documentation.

**Activities:**
- Automate batch data ingestion and transformation pipelines using Azure Fabric and Synapse
- Integrate QuickBooks data and assess data quality for CAC reporting
- Validate and finalize the L2A and CAC data model in alignment with business requirements
- Develop and deploy the Power BI reporting suite with KPI definitions and visualizations
- Produce solution architecture documentation and technical integration approach
- Prepare Phase 3B Implementation Plan for production rollout and future scalability

---

This structured, phased approach ensures PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC will gain an accurate, actionable, and scalable reporting suite for Lead-to-AUM and Customer Acquisition Cost insights. By progressing through discovery, design, and implementation phases, the project aligns deliverables with stakeholder needs, supports business growth objectives, and establishes a robust foundation for ongoing data maturity and analytical excellence.
