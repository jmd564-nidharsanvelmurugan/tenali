# Objectives

- Develop a comprehensive Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data model and reporting suite that delivers granular insights into pipeline performance and marketing return on investment.
- Automate data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes to ensure timely and accurate data for analytics and decision-making.
- Provide leadership with a detailed understanding of the journey from lead generation to asset acquisition, including clear visibility into marketing effectiveness and customer acquisition costs.
- Enhance user experience by improving Power BI dashboards with refined KPI definitions and intuitive visualizations tailored to data, technology, and marketing stakeholders.
- Strengthen business processes related to pipeline reporting and customer acquisition cost tracking to enable actionable, data-driven decision-making.
- Establish a scalable reporting platform capable of supporting future data sources and expanded analytics requirements as Pure Financial Advisors LLC grows.
- Ensure seamless integration and interoperability with Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Microsoft Fabric platforms to unify data and streamline reporting capabilities.
