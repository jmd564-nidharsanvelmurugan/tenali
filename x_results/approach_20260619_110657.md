# Approach

The engagement with **Pure Financial Advisors LLC** is designed to address the firm’s specific challenges in achieving granular visibility across its Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting. Operating within the financial advisory and wealth management industry under a B2C model, Pure’s initiative focuses on leveraging its existing Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse infrastructure to integrate Salesforce, Tamarac, and QuickBooks data sources. This approach will enable Pure to automate data ingestion and transformation processes, enhance Power BI reporting, and deliver actionable insights into marketing ROI and pipeline performance.

Our methodology follows a structured, phased approach that ensures alignment with Pure’s business objectives, technical environment, and stakeholder expectations. Each phase builds upon the previous one—starting with discovery and data assessment, progressing through model design and mock-up development, and culminating in a detailed implementation plan. This structure provides clarity, governance, and measurable progress throughout the engagement.

---

**Phase 3A: Discovery & Design, Duration: 3 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 to Week 3**

**Summary:** Establish the foundation for the L2A and CAC reporting solution through data assessment, KPI definition, and initial model and dashboard design.

**Activities:**  
- Conduct workshops with data, technology, and marketing teams to confirm reporting requirements and success metrics.  
- Assess existing data sources (Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, QuickBooks) for completeness and quality.  
- Define KPI logic and calculation methodology for L2A and CAC metrics.  
- Develop initial Power BI mock-ups (up to 8 views) to visualize priority insights.  
- Document data gaps and provide recommendations for remediation and enrichment.  
- Finalize the target-state data model design and confirm technical approach for QuickBooks integration.

---

**Phase 3B: Implementation Planning, Duration: 8–12 weeks, Timeline: Week 4 to Week 15**

**Summary:** Translate the design outputs into a detailed implementation roadmap, ensuring readiness for build and automation of data pipelines.

**Activities:**  
- Validate the finalized data model against business and technical requirements.  
- Develop the Phase 3B Implementation Plan outlining sequencing, dependencies, and resource needs.  
- Define automated batch data ingestion and transformation workflows within Microsoft Fabric.  
- Establish data quality and governance recommendations for CAC reporting.  
- Refine Power BI mock-ups based on stakeholder feedback and finalize dashboard specifications.  
- Prepare technical documentation covering integration architecture, data flow diagrams, and refresh schedules.

---

This structured, phased approach ensures that **Pure Financial Advisors LLC’s** data platform and reporting capabilities are developed with precision, agility, and a clear focus on delivering measurable business outcomes. By aligning technical design with business priorities and stakeholder input, the engagement will provide a scalable foundation for ongoing data maturity and insight-driven growth.
