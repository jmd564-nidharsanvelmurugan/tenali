# Approach

The engagement with **PURE Financial Advisors LLC** is designed to address the firm’s specific challenges in achieving granular visibility across its Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting. Operating within the financial advisory and wealth management industry under a B2C model, Pure’s initiative focuses on leveraging its existing Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse infrastructure to unify data from Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks. This approach ensures that Pure’s marketing and leadership teams gain actionable insights into pipeline performance and marketing ROI while maintaining scalability for future data sources.

Our methodology follows a structured, phased approach that balances discovery, design, and implementation planning. Each phase builds upon the previous one—starting with data access and understanding, progressing through model design and mock‑up development, and culminating in a detailed implementation plan. This ensures alignment with Pure’s business objectives, technical environment, and stakeholder expectations throughout the engagement.

---

**Phase 3A: Discovery & Design, Duration: 3 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 to Week 3**

**Summary:** Establish a clear understanding of Pure’s current data landscape and design the target‑state L2A and CAC data model and reporting framework.

**Activities:**  
- Conduct workshops with data, technology, and marketing teams to align on KPI definitions and reporting priorities.  
- Review existing systems (Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, QuickBooks) and assess data availability and quality.  
- Perform a data gap assessment to identify missing or inconsistent data required for CAC and L2A reporting.  
- Design the target‑state data model, mapping data sources to KPI logic and transformation requirements.  
- Develop high‑level Power BI mock‑ups (up to 8 views) to visualize priority metrics and reporting use cases.  
- Document technical approach for QuickBooks integration and automated data ingestion pipelines.

---

**Phase 3B: Implementation Planning, Duration: 8–12 weeks, Timeline: Week 4 to Week 15**

**Summary:** Translate the design outputs into a detailed implementation roadmap and technical plan for scalable deployment within Pure’s existing data platform.

**Activities:**  
- Finalize the KPI Book, including detailed metric definitions, calculation logic, and data source mapping.  
- Validate the data model design against business requirements and ensure compatibility with Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse.  
- Define automated batch processing workflows for data ingestion, transformation, and Power BI report refreshes.  
- Develop the Phase 3B Implementation Plan outlining sequencing, dependencies, and resource requirements.  
- Conduct stakeholder review sessions to confirm alignment on deliverables and success criteria.  
- Prepare technical documentation to support future build and integration activities.

---

This structured, phased approach ensures that **PURE Financial Advisors LLC’s** data platform and reporting capabilities are developed with precision, agility, and a clear focus on delivering measurable business outcomes. By progressing from discovery through design to implementation planning, the engagement provides a robust foundation for scalable, automated, and insight‑driven reporting aligned with Pure’s growth objectives and stakeholder expectations.
