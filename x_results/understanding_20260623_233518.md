# Understanding

- PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC seeks to enhance and improve existing pipeline reporting with a focus on detailed Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) insights.
- Current reporting lacks sufficient granularity, limiting visibility into the journey from lead generation to AUM and hindering assessment of marketing effectiveness.
- Integrated CAC insights are missing, resulting in fragmented data and challenges in understanding true customer acquisition costs.
- The initiative requires an enhanced L2A and CAC data model with improved Power BI reporting capabilities.
- Automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines are needed to streamline L2A and CAC reporting processes.
- Data quality assessment is necessary for incoming data, especially with the integration of QuickBooks for expanded CAC reporting.
- Mandatory integrations include Salesforce and QuickBooks to ensure comprehensive data coverage.
- System users will include data and technology stakeholders, senior leadership, and marketing teams involved in pipeline and CAC reporting.
