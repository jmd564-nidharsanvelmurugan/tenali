# Approach

This engagement with fc is focused on delivering a robust Data Advisory solution to address customer churn within their direct-to-consumer (D2C) business model in Europe. Our methodology combines rigorous design and discovery disciplines with advanced data science techniques, ensuring the solution seamlessly integrates with fc’s existing infrastructure. Through a phased approach, we aim to provide actionable intelligence that empowers fc’s leadership, improves operational workflows, and supports ongoing scalability.

Each phase of the engagement is designed to generate key deliverables through targeted activities—beginning with discovery and culminating in actionable recommendations for churn mitigation. Our approach leverages a mix of stakeholder collaboration, data-driven analysis, and best practices in technical discovery, ensuring that outcomes are aligned with both near-term objectives and longer-term business growth.

**Phase 1: Discovery & Assessment, Duration: 2 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 to Week 2**  
**Summary:** Establish a thorough understanding of fc’s current data landscape, churn patterns, and technical requirements.  
**Activities:**  
- Conduct stakeholder interviews to capture business goals and success metrics  
- Review and assess existing data sources related to churn  
- Evaluate current data infrastructure and integration points  
- Perform initial exploratory data analysis  
- Identify high-level gaps and opportunities in current retention strategies  

**Phase 2: Data Science Design & Validation, Duration: 3 weeks, Timeline: Week 3 to Week 5**  
**Summary:** Design data science experiments and validate initial findings to identify key churn drivers.  
**Activities:**  
- Develop hypotheses on customer churn using exploratory data insights  
- Select and engineer relevant features for churn analysis  
- Design and test data science models in alignment with business objectives  
- Collaborate with technical teams to validate data quality and model assumptions  
- Document key findings and refine the analysis approach based on feedback  

**Phase 3: Solution Integration & Recommendations, Duration: 2 weeks, Timeline: Week 6 to Week 7**  
**Summary:** Deliver actionable recommendations and integration guidelines for operationalizing churn management and supporting organizational adoption.  
**Activities:**  
- Produce the Churn Analysis Discovery Report summarizing insights and model findings  
- Define integration requirements with existing data infrastructure  
- Facilitate workshops with internal teams to present results and gather input  
- Develop tailored recommendations for operational workflow enhancements  
- Support planning for scalable solution roll-out  

This structured, phased approach ensures clarity of purpose, timely delivery of insights, and actionable pathways for reducing customer churn. By grounding our engagement in both business context and technical rigor, we help fc drive data-driven decisions that are practical, scalable, and impactful for their D2C operations.
