# Approach

This engagement with VONQ is focused on designing a comprehensive, scalable exit reporting suite to underpin the company’s near-term M&A sale process. Our methodology emphasizes collaborative discovery, business alignment, and practical design artefacts that directly support investor communications and future technical implementation. The project centers on extracting maximum strategic value from VONQ’s existing data ecosystem (Finance System, CRM, ERP, Qlik Warehouse), while identifying—and bridging—critical data gaps and workflow inefficiencies.

The approach is structured as a single, 4-week Discovery & Design phase. This phase will leverage structured workshops, stakeholder collaboration, data analysis, and rapid prototyping to define equity narratives, a prioritized KPI framework, static dashboard mock-ups, a conceptual data model, tooling recommendations, and an actionable implementation roadmap. All workstreams are aligned to the exit timeline and will be delivered iteratively to ensure real-time validation and preparedness for subsequent technical build-out.

---

**Phase 1: Discovery & Design, Duration: 4 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 (29 June 2026) to Week 4 (24 July 2026)**  
**Summary:** This phase focuses on defining VONQ’s equity narratives, designing a prioritized KPI framework, developing dashboard mock-ups, assessing data feasibility, mapping a conceptual data model, and producing tooling recommendations and a roadmap for implementation.

**Activities:**
- Conduct project kickoff session and stakeholder alignment workshops, including requirements gathering with finance, commercial, data/analytics, and leadership teams.
- Define and document investor-aligned equity narratives and “metrics that matter” KPI framework, prioritizing commercial, financial, and operational metrics.
- Inventory and assess existing Qlik Warehouse data, CRM, Finance, and ERP systems; perform a data gap analysis and feasibility assessment for three years of trended data.
- Develop static dashboard mock-ups highlighting layout, navigation, and representative KPI visualizations aligned to equity narratives.
- Create a conceptual data model and high-level integration approach, using the Qlik Warehouse as the core platform and mapping required connections to Finance, CRM, ERP, and potential legacy sources.
- Evaluate and recommend tooling options (including pros and cons) for data ingestion, transformation, visualization, and refresh processes; prepare a detailed implementation roadmap specifying use case priorities, dependencies, risks, and milestones for Phase 2.

---

At the conclusion of Phase 1, VONQ will have a validated set of design artefacts: equity narratives, KPI framework, dashboard mock-ups, a conceptual data model, tooling recommendations, and an actionable roadmap. These deliverables will provide a clear, investor-ready blueprint for technical implementation, mitigate risks associated with incomplete reporting, and position the client for a successful transaction outcome within the desired timeline. The phased and collaborative approach ensures business objectives are met while enabling rapid mobilization for Phase 2 development.
