# Business Context

PURE Financial Advisors LLC is a United States–based firm specializing in financial advisory and wealth management services. Operating under a B2C model, PURE provides clients with tailored financial guidance and asset management solutions designed to support long-term growth and financial stability. The company’s operations are supported by key stakeholders including Jason Carver, Client Engagement Manager, and Lee Equity Partners, its investment partner.

PURE is undertaking this initiative to strengthen its data-driven decision-making capabilities by enhancing its Lead-to-AUM (L2A) pipeline and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting. The current reporting environment offers limited granularity and lacks integrated insights into marketing effectiveness and conversion performance. By improving the L2A and CAC data models and automating data ingestion from systems such as Salesforce and QuickBooks, PURE aims to deliver more accurate, actionable intelligence on the customer journey from lead generation to asset management. This effort will enable leadership to better evaluate marketing ROI, optimize acquisition strategies, and advance the firm’s overall data maturity.
