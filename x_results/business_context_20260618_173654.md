# Business Context

PURE Financial Advisors LLC operates within the U.S. financial advisory and wealth management industry, providing clients with comprehensive financial advisory and asset management services. The firm follows a B2C business model, serving individual investors and households seeking professional guidance in managing their assets and achieving long-term financial goals. Key stakeholders include Jason Carver, Client Engagement Manager, and Lee Equity Partners, the firm’s investment partner.

PURE is undertaking this initiative to strengthen its data-driven decision-making capabilities by enhancing its Lead-to-AUM (L2A) pipeline and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting. The current reporting environment offers limited granularity and lacks integrated insights into marketing effectiveness and conversion performance. By improving the L2A and CAC data models and automating data ingestion and transformation processes, PURE aims to deliver more accurate, actionable insights into the journey from lead generation to asset under management and the return on marketing spend. This effort will enable leadership to better understand pipeline performance, optimize marketing investments, and support scalable growth through a more mature data and reporting foundation.
