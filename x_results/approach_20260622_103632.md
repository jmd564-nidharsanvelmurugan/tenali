# Approach

PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC seeks to enhance its Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting to provide greater insight into pipeline performance and marketing ROI. Our methodology centers on a collaborative, data-driven engagement, leveraging PURE's existing Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse data platforms, with a particular focus on integrating QuickBooks, Salesforce, and other primary data sources into a scalable Power BI reporting suite. By employing a phased approach that emphasizes detailed discovery, structured design, and seamless build, we ensure the solution is robust, actionable, and aligned with PURE’s strategic objectives.

Each phase of the project will drive incremental value through targeted discovery, solution architecture, technical validation, and implementation planning. We will use batch-based, automated data pipelines to support scalable reporting, and will work closely with stakeholders across data, technology, and marketing teams to ensure all KPIs, data models, and dashboards are relevant and actionable. The engagement is structured into two key phases, Phase 3A and Phase 3B, totaling 11–15 weeks, ensuring clear deliverables, accountability, and measurable progress as we design and build the enhanced reporting foundation.

---

**Phase 3A: Discovery, Design, and Technical Validation, Duration: 3 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 to Week 3**  
**Summary:** This phase defines requirements, validates technical feasibility, and sets the foundation for a scalable L2A and CAC reporting solution.

**Activities:**  
- Conduct stakeholder workshops to refine business objectives, reporting requirements, and key metrics.  
- Perform technical discovery of current systems and data sources (Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, QuickBooks).  
- Develop KPI Book with detailed definitions and calculation logic for L2A and CAC metrics.  
- Analyze existing data model and gaps, focusing on ingestion and quality assessment for QuickBooks integration.  
- Produce Power BI dashboard mock-ups (up to 8 views) for L2A and CAC reporting.  
- Deliver the Technical Approach document and Phase 3B Implementation Plan.

---

**Phase 3B: Data Model Build and Reporting Suite Implementation, Duration: 8–12 weeks, Timeline: Week 4 to Week 15**  
**Summary:** This phase operationalizes the design by building automated data pipelines, integrating key systems, and deploying a production-ready Power BI reporting suite.

**Activities:**  
- Design, test, and deploy automated batch data ingestion pipelines for QuickBooks, Salesforce, and other sources within Microsoft Fabric.  
- Build, validate, and document the enhanced L2A and CAC data model, ensuring conformance with defined KPIs and business logic.  
- Develop and QA the Power BI reporting suite (up to 8 pages) with clear KPI visualizations and drill-through for user roles.  
- Perform end-to-end data quality assessment and implement data transformation logic to ensure trusted analytics outputs.  
- Conduct user acceptance testing with data, marketing, and leadership teams, capturing feedback for refinements.  
- Provide knowledge transfer and documentation on technical solution, data flows, and dashboard usage.

---

By taking this comprehensive and phased approach, PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC will gain a modern, integrated platform for pipeline and CAC reporting. This methodology ensures business alignment, technical quality, and stakeholder adoption, while supporting future scalability as new data sources and reporting needs emerge. Our focus on collaboration, automation, and actionable insight will enable PURE to drive data maturity, optimize marketing spend, and accelerate growth through informed decision-making.
