# Understanding

- PURE Financial Advisors seeks to address limited visibility and granularity in its existing pipeline reporting, particularly around the Lead-to-AUM (L2A) journey and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) metrics.  
- Current reporting lacks integrated CAC insights, preventing a clear understanding of marketing efficiency and return on spend.  
- Data ingestion from QuickBooks is not yet established, creating a gap in financial data required for accurate CAC analysis.  
- The absence of automated data ingestion and transformation workflows results in manual effort and potential inconsistencies in reporting.  
- A unified L2A and CAC data model is required to provide consistent, detailed tracking of lead progression and conversion performance.  
- Data quality assessment is needed to ensure reliability of inputs feeding into L2A and CAC reporting.  
- The system must support multiple user groups, including data and technology teams, senior leadership, and marketing stakeholders, each requiring tailored access to insights.  
- Integration with Salesforce and QuickBooks is mandatory to enable end-to-end visibility across lead, client, and financial data.
