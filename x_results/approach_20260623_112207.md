# Approach

This engagement with VONQ is designed to deliver a robust and scalable exit reporting suite, supporting the company's upcoming sale process and underpinning all investor and stakeholder communications. Our methodology is anchored in a business-outcomes-driven approach, emphasizing rapid discovery, collaboration with key stakeholders, and alignment of technical deliverables to support the equity narrative and transaction objectives. We will leverage workshops, iterative playback sessions, and targeted data assessments to ensure all design artefacts are investor-ready and actionable for the next phase.

Our work will proceed in a structured two-phase model. Phase 1 is a focused, four-week Discovery and Design effort, from 29 June to 24 July 2026, culminating in a comprehensive suite of design artefacts—equity narratives, KPI frameworks, dashboard mock-ups, conceptual data model, tooling recommendations, and an implementation roadmap. Technical development, system integration, and data remediation activities will be explicitly out of scope for this engagement, allowing us to concentrate all efforts on laying a foundation that enables rapid execution in subsequent phases.

---

**Phase 1: Discovery & Design, Duration: 4 weeks, Timeline: 29 June – 24 July 2026**  
**Summary:** This phase defines the strategic and technical foundation for VONQ’s exit reporting suite, ensuring all investor and transaction requirements are captured and design artefacts are readied for immediate hand-off to Phase 2.

**Activities:**
- Facilitate stakeholder workshops to define equity narratives, prioritize business KPIs, and establish dashboard use cases.
- Conduct a data feasibility assessment of Finance, CRM, ERP, and legacy systems, including identification of key data gaps and reconciliation requirements.
- Develop static dashboard mock-ups reflecting agreed metrics, layouts, and navigation logic, aligned with investor expectations and transaction needs.
- Design a conceptual data model leveraging the Qlik Warehouse as the core platform, explicitly capturing data lineage, refresh, and integration requirements.
- Produce tooling recommendations (including alternative options, pros, and cons) and draft an actionable implementation roadmap, detailing dependencies, risks, and milestones for Phase 2.
- Playback current-state findings, design concepts, and roadmap to stakeholders for validation and refinement.

---

At the conclusion of Phase 1, VONQ will receive all necessary artefacts required to seamlessly initiate the technical build and implementation of the exit reporting suite in Phase 2. This phased and collaborative approach ensures that all investor-grade requirements are embedded in design, mitigates project risks tied to timeline and data quality, and positions the organization for a timely and well-supported transaction process.
