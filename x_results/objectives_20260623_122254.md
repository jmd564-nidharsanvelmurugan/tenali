# Objectives

- Achieve a fully designed exit reporting suite that clearly articulates equity narratives and presents a prioritized framework of key performance indicators aligned to investor and advisor expectations.
- Establish automated, refreshable data ingestion and update processes from core business systems (Finance, CRM, ERP, and legacy data sources) to ensure timely and accurate reporting throughout the transaction period.
- Provide senior leadership and stakeholders with investor-grade visibility into commercial, financial, and operational metrics that underpin the equity story and support the sale process.
- Enable intuitive, user-friendly dashboards with transparent KPI definitions, calculation logic, and clear navigation to improve the overall reporting and decision-making experience.
- Standardize exit reporting workflows and KPI frameworks to better prepare the organization for investor scrutiny and ensure data infrastructure readiness.
- Ensure the reporting suite, data model, and underlying infrastructure are extensible and scalable, supporting integration of new data sources and future automation needs post-transaction.
- Integrate existing core systems (Finance System, CRM, ERP, Qlik Warehouse) into a cohesive reporting platform, with options for future tooling and technology enhancements evaluated for long-term business benefit.
