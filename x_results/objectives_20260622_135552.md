# Objectives

- Establish a comprehensive Lead-to-AUM and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data model and reporting suite that provides detailed, actionable insights into pipeline performance and marketing ROI.
- Automate data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes to ensure timely and accurate information for decision-making.
- Enable leadership to gain a granular understanding of the customer journey from lead to asset under management, enhancing visibility into marketing effectiveness.
- Improve the user experience with enhanced Power BI dashboards featuring clear KPI definitions and intuitive visualizations.
- Strengthen pipeline reporting and customer acquisition cost tracking to support continuous process improvement.
- Build a scalable reporting foundation capable of integrating with Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Microsoft Fabric, supporting future data sources and evolving business needs.
