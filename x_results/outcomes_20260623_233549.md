# Outcomes

The proposed engagement will enable PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC to achieve measurable improvements in pipeline visibility, marketing effectiveness, and operational efficiency. Through the development of detailed Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting, leadership will gain actionable insights to inform strategic decisions and support sustainable growth. This transformation will lay the foundation for data-driven performance management and scalable reporting capabilities.

## Expected Business Outcomes

- **Actionable Pipeline Insights:** Pure will obtain granular reporting on pipeline performance, allowing for a deeper understanding of lead conversion dynamics and business growth drivers.
- **Enhanced Marketing ROI Measurement:** Improved CAC reporting will enable accurate assessment of marketing spend efficiency and return, supporting more informed allocation of resources.
- **Automated and Accurate Reporting:** Automation of data ingestion, transformation, and refresh processes will deliver timely, consistent, and reliable reports for the data, technology, and marketing teams.
- **Integrated Power BI Dashboards:** Intuitive and visually enriched Power BI dashboards will provide clear KPIs, facilitating swift interpretation and communication of critical business metrics.
- **Scalable Data Platform:** The enhanced data and reporting solution will support future integration needs and business expansion, driving long-term data maturity for Pure.
- **Improved Leadership Decision Support:** Leadership will be equipped with comprehensive Lead-to-AUM conversion and CAC insights, empowering more precise evaluation of the customer journey and marketing strategies.
