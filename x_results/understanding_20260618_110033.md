# Understanding

- PURE seeks to address limited visibility into the full Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) journey and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) performance within its current reporting environment.  
- Existing pipeline reporting lacks sufficient granularity and does not provide integrated CAC insights, constraining leadership’s ability to evaluate marketing ROI.  
- Data ingestion and transformation processes are not yet automated, creating inefficiencies in maintaining accurate and timely L2A and CAC metrics.  
- The firm requires an enhanced L2A and CAC data model, improved Power BI reporting suite, and reliable data ingestion from QuickBooks to support comprehensive analysis.  
- Automated workflows are needed for data ingestion and transformation to reduce manual intervention and improve reporting consistency.  
- Primary users will include data and technology stakeholders, senior leadership, and marketing teams who rely on accurate performance metrics for decision‑making.  
- Mandatory integrations include Salesforce and QuickBooks to ensure complete visibility across lead management and financial data.  
- Security and compliance requirements are not specified in the current documentation.
