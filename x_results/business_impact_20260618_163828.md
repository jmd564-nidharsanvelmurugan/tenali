# Business Impact

- **Enhanced Marketing ROI Visibility:** Establishes a unified Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting framework, enabling PURE Financial Advisors to precisely measure marketing effectiveness and optimize spend allocation across channels.  
- **Data-Driven Growth Decisions:** Provides leadership and Lee Equity Partners with granular insights into lead conversion and asset growth, supporting strategic decisions that accelerate AUM expansion and improve client acquisition efficiency.  
- **Operational Efficiency Gains:** Automates data ingestion and transformation processes across Salesforce, QuickBooks, and Tamarac, reducing manual reporting effort by an estimated 40–60% and improving reporting timeliness.  
- **Improved Data Accuracy and Consistency:** Introduces standardized KPI definitions and a consolidated data model within Microsoft Fabric and Power BI, ensuring consistent performance tracking and eliminating cross-departmental discrepancies.  
- **Scalable Reporting Foundation:** Builds a future-ready data platform capable of integrating additional data sources and supporting new reporting needs, positioning PURE for sustainable growth and data maturity advancement.  
- **Investment-Grade Data Asset:** Creates a credible, diligence-ready reporting suite that strengthens PURE’s ability to demonstrate marketing efficiency and business performance to stakeholders and potential investors.  

By delivering these outcomes, this engagement will enable PURE Financial Advisors LLC to transform its pipeline and marketing performance management approach, driving long-term growth, operational excellence, and value creation within the competitive U.S. financial advisory marketplace.
