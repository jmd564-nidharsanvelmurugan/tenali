# Deliverables

Throughout the engagement with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC, the following key deliverables and artifacts will be provided to ensure a comprehensive and actionable outcome aligned with the project objectives:

1. **KPI Book**
   - Documentation of Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) metric definitions, calculation logic, and business context.
   - Tabular lists of all primary and supporting KPIs to be tracked within the future-state reporting suite.
   - Delivered as a PDF and editable document for stakeholder review during Phase 3A.

2. **Power BI Mock-ups**
   - Wireframes for up to 8 Power BI report views, focused on L2A and CAC reporting scenarios.
   - Annotated page layouts illustrating proposed filters, visualizations, and data interactions.
   - Delivered as static image files and Power BI preview files for stakeholder feedback at the midpoint of Phase 3A.

3. **Data Model Design**
   - Entity-relationship diagrams articulating source, target, and transformation of data for L2A and CAC tracking.
   - Data dictionary covering all fields, relationships, and calculated columns.
   - Delivered as a diagram file and supporting documentation toward the end of Phase 3A.

4. **Data Gap Assessment**
   - Identification of gaps between required data for reporting and available data in Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks.
   - Recommendations for addressing priority data gaps and risks for L2A and CAC reporting.
   - Delivered as a structured assessment document during Phase 3A.

5. **Technical Approach**
   - Blueprint for integration and automated data pipelines, with emphasis on ingesting and transforming data from QuickBooks and Salesforce.
   - Step-by-step outline of processing batch jobs within Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse.
   - Delivered as a technical whitepaper and process flow diagrams upon completion of Phase 3A.

6. **Phase 3B Implementation Plan**
   - Roadmap detailing project milestones, timeline, resource allocation, and dependencies for Phase 3B build-out.
   - Activity sequencing from solution design handoff to production deployment and training.
   - Delivered as a Gantt chart and project plan document at the conclusion of Phase 3A.

Each deliverable will be iteratively reviewed with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC's leadership and technical teams to ensure alignment with business goals and to incorporate feedback promptly. This structured approach guarantees transparency, accountability, and measurable value throughout the engagement lifecycle.
