# Approach

This engagement with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC is designed to transform the organization's lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) reporting capabilities, enabling data-driven decision making and greater visibility into pipeline performance and marketing ROI. Our methodology centers on close collaboration with stakeholders across data, marketing, and technology teams to ensure alignment with business objectives, scalability, and measurable outcomes.

The proposed approach adopts a phased delivery model to maximize value and agility. By sequencing discovery, design, and implementation activities, we focus on rapid realization of foundational insights followed by systematized solution deployment. Each phase is structured to produce tangible deliverables, with outputs including a comprehensive KPI Book, Power BI mock-ups, a robust data model, technical documentation, and an actionable implementation plan. Automation and data integration are prioritized throughout, leveraging Microsoft Fabric, Azure Synapse, and Power BI platforms as well as mandatory QuickBooks and Salesforce connections.

---

**Phase 3A: Discovery & Design, Duration: 3 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 to Week 3**  
**Summary:** Lay the groundwork through requirements gathering, data gap assessment, solution design, and mock-ups.

**Activities:**
- Conduct discovery workshops with data, marketing, and technology stakeholders to clarify L2A/CAC business requirements and KPIs.
- Assess and document current-state data sources (Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, QuickBooks), with emphasis on data quality for CAC reporting.
- Design enhanced L2A and CAC data model, including entity relationships, data flows, and business logic.
- Create Power BI report mock-ups (up to 8 key views) and define report navigation, KPIs, and visualizations.
- Deliver a comprehensive KPI Book detailing metric definitions and calculations.
- Draft the Technical Approach and Phase 3B Implementation Plan, including the roadmap for QuickBooks integration.

---

**Phase 3B: Implementation, Duration: 8–12 weeks, Timeline: Week 4 to Week 15**  
**Summary:** Develop and deploy the automated data pipelines, reporting suite, and integration with QuickBooks.

**Activities:**
- Configure and implement automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines in Microsoft Fabric for L2A and CAC data flows.
- Integrate QuickBooks with Fabric using batch processing to enable regular CAC data capture.
- Develop and validate the enhanced L2A and CAC data model within Azure Synapse and associated storage.
- Build and publish the Power BI reporting suite based on approved mock-ups, ensuring alignment with defined KPI logic.
- Conduct data quality validation, user acceptance testing, and stakeholder training on new reports and dashboards.
- Finalize project documentation, solution architecture, and transition materials for support and scalability.

---

This phased approach ensures PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC receives early clarity on solution requirements and key metrics, followed by rapid deployment of a scalable, automated reporting environment. By structuring activities and deliverables around stakeholder priorities and integrating core systems such as Salesforce, QuickBooks, and the Microsoft enterprise data stack, the approach will drive sustainable improvements in marketing effectiveness and business growth.
