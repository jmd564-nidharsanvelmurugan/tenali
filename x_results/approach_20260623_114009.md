# Approach

The proposed engagement with VONQ is structured as a targeted, high-impact consulting partnership to design a scalable, investor-ready exit reporting suite. Leveraging proven methodologies in data design, KPI framework development, and dashboard conceptualization, we will ensure all outputs are tailored to support VONQ’s near-term M&A objectives. Our approach prioritizes collaboration with key stakeholders, rapid assessment of the current state, and pragmatic recommendations to underpin the transaction with robust, defensible data assets.

Our methodology follows a focused Discovery & Design phase, delivered over four weeks, which culminates in comprehensive design artefacts. This includes equity narratives, a prioritised KPI framework, dashboard mock-ups, a conceptual data model, data infrastructure recommendations, and a detailed roadmap for Phase 2 execution. All activities are aligned to VONQ’s timelines, constraints, and strategic outcomes, ensuring an actionable pathway from design to implementation while minimizing business disruption.

**Phase 1: Discovery & Design, Duration: 4 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 (29 June) to Week 4 (24 July 2026)**  
**Summary:** This phase will assess current data and reporting capabilities, define investor-centric equity narratives and KPIs, create dashboard mock-ups, and provide a scalable design blueprint to guide the exit reporting solution.

**Activities:**
- Conduct stakeholder workshops to define equity narratives, reporting requirements, and key business questions
- Map and assess all available data sources (Finance, CRM, ERP, Qlik Warehouse, and legacy/Excel data) for feasibility and gaps
- Develop a “Metrics that Matter” KPI framework, including definitions, calculation logic, and prioritisation
- Design dashboard mock-ups aligned with equity narratives and end‑user personas, focusing on intuitive navigation and clarity
- Assess current data infrastructure and propose a conceptual data model leveraging Qlik Warehouse, with tooling and automation recommendations
- Develop a detailed Phase 2 implementation roadmap outlining priority use cases, dependencies, risks, and key milestones

---

By following this phased approach, VONQ will secure the critical design assets necessary to enable a successful, scalable build of investor-grade reporting capabilities. Each activity ensures that the solution is not only technically sound and scalable but also strategically aligned with the M&A process, empowering leadership to communicate the equity narrative with confidence and clarity. This engagement will position VONQ to swiftly progress to technical implementation in Phase 2, minimizing exit timeline risk and maximizing value delivery.
