# Objectives

- Establish a comprehensive Lead‑to‑AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data model delivering granular insights into pipeline performance and marketing ROI.  
- Automate data ingestion, transformation, and reporting refresh processes to ensure timely and accurate insights.  
- Provide leadership with a clear, data‑driven view of the customer journey from lead generation through to assets under management.  
- Enhance Power BI dashboards with standardized KPI definitions and intuitive visualizations for improved user experience.  
- Build a scalable reporting foundation capable of supporting future data sources and expanded analytics needs.  
- Advance PURE’s overall data maturity by enabling consistent, trusted, and actionable reporting across business functions.
