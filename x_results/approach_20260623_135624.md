# Approach

The engagement with PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC focuses on designing and building an enhanced Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data model and reporting suite, leveraging existing data infrastructure and integrating additional data sources as needed. Our methodology is grounded in a phased approach that prioritizes discovery, solution architecture, and data integration, ensuring a robust foundation for Power BI reporting that delivers actionable business insights. Close collaboration with Pure Financial’s business and technology stakeholders will guide requirements, validate interim outputs, and drive alignment throughout.

The solution will leverage Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse platforms, supporting batch data integration from Salesforce, QuickBooks, Tamarac, and Azure Blob Storage. Automated pipelines will underpin the future-state data model, automating ingestion, transformation, and Power BI reporting refresh processes. Key deliverables include a KPI Book, Power BI mock-ups, data model documentation, data gap assessment, and a technical implementation roadmap—all designed to support Pure’s data-driven growth objectives and scalable reporting strategy.

---

**Phase 3A: Discovery & Solution Design, Duration: 3 weeks, Timeline: Week 1 to Week 3**  
**Summary:** Establish requirements, assess data gaps, and design the L2A and CAC solution architecture.

**Activities:**  
- Conduct stakeholder workshops (data, marketing, and tech teams) to refine reporting and integration requirements  
- Profile source data in Salesforce, Tamarac, Azure Blob Storage, and QuickBooks to assess readiness and gaps  
- Define comprehensive KPI logic for L2A and CAC metrics, documented in a KPI Book  
- Design the conceptual and logical data model for L2A/CAC, aligned with Microsoft Fabric architecture  
- Prepare Power BI report mock-ups (up to 8 views) and capture dashboard requirements  
- Develop a detailed Phase 3B Implementation Plan and technical approach for QuickBooks integration  

---

**Phase 3B: Data Integration, Build & Deployment, Duration: 8–12 weeks, Timeline: Week 4 to Week 15**  
**Summary:** Implement automated data pipelines, integrate QuickBooks, and build the Power BI reporting suite.

**Activities:**  
- Develop and operationalize batch data ingestion and transformation pipelines in Microsoft Fabric  
- Integrate QuickBooks data, resolving field-level mapping and data quality issues for CAC metrics  
- Implement the L2A and CAC data model within Fabric and Synapse, ensuring scalability for future sources  
- Configure and validate data sets and relationships for Power BI reporting  
- Build and test the 8-page Power BI report suite, incorporating KPIs and stakeholder feedback  
- Deliver training and knowledge transfer to Pure’s data and marketing teams  

---

By adhering to this phased approach, PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC will achieve accurate, granular, and automated L2A and CAC reporting within a scalable framework. The sequenced methodology ensures that all deliverables are actionable, integrated with core business systems, and positioned to provide stakeholders with critical insights into pipeline performance and marketing ROI.
