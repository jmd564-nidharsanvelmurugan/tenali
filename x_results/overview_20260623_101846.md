# Overview

PURE FINANCIAL ADVISORS LLC’s operations related to pipeline and customer acquisition reporting currently involve the data, technology, and marketing teams. These groups collaborate to manage and analyze information critical to tracking the lead-to-AUM (L2A) pipeline and customer acquisition cost (CAC) metrics.

Key systems supporting these processes include Salesforce and Tamarac for customer and asset data, Azure Blob Storage for scalable data storage, and Power BI as the primary analytics and reporting suite. Microsoft Fabric and Azure Synapse serve as the core cloud-based data platforms underpinning data integration, storage, and transformation activities. QuickBooks is also identified as a relevant data source, with ongoing initiatives to better connect and ingest its data for CAC reporting purposes.

At present, Power BI is leveraged for reporting, enabling the organization to visualize and monitor performance based on integrated data from Salesforce, Tamarac, and Azure platforms. There are active integration efforts focused on establishing data flows from QuickBooks while ensuring the quality of data ingested for reporting needs. The existing technology environment relies on batch processing for data pipelines within the Fabric data platform.
