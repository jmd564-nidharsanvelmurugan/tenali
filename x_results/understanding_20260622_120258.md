# Understanding

- Limited granularity in existing pipeline reporting prevents detailed insight into the journey from lead to assets under management (AUM) and marketing effectiveness.
- Lack of integrated Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) insights hinders PURE’s ability to accurately measure and optimize marketing ROI.
- Requirement for enhanced Lead-to-AUM (L2A) and CAC data models to improve the precision and utility of reporting.
- Mandatory integration with QuickBooks and Salesforce, with an emphasis on data ingestion and assessing data quality for accurate CAC analysis.
- Need for automated data ingestion and transformation pipelines to streamline L2A and CAC reporting processes.
- The Power BI reporting suite must be upgraded to deliver focused insights and visualizations tailored to pipeline and CAC metrics.
- Data and technology stakeholders, senior leadership, and marketing teams are the primary users of the enhanced reporting system.
- Time-sensitive dependencies exist, particularly concerning the ingestion of QuickBooks data and completion of data quality assessments.
